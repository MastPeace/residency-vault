## Changed (10)

1. **Japan** — Permanent residence rules overhauled: income threshold raised to ~5.75M yen/year, 30 years of pension contributions required, Japanese B1 proficiency, fee increased to 200,000 yen, spouse path tightened to 5 years marriage + 3 years residence. Most changes effective April 2027. ^[https://vialtopartners.com/regional-alerts/japan-immigration-significant-changes-to-permanent-residence-rules]

2. **Armenia** — New residence permit rules from November 1, 2026: state duty 150,000 dram ($414) for temporary, 250,000 dram ($690) for permanent, online application, 30-day processing. Affects individual entrepreneur and business routes. ^[https://internationalinvestment.biz/en/migration/8907-how-to-get-a-residence-permit-in-armenia-in-2026-what-changes-from]

3. **Turkey** — Updated citizenship-by-investment regulation published October 27, 2026: real estate threshold $400,000, valuation rules changed September 28, 2026. Council of Ministers can adjust thresholds by presidential decree. ^[https://www.delilavukatlik.com/en/post/turkish-citizenship-by-investment]

4. **Guyana** — Work entry permit validity de facto reduced from 6 to 3 months (PTL, EVOA, Work Permit Approval Letters). Work permits now issued simultaneously with entry rights, not after arrival. Two extensions possible: 3 months + 1 month, then full re-application. ^[https://www.fragomen.com/insights/guyana-approval-validity-reduced-work-authorization-timelines-tightened.html]

5. **Namibia** — New "Employment Permit to Conduct Business" for foreign investors since September 1, 2026: up to 5 years (was 2), application via NIPDB, fee 2,600 NAD. ^[https://www.fragomen.com/insights/namibia-new-investor-and-business-owner-permit-introduced.html]

6. **Malaysia** — DE Rantau Nomad Pass 2-year extension for holders reaching stay limit (Budget 2027, announced October 9). New 5% special tax rate for global service hubs. ^[https://world.medianama.com/malaysia-plans-two-year-extension-for-digital-nomad-pass-holders-reaching-stay-limit]

7. **Morocco** — Short-Term Work Authorization (STWA) introduced: no labor market test, all professions eligible, requires social security and tax registration. ^[https://www.fragomen.com/insights/morocco-new-short-term-work-authorization-available.html]

8. **New Zealand** — Four partner/child visa categories migrating to Immigration Online platform from November 16, 2026. Guest visa maximum duration for children of citizens/residents extended from 9 to 12 months. ^[https://www.legit.ng/people/1738124-new-zealand-lists-4-visa-categories-affected-by-immigration-rule]

9. **Cuba** — New migration law effective November 2026: two citizen categories (resident in national territory / resident abroad), "effective migratory residence" with 120-day minimum presence requirement, investor and entrepreneur status introduced. ^[https://en.cibercuba.com/noticias/2026-10-06-u1-e13-s27061-nid342057-cuba-cambia-sus-reglas-migratorias-1-noviembre-deben]

10. **Hong Kong** — First five-year plan and 2026 Policy Address: "30,000 Youth Employment and Internship" programme launched, Tier 2 vetting mechanism expanded to new industries, MPF pension portability introduced. ^[https://www.humanresourcesonline.net/hong-kong-unveils-first-five-year-plan-and-policy-address-prioritising-ai-talent-and-population-development]
