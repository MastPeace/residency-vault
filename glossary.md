## How to read this site

Every country page has a header with key facts, a monitoring panel, the conditions of each program, and a Country Snapshot table with sourced numbers. This glossary explains the columns, labels and abbreviations you will meet there.

## Country list columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Country | Country name, links to its page. |
| Region | Geographic region used for filtering. |
| Fit | Editorial tags assigned by this project (see **Fit tags** below). |
| To citizenship | Years of legal residence before you can apply for citizenship under the standard route. `10+ yr` means ten years or more; `no path` means there is no realistic route for a foreigner. |
| Passport | Passport strength: the number of destinations a holder of that country's passport can enter visa-free (a Henley-type visa-free count). It describes the passport you would get after naturalisation, not the one you hold now. |
| Confidence | How well the page is backed by sources (see **Confidence** below). |

## Confidence

- **High**: key facts are confirmed by official or authoritative sources.
- **Medium**: a mix of official sources and secondary or estimated data.
- **Low**: mostly unverified, or important data is missing. Check the sources before relying on it.

## Fit tags

Tags are editorial and help you filter the list. They are not official classifications.

- **Strong passport**: citizenship would give a high visa-free count.
- **IT-friendly**: a remote-work or tech-friendly route plus a workable environment for IT specialists.
- **Clear pathway**: a well-defined, documented route from residence to a passport.
- **Popular with RU**: a common destination for Russian-speaking applicants.
- **Yacht captain**: a country with a maritime or yachting infrastructure relevant to captains.

## Country header facts

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Years to citizenship | Years of legal residence required for naturalisation, with any physical-presence rule (for example 183 days a year). |
| Passport | Visa-free count of the passport after citizenship. |
| Remote-work visa | Whether a digital-nomad or similar remote-work residence exists, with its income threshold. |
| Tax, remote workers | Rough effective personal income tax for a remote worker. A planning estimate, not tax advice. |
| 1-bed rent / month | Monthly rent for a one-bedroom apartment, usually in the city centre. |
| Language | Main official language. |

## Country Snapshot fields

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Population | Total population. |
| Main religion / Main language / Main city | Largest religion, official language, and biggest or capital city. |
| On the coast? | Whether the country has a coastline, with a short note. |
| Tourism impact in season | How strongly seasonal tourism affects daily life and prices. |
| GDP per capita (PPP) | Economic output per person in US dollars, adjusted for purchasing power parity (PPP) so countries are comparable. |
| Median net monthly salary | Typical take-home monthly pay. |
| Cost of living index | Numbeo-style index where New York City is 100; lower means cheaper. |
| Monthly rent, 1-bedroom apartment | Same as 1-bed rent / month above. |
| Effective personal tax rate for remote workers | Approximate share of income paid as tax by a remote worker. |
| Remote-work / self-employment residence available? | Whether a residence route for remote work or self-employment exists. |
| Years to citizenship | See above. |
| Maximum permitted absence during residence | How long you may stay outside the country without breaking the residence clock. |
| Passport strength after citizenship | Visa-free count, see above. |
| Rule of Law Index | World Justice Project score from 0 to 1 (higher is better) and rank. |
| Freedom House score | Score out of 100 and status: Free, Partly Free or Not Free. |
| Average annual sunshine hours | Hours of sunshine per year, usually for the main city. |
| Average annual temperature | Mean yearly temperature, usually for the main city. |

## Source marks

- **✓**: the value comes from an official or authoritative source.
- **~**: an estimate or a secondary source; the value may be outdated.
- **No reliable data / No recent data**: nothing credible was found. The field stays empty on purpose rather than guessed.

## Program types

Each type has a comparison page across countries.

- [Investment residency](concepts/investment-residency.html): residence in exchange for an investment, often called a Golden Visa.
- [Citizenship by investment](concepts/citizenship-by-investment.html): a passport in exchange for a contribution or investment (CBI).
- [Passive-income residency](concepts/passive-income-residency.html): residence based on stable income that does not require local work.
- [Retired residency](concepts/retired-residency.html): residence for pensioners with a foreign pension.
- [Digital nomad visa](concepts/digital-nomad-visa.html): residence for people who work remotely for foreign clients or employers.
- [Employment residency](concepts/employment-residency.html): residence tied to a local job offer.
- [Highly skilled employment](concepts/highly-skilled-employment.html): fast-track permits for qualified specialists, including the EU Blue Card.
- [Startup / entrepreneur](concepts/startup-entrepreneur.html): residence for founders and business owners.
- [Student residency](concepts/student-residency.html): residence for enrolled students.
- [Family reunification](concepts/family-reunification.html): residence for relatives of a legal resident or citizen.

## Abbreviations

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Golden Visa (GV) | Investment residency programme. |
| CBI | Citizenship by investment. |
| DN / Digital Nomad Visa | Residence permit for remote workers. |
| FIP | Financially Independent Person, a passive-income route (for example in Greece). |
| D7 / D8 | Portuguese residence visas: D7 for passive income and retirees, D8 for digital nomads. |
| Non-Lucrative | Spanish residence for people living on savings or passive income without working locally. |
| Blue Card | EU permit for highly qualified workers. |
| Schengen | The EU zone without internal border checks; a short-stay visa works in all member states. |
| EU / EEA | European Union / European Economic Area; citizens move and work freely across them. |
| non-dom | Special tax regime where foreign-source income is taxed lightly or not at all. |
| PPP | Purchasing power parity. |
| FATF | Financial Action Task Force; its grey and black lists flag countries with weak anti-money-laundering controls and can slow bank transfers. |
| CRS | Common Reporting Standard: automatic exchange of bank-account information between tax authorities. |
| YoY | Year over year. |
| RU | Russian citizens or the Russian-speaking audience. |
