## Definition
A non-lucrative visa (NLV) grants residence to foreign nationals who can support themselves from passive income or savings without engaging in any gainful activity in the host country. This includes a prohibition on remote work for foreign employers — a critical distinction from [[digital-nomad]] visas. NLVs are the classic route for retirees, rentiers, and financially independent individuals.

## Current State (Sep 2026)
The Spanish NLV (Visado de Residencia No Lucrativa) is the best-known programme. With Spain's [[golden-visa]] closed, the NLV has become the primary residence route for financially independent non-EU nationals. Similar programmes exist under different names in Portugal ([[d7-visa]]), Italy (Elective Residence), Ireland (Stamp 0), and Austria (Settlement – Gainful Employment Excepted).

## Key Differences
- **Portugal D7**: allows local work — technically not "non-lucrative" but same financial self-sufficiency basis
- **Spain NLV**: strict no-work rule, including remote work
- **Italy Elective Residence**: also strict; competition with the [[digital-nomad]] visa for remote workers
