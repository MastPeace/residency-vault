## Definition
The D7 Visa is a Portuguese residence visa for non-EU nationals with stable passive income (pensions, rental income, dividends). The concept extends beyond Portugal — many countries offer residence permits for financially self-sufficient individuals who can support themselves without local employment. Common names: D7 (Portugal), [[non-lucrative-visa]] (Spain), Elective Residence Visa (Italy), Stamp 0 (Ireland), Settlement Permit – Gainful Employment Excepted (Austria).

## Current State (Sep 2026)
Passive income visas remain open across most EU countries. Income thresholds are generally tied to national minimum wage or welfare indicators. These visas typically prohibit ANY form of work (including remote work for foreign employers), distinguishing them from [[digital-nomad]] visas.

## Per-Country Summary

| Country | Programme | Income Threshold | Work Permitted | Path to Citizenship |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|----------------|---------------------|
| [[portugal]] | D7 Visa | ≥ €920/mo (2026 min wage) | Local work permitted | 10 years residence |
| [[spain]] | [[non-lucrative-visa]] | €2,400/mo (400% IPREM) | None (not even remote) | 10 years |
| [[italy]] | Elective Residence | ≥ €31,000/year | None | 10 years |
| [[ireland]] | Stamp 0 | €50,000/year + lump sum | None | Does not count |
| [[austria]] | Settlement – Gainful Employment Excepted | Sufficient means | None | 10 years (restrictive) |
| [[greece]] | Financially Independent Person | €2,000/mo | None | 7 years |

The D7's distinguishing feature is that it allows local work — most other passive-income visas prohibit it.
