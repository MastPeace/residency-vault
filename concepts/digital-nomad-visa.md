## Definition

Digital nomad visa — residence permits for remote workers employed by foreign companies or freelancing for foreign clients, performed via ICT. These are distinct from passive-income visas: active remote work is the defining feature (and typically the ONLY permitted work). Income thresholds range from ~$1,042/mo (Colombia) to €4,500/mo (Estonia). Some DN visas lead to permanent residency (Spain, Portugal); others are time-limited and non-renewable (Japan 6 months) or dead-end (Costa Rica 2 yr max).

Distinct from tourist visas: DN visas grant legal residence for remote work. The EU has emerged as the dominant bloc: Portugal D8, Spain DNV, Italy DNV, Greece DNV, Croatia DNV, Estonia DNV, Malta NRP are all active. Some notable absences: France banned remote work under visitor visa (Apr 2026); Germany has no dedicated DN visa.

## Current State

28 countries in the wiki offer digital nomad visas. The EU leads with ~12 programs. Latin America and the Caribbean are the second hub (Costa Rica, Panama, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Argentina, Barbados, Antigua — the latter's ended). Africa is emerging (Kenya, Mauritius, Namibia, Seychelles). Asia is sparse: Japan (6 months, non-renewable), South Korea (announced), Thailand (DTV). Notable DN hubs without a formal visa: Germany, France, UK, Switzerland.
