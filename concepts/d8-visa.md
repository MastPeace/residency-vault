## Definition
The D8 Visa is Portugal's dedicated residence visa for digital nomads and remote workers. It allows non-EU nationals to legally reside in Portugal while working remotely for foreign employers or freelancing with foreign clients. It is separate from the [[d7-visa]] (passive income) and the [[golden-visa]] (investment). The D8 was introduced as part of Portugal's broader strategy to attract remote talent.

## Current State (Sep 2026)
- **Income threshold**: ≥ 4× Portuguese minimum wage (~€3,680/month in 2026)
- **Initial validity**: 1 year; renewable for 2 + 2 years
- **After 5 years**: eligible for permanent residence and citizenship (under the new 10-year rule, affects timing)
- **Tax**: Standard resident taxation OR [[nhr]] (20% flat) if qualifying profession
- **Application**: at Portuguese consulate abroad or in-country for those already legally present
