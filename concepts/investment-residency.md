## Definition

Investment residency — residence permits granted in exchange for qualifying investment (real estate, funds, business, government bonds, or bank deposits). Also known as Golden Visa or RBI (Residence by Investment). Unlike citizenship-by-investment, this grants residence rights only — citizenship is a separate process requiring physical presence and language. Programs range from €250,000 (Greece conversion tier) to €2,000,000+ (NZ Active Investor). Minimum physical presence is often relaxed (7 days/year in Portugal, none in UAE/Greece).

## Current State

59 countries in the wiki offer investment residency programs, though quality varies dramatically. The EU is tightening: Spain closed its Golden Visa (Apr 2025), Netherlands ended its investor visa (2024), Ireland's IIP is closed. Portugal's real estate route closed (Oct 2023) — fund-only now. Greece remains the most affordable EU option (€250,000 conversion tier). Caribbean CBI overlaps with this category but grants citizenship directly — see [[citizenship-by-investment]]. Malta's MPRP grants permanent residency from day one (rent or buy options). Panama's Qualified Investor Visa gives direct PR from $300,000 (rising to $500,000 Oct 2026).
