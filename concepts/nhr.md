## Definition
NHR (Non-Habitual Resident) is Portugal's special tax regime that offers reduced taxation (20% flat or 0%) on qualifying foreign-source income for 10 years. The concept extends more broadly: many countries offer special tax regimes for new residents to attract talent, investment, and remote workers. This page covers the most relevant tax-haven-like regimes for individuals in the watchlist countries.

## Portugal — NHR
- 20% flat rate on qualifying Portuguese-source professional income
- 0% tax on most foreign-source income (dividends, interest, capital gains, rental income)
- Foreign pensions: 10% (since 2024 reform)
- Duration: 10 years
- **NHR 2.0 (IFICI)**: for R&D and innovation professionals (2024 onwards)
- Applies to new tax residents who were not Portuguese tax residents in the preceding 5 years
