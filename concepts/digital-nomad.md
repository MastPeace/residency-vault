## Definition
A Digital Nomad visa (or residence permit) allows non-EU remote workers and freelancers to legally reside in a country while working exclusively for foreign employers or clients. It differs from a tourist visa in that it explicitly permits work — just not for local companies. Most digital nomad visas do NOT lead directly to permanent residence or citizenship but time spent may count toward residence requirements.

## Current State (Sep 2026)
Digital nomad visas have proliferated since 2020. Croatia and Estonia were pioneers in the EU; both now have mature programmes. Italy launched its version in 2024. Kazakhstan launched an unusually generous 10-year full residence permit for IT specialists. Income thresholds vary widely from ~€2,700 (Malta) to ~€4,500 (Estonia).
