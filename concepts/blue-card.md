## Definition
The EU Blue Card is an EU-wide residence and work permit for highly qualified non-EU nationals. It is governed by EU Directive 2021/1883 (recast), which harmonizes conditions across member states. The Blue Card offers faster paths to permanent residence, intra-EU mobility, and family reunification benefits compared to national work permits.

## Current State (Sep 2026)
The Blue Card is implemented in 25 of 27 EU member states (Denmark and Ireland opt out). The recast Directive (2021/1883) lowered salary thresholds, shortened required contract duration to 6 months, and expanded eligibility to IT professionals with work experience (without a degree).

**Key benefits across all countries**:
- Family members can work immediately (no separate work permit)
- After 18 months: intra-EU mobility (apply in new country)
- After 5 years: EU long-term residence
- Time in one EU country counts toward Blue Card in another

**Not available**: Ireland and Denmark (opt-out).
