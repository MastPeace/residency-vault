## Definition

Student residency — residence permits for individuals enrolled in recognized educational institutions (universities, colleges, language schools). Student years count toward citizenship clocks in most countries (fully or partially — Spain counts 50%, Italy 50%). Post-study work visas (UK Graduate, Australia 485, Canada PGWP) are the critical bridge to permanent residency. Key parameters: proof of financial means (blocked accounts in Germany €11,208; France ~€615/mo), enrollment in an accredited institution, and health insurance. Some countries restrict work hours (Germany: 140 full/280 half days/year; UK: 20 hrs/wk term-time).

## Current State

84 countries offer student residency programs. The top destinations for international students are also the top immigration pathways: Canada (Study Permit → PGWP → PR), Australia (500 → 485 → skilled migration), UK (Student → Graduate → Skilled Worker), Germany (student → 18-month job-seeker → Blue Card), France (étudiant → job-seeker → Talent Passport). Post-study pathways are the key differentiator — countries without them (e.g., some developing nations) see limited student-to-PR conversion.
