## Definition

Passive income residency — residence permits for individuals who can support themselves from **passive income sources** (pensions, dividends, rental income, royalties, interest) without working locally. This is NOT remote work — active work (even for foreign employers) is typically prohibited. Programs like Portugal D7, Spain NLV, Italy Elective Residence, and Greece FIP are the SAME type: prove passive income, no local work, renewable residence leading to permanent residency and citizenship.

Income thresholds range from €920/mo (Portugal D7 — lowest in Western Europe) to €50,000/yr (Ireland Stamp 0). This is the most popular pathway for retirees, investors with dividend income, and financially-independent individuals seeking EU residence.

## Current State

32 countries in the wiki offer passive income residence. Western Europe dominates: Portugal (D7), Spain (NLV), Italy (Elective), Greece (FIP), France (VLS-TS Visiteur) are the headline programs. Latin America offers alternatives at lower cost: Costa Rica Rentista, Panama Friendly Nations CD route, Ecuador Rentista. Ireland's Stamp 0 exists but requires €50K/yr + lump sum. Germany, Netherlands, and most Nordic countries do NOT offer this route.
