## Definition
A Golden Visa is a residence-by-investment programme that grants a residence permit (temporary or permanent) in exchange for a qualifying investment — typically real estate, government bonds, business creation, or fund contributions. Golden Visas do NOT grant citizenship; they are residence rights that may lead to citizenship after additional residence years and integration requirements.

## Current State (Sep 2026)
Golden Visa programmes are in retreat across Europe. Spain closed its programme in April 2025. Portugal's real estate route ended in October 2023. The EU has pressured member states to phase out investor residence schemes.
