## Definition

Startup / entrepreneur residency — residence permits for founders, co-founders, and entrepreneurs who launch or invest in innovative businesses. These are distinct from passive investment residency ([[investment-residency]]): the applicant must actively manage or found the business. Programs range from structured startup visas (Estonia Startup Visa, French Tech Visa, Italy Startup Visa) to general self-employment/business permits (Germany §21, Portugal D2). Key differentiators: whether an endorsement body reviews the business plan (Estonia Startup Committee, UK endorsing body), minimum funding requirements (Italy €50K from recognized investor), or job creation expectations.

## Current State

69 countries offer startup/entrepreneur pathways. The EU leads: Estonia pioneered the world's first Startup Visa; France has French Tech Visa for founders; Italy, Portugal, Netherlands, Denmark all have active programs. Canada's Start-Up Visa is SUSPENDED (since Jan 2026). UK's Innovator Founder requires endorsement. The UAE's Golden Visa — Entrepreneur track (AED 500K + incubator letter) is popular. Latin America's programs are typically subsumed under investment visas (business ≥$200K in Costa Rica, $48K in Ecuador).
