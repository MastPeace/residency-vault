## Overview
The Union of the Comoros is an Indian Ocean archipelago off East Africa. It is best known in migration circles for its **Economic Citizenship Programme**, enacted in 2008 (Loi relative à la citoyenneté économique), which historically sold passports at a low headline price (~US$45,000 donation). The programme's credibility collapsed after reports that citizenship was granted in bulk to stateless "Bidoon" residents of Gulf states on diplomatic grounds rather than through genuine investment. Its formal status has been disputed or suspended at various points since 2018, and the passport offers weak mobility (~50 visa-free/on-arrival). Dual nationality has been permitted since 2001, though the 1979 Nationality Code conflicts with the constitution on this point. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comorian_nationality_law]

## Per-Program Detail

### citizenship_by_investment
- **Official name**: Loi relative à la citoyenneté économique en Union des Comores (Economic Citizenship Programme)
- **Conditions**: Historically marketed at ~US$45,000 government contribution for a single applicant, plus ~US$5,000 due-diligence and agent fees. The programme has been suspended or in disputed status at multiple points since 2018, and is not reliably confirmed active; applicants must independently verify status with Comorian authorities. ^[https://thecitizenshipdesk.com/citizenship/by-investment/comoros-citizenship]
- **Duration / renewal**: Direct citizenship in principle; no physical-presence requirement (where the programme operates).
- **Path to citizenship**: Immediate (grant of nationality). No language test.
- **Official source**: https://beit-salam.km/ (Presidency)
- **Community verdict**: Problematic — passport recognition and banking acceptance are weak; programme credibility damaged by the Bidoon issuance controversy.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Permis de séjour / work permit
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from a Comorian employer; employer sponsorship. Thin, unpublished thresholds.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Naturalisation via residence (5 years for certain categories under the Nationality Code).
- **Official source**: https://beit-salam.km/
- **Community verdict**: No reliable community data.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalisation**: The 1979 Nationality Code provides naturalisation after 5 years' residence for a foreign husband of a Comorian national and for persons making significant artistic/scientific contributions; a general residence period is not clearly published. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comorian_nationality_law]
- **Language**: Arabic and French are official; Comorian (Shikomori) is the lingua franca. No published language test.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted since 2001 (constitutional), but the 1979 code conflicts with this — legal uncertainty persists.
- **Passport**: ~50 visa-free/visa-on-arrival destinations. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comorian_nationality_law]

## Tax
- Tax data are thin and not reliably consolidated. Corporate income tax is commonly cited around 30%. Personal income tax is progressive. No special non-dom or foreign-income regime is published. Confidence low.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital nomad visa. Remote workers have no formal residence route; the economic-citizenship channel is the only marketed option and is of disputed status.

## Yacht / Maritime
Indian Ocean island nation with a small port sector (Moroni, Mutsamudu). Comoros operates a ship registry but has minimal leisure-yachting infrastructure. Not a yachting destination.

## Notes for RU citizens
- The CBI programme was historically marketed toward Gulf/Middle East applicants, not Russia; its disputed status makes it a poor vehicle for RU applicants.
- No visa-free Russia access; weak passport with limited banking acceptance.
- Comoros maintains relations with Russia but offers no meaningful residence pathway for Russian remote workers.

## Community Pulse
Very little expat activity. The dominant narrative is the Economic Citizenship Programme's credibility collapse (Bidoon bulk issuance, 2008–2018) and repeated suspensions. No active expat community reports. The passport is widely regarded as low-value.

## Country Card

### General description
Comoros is a small, lower-income island nation in the Indian Ocean with a Sunni-Muslim majority society and a fragile political history of coups. Its economy is agrarian and aid-dependent. For migrants it is relevant almost exclusively through its controversial economic-citizenship programme, whose low headline price masks weak passport quality and a disputed legal status.

### Well-known strong points
- Extremely low nominal CBI price point (~US$45,000 historical)
- Dual nationality permitted (since 2001)
- Tropical island climate

### Well-known problems
- CBI status disputed/suspended since 2018; credibility damaged by bulk Bidoon issuance
- Very weak passport (~50 destinations); poor banking acceptance
- Fragile governance and political instability
- Thin, unpublished residence and tax rules

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-12
- **Recent changes**: Economic Citizenship Programme suspended/disputed at various points since 2018; no confirmed reactivation.
- **Watch items**: Any official reactivation or reform of the CBI programme; clarification of dual-nationality conflict between constitution and 1979 code.
