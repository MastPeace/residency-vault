## Overview
Eswatini (formerly Swaziland) is a small, landlocked absolute monarchy in Southern Africa, bordering South Africa and Mozambique. Citizenship is acquired by birth (jus soli/sanguinis) or through the traditional _khonta_ system (acceptance by a chief); naturalisation requires 5 years' residence. Dual nationality is generally not recognised for naturalised persons. There is no citizenship-by-investment, residence-by-investment, or digital-nomad programme. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emaswati_nationality_law]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work permit
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from an Eswatini employer; employer sponsorship; published thresholds thin.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Naturalisation after 5 years' residence (residency requirement per CIA World Factbook). ^[https://www.cia.gov/the-world-factbook/countries/eswatini/]
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.sz/index.php/services-sp-22242747/citizenship
- **Community verdict**: No reliable community data; citizenship grants are discretionary under the absolute monarchy.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalisation**: 5 years' residence requirement. Acquisition can also occur via _khonta_ (chiefly acceptance, granted by the King). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emaswati_nationality_law]
- **Dual citizenship**: Not generally recognised (CIA); birth nationals may hold dual nationality with government authorisation, but naturalised persons typically may not. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emaswati_nationality_law]
- **Passport**: ~68 visa-free/on-arrival destinations.

## Tax
- Corporate income tax: 27.5% standard. VAT: 15%. Personal income tax progressive. Part of the Southern African Customs Union (SACU) with South Africa. Remote-worker tax data thin.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa or remote-work residence. Landlocked, with no meaningful nomad or tech community.

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked — no coastline. Not applicable.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Visa required; English and siSwati are official languages.
- No investment-residence or CBI route. Work permits are employer-driven.
- Tightly governed by the monarchy; immigration outcomes are discretionary.

## Community Pulse
Minimal expat activity outside development-sector and South Africa-linked business. No investor or nomad community. Citizenship is rarely pursued by foreigners given discretionary, monarchy-controlled naturalisation and restricted dual nationality.

## Country Card

### General description
Eswatini is a small, landlocked absolute monarchy in Southern Africa within the South African customs and currency orbit (SACU, rand-pegged lilangeni). Its economy is agrarian and manufacturing-based. It has no investment-residence, CBI, or nomad programmes, and citizenship is discretionary, making it a weak migration destination despite low living costs.

### Well-known strong points
- Low cost of living and mild highland climate
- English widely spoken (co-official)
- Currency pegged to the South African rand; SACU trade access
- Wildlife and game-reserve tourism

### Well-known problems
- Absolute monarchy; "Not Free" (FH 17/100)
- No residence-by-investment, CBI, or nomad visa
- Dual nationality generally not recognised for naturalised persons
- Landlocked; small economy and job market
- Discretionary, monarchy-controlled naturalisation
