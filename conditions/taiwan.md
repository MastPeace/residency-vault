## Overview
Taiwan runs a highly accessible immigration system centered on the Employment Gold Card — a 4-in-1 card combining work permit, resident visa, and Alien Resident Certificate (ARC) for skilled professionals — plus standard work, student, and family routes. Naturalization requires 5 years of residence (183 days/year) and renunciation of the original nationality, though high-level professionals can naturalize without renouncing. Taiwan does not recognize dual citizenship for ordinary naturalizations. ^[https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: 投資居留 (investor ARC)
- **Conditions**: Invest NT$6,000,000 (~US$190,000) or more in a Taiwan business and run it, or hold a senior role.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 year ARC, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence (183 days/yr), language + renunciation (except high-level professionals).
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.gov.tw/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — accessible threshold but genuine business operation expected.

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Employment Gold Card (就業金卡)
- **Conditions**: Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000 (~US$5,000) in a qualifying field, or recognized expertise (R&D, tech, finance, education, arts, etc.). 4-in-1: work permit + resident visa + ARC + re-entry. ^[https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/faq/how-do-i-meet-the-salary-requirements-of-the-gold-card-application]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years, renewable; no employer lock-in.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence; high-level professionals may naturalize without renouncing.
- **Official source**: https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/
- **Community verdict**: Works well — open work authorization, no local employer needed, fast processing (~30–60 days).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Entrepreneur Visa (創業家簽證)
- **Conditions**: Innovative business plan, usually endorsed by a recognized incubator/accelerator; proof of funds.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.gov.tw/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for incubator-backed founders.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work permit (工作許可) + ARC
- **Conditions**: Job offer; employer applies for work permit (labor market assessment).
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years, tied to employer.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.gov.tw/
- **Community verdict**: Works well — straightforward, fast.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student ARC (就學居留)
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized institution; post-graduation job-seeking extension available.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.gov.tw/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Dependent ARC (依親居留)
- **Conditions**: Spouse or minor child of ARC/APRC holder or Taiwanese citizen.
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to sponsor.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.gov.tw/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 5 years continuous residence (183 days/year); 3 years if married to a Taiwanese citizen. ^[https://www.ris.gov.tw/]
- **Language**: Basic Mandarin proficiency test.
- **Renunciation**: Must renounce original nationality within 1 year of naturalization approval (exempted for high-level professionals under the 2016 amendment).
- **Dual citizenship**: Not recognized for ordinary naturalizations.

## Tax
- **Personal income tax**: Progressive 5%–40% (top rate on income > NT$4,720,000). ^[https://www.mof.gov.tw/]
- **Residents vs non-residents**: Residents (183+ days) taxed on worldwide income; non-residents at flat 18% on Taiwan-source salary.
- **Foreign-special-professional regime**: Qualified Gold Card holders may qualify for favorable tax treatment.
- **No capital gains tax for individuals on listed securities.**

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated DN visa, but the Gold Card is one of Asia's best remote-work vehicles: open work authorization, no employer, NT$160,000/mo salary threshold, 1–3 years. Taiwan has strong internet, a large semiconductor/software industry, and a growing expat tech community (Taipei, Hsinchu). Practical option for remote workers with qualifying income/credentials.

## Yacht / Maritime
Island nation with a large maritime industry (fishing, shipping). Ports: Kaohsiung (major container port), Keelung, Taichung. Recreational yachting is modest; some marinas in Taipei and Kaohsiung.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Russian nationals can apply for the Gold Card (2-NDFL tax documents explicitly accepted for the salary requirement). Consulates may have reduced operations; apply via third-country missions. No special restrictions. Taiwan is a popular, stable alternative for Russian tech professionals.

## Community Pulse
The Gold Card is the flagship and widely praised for its speed and open work authorization. Naturalization is achievable but the renunciation requirement deters many. Taiwan is viewed as a safe, high-QoL, moderately-priced base for tech professionals.

## Country Card

### General description
Taiwan is a high-income island democracy with a world-leading semiconductor and technology industry, situated off China's southeast coast. It offers skilled professionals one of Asia's most flexible residence cards (the 4-in-1 Employment Gold Card), a clean path to naturalization (5 years), and a high quality of life at moderate cost — making it a top destination for tech workers and remote professionals.

### Well-known strong points
- Employment Gold Card: open work authorization, no employer needed, ~30–60 day processing
- Robust democracy and press freedom (Freedom House 94/100)
- Low crime, excellent public healthcare and infrastructure
- Moderate cost of living with high salaries in tech
- Naturalization in 5 years (and without renunciation for high-level professionals)

### Well-known problems
- Naturalization requires renouncing original citizenship for ordinary applicants
- High geopolitical risk (cross-strait tension)
- Typhoon season and earthquakes
- International (diplomatic) isolation; limited consular network
- Not measured in some global indices (WJP) due to non-UN-member status
