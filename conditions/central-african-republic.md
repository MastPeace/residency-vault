## Overview
The Central African Republic (CAR) is a landlocked, resource-rich but conflict-ridden country (pop. ~5.7M). Its nationality code (Loi 61.212, 1961, Art. 27) imposes an extraordinary naturalization requirement: 35 years of habitual residence — among the longest in the world. Passport: ~20 visa-free destinations. ^[https://data.worldbank.org/country/CF]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Permis de travail / Carte de sejour
- **Conditions**: Job offer from CAR employer; employer sponsorship. Mostly NGO/humanitarian sector.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 35 years habitual residence (Art. 27, Loi 61.212). ^[https://clr.africanchildforum.org/Legislation%20Per%20Country/CAR/car_nationality_1961_fr.pdf]
- **Official source**: Ministere de l'Interieur (no online portal; state capacity minimal).
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data (expat presence: humanitarian, diplomatic, mining).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Visa d'etude
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized CAR institution; proof of financial means.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: Residence time counts toward 35-year clock.
- **Official source**: CAR embassies / consulates (limited operations).
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Regroupement familial
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; proof of relationship and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 35 years. Woman marrying CAR national acquires nationality at marriage (Art. 13). ^[https://clr.africanchildforum.org/Legislation%20Per%20Country/CAR/car_nationality_1961_fr.pdf]
- **Official source**: Immigration authorities (limited capacity).
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

## Citizenship
- **Residence**: 35 years habitual residence (Art. 27, Loi 61.212, 1961; amended 1966). One of the highest naturalization bars worldwide. ^[https://clr.africanchildforum.org/Legislation%20Per%20Country/CAR/car_nationality_1961_fr.pdf]
- **Language**: French and/or Sango proficiency expected.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted (not explicitly prhibited).
- **Processing**: By decree; discretionary. Minimum 1-year investigation before decree.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Pogessive income tax; corporate tax ~30%.
- **Remote workers**: No specil regime. Tax enforcement vitually nonexistent outside Bangui
- **General**: CFA franc (XAF, pegged toEUR). State capacity extremly limited.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. Intenet is extremly limited(Bangui has ome connectivity; rural areas have none). Cowrking nonexistent. CAR is not and cannot become a nomad destination.[[cameroon]] and Chad are regional neigbors.

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked. No maritime relevance.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa required. No special RU programs. Wagner/Africa Corps and Russian mining presence notable. Passport is very weak. Extreme security risks.

## Community Pulse
No expat immigration community outside humanitarian workers and peacekeepers (MINUSCA). One of the world's most fragile states. The 35-year naturalization requirement makes citizenship practically inaccessible. Not discussed on any immigration forums.

The Central African Republic is a landlocked, resource-rich but conflict-ridden country at the crossroads of Central Africa. Despite diamonds, old, and uranium deposits, it ranks among the poorest and most fragile states globally. The 35-ear naturalization requirement is among the world'shighest,making citizenship ractically inaccessible.
