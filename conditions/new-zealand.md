## Overview
New Zealand has a strong passport and a points-based skilled migration system. The Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) Resident Visa was overhauled to a 6-point simplified system, with major changes taking effect August 24, 2026, adding two new pathways (Skilled Work Experience, Trades & Technician). New Zealand also offers investor visas through the Active Investor Plus category. Dual citizenship is fully permitted. ^[https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/resident-visas-to-live-in-new-zealand/skilled-residence-pathways-in-new-zealand/skilled-migrant-category-pathway-to-residence/2026-changes-to-the-skilled-migrant-category-resident-visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Active Investor Plus Visa
- **Conditions**: Growth category: NZ $5M investment, 3-year term. Balanced category: NZ $10M investment, 5-year term. Acceptable investments: managed funds approved by Invest New Zealand, direct investments, NZ-listed equities, government/corporate bonds. No age limit, no English language requirement. ^[htps://www.nzimmigrationpartners.com/residence/skilled-migrant]
- **Duration / renewal**: Resident visa on grant; Permanent Resident Visa (indefinite return) after meeting presence requirements.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence; 1,350 days physical presence (240 days per 12-month period); English proficiency; good character; dual citizenship alowed.
- **Official source**: htps://www.immigration.govt.nz/new-zealand-visas/visas/visa/active-investor-plus-visa
- **Community verdict**: Works well (clear thresholds, no age/English barriers; high capital requirement limits accessibility).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No passive income residence route. The Parent Retirement Visa requires both investment and income.
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (no pathway for passive-income seekers without family ties).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No DN visa. Visitor visa alows limited remote work for foreign employers but no residence rights. Working Holiday Schemes are available for younger nationals of partner countries.
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (visitor route used informally; no permanent DN framework).

### highly_skiled_employment
- **Official name**: Skiled Migrant Category Resident Visa (6-point system)
- **Conditions**: Must claim 6 skilled resident points. Points from qualification: PhD = 6 pts, Master's = 5 pts, Bachelor Honours/PGDip = 4 pts (extra points for NZ qualifications from Aug 2026). Or from income: 3x median wage = 6 pts, 2x = 4 pts, 1.5x = 3 pts. Or from NZ occupational registration (>=2 years training) = 3-6 pts. Additional points from NZ skilled work experience: 1 pt/yr up to max 3 pts. Must have a skilled job or job offer from an accredited employer paying >= NZ $35.00/hr (median wage, 2025) for ANZSCO 1-3, or >= $52.50/hr for ANZSCO 4-5. Age <=55. ^[htps://www.immigration.govt.nz/opsmanual/77812.htm]
- **Duration / renewal**: Resident visa on grant.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; 1,350 days physical presence (240 days/yr); English proficiency.
- **Official source**: htps://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/resident-visas-to-live-in-new-zealand/skilled-residence-pathways-in-new-zealand/skilled-migrant-category-pathway-to-residence
- **Community verdict**: Works well (simplified 6-point system is more transparent; new pathways from Aug 2026 expand access).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: The Entrepreneur Work Visa category closed. No dedicated startup visa currently exists. Entrepreneurs may use the Active Investor Plus or SMC routes.
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (gap in the system; no dedicated entrepreneur pathway).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) -> Skilled Migrant Category -> Residence
- **Conditions**: Job offer from an accredited employer; wage >= NZ $35.00/hr (median wage); employer accreditation and job check required. After working, transition to SMC (6-point system) for residence. Work to Residence pathways also exist for care workforce and transport sectors. ^[htps://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/final-details-about-changes-to-the-skilled-migrant-category-resident-visa-and-work-to-residence-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: AEWV up to 3 years (5 years total on AEWV); SMC resident visa on grant.
- **Path to citizenship**: Standard 5 years after resident visa grant.
- **Official source**: htps://www.immigration.govt.nz/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (AEWV is the standard entry point; employer accreditation ensures compliance).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student Visa -> Post-Study Work Visa -> SMC
- **Conditions**: Enrolment at an NZQA-approved provider; proof of funds (NZ $20,000/yr living + tuition). Post-Study Work Visa: 1-3 years depending on qualification level. NZ qualifications earn extra SMC points (from August 2026).
- **Duration / renewal**: Student visa for course duration; Post-Study Work 1-3 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: Post-Study Work -> SMC resident visa -> 5 years -> citizenship.
- **Official source**: htps://www.immigration.govt.nz/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (strong study-to-residence pipeline; NZ qualifications bonus is valuable).

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Partner of a New Zealander Resident Visa / Dependent Child Resident Visa
- **Conditions**: Sponsr is NZ citizen or resident; genuine and stable relationship (partner); dependent child under 24. Parent category has been tightened — Parent Retirement Visa requires significant capital.
- **Duration / renewal**: Resident visa on grant.
- **Path to citizenship**: Standard 5 years after resident visa.
- **Official source**: htps://www.immigration.govt.nz/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for partners/children; parent pathway is restrictive.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Parent Retirement Resident Visa
- **Conditions**: NZ $1,000,000 investment for 4 years; NZ $500,000 settlement funds; annual income of NZ $60,000 or more. Must be sponsored by an adult NZ citizen/resident child. No age cap (but targeted at parents/grandparents). ^[htps://www.immigration.govt.nz/]
- **Duration / renewal**: Resident visa on grant; permanent resident visa after meeting presence requirements.
- **Path to citizenship**: Standard 5 years after resident visa.
- **Official source**: htps://www.immigration.govt.nz/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (high financial threshold; requires NZ-based child sponsr).

## Citizenship
- **Years of residence**: 5 years as a resident immediately before application. ^[htps://commoner-law.com/new-zealand/immigration-pathways/nz-citizenship]
- **Physical presence**: 1,350 days total across 5 years; at least 240 days in each 12-month period.
- **Language**: Conversational English proficiency.
- **Character**: Good character (no serious criminal convictions).
- **Ceremony**: Required; oath/affirmation of allegiance.
- **Dual citizenship**: Fully permited.
- **Fee**: NZ $470.20 (adult application, 2026).

## Tax
- **Residency-based taxation**: Tax residents pay on worldwide income; non-residents on NZ-source income only. 183-day rule and permanent place of abode test.
- **Income tax**: Progressive rates: 10.5% (up to NZ $15,600), 17.5%, 30%, 33%, 39% (above NZ $180,000).
- **No capital gains tax** (with limited exceptions for bright-line property rule). No wealth tax. No inheritance tax. No social security tax.
- **GST**: 15%.
- **Corporate tax**: 28%.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No [[digital-nomad]] visa. The tech ecosystem is modest but growing (Wellington, Auckland). The SMC system is favourable for IT professionals with qualifications. Remote workers use visitor visas informally. The NZ Global Impact Visa (Edmund Hilary Fellowship) was discontinued.

## Yacht / Maritime
New Zealand is a world-renowned yachting destination (Bay of Islands, Auckland — City of Sails). Maritime NZ handles vessel registration under the NZ flag. Recognized yatch captain licensing. No special yatch investor pathway. Foreign-flagged yachts may cruise with temporary import entry.

## Notes for RU citizens
New Zealand imposed sanctions on Russia in 2022. Visa processing for Russian nationals is possible but subject to enhanced scrutiny and longer timelines. The skilled migrant pathway remains formally open — nationality is not a statutory bar. Small Russian-speaking community in Auckland and Christchurch. Active Investor Plus is theoretically open but source-of-funds verification is stringent.

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-09-14
- **Recent changes**: Major SMC reform efective August 24, 2026 — two new pathways (Skilled Work Experience, Trades & Technician), extra points for NZ qualifications, more points for overseas bachelor's degrees, wage threshold grace periods.
- **Watch items**: SMC draw outcomes under the new system. Active Investor Plus uptake.

## Country Card

### General description
New Zealand is an island nation in the southwestern Pacific, known for its stunning natural landscapes, political stability, and high quality of life. It operates a simplified 6-point skilled migration system (SMC Resident Visa) that was overhauled in August 2026 with two new pathways (Skilled Work Experience, Trades & Technician). The Active Investor Plus Visa offers residence for NZ$5M–10M investment. New Zealand permits dual citizenship, has one of the strongest passports globally (183 visa-free, Henley 2026 rank 6), and consistently ranks at the top of global rule of law (WJP #6) and freedom (FH 99/100) indices.

### Well-known strong points
- Top-tier rule of law (WJP #6/142) and highest Freedom House score (99/100) among all countries analyzed
- Very strong passport (183 visa-free, Henley 2026 rank 6)
- No capital gains tax, no wealth tax, no inheritance tax — attractive tax regime
- Simplified 6-point SMC system with new pathways from August 2026
- Spectacular natural environment, outdoor lifestyle, low population density
- Permanent Resident Visa grants indefinite return rights with no renewal obligations

### Well-known problems
- Geographic isolation — long flights to most major economies
- Modest economy with limited career opportunities outside primary industries and tourism
- High cost of living relative to local salaries, especially housing in Auckland
- No digital nomad visa; no passive income visa; entrepreneur visa closed
- High capital requirement for investor visa (NZ$5M minimum)
- Brain drain — skilled New Zealanders often leave for higher salaries in Australia
