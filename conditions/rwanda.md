## Overview
Rwanda is one of Africa's most digitally progressive nations, offering a notably affordable Digital Nomad Visa (1 year USD 100, 5 years USD 500) via the Irembo e-government portal. The Directorate General of Immigration and Emigration (DGIE) manages residence and citizenship. Rwanda permits dual citizenship since 2003. The country is safe, clean, and English/French/Kinyarwanda-speaking, with Kigali as a growing regional tech hub. ^[https://www.migration.gov.rw/visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### digital_nomad_visa — Digital Nomad Visa
- **Official name**: Rwanda Digital Nomad Visa (via Irembo Portal)
- **Conditions**: Remote employee or freelancer with clients/employer outside Rwanda. Proof of stable remote income (no fixed minimum published, but officers expect sustainable income). Health insurance. Clean criminal record. Proof of accommodation. ^[https://nomadsafrica.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-rwandas-5-year-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Duration**: 1 year (USD 100) or 5 years (USD 500). The 5-year option at USD 100/year is among the world's cheapest. ^[https://nomavisa.com/country/rwanda]
- **Path to citizenship**: None — DN visa does NOT count toward the 15-year residence requirement for naturalisation.
- **Official source**: https://irembo.gov.rw (application portal); https://www.migration.gov.rw/visa
- **Community verdict**: Works well — affordable, fully online application via Irembo, 5–10 business day processing. Kigali is safe and clean. Limitation: no PR path from DN visa. Source: NomadsAfrica, expat forums.

### investment_residency — Investor Permit
- **Official name**: Investor Permit (DGIE — various classes depending on sector)
- **Conditions**: Registered business in Rwanda. Capital requirements vary by sector. TIN, business license, lease/ownership proof. ^[https://www.migration.gov.rw/visa]
- **Duration**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Rwanda's nationality law (Organic Law 30/2008) requires 15 years of legal residence for most naturalisation applicants. Substantial sustainable investments may qualify under "national interest" or "special skills" grounds with shorter timelines (discretionary). ^[https://www.migration.gov.rw/our-services/citizenshipss]
- **Official source**: https://www.migration.gov.rw/visa
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — investor environment improving but bureaucratic; Rwanda Development Board (RDB) offers facilitation.

## Citizenship
- **By acquisition on grounds of residence**: 15 years consecutive legal residence and physical presence. ^[https://www.irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/country-information/rir/Pages/index.aspx?doc=458434]
- **By marriage**: 3 years of marriage + residence.
- **By national interest / special skills / substantial investment**: Discretionary, shorter timelines possible. ^[https://www.migration.gov.rw/our-services/citizenshipss]
- **By immigrant/descent**: 25 years residence + lost connection to country of origin.
- Language: Kinyarwanda/English/French. Knowledge of Rwandan culture and civic values required.
- Dual citizenship: Permitted since 2003. ^[https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/en/legality-on-dual-nationality-in-rwanda]
- Passport: visa-free to ~65 destinations, growing (EAC, COMESA). Not strong by EU standards.
- **Honest assessment**: Citizenship is a very long path (15+ years) — realistic only for those genuinely relocating permanently.

## Tax
- Personal income tax: progressive 0%–30% (top rate on income above RWF 7,200,000/year).
- Corporate tax: 30% standard.
- VAT: 18%.
- Rwanda's tax system is residence-based. Digital nomads on the DN visa with foreign income should seek advice — tax residence triggers at 183+ days.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Kigali is Africa's cleanest capital, with growing co-working spaces (Impact Hub, Westerwelle Startup Haus), reliable internet (4G/5G, fibre), and a pro-business government. Rwanda is landlocked but well-connected regionally. Cost of living: USD 800–1,500/month. The 5-year DN visa at USD 500 is an exceptional deal. Kigali International Airport connects to major African/ME hubs.

## Yacht / Maritime
Not applicable — landlocked country.

## Notes for RU citizens
Rwanda offers visa-free entry for 30 days (or eVisa). Russian citizens can apply for the DN visa. Banking and immigration are accessible. Rwanda maintains neutral international relations. Small Russian-speaking community. Kigali is safe and welcoming to foreigners generally.
