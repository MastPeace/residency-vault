## Overview
Ghana's immigration system is administered by the Ghana Immigration Service (GIS). Foreign nationals require both a Work Permit and a Residence Permit (separate documents) for stays exceeding 180 days. The Ghana Investment Promotion Centre (GIPC) facilitates investor permits with minimum capital thresholds. Ghana introduced a Citizenship by Investment clause (Clause 37) in the 2025 GIPC Bill — not yet active. There is no digital nomad visa. Ghana permits dual citizenship. ^[https://gis.gov.gh/service/work-and-residence-permit-missionaries-ngos-immigrant-quota-gipc-shareholders]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency — GIPC Investor Work & Residence Permit
- **Official name**: Work and Residence Permit (GIPC Immigrant Quota / Shareholders)
- **Conditions**: Company registered with GIPC. Minimum foreign capital: USD 200,000 (joint venture with Ghanaian) or USD 500,000 (wholly foreign-owned) for most sectors; some sectors lower. Certificate of Incorporation, Certificate to Commence Business, GIPC Registration Certificate, tax clearance, audited accounts, medical report from GIS clinic, police report from home country. ^[https://gis.gov.gh/service/work-and-residence-permit-missionaries-ngos-immigrant-quota-gipc-shareholders]
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Indefinite Residence Permit after substantial economic contribution (documented donations, tax compliance, social contributions). Citizenship: generally 5+ years continuous lawful residence. ^[https://gis.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/FREQUENTLY-ASKED-QUESTIONS.pdf]
- **Official source**: https://gis.gov.gh; https://gipc.gov.gh
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — bureaucracy is significant, but Accra has a growing international business community.

### employment_residency — Work Permit + Residence Permit
- **Official name**: Work and Residence Permit
- **Conditions**: Separate Work Permit and Residence Permit required. Employer sponsorship. Medical examination at GIS clinic (USD 150–250). Non-Citizen ID Card (USD 120 initial, USD 60 renewal). Police clearance. Offer/appointment letter. CV, qualifications. ^[https://gis.gov.gh/service/work-and-residence-permit-missionaries-ngos-immigrant-quota-gipc-shareholders]
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5+ years continuous residence.
- **Official source**: https://gis.gov.gh
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — procedural but functional. Overstay penalty: GHS 300/month for visitors.

## Citizenship
- Naturalisation: generally 5+ years of continuous lawful residence. ^[https://gis.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/FREQUENTLY-ASKED-QUESTIONS.pdf]
- Language: English (official).
- Dual citizenship: Permitted since 1996 (Amendment to Citizenship Act). Ghanaian citizens by birth may hold dual. Naturalised citizens may retain original citizenship (check home country rules). ^[https://gis.gov.gh]
- **Citizenship by Investment (pending)**: Clause 37 of the GIPC Bill 2025 empowers the Ministry of Interior, in consultation with GIPC, to enact legislation specifying categories of investors qualifying for citizenship. NOT YET ACTIVE (as of Sept 2026). ^[https://www.brr.gov.gh/acc/consultation/docs/The%20New%20GIPC%20Bill-2025.pdf]
- Passport: visa-free to ~65 destinations. ECOWAS free movement.
- **Honest assessment**: The 5-year timeline is moderate. The pending CBI legislation could transform Ghana into a stronger residence/citizenship destination.

## Tax
- Residence-based taxation. Tax resident if >183 days or ordinarily resident.
- Personal income tax: progressive 5%–35% (top rate at GHS 240,000+/yr, ~USD 20,000).
- Corporate tax: 25% standard.
- VAT: 15% (plus 2.5% NHIL, 2.5% GETFund = effective 20%).
- No capital gains tax on listed securities.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated DN visa. Accra is the tech hub (Kempinski area, Osu, Airport City). Internet: 4G/LTE (MTN, Vodafone), improving fibre. Co-working spaces: Impact Hub Accra, iSpace Foundation. Cost of living: moderate. Remote workers typically use tourist visas or find a local employer for permit sponsorship. The absence of a DN visa is a gap.

## Yacht / Maritime
Ghana has an Atlantic coastline (Tema, Takoradi). Ghana Maritime Authority handles vessel registration. Limited yacht infrastructure compared to South Africa or Mauritius. Not a major yachting destination.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa on arrival or eVisa for Russian citizens. Ghana maintains cordial relations with Russia. Small Russian-speaking community in Accra. Banking: functional. The pending CBI legislation could be transformative if enacted.

## Country Card

Ghana is one of West Africa's most stable democracies, a former British colony with English as the official language, and a regional leader in governance. Accra is a growing business and tech hub. The Ghana Immigration Service (GIS) operates a dual Work Permit + Residence Permit system, and the Ghana Investment Promotion Centre (GIPC) facilitates investor permits (min USD 200,000–500,000). Ghana permits dual citizenship — a significant advantage. The pending GIPC Bill 2025 (Clause 37) may introduce Citizenship by Investment. There is currently no digital nomad visa.

### Well-known strong points
- Freedom House 80/100 (Free) — one of Africa's strongest democracies; regular peaceful transitions
- Dual citizenship permitted (since 1996 Amendment) — rare and valuable in Africa
- English is the official language — no language barrier for most expats
- Moderate citizenship timeline (5+ years) with dual citizenship allowed
- Pending CBI legislation (GIPC Bill 2025 Clause 37) could transform Ghana into a passport destination
- Rich cultural heritage and diaspora appeal; 'Year of Return' initiative boosted international visibility

### Well-known problems
- No digital nomad visa — remote workers need employer sponsorship or investor route
- GIPC investor thresholds are high: USD 200,000 (joint venture) or USD 500,000 (wholly foreign)
- Personal income tax up to 35%; effective VAT ~20% (15% + 2.5% NHIL + 2.5% GETFund)
- Cost of living in Accra expat areas can be surprisingly high; imported goods expensive
- Passport is weak (~65 visa-free destinations) — not a passport-shopping destination (yet)
- Bureaucracy and corruption remain challenges; overstay penalties apply (GHS 300/month)
