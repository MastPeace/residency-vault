## Overview
The UAE operates the region's most comprehensive [[golden-visa]] programme (5–10 years) plus a dedicated remote-work visa. Personal income tax is 0%, and the UAE passport ranks among the strongest in the Middle East (~183 visa-free destinations). Dubai and Abu Dhabi are global hubs for entrepreneurs, IT professionals, and yacht owners. The UAE does NOT offer citizenship by investment or naturalization for expatriates — citizenship is by nomination only. ^[http://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/residence-visas/golden-visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### Golden Visa — Investor
- **Official name**: الإقامة الذهبية (Golden Visa) — Public Investment / Real Estate
- **Conditions**: AED 2,000,000+ (~$545,000) in approved investment fund, company capital, or real estate. Property must be wholly owned; mortgage from local bank permitted. As of Feb 2026, no minimum paid-up requirement — total value suffices. ^[https://www.moet.gov.ae/en/-/what-are-the-requirements-for-issuing-a-golden-visa-to-an-investor-in-public-investments-]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 years (real estate) or 10 years (public investment), renewable. No minimum stay; can remain outside UAE indefinitely.
- **Path to citizenship**: None. Golden Visa grants permanent residency only.
- **Official source**: http://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/residence-visas/golden-visa
- **Community verdict**: Works well — widely used, efficient processing, strong expat satisfaction.

### Golden Visa — Exceptional Talent
- **Official name**: Golden Visa — Exceptional Talent (doctors, scientists, PhD, executives)
- **Conditions**: Recommendation letter from relevant authority, accredited degree/credentials, employment contract in UAE.
- **Duration / renewal**: 10 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: http://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/residence-visas/golden-visa
- **Community verdict**: Works well for qualified professionals.

### Golden Visa — Entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Golden Visa — Entrepreneur
- **Conditions**: Project ≥ AED 500,000 (~$136,000), innovation/technical nature, letter from business incubator or relevant authority.
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: http://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/residence-visas/golden-visa
- **Community verdict**: Works well in Dubai free zones (DMCC, DIFC).

### Golden Visa — Outstanding Students
- **Official name**: Golden Visa — Outstanding Students
- **Conditions**: Top GPA from UAE secondary school (5 years) or top-100 global university (10 years), recommendation letters.
- **Duration / renewal**: 5–10 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: http://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/residence-visas/golden-visa
- **Community verdict**: Niche but functional.

### Virtual Work Programme
- **Official name**: Virtual Work Programme (Remote Work Visa)
- **Conditions**: $3,500/mo minimum income from foreign employer or owned business abroad ($5,000/mo for business owners). 6 months bank statements required (since Jan 2026). Valid health insurance. No UAE clients/work permitted. ^[https://www.investindubai.gov.ae/en/live-and-work/visas-and-entry/work-remotely-from-dubai]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.investindubai.gov.ae/en/live-and-work/visas-and-entry/work-remotely-from-dubai
- **Community verdict**: Works well — fast 5–7 day processing, fees ~AED 1,500–1,700.

### Employment Residency
- **Official name**: Standard Employment Visa (MoHRE)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from UAE employer, employer sponsorship, labour contract. Minimum salary varies by free zone and emirate.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2–3 years, tied to employer.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/residence-visas
- **Community verdict**: Standard GCC employment model — functional but tied to employer.

### Family Reunification
- **Official name**: Family Sponsorship Visa
- **Conditions**: Sponsor must hold valid residence, meet minimum salary (AED 4,000 or AED 3,000 + accommodation). Covers spouse, children, parents.
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to sponsor's visa.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/residence-visas
- **Community verdict**: Standard.

### Retirement Visa
- **Official name**: Retirement Visa (Abu Dhabi/Dubai)
- **Conditions**: Age 55+. EITHER AED 20,000/mo income, OR AED 1M in savings, OR AED 2M property. Valid health insurance.
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.added.gov.ae/en/live/long-term-residency/abu-dhabi-non-work-visa/for-retirement-visa
- **Community verdict**: Works well for eligible retirees.

## Citizenship
**Not available for expatriates.** UAE citizenship is granted exclusively through:
- Nomination by Rulers'/Crown Princes' Courts or Cabinet (extremely rare)
- Birth to an Emirati father
- Marriage to an Emirati citizen (7 years, limited and discretionary)

The Golden Visa provides permanent residency, not citizenship. No investment amount unlocks citizenship. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/golden-visa-uae]

## Tax
- **0% personal income tax** — no tax on salary, dividends, capital gains, rental income, or inheritance.
- **9% corporate tax** on profits above AED 375,000 (~$102,000) introduced 2023. Free zone entities may qualify for 0% on qualifying income.
- **5% VAT** on most goods and services.
- **No wealth tax, no capital gains tax, no inheritance tax.**
- Extensive double taxation treaty network (140+ treaties).

## IT / Digital Nomad
The UAE is one of the world's leading destinations for digital nomads and tech professionals:
- **Virtual Work Programme** (see above): $3,500/mo income, 1-year renewable.
- World-class digital infrastructure, fastest mobile internet globally.
- Free zones (DMCC, DIFC, ADGM, DSO) provide 100% foreign ownership, 0% corporate tax on qualifying income.
- Large IT/startup ecosystem with venture capital availability.
- No income tax makes it exceptionally attractive for high-earning remote workers.

## Yacht / Maritime
The UAE is a premier global yachting hub:
- Dubai Harbour, Dubai Marina, Yas Marina (Abu Dhabi), Mina Rashid — world-class superyacht berthing.
- UAE-flag yacht registration available for foreign owners.
- Recognizes RYA, MCA, IYT captain licenses.
- 0% tax on yacht purchases in designated free zones.
- Strategic location between Mediterranean and Indian Ocean.
- Full-service maintenance at Dubai Maritime City.

## Notes for RU citizens
The UAE is the #1 destination for Russian HNWI, entrepreneurs, and IT professionals post-2022:
- **Visa-free entry** (90 days) for Russian passport holders.
- **No sanctions**, accessible banking (though enhanced compliance since 2023).
- **0% tax**, direct flights to Moscow/SPB/regions.
- **Large Russian-speaking community** — Dubai Marina, JLT, Palm Jumeirah, Business Bay.
- **Golden Visa accessible** — AED 2M property route is the most common entry path for Russian nationals.
- Russian capital inflows have significantly impacted Dubai real estate since 2022; market has adjusted but remains accessible.
- UAE banks have tightened KYC for Russian nationals; corporate accounts require substance and clean source-of-funds documentation.

## Community pulse
Largely positive. Golden Visa is well-established and functional. Processing times are fast (5–10 working days for most categories). Main friction points: bank account opening for new residents, rental price inflation in Dubai, and 90-day tourist visa overstay enforcement for those between residence permits.
