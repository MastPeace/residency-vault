## Overview
The UAE operates one of the most comprehensive residence-by-investment programmes: the [[golden-visa]] (5 or 10 years). The UAE does NOT offer citizenship by investment or by naturalization (citizenship is by nomination only). Dubai, in particular, has become a global hub for entrepreneurs, IT professionals, and yacht owners, with 0% personal income tax. ^[http://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/residence-visas/golden-visa]

## Residence
**[[Golden-visa]] (5 or 10 years)**: ^[http://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/residence-visas/golden-visa]
| Category | Duration | Key Requirement |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| Public investment investor | 10 years | AED 2M+ capital |
| Real estate investor | 5 or 10 years | Property ≥ AED 2M |
| Entrepreneur | 5 years | Project ≥ AED 500K, innovation-based |
| Exceptional talent (doctors, scientists, PhD, executives) | 10 years | Recommendation letter, credentials |
| Outstanding students | 5–10 years | Top GPA from UAE/top-100 global university |
| Humanitarian pioneers | 10 years | At least 5 years of service |

Key benefits: self-sponsored (no employer tie), extended absence permitted (no 180-day rule), sponsor family regardless of children's age. ^[https://sarmat.ae/resources/golden-visa-uae-2026]

**Blue Residency** (launched 2025): 10-year self-sponsored permit for environmental/sustainability contributions.

**Standard residence visas**: employment visa (tied to employer), 2-year property investor visa (≥ AED 750K property), freelance visa, retirement visa.

## Citizenship
**UAE does NOT offer citizenship by investment or naturalization for expatriates.** Citizenship can only be obtained through:
- Nomination by federal entities, the Cabinet, or the Rulers' and Crown Princes' Courts
- Marriage to an Emirati citizen (after 7 years, limited circumstances)
- Birth to Emirati father

The Golden Visa grants permanent residency, not citizenship. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/golden-visa-uae]

## Tax
**0% personal income tax.** 9% corporate tax on profits above AED 375,000 (introduced 2023). 5% VAT. No wealth tax, no capital gains tax, no inheritance tax. Extensive double taxation treaty network. The UAE is one of the world's premier tax-free jurisdictions.

## IT / Digital Nomad
UAE offers a **Virtual Work Programme** (1-year residence permit) for remote workers employed by foreign companies. Requirements: income ≥ $3,500/month, valid health insurance. Dubai and Abu Dhabi have world-class digital infrastructure, startup ecosystems, and co-working spaces. Free zones (DMCC, DIFC, ADGM) provide 100% foreign ownership for tech companies.

## Yacht / Maritime
The UAE is one of the world's premier yachting destinations:
- Dubai Harbour, Dubai Marina, Yas Marina (Abu Dhabi) — world-class superyacht facilities
- Yacht registration under the UAE flag is available
- Maritime City (Dubai) provides full-service yacht maintenance
- UAE recognizes international captain licenses (RYA, MCA, IYT)
- 0% tax on yacht purchases in certain free zones
- Prime location between Mediterranean and Indian Ocean cruising grounds

## Notes for RU citizens
The UAE is arguably the #1 destination for Russian HNWI and entrepreneurs post-2022. Reasons: visa-free entry for Russian citizens (90 days), no sanctions, accessible banking, 0% tax, direct flights, large Russian-speaking community (especially in Dubai Marina, JLT, Palm Jumeirah), and Golden Visa accessibility. The AED 2M property investor route is the most common entry path. Russian capital inflows have transformed Dubai's real estate market.
