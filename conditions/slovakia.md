## Overview
Slovakia is an EU/Schengen/Eurozone member in Central Europe with a growing automotive and IT sector. Immigration is managed by the Foreign Police (Cudzinecká polícia). The primary route for skilled non-EU workers is the EU [[blue-card]] (5 years validity). Slovakia does NOT offer a dedicated digital nomad visa, golden visa, or passive-income residence. Citizenship requires 8 years of permanent residence. ^[https://mic.iom.sk/en/citizenship/conditions-for-granting-slovak-citizenship-naturalization.html]

## Per-Program Detail

### Highly Skilled Employment — EU Blue Card
- **Official name**: Modrá karta EÚ (EU Blue Card)
- **Conditions**: University degree (recognized higher education). Employment contract ≥6 months. **Salary ≥ 1.5× Slovak national average**. No labor market test since July 2024 (employer must still report vacancy but confirmation not required for Blue Card). Fee: €250 application + €10–39 card issuance. ^[https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card/eu-blue-card-slovakia_en]
- **Duration / renewal**: **5 years** (or contract duration + 90 days if shorter). Processing: 30 days from complete application. ^[https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card/eu-blue-card-slovakia_en]
- **Path to citizenship**: After 5 years residence → permanent residence → citizenship at 8 years total PR. ^[https://mic.iom.sk/en/citizenship/conditions-for-granting-slovak-citizenship-naturalization.html]
- **Official source**: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card/eu-blue-card-slovakia_en
- **Community verdict**: Works well — 5-year card is the longest Blue Card validity in the EU. Processing is fast (30 days).

### Employment Residency — Standard Work Permit
- **Official name**: Prechodný pobyt na účel zamestnania (Temporary residence for employment)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Slovak employer. Labor market test (employer reports vacancy to Labour Office). Valid for 6 months to 2 years.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years → permanent residence → citizenship at 8 years PR.
- **Official source**: https://www.minv.sk/?naturalizacia
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard Schengen process, slower than Blue Card.

### Startup / Entrepreneur — Trade License
- **Official name**: Živnostenský list → prechodný pobyt na účel podnikania
- **Conditions**: Register a trade license (živnosť). Proof of funds (~€2,500–5,000 depending on trade). Business plan. Accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years, renewable, max 6 years total.
- **Path to citizenship**: After 5 years residence → PR → citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://www.minv.sk/?zivnostnici
- **Community verdict**: Works well — common self-employment route, but requires Slovak-language setup.

## Citizenship
- **Standard**: 8 consecutive years of **permanent residence** ^[https://www.mzv.sk/web/washington-en/services/slovak-citizenship/naturalization]
- **Marriage to Slovak**: 5 years living together in Slovakia ^[https://mic.iom.sk/en/citizenship/conditions-for-granting-slovak-citizenship-naturalization.html]
- **10 years continuous residence** (any status) + current PR
- **Born in Slovakia**: 3 years PR before application
- **Asylum holders**: 4 years
- **Language**: Slovak proficiency (oral + written) + general knowledge test ^[https://www.mzv.sk/web/washington-en/services/slovak-citizenship/naturalization]
- Dual citizenship: **NOT generally permitted** — applicants must renounce previous citizenship unless by birth or marriage ^[https://mic.iom.sk/en/citizenship/conditions-for-granting-slovak-citizenship-naturalization.html]

## Tax
- Progressive income tax: 19% (up to ~€41,500) / 25% above
- Social + health contributions: ~13.4% employee
- Corporate tax: 21% (15% for small businesses up to €49,790 revenue)
- Dividend tax: 7%

## IT / Digital Nomad
Slovakia has NO dedicated digital nomad visa. IT professionals primarily use:
- **EU Blue Card** — fast (30 days), 5-year validity, no labor market test
- **Trade license** — for freelancers and self-employed
Bratislava and Košice are the main tech hubs. IT salaries are competitive with Central European peers.

## Yacht / Maritime
Not applicable — Slovakia is landlocked.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Russian citizens need a Schengen visa. Blue Card and work permit routes are formally open but face enhanced scrutiny. Slovak language requirement for citizenship is demanding (B1+ level). Small Russian-speaking community, mostly in Bratislava. Slovakia's political stance toward Russia is aligned with EU/NATO.
