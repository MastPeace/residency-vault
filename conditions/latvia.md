## Overview
Latvia is an EU/Schengen/NATO/Eurozone member in the Baltic region. It offers the **cheapest Golden Visa in the EU** (€50,000 business equity route), a **Digital Nomad Visa** (for OECD-country employers), and standard work/Blue Card routes. Latvia imposes **no minimum physical stay** requirement for golden visa holders. Citizenship requires 5 years of permanent residence + Latvian language + Constitution/history exam. ^[https://workvisa.guide/blog/latvia-golden-visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### Investment Residency — Golden Visa
- **Official name**: Termiņuzturēšanās atļauja ieguldījumu dēļ (Temporary residence permit by investment)
- **Conditions**: Non-EU/EEA national. Four investment routes: ^[https://workvisa.guide/blog/latvia-golden-visa]
  1. **€50,000** — equity in a qualifying Latvian company (must have ≥€50,000 equity, pay taxes in Latvia)
  2. **€250,000** — purchase of residential real estate (+5% state duty non-recoverable)
  3. **€280,000** — subordinated bank deposit in a Latvian bank (0% interest)
  4. **€250,000** — interest-free government bonds
  Clean criminal record. Documented source of funds. Health insurance. State fee: ~€10,000 for business route. ^[https://workvisa.guide/blog/latvia-golden-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to **5 years**, renewable. Annually must enter Latvia and complete short registration.
- **Path to citizenship**: ~10 years genuine residence required. Years spent outside Latvia on minimal-stay basis do NOT count. Latvian language + constitution/history exam required. Dual citizenship restrictive.
- **Official source**: https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en
- **Community verdict**: Works well — cheapest EU golden visa. The €50K business route requires genuine company operations, not passive investment. No minimum stay = excellent for mobility but useless for citizenship clock.

### Digital Nomad Visa — Remote Work Visa
- **Official name**: Attālinātā darba vīza (Remote Work Visa)
- **Conditions**: Launched 2022. Non-EU citizen employed by company registered in an **OECD country** (this is a limiting factor — excludes many non-OECD nations). Remote work using ICT. Income sufficient to sustain stay. ^[https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/article/government-approves-regulation-remote-work-visas]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Does NOT directly lead to permanent residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en
- **Community verdict**: Limited — OECD-country employer requirement significantly restricts eligibility.

### Highly Skilled Employment — EU Blue Card
- **Official name**: ES Zilā karte
- **Conditions**: University degree. Job offer from Latvian employer. Salary ≥ 1.5× Latvian average.
- **Duration / renewal**: Duration of contract + 3 months, max 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence → PR → citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard Blue Card route.

## Citizenship
- **Standard**: 5 years of permanent residence + Latvian language proficiency + knowledge of National Anthem, history, culture, and Constitution ^[https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/naturalisation]
- **State fee**: €28.46 (reduced: €4.27)
- **Dual citizenship**: permitted only with **NATO, EU, EFTA** member states and a few other approved countries; otherwise renunciation required ^[https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/naturalisation]
- Processing: 6–12 months after tests passed ^[https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/naturalisation]
- Language exam: Latvian proficiency test + Constitution/history/culture exam

## Tax
- Progressive income tax: 20% (up to €20,004) / 23% (€20,004–€78,100) / 31% above
- Social contributions: ~10.5% employee, ~24.09% employer
- Corporate tax: **0% on reinvested profits**, 20% on distributed profits (similar to Estonia)
- Micro-enterprise tax: 25% of turnover (for qualifying micro-enterprises)

## IT / Digital Nomad
Latvia's Remote Work Visa (2022) requires OECD-country employment — a significant limitation. Riga is a growing tech hub with a strong startup scene and excellent internet. The Golden Visa (€50K business route) might appeal to IT entrepreneurs who want a Latvia-registered company.

## Yacht / Maritime
Baltic Sea coastline (Riga, Ventspils, Liepāja). Yacht registration under Latvian flag available. Small but growing yachting infrastructure. Not a primary yachting destination.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Latvia has adopted a highly restrictive stance toward Russian citizens since 2022. Golden Visa and work permit routes are formally open but face enhanced scrutiny. Latvian language requirement for citizenship is demanding. Significant Russian-speaking minority (~30% of population) provides cultural familiarity, but the political environment is unwelcoming for new Russian applicants. Banking for RU nationals is extremely difficult in Latvia.

## Country Card

### General description
Latvia is an EU/Schengen/NATO/Eurozone member in the Baltic region, offering the cheapest Golden Visa in the EU (€50,000 business equity route). It also has a Remote Work Visa (for OECD-country employers), standard EU Blue Card, and Startup Visa. Latvia imposes no minimum physical stay requirement for Golden Visa holders — an annual entry is sufficient to maintain the permit. Citizenship requires 5 years of permanent residence plus Latvian language and a Constitution/history exam. Dual citizenship is permitted only with NATO/EU/EFTA states and a few approved countries. Latvia has a progressive income tax (20–31%), 0% corporate tax on reinvested profits, and a significant Russian-speaking minority (~30%).

### Well-known strong points
- Cheapest Golden Visa in the EU (€50,000 business equity route)
- No minimum physical stay for Golden Visa holders — excellent for mobility
- 0% corporate tax on reinvested profits (similar to Estonia)
- EU/Schengen/NATO/Eurozone membership with strong passport (182 visa-free)
- Riga is a growing tech hub with excellent internet and vibrant startup scene
- Moderate cost of living in a historic Baltic capital

### Well-known problems
- Golden Visa under political pressure — President signed restriction law in Sep 2026
- Remote Work Visa limited to OECD-country employers — excludes many nationalities
- Low median salary (~€1,300–1,500) relative to Western Europe
- Dual citizenship highly restrictive (NATO/EU/EFTA only, generally)
- Golden Visa years on minimal stay do not count toward citizenship clock
- Demographic decline — population has shrunk ~30% since 1990
