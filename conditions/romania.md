## Overview
Romania is an EU member (since 2007) and a **full Schengen member since January 2025**. It has the fastest broadband in Europe, low cost of living, and a competitive **digital nomad visa** (€3,700/mo income). A proposed Golden Visa (€400,000) is being discussed but not yet law. Citizenship requires **8 years** of residence (2025 law clarified permanent residence requirement). ^[https://remoteworkeurope.eu/insights/romania-digital-nomad-visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### Digital Nomad Visa
- **Official name**: Digital Nomad Visa (Law no. 69/2023)
- **Conditions**: Non-EU/EEA citizen. Remote work using ICT for employers/clients based OUTSIDE Romania. **Income ≥ ~€3,700/month** (3× Romanian average gross salary, ~18,500 RON). Must prove income for preceding **6 months** (bank statements/payslips). Clean criminal record. Health insurance. Accommodation proof. ^[https://remoteworkeurope.eu/insights/romania-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: D visa (90 days) → residence permit 12 months, renewable annually, **max 36 months total**. ^[https://remoteworkeurope.eu/insights/romania-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Path to citizenship**: Does NOT directly lead to permanent residence. After 36 months, must switch to another permit type (work, business) for long-term path.
- **Official source**: https://igi.mai.gov.ro/en
- **Community verdict**: Works well — one of Europe's best DN visas: low cost of living, fastest internet, full Schengen access since 2025. Major hidden gem.

### Tax note for digital nomads
Under Law no. 69/2023, DN visa holders are **exempt from Romanian income tax** on foreign income for stays **up to 183 days** in any 12-month period. After 183 days, tax residency triggers: flat **10% income tax** applies. ^[https://remoteworkeurope.eu/insights/romania-digital-nomad-visa]

### Highly Skilled Employment — EU Blue Card
- **Official name**: Cartea Albastră a UE
- **Conditions**: University degree or 5+ years experience. Employment contract ≥1 year. Salary ≥ threshold (set at 1.5× national average). ^[https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card/eu-blue-card-romania_en]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years → EU long-term residence → citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card/eu-blue-card-romania_en
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard Blue Card route.

### Employment Residency — Single Permit
- **Official name**: Permis unic (Single Permit)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Romanian employer. Labor market test may apply.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence → permanent residence → citizenship at 8 years.
- **Official source**: https://igi.mai.gov.ro/en
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard EU work permit.

## Citizenship
- **Standard**: 8 years of residence (2025 law: **permanent residence now mandatory** before applying) ^[https://rrpb.ro/dreptul-imigrarii-en/the-romanian-citizenship-law-changes-from-2025]
- **Marriage to Romanian**: 5 years (reduced from 8)
- **Born in Romania**: 5 years (reduced from 8)
- **Language**: Romanian proficiency (B1) — Latin language, easier for Romance-language speakers
- 2025 law introduced: mandatory permanent residence before citizenship + Romanian language test required for ALL routes (including ancestry-based reacquisition) ^[https://rrpb.ro/dreptul-imigrarii-en/the-romanian-citizenship-law-changes-from-2025]
- Dual citizenship: **permitted**

## Tax
- Flat income tax: **10%** (one of EU's lowest)
- Social contributions: 35% (25% employer + 10% employee)
- Corporate tax: 16% (3% for micro-enterprises with revenue ≤€500,000 and ≥1 employee)
- Dividend tax: 8%
- DN holders: 0% tax on foreign income under 183 days; 10% flat rate after tax residence established ^[https://remoteworkeurope.eu/insights/romania-digital-nomad-visa]

## IT / Digital Nomad
Romania's digital nomad visa (see above) is one of Europe's standout offerings. With Europe's fastest broadband, low cost of living, full Schengen access, and a flat 10% tax (or 0% under 183-day rule), Romania is highly competitive. Major tech hubs: Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, Timișoara, Iași. Significant IT sector and English-speaking tech workforce.

## Yacht / Maritime
Black Sea coastline (Constanța). Danube River access. Limited yachting infrastructure compared to Mediterranean countries. Yacht registration under Romanian flag available.

## Notes for RU Citizens
The digital nomad visa is open to Russian nationals — one of the more accessible EU routes. Romanian language (Latin-based) is significantly easier for Slavs with any Romance-language exposure. No special RU restrictions beyond standard EU sanctions. Small but growing Russian-speaking community in Bucharest.

## Country Card

Romania is an EU member (since 2007) and full Schengen member since January 2025. It has Europe's fastest broadband, the EU's lowest personal income tax (10% flat), and one of the most competitive digital nomad visas (€3,700/mo; DN holders pay 0% tax if staying under 183 days). Citizenship requires 8 years with mandatory permanent residence since a 2025 law reform. Romanian is a Latin-based Romance language — significantly easier for speakers of French, Spanish, Italian, or Portuguese than Slavic languages. A proposed Golden Visa (€400,000) is under discussion but not yet enacted.

### Well-known strong points
- EU's lowest flat income tax (10%); 0% for DN holders under 183 days
- Fastest broadband in Europe; strong IT sector in Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, Timișoara
- Full Schengen since January 2025 — unrestricted EU travel
- Very low cost of living (66% below NYC); Bucharest rent among EU's cheapest capitals
- Romanian is a Romance (Latin) language — accessible for Romance-language speakers
- Dual citizenship permitted

### Well-known problems
- Low median net salary (~€950/month); significant income inequality
- DN visa max 36 months total — no direct path to permanent residence
- Citizenship requires permanent residence status (mandatory since 2025 law) plus B1 Romanian
- Freedom House score declining (82 in 2025, down from 83); corruption remains a concern
- Proposed Golden Visa (€400,000) not yet enacted — no passive-investment residency route
- Limited yachting infrastructure on the Black Sea; not a premier maritime destination
