## Overview
India's immigration system offers short-stay visas (e-visa, business, employment, student) but no residence-by-investment, passive-income, or digital-nomad program. Long-term residence is effectively reserved for persons of Indian origin via the OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) card — a lifelong, multi-entry visa that is not citizenship. Naturalization requires 12 years of residence and renunciation of prior nationality; India does not recognize dual citizenship. ^[https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-08/OCIIntroduction_23072021%5B1%5D.pdf]

## Per-Program Detail

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Employment Visa (E visa)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from an Indian employer; minimum annual salary INR 2,400,000 (~US$28,600) for most foreign nationals (raised from INR 1,625,000 in Oct 2026) (lower for ethnic cooks, language teachers, embassy staff). ^[https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year initial, renewable up to 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 12 years residence (11 of 14 years + 12 continuous months).
- **Official source**: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/
- **Community verdict**: Works well but the US$25,000 salary floor and employer sponsorship are hurdles for smaller firms.

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Business Visa (B visa)
- **Conditions**: Genuine business purpose (trade, investment, meetings); no fixed capital requirement published; income evidence may be requested.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 5 years.
- **Official source**: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — widely used but does not confer residence rights; not a settlement route.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Employment Visa (E)
- **Conditions**: As above — employer-sponsored, salary floor.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–5 years.
- **Official source**: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for MNC postings; bureaucratic FRRO registration required.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student Visa (S)
- **Conditions**: Admission to recognized institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration (up to 5 years).
- **Official source**: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Entry (X) visa / dependent
- **Conditions**: Spouse/dependent of Indian citizen or OCI holder.
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to sponsor.
- **Official source**: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalization**: 12 years of residence (11 of the preceding 14 years + 12 months immediately preceding application). ^[https://www.mha.gov.in/]
- **OCI route**: OCI holders can apply for citizenship after 5 years registered as OCI + 1 year residence. ^[https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-08/OCIIntroduction_23072021%5B1%5D.pdf]
- **Dual citizenship**: Not recognized. OCI is the workaround (lifelong visa, no political rights, no property in some agricultural categories).
- **Language/renunciation**: Renunciation of prior nationality required.

## Tax
- **Individual income tax**: Progressive (new regime) up to 30% + surcharge (up to 25% on high earners) + 4% cess; effective top rate ~39%–42.7%. ^[https://www.incometax.gov.in/]
- **Residency**: Resident and Ordinarily Resident (ROR) taxed on worldwide income; Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident and non-residents taxed on India-source income only.
- **No special nomad regime.**

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Remote workers for foreign employers cannot legally work long-term on a tourist/e-visa; the practical options are the business visa (limited) or an employment visa with a local entity. India is a major IT/outsourcing hub (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune) but primarily for domestic employment, not foreign remote workers.

## Yacht / Maritime
Long coastline (~7,500 km); major ports: Mumbai, Chennai, Kochi, Visakhapatnam. Recreational yachting is limited; foreign yacht cruising is niche and bureaucratically heavy. Not a mainstream yachting destination.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Visa required (e-visa available). No visa-free entry. Employment visa requires a sponsoring employer; OCI not available to RU nationals without Indian origin. Banking for non-residents is possible but compliance-heavy. Some RU nationals use Goa as a seasonal base on tourist/e-visa (30-day tourist eVisa, extendable only in limited cases).

## Community Pulse
India is not a residence-by-investment market; the only true long-stay vehicle is OCI (for those of Indian origin). Foreigners typically stay on employment or business visas. There is consistent expat frustration with FRRO bureaucracy and the INR 2,400,000 (~US$28,600) employment salary floor.

## Country Card

### General description
India is the world's most populous country and fifth-largest economy, a rising tech and services powerhouse with a young workforce and fast-growing startup scene. For foreigners it is chiefly a career market (MNC postings, tech employment) rather than a relocation or second-citizenship destination: there is no investment, passive-income, or nomad visa, and naturalization takes 12 years with no dual citizenship.

### Well-known strong points
- Very low cost of living (Numbeo index ~24)
- Large, dynamic tech/startup ecosystem (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR)
- English widely used in business and government
- OCI gives persons of Indian origin a lifelong visa and parity in most rights
- Rapid economic growth and expanding infrastructure

### Well-known problems
- No golden visa, DN visa, or self-sponsored residence route
- 12-year naturalization and no dual citizenship
- FRRO/bureaucratic friction for foreign residents
- INR 2,400,000 (~US$28,600) employment-visa salary floor
- Infrastructure and pollution challenges in major cities
