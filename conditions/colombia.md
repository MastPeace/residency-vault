## Overview
Colombia's immigration is managed by the Cancillería (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) and Migración Colombia under Resolution 5477 of 2022. The visa system has three tiers: **Type V** (Visitor — digital nomad, tourism, up to 2 years, no residency clock), **Type M** (Migrant — work, investment, marriage, up to 3 years, accumulates toward residency), and **Type R** (Resident — permanent, indefinite). The Colombian passport provides ~130 visa-free destinations. ^[https://migraciones.co/en]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Type M — Inversionista (Resolución 5477 de 2022)
- **Conditions**: Foreign direct investment minimum ~350× Colombian minimum wage, approximately COP 497 million (~USD 120,000 at 2025 rates) in real estate or a Colombian business. ^[https://nexo.legal/colombian-visa-guide-2025]
- **Duration / renewal**: Type M up to 3 years. Convert to Type R (Resident) after 5 years of accumulated M time.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years on R visa (standard), 2 years if married to Colombian / have Colombian child, 1 year for LATAM/Caribbean nationals.
- **Official source**: https://www.cancilleria.gov.co
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (investment amounts are moderate but R conversion takes 5 years of M time; safe-conduct periods break continuity).

### passive_income_residency
Not available as a distinct category. Passive income from abroad may qualify via the Rentista route (Type M — Rentista) with proof of regular foreign income, though this category is not formally separated from pension/investment in recent regulations.

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Type V — Nómada Digital (Visa V — Visitante)
- **Conditions**: Only for nationals of visa-exempt countries. Minimum monthly income 3× Colombian minimum wage (~COP 4,270,500 / ~USD 1,042/mo). Work must be for foreign employers/clients only. Cannot serve Colombian clients. ^[https://nexo.legal/digital-nomad-colombia-visa-complete-guide]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 2 continuous years, renewable. Does NOT accumulate toward permanent residency or citizenship.
- **Path to citizenship**: No direct path. Must switch to Type M visa to start the residency clock.
- **Official source**: https://www.cancilleria.gov.co
- **Community verdict**: Works well for lifestyle (cheap, long duration, no exit trips). Dead-end for citizenship seekers.

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Type M — Trabajador
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with a Colombian company. Open work permit on M visa. Professional qualifications may be required. ^[https://nexo.legal/colombian-visa-guide-2025]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years, renewable. Convert to R after 5 years of M time.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years on R visa (standard); 2 years R (married/parent); 1 year R (LATAM).
- **Official source**: https://www.cancilleria.gov.co
- **Community verdict**: Works well (standard employment route; Colombian employer sponsorship needed).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Type M — Empresario / Inversionista (business route)
- **Conditions**: Business plan + qualifying investment in a Colombian enterprise. Part of the broader Investor category under Resolution 5477.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years → R after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: Same as investment residency.
- **Official source**: https://www.cancilleria.gov.co
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (requires active business; passive investment in real estate is simpler).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Type M — Trabajador
- **Conditions**: Binding employment contract with Colombian employer.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years R (standard); reduced periods per nationality/family.
- **Official source**: https://www.cancilleria.gov.co
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Type V — Estudiante (short courses) / Type M — Estudiante (degree programs)
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in a Colombian educational institution. Type V for short courses; Type M for degree programs (counts toward residency).
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Path to citizenship**: Only Type M student time counts.
- **Official source**: https://www.cancilleria.gov.co
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Type M — Cónyuge de Colombiano / Padre de Colombiano
- **Conditions**: Marriage to a Colombian citizen (valid up to 3 years). Parent of Colombian-born child. ^[https://nexo.legal/colombian-visa-guide-2025]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years. Convert to R after: 3 years (marriage), 2 years (parent of Colombian child).
- **Path to citizenship**: 2 years on R visa (married/parent category).
- **Official source**: https://www.cancilleria.gov.co
- **Community verdict**: Works well (fastest path to R + citizenship).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Type M — Pensionado
- **Conditions**: Lifetime pension ≥ 3× Colombian minimum wage (~USD 1,042/mo). Pension documentation must be apostilled and translated.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years. Convert to R after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years on R visa (standard).
- **Official source**: https://www.cancilleria.gov.co
- **Community verdict**: Works well (moderate income threshold; cheap cost of living).

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. No direct CBI program. Investment residency (Type M → R) is the pathway to naturalization after 5 years on the R visa.

## Citizenship
- **Standard naturalization**: 5 years continuous on a Resident (R) visa under Article 96 of the Constitution. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/colombian-citizenship]
- **Reduced periods (R visa time)**: 2 years for Spaniards by birth, spouses/permanent partners of Colombians, parents of Colombian children. 1 year for Latin American and Caribbean nationals by birth.
- **Language requirement**: Spanish proficiency + civics exam (Constitution, history, geography). Exempt if holding Colombian high school or university degree, or aged 65+.
- **Processing time**: 6–12 months after filing.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted. Multiple citizenship recognized under 1991 Constitution.
- **Important**: Time on V visa (including digital nomad) does NOT count. Only M visa time counts toward R eligibility; only R time counts toward citizenship.

## Tax
- **Residency trigger**: 183 days in Colombia in any 365-day period, or center of vital interests.
- **Tax system**: Worldwide income. Progressive rates 0%–39%. ^[https://nexo.legal/colombian-visa-guide-2025]
- **Foreign tax credits**: Available under double-taxation treaties.
- **Wealth tax**: Applied periodically (currently suspended for most brackets post-2023 reform).

## IT / Digital Nomad
- Digital nomad visa (Type V) is well-structured: up to 2 years, USD ~1,042/mo threshold, visa-exempt nationalities only. Medellín and Bogotá are major nomad hubs with strong coworking infrastructure and affordable living.
- Major limitation: Type V time does NOT count toward permanent residency or citizenship. Long-term nomads must switch to Type M.

## Yacht / Maritime
Not a major yachting hub. Cartagena has marina facilities. No special yacht/captain residency program.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian citizens require a tourist visa for Colombia (not visa-exempt). Type V digital nomad visa is NOT available to RU citizens (visa-exempt nationalities only).
- RU nationals must apply for Type M visas (work, investment, marriage) at a Colombian consulate.
- Banking: possible with cédula de extranjería; compliance checks may be intensive post-2022.
- No specific bilateral restrictions targeting Russian applicants.

## Community pulse
- **Overall**: Colombia is popular among US/European expats for low cost of living and friendly culture. The 3-tier system (V/M/R) is bureaucratic but navigable.
- **Rejection risk**: Moderate; safe-conduct periods break residency continuity. Visa lapses reset the clock.
- **Costs**: V visa ~USD 393–607 total (govt fees + issuance); M visa ~USD 427–642; R visa ~USD 863.

## Country Card

### General description
Colombia is South America's third-most populous country, located at the continent's northwestern tip with both Caribbean and Pacific coastlines. Its 3-tier visa system (Type V — Visitor / Type M — Migrant / Type R — Resident) offers clear pathways but requires patience: the digital nomad visa (Type V, up to 2 years) does not count toward residency, and citizenship requires 5 years on the R visa after accumulating M time. Medellín, Bogotá, and the Caribbean coast are major expat and digital-nomad hubs with very low costs of living. Colombia has transformed significantly since the 2016 peace accord, though security concerns persist in some regions.

### Well-known strong points
- Very low cost of living (Numbeo index 22.5) — among the cheapest in Latin America
- Well-structured digital nomad visa (Type V): $1,042/mo, up to 2 years, straightforward
- Medellín is one of the world's most famous nomad hubs — strong community, coworking, "eternal spring" climate
- Caribbean and Pacific coastlines, Amazon rainforest, Andes — exceptional geographic/cultural diversity
- Friendly, welcoming culture; large English-speaking expat communities in major cities
- Investment residency threshold is moderate (~$120,000 real estate for Type M)

### Well-known problems
- Type V (digital nomad) time does NOT count toward permanent residency or citizenship
- Weak passport (132 visa-free) compared to MERCOSUR peers (Brazil 170, Argentina 170, Chile 176)
- Safety concerns: petty crime in cities, no-go zones in some rural departments
- Complex 3-tier system: V → M → R → citizenship can take 10+ years total for non-LATAM nationals
- Safe-conduct periods and visa lapses break residency continuity — strict rules
- Russian citizens cannot access the digital nomad visa (visa-exempt only)
