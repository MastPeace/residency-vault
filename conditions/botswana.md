## Overview
Botswana is a politically stable, upper-middle-income Southern African country with a strict but functional immigration system. Residence is primarily through employer-sponsored work permits. There is no dedicated digital nomad visa, investment residency scheme, or retirement visa. Permanent residence requires 5+ years continuous lawful residence. Citizenship requires 10+ years and does not permit dual nationality. ^[https://www.gov.bw/residency-and-work/work-permit-application]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency — Work Permit + Residence Permit
- **Official name**: Work Permit Application (Ministry of Labour) + Residence Permit (Immigration)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from registered Botswana employer. Job must be advertised locally for 6 months (labour market test). Contract of employment. CV, certified educational certificates (foreign language certificates must be translated to English and confirmed by Botswana Qualifications Authority — BQA). ^[https://www.gov.bw/residency-and-work/work-permit-application]
- **Duration**: Up to 5 years (aligned with employment contract). Renewal applications must be submitted 6 months before expiry.
- **Path to citizenship**: Permanent residence after 5+ years continuous lawful residence. Citizenship: minimum 10 years residence (5+ years of PR). ^[https://www.gov.bw/taxonomy/term/115?page=1]
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.bw/residency-and-work/work-permit-application
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — stringent requirements; processing takes 14 working days for work permits; residence permits take longer.

### investment_residency — Investor Work Permit
- **Official name**: Investor Work Permit
- **Conditions**: Proof of investment (bank statement). Company registration documents (Certificate of Incorporation, trading license). Lease/proof of place of operation. List of employees (citizen IDs for Batswana; work/residence permits for non-citizens). Fee: BWP 1,500. ^[https://www.gov.bw/residency-and-work/work-permit-application]
- **Duration**: 1–5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR after 5+ years; citizenship after 10 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.bw/residency-and-work/work-permit-application
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — suitable for genuine business operators. No minimum capital threshold published, but investment must be "substantial."

## Citizenship
- Naturalisation: minimum 10 years of lawful residence (some sources indicate 5 years PR). ^[https://www.gov.bw/taxonomy/term/115?page=1]
- Marriage to Motswana citizen: reduced timeline (varies).
- Language: Setswana or English.
- Dual citizenship: NOT permitted. Botswana requires renunciation of original citizenship upon naturalisation. ^[https://www.gov.bw]
- Passport: visa-free to ~85 destinations including UK, South Africa, Russia (visa-free).
- **Honest assessment**: The 10-year path + prohibition of dual citizenship makes Botswana one of the less attractive naturalisation destinations in the region.

## Tax
- Residence-based taxation. Tax resident if >183 days physical presence or permanent home in Botswana.
- Personal income tax: progressive 0%–25% (top rate on BWP 144,000+/yr, ~USD 10,500).
- Corporate tax: 22%.
- VAT: 14%.
- No capital gains tax (generally).
- Relatively low tax burden compared to South Africa or Namibia.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital nomad visa. Gaborone and Francistown are the main cities. Internet connectivity is decent (4G/LTE, fibre in urban areas). Co-working spaces are limited. Botswana is more suited for traditional employment-based relocation than DN lifestyle. Cost of living: moderate.

## Yacht / Maritime
Not applicable — landlocked country.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa-free for 30 days for Russian citizens. Work and investor permits accessible. Small Russian-speaking community. Botswana maintains cordial relations with Russia. Banking: functional but limited in scope.

## Status

## Country Card

Botswana is one of Africa's most stable democracies and an upper-middle-income country, transformed by diamond wealth since independence in 1966. It's a premier safari destination (Okavango Delta, Chobe National Park) with strict conservation policies. Gaborone is the capital. The immigration system is employer-driven — work permits require local job advertising (6 months) and BQA qualification evaluation. Permanent residence requires 5+ years; citizenship 10+ years. Dual citizenship is not permitted.

### Well-known strong points
- Exceptional governance: Rule of Law 0.62 (rank 45/142), Freedom House 75/100 (Free)
- Low personal income tax (max 25% at BWP 144K/yr, ~USD 10,500 — very low threshold)
- World-class safari tourism (Okavango Delta — UNESCO World Heritage) with high-value, low-volume model
- English is the official language; strong democratic traditions and political stability
- Relatively high GDP per capita (~$19,500 PPP) for sub-Saharan Africa
- No capital gains tax (generally); low corporate tax (22%)

### Well-known problems
- No digital nomad visa, no retirement visa — limited residency options beyond employment
- Dual citizenship is NOT permitted — naturalised citizens must renounce original nationality
- Small, landlocked economy heavily dependent on diamonds; limited economic diversification
- Work permits require employer sponsorship and 6-month local advertising — stringent process
- 10-year citizenship path + dual citizenship ban make naturalisation unattractive
- Small population (~2.7M) and market limits economic opportunities
