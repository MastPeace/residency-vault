## Overview
Malta is the smallest EU member state with a strong passport. It operates the Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP), a [[golden-visa]] that grants permanent residency from day one — rare among EU programmes. Former citizenship-by-investment was struck down by the ECJ in April 2025 (Commission v Malta). Naturalization now follows ordinary rules: 7 years of residence. ^[https://workvisa.guide/blog/malta-golden-visa]

## Residence
**[[Golden-visa]] — Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP)**:
- Immediate permanent residency (not a temporary permit) ^[https://www.ccmalta.com/publications/malta-golden-visa]
- **Rental route**: lease property ≥ €10,000/year (South/Gozo) or ≥ €12,000/year (rest of Malta) for 5 years + €58,000 government contribution
- **Purchase route**: buy property ≥ €275,000 (South/Gozo) or ≥ €320,000 (rest of Malta) + €28,000 government contribution
- **Administrative fee**: €40,000 (non-refundable)
- **Donation**: €2,000 to a local NGO
- **Net worth**: must demonstrate ≥ €500,000 in assets (≥ €150,000 liquid)
- Residence card valid 5 years, renewed on compliance
- **No minimum physical presence** to maintain status
- Processing: ~12 months (rigorous vetting)

**Other residence permits**: work visa, student visa, digital nomad visa (Nomad Residence Permit — 1 year, renewable), family reunification (sponsor income ≥ €18,940/year +20% per dependent in 2026). ^[https://imperiallegal.com/malta-residence]

## Citizenship
- 7 years of legal residence (naturalization)
- Maltese or English language proficiency
- Clean criminal record
- Genuine integration assessment
- **No citizenship-by-investment** (ECJ struck down in April 2025)
- Dual citizenship permitted

## Tax
Malta's tax system is based on remittance: foreign income is taxed only if remitted to Malta. Non-domiciled residents benefit from 0% tax on foreign-source income not remitted. Personal income tax: progressive 0%–35%. Corporate tax: 35% (with full imputation system, effective rate ~5% for shareholders).

## IT / Digital Nomad
Malta's **Nomad Residence Permit** (launched 2021):
- 1-year residence permit, renewable
- Income: ≥ €2,700/month (gross)
- Remote work for foreign employers/clients
- Application fee: €300
- The permit does NOT lead to permanent residence or citizenship directly

## Yacht / Maritime
Malta has the largest ship registry in the EU and a premier maritime jurisdiction. Yacht registration under the Maltese flag is highly popular due to competitive tonnage tax and favorable VAT treatment (yacht leasing scheme). Recognized captain and crew licensing. Major marinas: Grand Harbour (Valletta), Msida, Portomaso.

## Notes for RU citizens
The MPRP ([[golden-visa]]) is accessible to Russian nationals subject to enhanced due diligence screening. Banking in Malta for RU passport holders may require additional documentation. The remittance-based tax system is particularly favorable for RU citizens with income sources outside Malta.
