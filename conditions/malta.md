## Overview
Malta is the smallest EU member state with a strong passport and full Schengen membership. It operates the **Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP)** — a golden visa that grants **permanent residency from day one** (rare among EU programmes). Former citizenship-by-investment was struck down by the ECJ in April 2025. Naturalization now follows ordinary rules: **7 years** of residence. Malta permits dual citizenship and offers a remittance-based tax system. ^[https://workvisa.guide/blog/malta-golden-visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### Investment Residency — MPRP (Golden Visa)
- **Official name**: Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP)
- **Conditions**: Grants **permanent residency from day one** — NOT a temporary permit. ^[https://residencymalta.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/0650-RM-MPRP-Summary-Sheet-amends_OUTPUT_Jan24.pdf]
  **Rental route**:
  - Lease property ≥ €12,000/year (Malta) or €10,000/year (South Malta/Gozo) for 5 years
  - Government contribution: **€58,000**
  - Admin fee: **€40,000** (non-refundable: €10,000 on submission + €30,000 on approval)
  - Donation: **€2,000** to local NGO
  - Total cash outlay: ~€100,000 minimum
  **Purchase route**:
  - Buy property ≥ €350,000 (Malta) or €300,000 (South/Gozo), hold ≥5 years
  - Government contribution: **€28,000**
  - Admin fee: **€40,000** (same)
  - Donation: **€2,000**
  **All routes**: Net worth ≥€500,000 (≥€150,000 liquid). Clean criminal record. Rigorous due diligence. ^[https://residencymalta.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/0650-RM-MPRP-Summary-Sheet-amends_OUTPUT_Jan24.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: **Permanent**. Card valid 5 years, renewed on compliance. No expiration of status. ^[https://www.ccmalta.com/publications/malta-golden-visa]
- **Path to citizenship**: 7 years of legal residence → naturalization. Maltese or English language. Genuine integration assessment. ^[https://iworld.com/en/blog/malta-citizenship]
- **Official source**: https://residencymalta.gov.mt
- **Community verdict**: Works well — rare "permanent from day one" design. Rigorous vetting but high-value outcome. Expensive (~€100K-150K+ total outlay) but grants immediate permanent status.

### Digital Nomad Visa — Nomad Residence Permit
- **Official name**: Nomad Residence Permit
- **Conditions**: Launched 2021. Non-EU citizen working remotely for foreign employers/clients. **Income ≥ €2,700/month (gross)**. ^[https://imperiallegal.com/malta-residence] Health insurance. Accommodation in Malta. Application fee: €300. ^[https://imperiallegal.com/malta-residence]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Does NOT lead to permanent residence or citizenship directly. Must switch to other permit type (employment, MPRP, etc.) for long-term path.
- **Official source**: https://residencymalta.gov.mt
- **Community verdict**: Works well — relatively low income threshold (€2,700/mo), English-speaking country, good quality of life. No path to PR from the permit alone.

### Family Reunification
- **Official name**: Family reunification residence permit
- **Conditions**: Sponsor income ≥ **€18,940/year** + 20% per additional dependent (2026 threshold). Valid health insurance. Accommodation. ^[https://imperiallegal.com/malta-residence]
- **Duration / renewal**: Matching sponsor permit validity, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Same as sponsor — 7 years total.
- **Official source**: https://identitymalta.com
- **Community verdict**: Works well — clearly defined income thresholds.

## Citizenship
- **Standard naturalization**: **7 years** of legal residence ^[https://iworld.com/en/blog/malta-citizenship]
- **Language**: Maltese or **English** proficiency (English is an official language — major advantage)
- **Clean criminal record**
- **Genuine integration assessment**
- **Dual citizenship**: **permitted** ^[https://iworld.com/en/blog/malta-citizenship]
- **CBI (Citizenship by Investment)**: TERMINATED — ECJ struck down in April 2025 (Commission v Malta). Existing CBI holders' status being evaluated. ^[https://freemalta.com/know/malta-citizenship-mein]

## Tax
Malta's tax system is **remittance-based** for non-domiciled residents:
- Foreign income taxed **only if remitted to Malta**
- Non-domiciled residents: 0% tax on foreign-source income not remitted
- Personal income tax: progressive **0%–35%**
- Corporate tax: 35% (with **full imputation system**, effective rate ~5% for shareholders)
- No wealth tax, no inheritance tax
- English is an official language — tax system operates in English

## IT / Digital Nomad
Malta's Nomad Residence Permit (€2,700/mo) is one of the EU's more accessible DN visas. English-speaking country — major advantage for IT professionals. Strong digital infrastructure, EU's first regulated blockchain/DLT framework (attracting crypto/fintech). The €300 application fee is low. Small island — limited domestic market for business development.

## Yacht / Maritime
Malta has the **largest ship registry in the EU** and a premier maritime jurisdiction. Yacht registration under Maltese flag is highly popular due to competitive tonnage tax and favorable VAT treatment (yacht leasing scheme). Recognized captain and crew licensing. Major marinas: Grand Harbour (Valletta), Msida, Portomaso. A global maritime hub.

## Notes for RU Citizens
The MPRP (permanent residency, ~€100K+ total outlay) is accessible to Russian nationals subject to enhanced due diligence screening. Banking in Malta for RU passport holders may require additional documentation. The remittance-based tax system is favorable for RU citizens with income sources outside Malta. English as an official language removes the language barrier hurdle that exists in most other EU states. The Russian-speaking community in Malta is small but present.

## Country Card

Malta is the smallest EU member state — a Mediterranean island nation with full Schengen membership and a strong passport. It operates the Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP), one of the rare EU golden visas that grants permanent residency from day one (~€100K+ total outlay). Its citizenship-by-investment programme was terminated by ECJ ruling in April 2025. English is an official language, making Malta uniquely accessible for Anglophone expats. The economy is service-driven with strengths in gaming, financial services, and maritime. As a densely populated micro-state, space and infrastructure are limited.

### Well-known strong points
- MPRP grants permanent residency from day one — rare in EU golden visa programmes
- English as an official language — no language barrier for citizenship (English accepted alongside Maltese)
- 7-year citizenship path with genuine integration assessment
- Remittance-based taxation: 0% on foreign-source income not remitted to Malta
- Largest EU ship registry; premier maritime and yachting jurisdiction
- 2,900+ sunshine hours annually; strong expat community

### Well-known problems
- Citizenship-by-investment terminated by ECJ (April 2025) — existing holders' status under evaluation
- Very high population density; limited housing supply drives up rents (€1,050+ for 1-bed)
- Nomad Residence Permit does not lead to permanent residence — must switch routes
- Small domestic market limits business growth opportunities
- Nomad permit income threshold of €2,700/mo is relatively high compared to cost of living
- Infrastructure strain and construction pressure from rapid population growth
