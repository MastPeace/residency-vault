## Overview
Madagascar is a large island nation in the Indian Ocean off East Africa. Immigration is governed by the Foreigners' Code and, for investors, the 2023 Investment Law (which dedicates a chapter to visas and employment permits). There is no digital-nomad visa. Citizenship requires 5 years of residence. Dual nationality is not permitted for Malagasy of origin (naturalised persons may retain their prior nationality). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malagasy_nationality_law]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency — Work Permit
- **Official name**: Permis de Travail (Work Permit) + Carte de Résident
- **Conditions**: Employment contract; employer sponsorship; employer must justify hiring a foreigner.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable, tied to the contract.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence (Nationality Code, Art. 27). ^[https://expatsi.com/visas/madagascar/investor-visa]
- **Official source**: https://www.edbm.mg (Economic Development Board of Madagascar)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — slow, bureaucratic; French and Malagasy essential.

### investment_residency — Investor Visa
- **Official name**: Investor Visa (under the 2023 Investment Law, Ch. IV on visas/employment permits) ^[https://investmentpolicy.unctad.org/investment-laws/laws/367/madagascar-madagascar-investment-law-2023]
- **Conditions**: Registered investment project; company formation typically precedes the visa; EDBM one-stop facilitation.
- **Duration / renewal**: Renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.edbm.mg
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — facilitation exists but practical experience varies; French essential.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 5 years residence for naturalisation. ^[https://expatsi.com/visas/madagascar/investor-visa]
- **Language**: Malagasy/French; integration assessed.
- **Dual citizenship**: Not permitted for Malagasy of origin; naturalised persons may retain their prior nationality. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malagasy_nationality_law]
- **Passport**: ~58 destinations visa-free or on arrival.

## Tax
- Residence-based; tax resident if >183 days or centre of economic interests.
- Personal income tax (IRSA): progressive up to 20%.
- Corporate tax: 20% standard. VAT: 20%.
- No special expat flat-tax regime.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Antananarivo has a nascent tech/BPO scene and improving mobile internet, but power and connectivity remain uneven outside the capital. Remote workers generally need an investor or employer route. French is essential.

## Yacht / Maritime
Island nation with a long coastline and the port of Toamasina. Niche diving/marine tourism (Nosy Be, Île Sainte-Marie). Limited yacht infrastructure; not a major yachting destination.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa on arrival is available for many nationalities (check current RU status). Madagascar maintains neutral relations with Russia. French is the primary administrative language; English is limited. Small expat community, concentrated in Antananarivo, tourism and mining.

## Community pulse
Limited English-language expat forum data. The expat presence is small and concentrated in tourism, mining and NGOs. No significant digital-nomad base; bureaucracy and infrastructure are the main reported frictions.

## Country Card

### General description
Madagascar is a large, biodiverse island nation in the Indian Ocean, famed for unique wildlife and eco-tourism. Its economy is agricultural with growing mining (nickel, cobalt) and tourism. Immigration is employment- and investment-led, with a 2023 Investment Law streamlining investor visas. Citizenship requires only 5 years of residence, but dual nationality is restricted.

### Well-known strong points
- Short 5-year path to citizenship
- Extraordinary natural environment and eco-tourism
- Low cost of living
- 2023 Investment Law facilitates investor visas/permits
- Long coastline with marine-tourism potential

### Well-known problems
- Dual nationality not permitted for Malagasy of origin
- No digital-nomad or passive-income visa
- French (and often Malagasy) language effectively mandatory
- Weak infrastructure — power and transport outside the capital
- Poverty and political instability episodes (FH 55/100)
- Very low median incomes

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-11
- **Recent changes**: 2023 Investment Law (Ch. IV on visas/employment permits) is the current framework.
- **Watch items**: Any digital-nomad visa; dual-citizenship reform; EDBM permit processing.
