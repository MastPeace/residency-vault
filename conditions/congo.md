## Overview
The Republic of the Congo (Congo-Brazzaville) is an oil-rich Central African coastal nation (pop. ~6.1M). Immigration is governed by the Nationality Code (Loi 35-61, 1961). Naturalization: ~10 years residence. Passport: 19 visa-free destinations. ^[https://data.worldbank.org/country/congo-rep]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Permis de travail / Carte de sejour
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Congolese employer; labor market approval; employer sponsorship.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: ~10 years residence. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_the_Congo_nationality_law]
- **Official source**: Ministere de l'Interieur (no online portal).
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data (expat presence: oil, diplomatic, development).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Visa d'etude
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Congolese institution; proof of financial means.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: Residence time counts toward naturalization clock.
- **Official source**: Congolese embassies / consulates.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Regroupement familial
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; proof of relationship and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: ~10 years residence.
- **Official source**: Immigration authorities.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

## Citizenship
- **Residence**: ~10 years under Nationality Code (Loi 35-61). Spouse: 5 years marriage + residence. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_the_Congo_nationality_law]
- **Language**: French proficiency expected.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted (since 2002).
- **Processing**: Discretionary, by decree.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive income tax; corporate tax ~28%. Oil sector has special regimes.
- **Remote workers**: No special regime. CFA-franc-zone member (XAF pegged to EUR).
- **General**: Tax administration developing; large informal sector.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. Internet in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire is adequate (4G). Coworking virtually nonexistent. Not a nomad destination. [[angola]] and [[gabon]] are regional alternatives.

## Yacht / Maritime
Atlantic coastline (~170 km); major port of Pointe-Noire (oil export hub). Limited yacht tourism. Maritime economy dominated by offshore oil.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Visa required. No special RU programs. Russian oil/mining interests present. Passport is weak (19 destinations).

## Community Pulse
No expat immigration community outside oil/diplomatic sectors. Not discussed on nomad/retiree/investor forums. Bureaucratic processes dominate.

## Country Card

### General description
The Republic of the Congo (Congo-Brazzaville) is an oil-rich Central African coastal nation with a population concentrated in the south. CFA-franc-zone member with moderately high GDP per capita for the region (~$6,700 PPP), but extreme political inequality and authoritarian rule (FH 17/100). Minimal immigration infrastructure.

### Well-known strong points
- CFA-franc-zone stability (XAF pegged to EUR)
- Atlantic coast with deep-water port (Pointe-Noire)
- Dual citizenship permitted
- Relatively high GDP per capita for Central Africa

### Well-known problems
- Authoritarian rule (FH 17/100); endemic political patronage
- Oil-dependent economy; high inequality
- Long naturalization period (~10 years); no formal residency-by-investment
- Weak passport (19 destinations

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-11-02
- **Recent changes**: No known recent changes. Stable though authoritarian rule under Preident Denis Sassou Nguesso (since 1997).
- **Watch items**: Oil revenue volatility. Political succession. CFA reform debate.
