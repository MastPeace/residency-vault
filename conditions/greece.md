## Overview
Greece operates Europe's most affordable [[golden-visa]] programme, starting at €250,000 for qualifying property investments. Citizenship requires 7 years of genuine residence (183 days/year). Law 5275/2026 brought significant immigration reforms in 2026. ^[https://getgoldenvisa.com/ultimate-guide-to-greece-golden-visa]

## Residence
**[[Golden-visa]] (B.5 residence permit)** under Law 5038/2023: ^[https://getgoldenvisa.com/ultimate-guide-to-greece-golden-visa]
- **€250,000**: commercial-to-residential conversions or listed building restorations (once per property)
- **€400,000**: rest of Greece
- **€800,000**: high-demand zones (Athens, Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Santorini, most islands)
- Valid: 5 years, renewable indefinitely while investment held
- **No minimum stay** requirement to maintain permit
- **No short-term letting** of Golden Visa properties (prohibited, €50,000 fine)
- Application fee: €2,000 main applicant, €150 per family member
- Processing: ~6–9 months

**Note**: The Golden Visa permit does NOT automatically accumulate toward citizenship — physical presence (183 days/year) is required. The 5-year permit validity now runs from card issuance date (Law 5275/2026). ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/greece-golden-visa]

**Digital nomad visa**: Greece introduced a digital nomad visa in 2021 for remote workers. Income requirement: ~€3,500/month. 1-year validity, renewable.

**Work permits**: available through employer sponsorship with labor market test.

## Citizenship
- 7 consecutive years of legal residence (183 days/year minimum)
- B1 Greek language proficiency + civic knowledge exam
- Clean criminal record
- Dual citizenship permitted ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/greek-dual-citizenship]
- Spouses of Greek citizens: 3 years if they have a child together, 5 years without children

## Tax
Progressive income tax: 9%–44%. 7% flat tax on foreign pension income for qualifying retirees. 50% income tax exemption for qualifying impatriate workers (for 7 years). Corporate tax: 22%. No wealth tax.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Greece's [[digital-nomad]] visa, introduced in 2021, allows remote workers to live in Greece for up to 1 year (renewable). Income requirement ~€3,500/month. 50% tax exemption available for qualifying digital nomads who relocate their tax residence to Greece for 7 years.

## Yacht / Maritime
Greece has one of the world's largest coastlines and is a premier yachting destination. Yacht registration under the Greek flag is available. The country issues recognized captain and crew licenses. Major maritime hubs: Piraeus, Rhodes, Corfu, and thousands of islands with substantial marina infrastructure.

## Notes for RU citizens
Greece's Golden Visa was extremely popular with Russian investors before 2022. Current sanctions/restrictions make property purchases and bank transfers for RU citizens more complex but not impossible via third-country structures. Despite the €800,000 threshold in premium areas, the €250,000 conversion/restoration route remains available. The digital nomad visa is also accessible. Greece has a small but established Russian-speaking community.
