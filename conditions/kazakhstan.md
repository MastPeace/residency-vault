## Overview
Kazakhstan launched an innovative [[digital-nomad-visa|digital nomad visa]] Residency programme (10-year permanent residence) and a [[investment-residency|golden visa]]-equivalent investor route ($300K) (10-year permanent residence for qualifying IT specialists) and a Digital Nomad Visa (Neo Nomad Visa) in late 2024. However, traditional residence permit rules were tightened under a 2026 pilot project restricting new applicants and channeling them to specific regions. Kazakhstan is an EAEU member. ^[https://timesca.com/tag/digital-nomads]

## Residence
**General residence permit (permanent)**: ^[https://en.tengrinews.kz/article/kazakhstan-has-tightened-migration-policy-who-is-affected-200]
- Valid: 10 years
- Processing: up to 45 calendar days
- **2026 pilot project restrictions** (effective until Dec 31, 2026):
  - Annual proof of ≥ 5.7M tenge (~$12,000) in a bank account
  - Mandatory 1-year residence in designated regions: East Kazakhstan, Kostanay, Pavlodar, or North Kazakhstan (Almaty and Astana effectively closed for new applicants)
  - Exceptions: certain categories (healthcare, education, specific skills)

**Temporary residence permit**: issued for 1 year, renewable, based on employment, study, or family reunification.

**Investor visa (Golden Visa)**: ≥ $300,000 investment in Kazakh companies/securities on local exchanges → 10-year residence permit. Electronic application available. ^[https://timesca.com/tag/digital-nomads]

As of March 2026: 241,000 foreigners permanently reside in Kazakhstan; 40.4% (97,500) are Russian citizens. ^[https://en.tengrinews.kz/kazakhstan_news/legal-status-of-a-foreigner-in-kazakhstan-residence-permit-271239]

## Citizenship
- 5 years of permanent residence (after obtaining residence permit)
- 183 days/year physical presence required
- Kazakh language proficiency (basic)
- Renunciation of previous citizenship required
- ~7 years total from arrival to citizenship

## Tax
Flat income tax: 10%. Corporate tax: 20%. Social tax: 11%. Kazakhstan's Investment Tax Residency Programme grants investors tax residency after only 90 days in the country. EAEU membership provides tax coordination with Russia, Belarus, Armenia, and Kyrgyzstan.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Kazakhstan has two dedicated programs: ^[https://astanatimes.com/2026/08/262-foreign-it-specialists-receive-digital-nomad-status-in-kazakhstan]

**Digital Nomad Residency** (launched January 2025):
- **10-year permanent residence permit** for qualified IT specialists
- As of August 2026: 250 IT specialists have received permits
- 1,069 petitions issued by Astana Hub out of 1,517 applications from 71 countries
- No income requirement (unlike most other DN programmes)

**Digital Nomad Visa (Neo Nomad Visa)**:
- **$3,000/month income requirement**
- Valid health insurance, clean criminal record
- 12 visas issued as of August 2026
- Permits work with both foreign and Kazakh companies

## Yacht / Maritime
Not applicable. Kazakhstan is landlocked (Caspian Sea access but limited yachting infrastructure).

## Notes for RU citizens
Kazakhstan is a primary destination for Russian relocants: visa-free entry, no language barrier, accessible banking, EAEU membership (mutual recognition of qualifications). The 2026 pilot project tightening has made Almaty and Astana inaccessible for new residence permit applicants — this is a significant change affecting Russian relocants who typically cluster in these cities. The Digital Nomad Residency (10-year permit, no income requirement) is exceptionally generous and widely used by Russian IT professionals. ^[https://en.tengrinews.kz/article/kazakhstan-has-tightened-migration-policy-who-is-affected-200]

## Country Card

### General description
Kazakhstan is the largest Central Asian economy and an EAEU member, spanning from the Caspian Sea to China. It launched an innovative Digital Nomad Residency programme (10-year permanent residence for IT specialists, no income requirement) and a Neo Nomad Visa ($3,000/month) in late 2024 — both exceptionally generous compared to global norms. An investor route ($300,000) also grants a 10-year residence permit. However, a 2026 pilot project severely restricted new residence permit applications: mandatory 1-year stay in designated northern regions and annual proof of ~$12,000 in a bank account, effectively closing Almaty and Astana for new applicants. Kazakhstan has a flat 10% income tax, EAEU mutual recognition of qualifications, and is the primary destination for Russian relocants. Citizenship requires 5 years of PR, Kazakh language, and renunciation of previous citizenship.

### Well-known strong points
- Digital Nomad Residency: 10-year PR for IT specialists with no income requirement — most generous globally
- Flat 10% income tax — one of the lowest in the world
- Very low cost of living (Numbeo ~30) with modern infrastructure in major cities
- Visa-free entry for Russian and many CIS citizens; EAEU mutual recognition of qualifications
- No language barrier for Russian speakers — Russian is an official language
- Investment Tax Residency: tax residency after only 90 days for qualifying investors

### Well-known problems
- 2026 pilot project severely restricts new residence permits — Almaty/Astana closed to new applicants
- Low Freedom House score (23/100 Not Free) — authoritarian governance, restricted civil liberties
- Weak rule of law (WJP 0.54, #65) — corruption and arbitrary enforcement are common
- Weak passport (78 visa-free destinations, Henley #61) — limited global mobility
- Citizenship requires renunciation of previous citizenship — dual citizenship not permitted
- Harsh continental climate — Astana is the second-coldest capital in the world
