## Overview
Lesotho is a high-altitude, landlocked kingdom entirely surrounded by South Africa (pop. ~2.3M). Immigration is governed by the Immigration and Citizenship Bill (2018 framework). Naturalization: 5 years residence. Passport: 73 visa-free destinations (strong for Africa via SADC + Commonwealth). ^[https://data.worldbank.org/country/LS]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work permit / Residence permit
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from Basotho employer; labor market preference for locals.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence. ^[https://www.migrationdataportal.org/sites/g/files/tmzbdl251/files/2018-09/Migration%20Governance%20Snapshot-The%20Kingdom%20Republic%20of%20Lesotho.pdf]
- **Official source**: Ministry of Home Affairs — Immigration Department.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data (small expat presence; textile/diamond sectors).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student visa
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Basotho institution; proof of financial means.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path o citizenship**: Residence time counts toward 5-year clock.
- **Official source**: Lesotho embassies / consulates.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Family residence
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; proof of relationship and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence.
- **Official source**: Immigration authorities.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

## Citizenship
- **Residence**: 5 years for permanent residence, leading to eligibility for naturalization under the Immigration and Citizenship Bill. ^[https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/1971/01/Lesotho-Citizenship-Order-1971.compressed.pdf]
- **Language**: Sesotho and/or English proficiency expected.
- **Dual citizenship**: Not generally permitted under the 1971 Citizenship Order (framework under revision).
- **Processing**: Discretionary; Bill 2018 pending full implementation.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive income tax; corporate tax ~25%.
- **Remote workers**: No special regime. Lesotho is part of the SACU (Southern African Customs Union) and CMA with South Africa.
- **General**: Loti (LSL) pegged to ZAR. Tax administration developing.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. Internet infrastructure is developing (4G in Maseru, limited in highlands). Coworking is virtually nonexistent. Lesotho is not a nomad destination. [[south-africa]] dominates the regional market.

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked country. No maritime or yachting relevance.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Visa required (obtainable at Lesotho embassies, often via South Africa). No special RU programs. Lesotho has diplomatic relations with Russia but no significant bilateral migration.

## Community Pulse
Small expat community centered on textile manufacturing, diamonds, and development aid. Not discussed on nomad or investor-immigration forums. Lesotho's main immigration relationship is with South Africa (labor migration).

## Country Card

### General description
Lesotho is a high-altitude, landlocked kingdom entirely surrounded by South Africa. Its economy is tightly integrated with South Africa's via the SACU and CMA. It has one of the stronger passports in Africa (73 destinations) and is rated "Free" by Freedom House (66/100). Naturalization at 5 years is relatively accessible. The immigration framework is under legislative update.

### Well-known strong points
- Strong passport for Africa (73 destinations; SADC + Commonwealth)
- Rated "Free" by Freedom House (66/100) — rare in the region
- Relatively short naturalization period (5 years)
- Cool highland climate; unique geography

### Well-known problems
- Landlocked; completely dependent on South Africa economically
- High HIV prevalence; limited healthcare infrastructure
- Small economy with few formal employment opportunities
- Dual citizenship not generally permitted (under revision)
