## Overview
Burkina Faso is a landlocked Sahelian country in West Africa (pop. ~23M). Immigration is governed by nationality law with limited formal residency programs. Naturalization: 10 years residence (2 years for those born in the country or with distinguished service). Passport: 30 visa-free destinations. ^[https://data.worldbank.org/country/BF]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Permis de travail / Carte de sejour
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from Burkinabe employer; labor market approval.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years residence (2 years reduced). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burkinabe_nationality_law]
- **Official source**: Ministere de l'Administration Territoriale (no online portal).
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data (tiny expat presence; mostly NGO/diplomatic).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Visa d'etude
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized institution; proof of financial means.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: Residence time counts toward 10-year clock.
- **Official source**: Burkinabe embassies / consulates.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Regroupement familial
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; proof of relationship and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years.
- **Official source**: Immigration authorities.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

## Citizenship
- **Residence**: 10 years continuous; 2 years if born in Burkina Faso or distinguished service. Spouse: 5 years marriage + residence. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burkinabe_nationality_law]
- **Language**: French proficiency expected.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted since independence.
- **Processing**: By decree; discretionary.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive personal income tax; corporate tax ~25%.
- **Remote workers**: No special regime. CFA-franc-zone member (XOF pegged to EUR).
- **General**: Tax administration developing; limited enforcement outside formal sector.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. Internet penetration low (~20%); major cities (Ouagadougou, Bobo-Dioulasso) have 4G. Coworking nascent. [[senegal]] and [[cote-divoire]] are francophone-Africa alternatives.

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked. No maritime or yachting relevance.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Visa required from Burkinabe embassies. No RU-friendly programs. Minor Russian mining interests. Passport is weak.

## Community Pulse
No expat immigration community. Security crisis (jihadist insurgency since 2015) severely constrains relocation interest. Not discussed on nomad/retiree/investor forums.

## Country Card

### General description
Burkina Faso is a landlocked, low-income Sahelian country facing severe jihadist insurgencies. CFA franc zone member. Minimal immigration infrastructure; no dedicated residency-by-investment or digital-nomad programs. Naturalization: 10 years (2 years exceptional).

### Well-known strong points
- CFA-franc-zone stability (XOF pegged to EUR)
- Francophone gateway to the Sahel
- Low cost of living (data scarce)
- Naturalization reduced to 2 years for those born in country

### Well-known problems
- Major security crisis; large-scale internal displacement
- Extremely weak rule of law (FH 25/100, WJP rank 98/142)
- Landlocked, minimal infrastructure
- No formal residency programs; weak passport

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-11
- **Recent changes**: Junta government (since 2022 coups). Ongoing security deterioration.
- **Watch items**: Political transition. Nationality-law reforms. Security situation.
