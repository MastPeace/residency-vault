## Overview
Canada has one of the world's strongest passports and a large, structured immigration system with annual targets. The 2026–2028 Immigration Levels Plan sets 380,000 permanent resident admissions in 2026, with a strong emphasis on economic immigration. Express Entry is the flagship system — it issued a record 114,865 ITAs by mid-August 2026. Canada permits dual citizenship and is among the most accessible G7 nations for skilled migration. ^[https://immigration.ca/express-entry-sets-new-annual-ita-record-2026]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Programme des investisseurs immigrants du Québec (QIIP)
- **Conditions**: Minimum net worth CAD $2,000,000; at least 2 years of management experience in the preceding 5 years; French oral proficiency at level 7 (Échelle québécoise); through an approved financial intermediary: 5-year government-guaranteed investment of CAD $1,000,000 plus a non-refundable contribution of CAD $200,000. This is the ONLY cash-for-PR program in Canada; the federal Start-Up Visa is suspended. ^[https://immigration.ca/quebec-immigration-overview/]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent residence on grant. Must intend to settle in Quebec.
- **Path to citizenship**: 1,095 days physical presence in Canada during the 5 years before application; English or French at CLB/NCLC 4; citizenship test; dual citizenship allowed.
- **Official source**: https://www.quebec.ca/en/immigration
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (high cost, strict French requirement, Quebec-only settlement; active program but limited intake).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: Canada has no passive income / independent means residence route.
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (no pathway; visitors are limited to 6 months).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No DN visa. Canada's visitor visa allows remote work for foreign employers for up to 6 months but does not grant residence status.
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (visitor route works informally; no formal DN pathway).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Express Entry — Entrée express (Federal Skilled Worker / Canadian Experience Class / Federal Skilled Trades)
- **Conditions**: Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score-based. Recent CEC-only draw cutoffs: 521–523 (Aug–Sep 2026). PNP nomination adds 600 CRS points (effective base ~160). Category-based draws for healthcare, French speakers, trades. Age 18–44 scores best (max points at 20–29). Minimum CLB 7 English/French. Job offer NOT required. ^[https://ircc.com/express-entry]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent residence on grant after ITA + 60-day submission. Processing: ~6 months standard.
- **Path to citizenship**: 1,095 days physical presence (3 years) in 5 years before application; CLB/NCLC 4; citizenship test; dual citizenship allowed.
- **Official source**: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html
- **Community verdict**: Works well (fastest skilled migration route to PR among G7; high CRS cutoffs are the main challenge).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Start-Up Visa (SUV) — Programme de visa pour démarrage d'entreprise
- **Conditions**: SUSPENDED to new applications since Dec 31, 2025. Previously: CAD $200K from designated VC fund, or $75K from angel group, or acceptance into incubator. IRCC promises a "new, targeted pilot program" later in 2026. ^[https://immigration.ca/canada-business-and-investment-immigration-overview]
- **Duration / renewal**: Not accepting applications.
- **Path to citizenship**: N/A (program suspended).
- **Official source**: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/start-visa.html
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (program closed; replacement timeline uncertain).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Temporary Foreign Worker Program (TFWP) / International Mobility Program (IMP) → Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) → PR
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Canadian employer; LMIA for TFWP (not always for IMP). PNP streams vary by province (each has occupation lists, investment thresholds for entrepreneur streams). Many PNPs are aligned with Express Entry for faster PR processing.
- **Duration / renewal**: Work permit typically 1–3 years; PNP → PR pathway.
- **Path to citizenship**: After PR: 1,095 days physical presence; CLB/NCLC 4; citizenship test; dual allowed.
- **Official source**: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html
- **Community verdict**: Works well (PNP is a reliable PR bridge; LMIA process can be lengthy).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Study Permit → Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) → Express Entry / PNP
- **Conditions**: Enrollment at a Designated Learning Institution (DLI); proof of funds (tuition + CAD $20,635 living expenses for 2026); PGWP up to 3 years depending on program length. Canadian education and work experience add CRS points.
- **Duration / renewal**: Study permit for program duration; PGWP for 1–3 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: PGWP → PR via Express Entry/PNP → 1,095 days physical presence.
- **Official source**: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada.html
- **Community verdict**: Works well (strong study-to-PR pipeline; PGWP is valuable bridge).

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Family Class Sponsorship
- **Conditions**: Sponsr must be Canadian citizen or PR aged 18+; demonstrate financial ability to support (no social assistance); undertaking for 3–10 years. Spouse/common-law/children: priority processing. Parents/grandparents: lottery-based Super Visa or PGP (limited quota).
- **Duration / renewal**: PR on grant for sponsored family members.
- **Path to citizenship**: Standard 1,095 days after PR.
- **Official source**: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/family-sponsorship.html
- **Community verdict**: Works well for spouses; PGP lottery is problematic (limited availability).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No retirement visa. Options: visitor visa (6 months), or PR through economic/family routes. Some PNPs have entrepreneur streams that older applicants may use.
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (no dedicated retirement pathway).

## Citizenship
- **Years of residence**: 1,095 days (3 years) physical presence in the 5 years before application. ^[https://ircc.com/news/canadian-citizenship-rules-abroad-2026-residency-and-tax-obligations]
- **Language**: CLB/NCLC 4 in English or French (ages 18–54).
- **Exam**: Citizenship test on Canadian history, geography, government, rights and responsibilities ("Discover Canada" guide).
- **Timeline**: 8–15 months processing for certificate; oath ceremony.
- **Dual citizenship**: Fully permitted since 1977. Bill C-3 (Dec 2025) extended citizenship by descent beyond first generation. ^[https://ircc.com/news/canadian-citizenship-law-changes-2026-whats-new-and-who-benefits]
- **Fee**: CAD $653 adults, $100 minors (2026).

## Tax
- **Residency-based taxation**: Tax residents pay federal + provincial tax on worldwide income. Progressive federal rates: 15%–33% (top rate above CAD $246,752). Provincial rates add ~5%–15%. Combined top marginal rate ~53.3% (varies by province).
- **Non-residents**: Taxed only on Canadian-source income.
- **Capital gains**: 50% inclusion rate. No wealth tax, no inheritance tax. TFSA and RRSP tax-advantaged accounts available to residents.
- **Corporate tax**: 15% federal + provincial (~12%), combined ~27%.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Canada has no dedicated [[digital-nomad]] visa. Remote workers on visitor status may work for foreign employers but cannot establish tax residence. The tech ecosystem (Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal) is strong; Express Entry favours tech occupations in category-based draws. Global Talent Stream (LMIA-exempt work permit) processes tech hires in ~2 weeks.

## Yacht / Maritime
Canada has extensive coastlines and well-developed marinas (Vancouver, Halifax, Toronto). Foreign-flagged yachts may cruise Canadian waters with a cruising permit. No special yacht residence pathway.

## Notes for RU citizens
Canada maintains sanctions against Russia. Visitor visa and study permit processing for RU nationals is slow but possible. Express Entry remains open — no nationality-based exclusion — but background checks are protracted. Quebec's QIIP requires French, which some RU applicants have. No ban on applications, but approval rates have declined. Significant Russian-speaking communities exist in Toronto and Vancouver.

## Country Card

### General description
Canada is a vast North American country bordering the United States, with coastlines on the Atlantic, Pacific, and Arctic Oceans. It has a highly developed, service-based economy with significant natural resource wealth (oil, gas, minerals, timber). Canada is one of the world's most active immigration destinations, operating a points-based Express Entry system that grants permanent residence directly to skilled workers — one of the fastest pathways to citizenship (3 years) among G7 nations. It has a strong social safety net, publicly funded healthcare, and large immigrant communities in Toronto, Vancouver, and Montreal.

### Well-known strong points
- One of the fastest citizenship timelines among G7 nations (3 years physical presence)
- Express Entry grants permanent residence directly — no temporary-to-PR bridge required
- Strong passport (181 visa-free destinations, Henley 2026 rank 8)
- High quality of life: universal healthcare, clean environment, safe cities
- Very high Freedom House score (97/100) and robust rule of law (WJP 0.80)
- Large, established immigrant communities with strong settlement support

### Well-known problems
- Very high housing costs in major cities (Toronto, Vancouver among world's least affordable)
- Harsh winters across most of the country (except coastal BC)
- High cost of living relative to median salary, especially for newcomers
- No digital nomad or passive income visa — immigration is PR-focused, not flexible
- High CRS score cutoffs (521–523 in 2026) make Express Entry competitive
- Startup Visa suspended; entrepreneur pathway uncertain
