## Overview
San Marino is a microstate entirely enclaved within Italy. It does not issue its own visas (entry is via Italy/Schengen) and grants residence through four channels: registered, **elective** (investment), **atypical for pensioners**, and **atypical with a facilitated tax regime**. Citizenship is attainable by naturalisation but requires long residence (15 years under Law 114/2000) and renunciation of prior nationality. ^[https://www.esteri.sm/pub1/EsteriSM/en/Servizi-per-utenza/Permessi-di-Soggiorno-e-Residenze.html]

## Per-Program Detail

### Elective residence
- **Official name**: Residenza elettiva
- **Conditions**: Foreign nationals making a property purchase of **€500,000** or a financial deposit of **€600,000**; mandatory health insurance (min €30,000 coverage); cannot work in the public sector or draw state benefits. ^[https://www.castellidisanmarino.com/en/san-marino-residency-complete-guide]
- **Duration / renewal**: Elective residence converts to registered residence after 10 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 15 years continuous residence (naturalisation).
- **Official source**: https://www.esteri.sm
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — high capital bar for a small jurisdiction; small quota and discretionary grant.

### Atypical residence
- **Official name**: Atypical residence subject to a facilitated tax regime (Law 223/2020)
- **Conditions**: Foreign citizens with income generated abroad; never previously San Marino tax-resident; health insurance; no public-sector work or state benefits. No minimum investment. ^[https://www.esteri.sm/pub1/EsteriSM/en/Servizi-per-utenza/Permessi-di-Soggiorno-e-Residenze.html]
- **Duration / renewal**: Residency, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 15 years residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.esteri.sm
- **Community verdict**: Works well — the low-bar route for foreign-income earners.

### Economic residence
- **Official name**: Economic residence (business owner)
- **Conditions**: Undertake business via a San Marino company, holding ≥51%; pay €350/month flat-rate contribution; health insurance. ^[https://www.camcom.sm/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Country-profileEN_2025.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: Definitive after 10 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 15 years residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.camcom.sm
- **Community verdict**: Works well for entrepreneurs; note the €350/mo contribution.

### Pensioner residence
- **Official name**: Atypical residence for pensioners
- **Conditions**: EU/Swiss/listed-country citizens with foreign pension; annual income >€50,000 or movable assets ≥€300,000; pension income taxed at 6%. ^[https://www.castellidisanmarino.com/en/san-marino-residency-complete-guide]
- **Duration / renewal**: Residency, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 15 years residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.esteri.sm
- **Community verdict**: Works well for qualifying retirees (6% pension tax is competitive).

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: **15 years** continuous residence for ordinary naturalisation (Law 114/2000); reduced for spouses of citizens. Renunciation of prior citizenship required. ^[https://www.castellidisanmarino.com/en/san-marino-residency-complete-guide]
- **Language**: Italian; knowledge of San Marino history and culture.
- **Dual citizenship**: NOT permitted.

## Tax
- **Personal income tax (IGR)**: progressive **9%–35%** (9% ≤€10,000 … 35% >€80,000). ^[https://www.camcom.sm/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Country-profileEN_2025.pdf]
- **Corporate tax**: 17% standard (18% 2026–2030 for ordinary activities); 8.5% for new companies (first 5 years); 0%/4%/8% ladder for innovative startups (SUT/SAT).
- **Atypical residence (facilitated)**: favourable regime for foreign income; pensioners pay 6% on pensions.
- **No VAT** (single-phase tax instead); capital gains on certain disposals 10%.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. IT entrepreneurs use the economic-residence route or the facilitated atypical residence (foreign income). The small domestic market limits startup scale, but the low corporate ladder (0–8%) for innovative firms is a genuine incentive.

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked — no coastline. Not applicable.

## Notes for RU citizens
No specific restriction, but entry is only via Italy/Schengen (no Sammarinese visas). Banking access for Russian nationals is constrained by EU sanctions and San Marino's MONEYVAL scrutiny (a 2023 framework overhaul addressed AML deficiencies). The atypical residence (foreign income) is the practical route for RU applicants.

## Community pulse
San Marino is a niche relocation option, praised for its facilitated tax regime and quiet, safe setting, but limited by its tiny size, Italian-language environment, and the long 15-year citizenship path with mandatory renunciation. Little active expat community beyond a modest Italian-adjacent presence.

## Country Card

### General description
San Marino is a 61 km² landlocked republic entirely surrounded by Italy, claiming to be the world's oldest republic. Its economy is built on tourism, banking, and manufacturing, with a competitive corporate-tax ladder for new and innovative firms. It offers a genuine facilitated tax regime for foreign-income earners, making it a niche but legitimate European low-tax residence, though its passport and citizenship remain hard to reach.

### Well-known strong points
- Facilitated tax regime for foreign income (atypical residence)
- 6% tax on foreign pensions for qualifying retirees
- Very high political freedom (FH 97/100) and safety
- Competitive corporate ladder (0–8%) for innovative startups
- Strong passport (167 visa-free, Henley 2026)

### Well-known problems
- 15-year citizenship path with mandatory renunciation of prior nationality
- No own visa system — entry only via Italy/Schengen
- Tiny size limits housing, services, and business scale
- Elective residence requires €500,000–600,000 investment
- Italian language required for citizenship

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-26

- **Watch items**: Any change to atypical/facilitated residence thresholds; citizenship reform.
