## Overview
Panama's immigration is managed by the Servicio Nacional de Migración (SNM). Panama offers two flagship residency programs: the **Friendly Nations Visa** (50+ qualifying countries, USD 200K real estate or CD or employment, 2-year provisional → permanent) and the **Qualified Investor Visa** (direct permanent residency from USD 300K real estate — rising to USD 500K on 15 October 2026). Panama also has a **digital nomad visa** (Short-Stay Visa, 9+9 months) and the **Pensionado Visa** (USD 1,000/mo lifetime pension). The Panamanian passport provides ~140 visa-free destinations. Panama uses the US dollar as its currency and operates a territorial tax system (foreign income 0%). Citizenship requires 5 years of permanent residency; dual citizenship is not recognized (renunciation may be required). ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/panama-citizenship-by-investment]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residente Permanente en Calidad de Inversionista Calificado (Qualified Investor Visa, Executive Decree 722 of 2020, amended by Decree 193 of 2024)
- **Conditions**: Three investment tracks: USD 300,000 real estate (rising to **USD 500,000 on 15 October 2026** — confirmed), USD 500,000 in Panamanian securities via licensed brokerage, or USD 750,000 fixed-term bank deposit. Investment must be held 5 years. ^[https://kraemerlaw.com/en/immigration/panama-qualified-investor-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: Direct permanent residency (no temporary stage). Processing time: 30–45 business days.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years of permanent residency. Doubled timeline for spouse of Panamanian or parent of Panamanian-born child: 3 years PR.
- **Official source**: https://www.migracion.gob.pa
- **Community verdict**: Works well (fastest PR in the region; QIV is the premium path; Friendly Nations is a cheaper but slower alternative).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Friendly Nations Visa — Certificate of Deposit route
- **Conditions**: Citizenship of one of ~50 qualifying countries (US, Canada, UK, EU, Australia, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Mexico, etc.). USD 200,000 fixed-term deposit in a Panamanian bank, unencumbered, minimum 3-year term. ^[https://startabroad.com/resource/panama-visa-residency-and-citizenship-options]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year provisional residency → permanent residency. Government fees: ~USD 1,250 per applicant.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years of permanent residency (total ~7 years: 2 yr provisional + 5 yr PR).
- **Official source**: https://www.migracion.gob.pa
- **Community verdict**: Works well (popular; ~1,997 approvals through August 2025. Since 2021, provisional not direct PR — the bar is meaningfully higher than pre-2021).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Visa de Corta Estancia como Trabajador Remoto (Short-Stay Visa for Remote Workers, Executive Decree 198, May 2021)
- **Conditions**: Contract with foreign transnational company OR self-employed with foreign clients. Annual income ≥ USD 36,000 from foreign sources. Health insurance with Panama coverage. No work for Panamanian clients. ^[https://kraemerlaw.com/en/immigration/panama-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 9 months, renewable once (18 months max). Does NOT count toward permanent residency or citizenship.
- **Path to citizenship**: No direct path. Can transition to Friendly Nations Visa or Qualified Investor Visa for permanent residency.
- **Official source**: https://www.migracion.gob.pa
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (USD 36K/yr threshold is high for Latin America; 18-month max; no PR path. Dollarized economy and territorial tax are pluses).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Friendly Nations Visa — Employment route
- **Conditions**: Formal job offer from Panamanian employer + USD 5,000 deposit in Panamanian bank. Must be a citizen of a qualifying country. Professional qualifications may be required. ^[https://startabroad.com/resource/panama-visa-residency-and-citizenship-options]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year provisional → permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 7 years total (2 yr provisional + 5 yr PR).
- **Official source**: https://www.migracion.gob.pa
- **Community verdict**: Works well (for qualified FN applicants with genuine job offers).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Friendly Nations Visa — Business/Professional route
- **Conditions**: Genuine professional or economic connection to Panama (business registration, professional practice). Must qualify under Friendly Nations (50+ countries citizenship) OR use Qualified Investor track for any nationality.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 yr provisional (FN) → permanent, or direct PR (QI).
- **Path to citizenship**: 7 years (FN) or 5 years (QI).
- **Official source**: https://www.migracion.gob.pa
- **Community verdict**: Works well (FN business route scrutinized more since 2021 tightening; superficial connections rejected).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Permiso de Trabajo / Friendly Nations Visa (employment)
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with Panamanian employer. FN route available for qualifying nationalities; standard work permit for others.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years → permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: Varied (5–7 years PR depending on route).
- **Official source**: https://www.migracion.gob.pa
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Estudiante
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in Panamanian institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student time may count if converted to residency.
- **Official source**: https://www.migracion.gob.pa
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Dependiente (under primary residency)
- **Conditions**: Spouse and dependent children up to age 18 (parents may be included on pensionado). Must be applied alongside primary applicant.
- **Duration / renewal**: Matches primary applicant.
- **Path to citizenship**: Same as primary.
- **Official source**: https://www.migracion.gob.pa
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Pensionado (Retiree/Pensioner Visa)
- **Conditions**: Lifetime pension ≥ USD 1,000/mo (+ USD 250 per additional dependent). Extensive benefits: discounts on utilities, transportation, entertainment, medical services. ^[https://theglobalwealth.com/residency/panama-friendly-nations-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent residency (indefinite).
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years of permanent residency.
- **Official source**: https://www.migracion.gob.pa
- **Community verdict**: Works well (one of the most famous retiree programs in the world; extensive discount benefits; dollarized economy).

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. No direct CBI. Qualified Investor Visa provides immediate permanent residency; citizenship available after 5 years of PR (naturalization, with Spanish + civics requirements).

## Citizenship
- **Naturalization**: After 5 years of permanent residency. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/panama-citizenship-by-investment]
- **Reduced periods**: 3 years of PR for spouses of Panamanians or parents of Panamanian-born children.
- **Language**: Spanish proficiency required.
- **Civics exam**: Required.
- **Processing time**: Variable (months to years).
- **Dual citizenship**: Panama does NOT recognize dual citizenship — naturalized citizens must renounce prior nationality on paper. However, enforcement varies and naturalized citizens often retain their original passport de facto. **This is a significant deterrent for many applicants.**
- **Physical presence**: At least one visit every 2 years to maintain permanent residency. Substantial presence strengthens naturalization case.
- **Passport**: ~140 visa-free destinations; 90/180 days Schengen.

## Tax
- **Tax system**: Territorial. Foreign-source income taxed at 0%. Panama-source income 0%–25%. ^[https://theglobalwealth.com/residency/panama-friendly-nations-visa]
- **Capital gains**: 10% on Panamanian-source gains.
- **Dividends**: 5%/10%/20% depending on source.
- **Wealth tax**: None.
- **Inheritance tax**: None.
- **Digital nomads**: Foreign income explicitly not taxed on the Short-Stay Visa (non-resident classification).
- **Dollarized economy**: No exchange-rate risk for USD earners.

## IT / Digital Nomad
- Short-Stay DN Visa: 9+9 months, USD 36K/yr threshold. No PR path. Panama City has excellent infrastructure (fiber internet, coworking spaces, Tocumen hub).
- Long-term remote workers typically use Friendly Nations (CD or real estate route) for permanent residency + territorial tax.
- Panama is a major business hub (Panama Canal, banking center, multinational regional HQs).

## Yacht / Maritime
Panama is one of the world's largest vessel registries (Panama Ship Registry). Yacht owners can register vessels under the Panamanian flag. No special residency program for yacht captains, but Friendly Nations / Qualified Investor provide straightforward residency for maritime professionals.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian citizens are NOT on the Friendly Nations qualifying list. RU nationals must use Qualified Investor (USD 300K → 500K real estate, USD 500K securities, or USD 750K deposit) or standard residency routes.
- RU nationals: tourist visa required (not visa-exempt).
- Banking: Panama is a major international banking center but compliance for RU nationals post-2022 is intensive. Several Panamanian banks have closed or restricted RU-linked accounts.
- Documents: apostilled (or consularly legalized) and translated into Spanish.
- Panama is a high-value destination for RU citizens seeking territorial tax + dollar stability, but entry barriers (no Friendly Nations access, banking friction) are higher than Paraguay.

## Community pulse
- **Overall**: Panama offers the clearest investment-residency framework in Central America. Qualified Investor = direct PR, 30–45 days. Friendly Nations = slower but cheaper. Pensionado = legendary retiree program.
- **Processing**: Qualified Investor ~30–45 business days. Friendly Nations provisional ~6 months, permanent ~6 months.
- **Rejection risk**: Low for Qualified Investor (clear thresholds). Friendly Nations since 2021 scrutinizes genuine economic connection.
- **Costs**: Qualified Investor: USD 5,000 SNM + USD 5,000 Treasury (+ USD 1,000/dependent 12+, USD 1,000/dependent under 12 to Treasury only). Friendly Nations: ~USD 1,250 government fees.
- **Red flag**: USD 300K real estate threshold RISES to USD 500K on 15 October 2026 — a confirmed deadline.
