## Overview
Djibouti is a small Horn of Africa nation (pop. ~1.1M) strategically located on the Bab el-Mandeb strait, hosting multiple foreign military bases. Immigration is governed by the Djiboutian Nationality Code. Naturalization: 10 years residence (5 years for exceptional service). Passport: 15 visa-free destinations. ^[https://data.worldbank.org/country/DJ]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Permis de travail / Carte de sejour
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from Djiboutian employer; employer sponsorship.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years residence (5 years if exceptional service to the nation). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djiboutian_nationality_law]
- **Official source**: Ministere de l'Interieur (no comprehensive online portal).
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data (expat presence mostly military/diplomatic/logistics).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Visa d'etude
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Djiboutian institution; proof of financial means.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: Residence time counts toward 10-year clock.
- **Official source**: Djiboutian embassies / consulates.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Regroupement familial
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; proof of relationship and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years (spouse of Djiboutian national: 10 years marriage, 5 with children). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djiboutian_nationality_law]
- **Official source**: Immigration authorities.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

## Citizenship
- **Residence**: 10 years general; 5 years for exceptional service. Spouse: 10 years marriage (5 years if couple has children). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djiboutian_nationality_law]
- **Language**: French and Arabic proficiency expected.
- **Dual citizenship**: Not permitted (renunciation required).
- **Processing**: Discretionary, by decree.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive income tax; corporate tax ~25%. Free-zone incentives exist.
- **Remote workers**: No special regime. Tax enforcement limited.
- **General**: Djiboutian franc (DJF) pegged to USD. Strategic logistics hub.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. Internet infrastructure is developing (Djibouti Telecom monopoly; submarine cable hub). Coworking is virtually nonexistent. Not a recognized nomad destination. [[ethiopia]] and [[kenya]] are regional alternatives.

## Yacht / Maritime
Strategic Red Sea / Gulf of Aden coastline. Major international port (Djibouti City). Yacht tourism is minimal (piracy concerns in the region). Port and logistics are the main maritime industries, not leisure boating.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Visa obtainable at Djiboutian embassies or on arrival. No special RU programs. Russia is not among the major foreign military presences (France, US, China, Japan). Passport is very weak (15 destinations).

## Community Pulse
Essentially no expat immigration community outside military/diplomatic/logistics personnel. Not discussed on nomad, retiree, or investor-immigration forums. High cost of living relative to the region.

## Country Card

### General description
Djibouti is a small, arid Horn of Africa nation strategically located on one of the world's busiest shipping lanes. Its economy relies on port/logistics services and foreign military base rentals. Immigration infrastructure is minimal; no formal residency-by-investment or nomad programs. Naturalization is long (10 years) and restrictive.

### Well-known strong points
- Strategic location: Red Sea/Gulf of Aden gateway
- Currency pegged to USD; stable exchange regime
- Multiple foreign military bases provide security umbrella
- Regional telecommunications hub (submarine cables)

### Well-known problems
- Authoritarian regime (FH 24/100, Not Free)
- Very high cost of living for the region
- Harsh climate (extreme heat, arid)
- No formal residency programs; dual citizenship not permitted

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-11
- **Recent changes**: No known recent changes. Stable authoritarian rule under President Guelleh (since 1999).
- **Watch items**: Regional security (Yemen conflict spillover, Red Sea instability). Political succession.
