## Overview
São Tomé and Príncipe is a small, Portuguese-speaking island nation in the Gulf of Guinea (CPLP member). It launched a **Citizenship by Investment programme on 1 August 2025** (Decree-Law No. 07/2025) — one of the cheapest legitimate CBI options globally at **US$90,000** donation — alongside a traditional naturalisation route requiring 5 years' residence and Portuguese. Dual nationality is permitted since 2003. ^[https://globalresidenceindex.com/sao-tome-citizenship-by-investment]

## Per-Program Detail

### citizenship_by_investment
- **Official name**: Programa de Cidadania por Investimento ou Doação (Decree-Law No. 07/2025) — Citizenship by Investment or Donation
- **Conditions**: Non-refundable donation to the National Transformation Fund (NTF): **US$90,000** single applicant; **US$95,000** family (2–4 people); **+US$5,000** per additional dependent. No physical presence, no interview, no language test. Restricted nationality: North Korea. ^[https://globalresidenceindex.com/sao-tome-citizenship-by-investment]
- **Duration / renewal**: Direct citizenship; passport valid 7 years (citizenship itself permanent). ^[https://globalresidenceindex.com/sao-tome-citizenship-by-investment]
- **Path to citizenship**: Immediate grant via special naturalisation (investment/donation grounds waive the 5-year residence and Portuguese-language requirements). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9an_nationality_law]
- **Official source**: https://cip.gov.st/
- **Community verdict**: New programme (2025) — thin track record; marketed as cheapest legitimate CBI.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work permit / residence permit
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer; employer sponsorship. Thin, unpublished thresholds; small job market.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years' residence + Portuguese + renunciation of prior nationality (traditional route).
- **Official source**: https://cip.gov.st/
- **Community verdict**: No reliable community data.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalisation**: Minimum 5 years' residence, understanding of Portuguese, self-sufficiency; applicants must renounce other nationality (traditional route). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9an_nationality_law]
- **CBI route**: Residence and language requirements waived.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted for birth nationals since 2003; CBI grants dual citizenship without restriction. ^[https://globalresidenceindex.com/sao-tome-citizenship-by-investment]

## Tax
- Personal income tax: progressive 0–25% (residents). No global income tax for non-residents; no inheritance tax or wealth tax. Corporate tax competitive, with incentives in special development zones. Double-taxation treaty with Portugal. ^[https://globalresidenceindex.com/sao-tome-citizenship-by-investment]

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. Remote workers without CBI enter as tourists. Internet and infrastructure are limited.

## Yacht / Maritime
Small Gulf of Guinea island nation with limited port infrastructure. Not a yachting destination.

## Notes for RU citizens
- CBI is open to most nationalities (only North Korea restricted); no physical presence required.
- Weak passport (~62 destinations); requires visas for EU/UK/USA. Useful mainly as a second-citizenship diversifier, not for travel power.
- Portuguese-language CPLP community offers some regional mobility upside.

## Community Pulse
Very limited data — the CBI programme only launched August 2025. Early marketing emphasises the $90,000 price point and CPLP access, but there is no meaningful applicant or approval track record yet.

## Country Card

### General description
São Tomé and Príncipe is a small, stable, Portuguese-speaking island democracy in the Gulf of Guinea and a CPLP member. Its economy is aid-dependent, built on cocoa, tourism and fishing. It is relevant to migrants for its 2025 citizenship-by-investment programme — one of the world's cheapest legitimate CBI options at $90,000 — and a traditional 5-year naturalisation route.

### Well-known strong points
- Cheapest legitimate CBI globally ($90,000 donation)
- Stable democracy (FH 84/100 Free); dual citizenship permitted
- No physical presence, language, or interview requirements for CBI
- CPLP membership (Portuguese-speaking market access)
- No inheritance or wealth tax; no global income tax for non-residents

### Well-known problems
- Weak passport (~62 destinations); EU/UK/US visas required
- Very small, aid-dependent economy
- CBI programme is brand new with no track record
- Traditional naturalisation requires renouncing prior nationality
- Limited infrastructure, internet and services
