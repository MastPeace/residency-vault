## Overview
Mozambique is a Lusophone country on the Indian Ocean coast of Southeast Africa, with a rapidly growing economy built on gas, coal and agriculture. Immigration is governed by the 1993 Law on Foreigners and its regulations. There is no digital-nomad visa; residence runs through work permits and an investment/DIRE route. Citizenship requires 5 years of residence. Dual nationality is not permitted. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozambican_nationality_law]

Related: [[south-africa]], [[zambia]], [[investment-residency]].

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency — Work Permit + DIRE
- **Official name**: Autorização de Trabalho (Work Permit) + DIRE (Documento de Identificação de Residente Estrangeiro)
- **Conditions**: Employment contract; employer sponsorship; employer must justify hiring a foreigner (quota system).
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable, tied to the contract.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence. ^[http://multiplecitizenship.com/wscl/ws_MOZAMBIQUE.html]
- **Official source**: https://www.mitra.gov.mz (Ministry of Labour)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — bureaucratic; Portuguese essential; Maputo has a small but established expat community.

### investment_residency — Investment Visa / DIRE
- **Official name**: Visto de Investimento (Investment Visa) + DIRE
- **Conditions**: Approved investment project registered with CPI (Investment Promotion Centre) / GAZEDA; facilitates visas, work and residence permits. ^[https://evisa.gov.mz/investment-visa.html]
- **Duration / renewal**: Renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.cpi.co.mz
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — facilitation exists; gas/mining projects dominate foreign investment.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 5 years residence for naturalisation; must understand Portuguese and customs. ^[http://multiplecitizenship.com/wscl/ws_MOZAMBIQUE.html]
- **Language**: Portuguese (official); comprehension required.
- **Dual citizenship**: Not permitted — Mozambican nationality is lost upon acquiring another. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozambican_nationality_law]
- **Passport**: ~56 destinations visa-free or on arrival.

## Tax
- Residence-based; tax resident if >183 days or habitual abode.
- Personal income tax (IRPS): progressive up to 32%.
- Corporate tax (IRPC): 32% standard. VAT: 17%.
- No special expat flat-tax regime.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Maputo has a growing tech scene and 4G/LTE, but remote workers generally need an employer or investor route. Portuguese is essential. Cost of living is moderate for the region.

## Yacht / Maritime
Extensive Indian Ocean coastline (~2,470 km) with Maputo, Beira, Nacala and Pemba ports. Growing marine-tourism and diving scene (Pemba, Bazaruto). Limited leisure-yacht infrastructure compared to South Africa.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa required (or eVisa for some purposes). Mozambique maintains neutral-to-friendly relations with Russia. Portuguese is the working language. Small expat community, concentrated in Maputo and gas-project areas (Cabo Delgado). Dual citizenship is not available to naturalised Russians.

## Community pulse
Limited English-language expat forum data. Expat presence is concentrated in gas, mining, infrastructure and NGOs. Maputo has a small but established international community. No significant digital-nomad base.

## Country Card

### General description
Mozambique is a long Lusophone country on the Indian Ocean coast of Southeast Africa, with a fast-growing economy driven by natural gas, coal and agriculture. Immigration is employment- and investment-led, with no digital-nomad route. Citizenship requires only 5 years of residence, but dual nationality is prohibited — a significant deterrent.

### Well-known strong points
- Short 5-year path to citizenship
- Long Indian Ocean coastline with growing beach/diving tourism
- Major gas discoveries (Rovuma Basin) drive expat employment
- Investment Visa / DIRE route facilitated by CPI/GAZEDA
- Portuguese-language continuity with Portugal and Brazil

### Well-known problems
- Dual citizenship is NOT permitted
- No digital-nomad or passive-income visa
- Portuguese language effectively mandatory
- Weak rule of law and corruption (Freedom House 41/100, Partly Free)
- Insurgency risk in Cabo Delgado (north)
- Low median incomes and limited infrastructure outside Maputo
