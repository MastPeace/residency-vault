## Overview
Thailand is Southeast Asia's most mature lifestyle-relocation market. It offers the Long-Term Resident (LTR) visa (10-year, four categories), the Destination Thailand Visa (DTV, 5-year digital-nomad/workcation), the paid Thailand Privilege/Elite visa, and classic Non-Immigrant O-A/O-X retirement visas. Naturalization is legally possible after 5 years of continuous residence following permanent residence, but in practice is extremely rare; Thailand does not recognize dual citizenship. ^[https://ltr.boi.go.th/]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: LTR Visa — Wealthy Global Citizen
- **Conditions**: ≥ US$1,000,000 in assets; ≥ US$80,000/yr income (past 2 years); ≥ US$500,000 investment in Thai bonds/real estate/FDI. ^[https://ltr.boi.go.th/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 10 years, renewable; 17% flat PIT available; no 4:1 Thai-hiring ratio.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years continuous residence after permanent residence (very difficult in practice).
- **Official source**: https://ltr.boi.go.th/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for high-net-worth applicants; BOI endorsement required.

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: LTR Visa — Wealthy Pensioner
- **Conditions**: Age 50+; ≥ US$80,000/yr passive income (or US$40,000–80,000 + ≥ US$250,000 investment). ^[https://ltr.boi.go.th/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 10 years; 17% flat PIT option.
- **Official source**: https://ltr.boi.go.th/
- **Community verdict**: Works well — the premium retirement route (richer than O-A).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Destination Thailand Visa (DTV), effective 15 Jul 2024
- **Conditions**: Remote worker/freelancer with foreign employer/clients, OR soft-power activities (Muay Thai, cooking, medical); bank balance ≥ THB 500,000. ^[https://image.mfa.go.th/mfa/0/91fPdh6NtO/VISA_Information/DTV_Revised_16_July_2024.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 years, multiple entry; 180 days per entry (extendable 180 days once, then re-enter).
- **Path to citizenship**: Does not count toward permanent residence/naturalization.
- **Official source**: https://thaievisa.go.th/
- **Community verdict**: Works well — hugely popular; main caveat is it doesn't lead to PR and taxation of foreign income since 2024.

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: LTR Highly-Skilled Professional / SMART Visa
- **Conditions**: ≥ US$80,000/yr (or US$40,000 + Master's in STEM) in target industries (EV, electronics, biotech, digital). ^[https://ltr.boi.go.th/]
- **Duration / renewal**: LTR 10 years; SMART 1–4 years.
- **Official source**: https://ltr.boi.go.th/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for qualifying STEM professionals.

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: SMART Visa (S) — startup
- **Conditions**: Endorsement by a BOI-designated agency; equity stake in a registered tech startup.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–4 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.boi.go.th/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — endorsement requirement narrows eligibility.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Non-Immigrant B + Work Permit
- **Conditions**: Job offer; employer obtains work permit (subject to 4:1 Thai-to-foreigner ratio unless exempt).
- **Duration / renewal**: 90-day visa, annual extension; permanent residence possible after 3 years of continuous work + income thresholds.
- **Official source**: https://thaievisa.go.th/
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard employer route.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Non-Immigrant ED
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Official source**: https://thaievisa.go.th/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Non-Immigrant O (Thai family)
- **Conditions**: Spouse/parent of Thai national or dependent of a resident.
- **Duration / renewal**: 90-day, annual extension.
- **Official source**: https://thaievisa.go.th/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Non-Immigrant O-A (1 yr) / O-X (5+5 yr)
- **Conditions**: Age 50+; THB 800,000 deposit OR THB 65,000/mo income; health insurance for O-A/O-X. ^[https://thaievisa.go.th/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year (O-A), renewable; 5+5 years (O-X, age 50+, additional funds).
- **Official source**: https://thaievisa.go.th/
- **Community verdict**: Works well — the classic retiree visa; many now prefer DTV or LTR.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalization**: Requires permanent residence first (typically 3 years of work + income/points), then 5 years of continuous residence, Thai language, and renunciation of prior nationality. ^[https://www.mfa.go.th/]
- **Dual citizenship**: Not recognized.
- **Practical reality**: Very few foreigners naturalize; most remain on long-term visas.

## Tax
- **Individual income tax**: Progressive 0%–35% (top rate on income > THB 5,000,000/yr). ^[https://www.rd.go.th/]
- **2024 remittance change**: From 2024, assessable foreign income remitted to Thailand in the same year it is earned is taxable for tax residents (180+ days) — a major change for expats/nomads.
- **LTR holders**: 17% flat PIT on Thai-source employment; exemption from the foreign-remittance rule.

## IT / Digital Nomad
The DTV (2024) made Thailand a top nomad destination: 5-year visa, THB 500k balance, remote work allowed. Chiang Mai, Bangkok, Phuket, and Koh Phangan are major hubs. Note the 2024 tax change on foreign remittances and that DTV does not lead to permanent residence.

## Yacht / Maritime
Major yachting hub: Phuket (Ao Po, Royal Phuket Marina), Pattaya, Koh Samui. Foreign-flagged yachts can enter under temporary import; Thai flag registration available. Well-developed charter and marina infrastructure.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Thai e-visa and DTV are accessible to Russian passport holders; large, long-established Russian community (Phuket, Pattaya). No visa-free entry (visa on arrival 15 days / e-visa). Banking for RU nationals is possible but increasingly compliance-heavy. The 2024 foreign-income tax change is the key watch item for RU remote workers.

## Community Pulse
Thailand is consistently a top global destination for retirees and nomads. The DTV (2024) is the most-discussed program — praised for ease, but with ongoing uncertainty about the 2024 tax change and non-conversion to PR. LTR is well-regarded but its income/asset bars are high. Naturalization is effectively out of reach for most.

## Country Card

### General description
Thailand is a Southeast Asian constitutional monarchy known for its tourism economy, tropical climate, and world-class expat infrastructure. It offers the broadest residence menu in the region — DTV (nomads), LTR (wealthy/skilled), Elite/Privilege (paid), and O-A/O-X (retirees) — plus very low living costs, making it the region's top lifestyle-relocation destination.

### Well-known strong points
- Broadest visa menu in SEA: DTV, LTR, Elite, retirement O-A/O-X
- Very low cost of living with excellent healthcare and food
- Huge, mature expat and nomad community
- Warm climate, beaches, and strong tourism infrastructure
- LTR offers 17% flat tax and foreign-remittance exemption

### Well-known problems
- Naturalization extremely rare; no dual citizenship
- 2024 foreign-income remittance tax change creates uncertainty for nomads
- Political volatility (Freedom House 34, Not Free)
- DTV does not convert to permanent residence
- Seasonal air-quality (Bangkok/Chiang Mai) and monsoon/heat
