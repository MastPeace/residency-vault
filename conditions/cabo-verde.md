## Overview
Cabo Verde (Cape Verde) is a ten-island Atlantic archipelago off West Africa, a stable parliamentary democracy and Portuguese-speaking CPLP member. It is the region's clearest residence pathway: a **Green Card** residence-by-investment scheme (real estate from €80,000), a **Remote Working Cabo Verde** digital-nomad permit, and naturalisation after 5 years of habitual residence with dual nationality permitted. No citizenship-by-investment programme exists. ^[https://uglobal.com/immigration/cape-verde]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Green Card (Residence by Investment) — Law on the regime for foreign investors
- **Conditions**: Real estate investment of **€80,000** in municipalities with below-average GDP per capita, or **€120,000** in above-average areas. Funds must be transferred from abroad via a licensed Cabo Verdean financial institution. No minimum physical-presence requirement. ^[https://uglobal.com/immigration/cape-verde]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent residence, renewable every 5 years while the property is held.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years of habitual residence, then naturalisation (Portuguese language, good character, self-sufficiency). Dual nationality permitted. ^[https://www.sebsauerborn.com/countries/cape-verde]
- **Official source**: https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/en/vistos
- **Community verdict**: Works well (low threshold vs EU Golden Visas; low uptake, straightforward).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Remote Working Cabo Verde (Programa Nómadas Digitais)
- **Conditions**: Average bank balance of ~€1,500/month over the last 6 months; health insurance; clean criminal record; proof of accommodation. ^[https://cabo-verde.cv/cape-verde-digital-nomad-visa-your-guide-to-working-remotely]
- **Duration / renewal**: 6-month temporary residence + work permit, renewable for another 6 months.
- **Path to citizenship**: Time does not reliably count toward naturalisation; nomads typically convert to Green Card.
- **Official source**: https://cabo-verde.cv/work-remote
- **Community verdict**: Works well (cheap, easy, low threshold; but 6-month ceiling and limited island infrastructure).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Visto de residência — exercício de atividade profissional
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer; transport permit; vaccination certificate; proof of accommodation; clean record. ^[https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/en/vistos]
- **Duration / renewal**: 6-month residence visa, extendable until the residence permit is decided; permit renewed annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years habitual residence.
- **Official source**: https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/en/vistos
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — small formal job market; most expats use Green Card or nomad route.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalisation**: 5 years of continuous/habitual legal residence, Portuguese language, good character, financial self-sufficiency. ^[https://www.sebsauerborn.com/countries/cape-verde]
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted; no renunciation required.
- **No citizenship-by-investment programme.**

## Tax
- **Corporate tax**: 20% (reduced from 21% for 2025). ^[https://www.state.gov/reports/2025-investment-climate-statements/cabo-verde]
- **VAT**: 15%.
- **Personal income**: Progressive. Green Card holders are exempt on foreign-source income; no capital gains tax on real estate held >2 years; no inheritance or wealth tax. A 10% flat regime exists for qualifying foreign investors. ^[https://www.sebsauerborn.com/countries/cape-verde]

## IT / Digital Nomad
Cabo Verde was an early adopter of a nomad visa (Remote Working Cabo Verde, late 2020). Threshold ~€1,500/mo average balance. UTC-1 timezone suits European teams. Praia and Mindelo are the main hubs, with a planned "Cabo Verde Tech Park". Internet is adequate but not top-tier.

## Yacht / Maritime
Atlantic island nation with marinas (Mindelo on São Vicente is a key stopover for transatlantic yachts). Cabo Verde operates a ship registry. Growing but modest yachting sector.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Visa required; Portuguese is the working language. Stable, safe, and visa-friendly relative to the region.
- Green Card (€80K–€120K real estate) is the main RU-relevant residence route; nomad visa also accessible.
- EU banking/CBI screening not a factor (no CBI). No visa-free Russia with Cabo Verdean passport.

## Community Pulse
Low but positive. The Green Card is cheap but little-known; the nomad visa is well-reviewed for simplicity but capped at 12 months total. Practitioners recommend it as a 5-year path to a second passport with dual nationality allowed, rather than a place to settle permanently.

## Country Card

### General description
Cabo Verde is a stable, democratic Portuguese-speaking archipelago off West Africa, a CPLP member with close ties to the EU. Its economy is service-based (tourism, remittances, transport). It offers a low-cost, low-friction residence pathway — a real-estate Green Card from €80,000 and a digital-nomad permit — with citizenship after 5 years and dual nationality allowed.

### Well-known strong points
- One of Africa's most stable democracies (FH 92/100 Free)
- Green Card from €80,000 with no stay requirement; dual citizenship allowed
- Citizenship after 5 years — shortest in the region with clear rules
- 0% tax on foreign-source income for Green Card holders
- Early, easy digital-nomad visa (~€1,500/mo balance)
- Very sunny, mild climate (~3,000 h sunshine)

### Well-known problems
- Small economy; few formal jobs and limited infrastructure
- Nomad visa capped at 12 months total
- No citizenship-by-investment; naturalisation is discretionary
- Island logistics (inter-island transport, cost of imports)
- Limited banking and professional services depth
