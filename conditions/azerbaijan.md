## Overview

Azerbaijan offers several pathways to residence, including **investment-based residence** through property purchase (≥ AZN 100,000), bank deposits (≥ AZN 100,000), or securities. However, **no citizenship-by-investment program exists** — naturalization requires 5 years of permanent residence plus language testing. The Azerbaijani passport is relatively weak (~70 visa-free destinations). Dual citizenship is not recognized. Azerbaijan is not in the EU/EEA. ^[https://www.caspianlegalcenter.az/insights/more/residency-investment-azerbaijan]

## Per-Program Detail

### Investment Residency (Property / Deposit / Securities)

- **Official name**: Müvəqqəti yaşama icazəsi (investisiya əsasında) — Temporary residence permit (investment basis)
- **Conditions** (choose one): ^[https://www.caspianlegalcenter.az/insights/more/residency-investment-azerbaijan] ^[https://oktamam.com/en/residence-permit-in-azerbaijan]
  - **Real estate**: Purchase property valued at ≥ **AZN 100,000** (~$59,000 / ~€55,000)
  - **Bank deposit**: Place ≥ **AZN 100,000** in an Azerbaijani bank
  - **Securities**: Purchase securities with nominal value meeting threshold
  - **Business investment**: Invest in the domestic economy
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable while investment is maintained.
- **Path to permanent residence**: After 2 years on temporary residence → eligible for permanent residence if:
  - Property value ≥ **AZN 200,000** (~$118,000)
  - Bank deposit ≥ **AZN 200,000**
  - Securities ≥ AZN 200,000 ^[https://www.caspianlegalcenter.az/insights/more/residency-investment-azerbaijan]
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years of permanent residence + language test. ^[https://www.caspianlegalcenter.az/insights/more/residency-investment-azerbaijan]
- **Official source**: [State Migration Service — Residence Permits](https://www.migration.gov.az/index.php/en/page_detail/591)
- **Community verdict**: Works well — investment thresholds are moderate in USD terms. Property market is accessible. The 2+5 year timeline to citizenship is reasonable. Main drawback: weak passport, no dual citizenship.

### Employment Residency / Highly Skilled Employment

- **Official name**: Müvəqqəti yaşama icazəsi (əmək əsasında)
- **Conditions**: Valid employment contract with an Azerbaijani employer. Work permit required. Employer must sponsor. ^[https://www.migration.gov.az/index.php/en/page_detail/591]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable with continued employment.
- **Path to permanent residence**: 2 years → PR eligibility (with sufficient means).
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years PR + language test.
- **Official source**: [State Migration Service](https://www.migration.gov.az/index.php/en/page_detail/591)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — work permit process is bureaucratic. Quota system for foreign workers. Oil/gas sector is the primary employer of foreign professionals.

### Startup / Entrepreneur

- **Official name**: Müvəqqəti yaşama icazəsi (sahibkarlıq fəaliyyəti əsasında)
- **Conditions**: Register a business in Azerbaijan. For permanent residence path: authorized capital ≥ AZN 200,000 or annual turnover ≥ AZN 200,000 for legal entities. ^[https://www.caspianlegalcenter.az/insights/more/residency-investment-azerbaijan]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 2 years TR → 5 years PR → citizenship (with investment thresholds met).
- **Official source**: [State Migration Service](https://www.migration.gov.az/index.php/en/page_detail/591)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — market is small and dominated by state-connected enterprises. Non-oil business environment is challenging.

### Student Residency

- **Conditions**: Enrollment in an accredited Azerbaijani educational institution.
- **Duration**: Duration of studies.
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### Family Reunification

- **Conditions**: Spouse or minor child of an Azerbaijani resident or citizen.
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

## Citizenship

- **By naturalization**: 5 years of permanent residence in Azerbaijan. ^[https://www.caspianlegalcenter.az/insights/more/residency-investment-azerbaijan]
- **Language**: Azerbaijani language proficiency test required.
- **History/law exam**: Knowledge of the Constitution and laws.
- **Dual citizenship**: **NOT recognized**. Renunciation of existing citizenship is mandatory (Azerbaijan strictly enforces this). ^[https://www.caspianlegalcenter.az/insights/more/residency-investment-azerbaijan]
- **By descent**: Available if at least one parent is an Azerbaijani citizen.
- **CBI**: Not available.
- **Total timeline via investment**: 2 years TR + 5 years PR = ~7 years minimum.

## Tax

- **Personal income tax**: Progressive, **14%** (standard). Higher rate of **25%** for income above AZN 30,000/month from non-oil/gas activities.
- **Corporate tax**: 20%.
- **Social security**: ~25% total contributions.
- **No wealth tax**.
- **Double taxation treaties**: ~50 countries.
- **Tax residency**: 182 days physical presence.

## IT / Digital Nomad

Azerbaijan does **not** have a [[digital-nomad]] visa. No formal remote work pathway exists. Internet infrastructure is adequate in Baku. Not a significant nomad destination. Censorship and internet restrictions (especially during political events) are noted concerns.

## Yacht / Maritime

Azerbaijan has a Caspian Sea coastline (~713 km). The Caspian is landlocked — no access to world oceans. Baku has a Caspian waterfront (Baku Boulevard) with some marina facilities. Not a significant yachting destination. Caspian Sea shipping is primarily commercial (oil/gas).

## Notes for RU citizens

Russian citizens enjoy visa-free entry to Azerbaijan (up to 90 days). Russian is widely spoken in Baku. Banking is accessible for RU citizens (some Azerbaijani banks serve Russian clients). However, Azerbaijan is not a primary relocation destination due to: authoritarian governance, limited civil liberties, weak passport, no dual citizenship, and a state-dominated economy. Property investment at AZN 100,000 (~$59,000) is affordable but the renunciation-of-citizenship requirement for naturalization is a deal-breaker for most.

## Community Pulse

Azerbaijan is not a popular expat destination outside the oil/gas sector. Those who relocate are typically energy industry professionals. Positive aspects: Baku is a modern city, low cost of living, Russian is widely spoken. Negative aspects: authoritarian political environment, limited civil liberties, internet censorship, weak passport, mandatory citizenship renunciation, closed economy. Residence process is functional but bureaucratic.
