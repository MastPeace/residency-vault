## Overview
Namibia launched its Digital Nomad Visa in 2023, offering a 6-month stay for remote workers earning USD 2,000+/month. The Ministry of Home Affairs, Immigration, Safety and Security (MHAISS) administers permits, with the Namibia Investment Promotion and Development Board (NIPDB) promoting the DN program. Namibia is politically stable, English-speaking, sparsely populated, and safe. The DN visa is non-renewable — reapplication requires a 12-month gap. Citizenship is a long, restrictive path. ^[https://www.nipdb.com/digital-nomad-visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### digital_nomad_visa — Digital Nomad Visa
- **Official name**: Namibia Digital Nomad Visa
- **Conditions**: Minimum monthly income USD 2,000 (main applicant), USD 1,000 (spouse), USD 500 (child). Proof of remote employment/freelance outside Namibia. Health/travel insurance. Medical certificate + radiological report. Police clearance (translated to English). Completed Form 3-1/0033. Fee: NAD 3,300 (approx. USD 200). ^[https://www.nipdb.com/digital-nomad-visa]
- **Duration**: 6 months. NON-RENEWABLE. Applicant must wait 12 months after expiry before reapplying. ^[https://www.nipdb.com/digital-nomad-visa]
- **Path to citizenship**: None — the DN visa is strictly for short-term stay. Does not count toward permanent residence or citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://www.nipdb.com/digital-nomad-visa
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — easy application (email to nomadvisa@nipdb.com), but the 6-month non-renewable limit is a significant restriction. Good for a temporary stint, not for long-term base-building. Source: ENS Africa, IMI Daily.

### employment_residency — Long-Term Work Permit
- **Official name**: Long-Term Work Permit
- **Conditions**: Employer sponsorship, proof no qualified Namibian available, qualifications. Application to MHAISS. ^[https://www.ibn.co.za/namibia]
- **Duration**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Permanent residence possible after 5+ years of continuous residence. Citizenship requires additional years.
- **Official source**: https://mhaiss.gov.na (Ministry of Home Affairs)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — bureaucratic processing; employer sponsorship is critical.

## Citizenship
- Naturalisation: 5 years of ordinary residence (10 years for non-Commonwealth). ^[requires official source confirmation]
- Language: English (official).
- Dual citizenship: Generally NOT permitted. Namibia restricts dual nationality.
- Passport: visa-free to ~75 destinations.
- **Honest assessment**: Citizenship is a long, uncertain path. Namibia is not a practical citizenship-by-naturalisation destination for most expats.

## Tax
- Residence-based taxation. Tax resident if ordinarily resident or >183 days physical presence.
- Personal income tax: progressive 0%–37% (top rate on NAD 1.5M+/yr, ~USD 80,000).
- Corporate tax: 32%.
- VAT: 15%.
- No capital gains tax (except on sale of immovable property by non-residents).
- Digital nomads: 6-month stay under the non-renewable DN visa is unlikely to trigger tax residence, but seek advice.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Windhoek and Swakopmund are the main hubs. Good mobile internet (4G/LTE), expanding fibre. Co-working spaces are limited but growing. Namibia's key draw is lifestyle: dramatic landscapes (Namib Desert, Skeleton Coast, Etosha), low population density, safety, outdoor recreation. Cost of living: moderate. The 6-month DN visa makes this a seasonal destination, not a permanent base.

## Yacht / Maritime
Namibia has an Atlantic coastline (Walvis Bay, Lüderitz). Limited yacht facilities. Walvis Bay is a commercial port. The Namibian Maritime and Fisheries Institute handles maritime affairs. Not a major yachting destination.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa-free entry for 30 days for Russian citizens. DN visa accessible. Namibia maintains neutral relations with Russia. Small expat community. Banking: available but limited compared to South Africa or Mauritius.
