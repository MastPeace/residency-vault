## Overview
Paraguay's immigration is managed by the Dirección Nacional de Migraciones (DNM) and, for investors, by SUACE (Sistema Unificado de Apertura y Cierre de Empresas) under the Ministry of Industry and Commerce. Paraguay offers one of the fastest investment residencies in South America: the **Investor Pass** (Resolution 0283/2026) grants direct permanent residency from USD 70,000, bypassing the 2-year temporary stage. Citizenship requires 3 years of permanent residency. The Paraguayan passport provides ~145 visa-free destinations including all Schengen. Territorial tax system: foreign income at 0%. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/paraguay-residency-visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Investor Pass / Constancia de Inversionista (Resolución 0283/2026, via SUACE)
- **Conditions**: Four tracks: USD 70,000 (productive business), USD 150,000 (tourism), USD 200,000 (real estate or financial instruments). Certificate (CIE) issued in ~5 business days from complete file. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/paraguay-residency-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: Direct permanent residency — no temporary stage. Cédula de Identidad issued; valid 10 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years of permanent residency (Constitution Art. 148).
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gov.py
- **Community verdict**: Works well (fastest meaningful residency in South America; residency applications surged from ~28,000 in 2024 to ~47,000 in 2025).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporal — Rentista (Law 6984/2022)
- **Conditions**: No published minimum investment. Economic solvency proven via Paraguayan bank deposit (savings/current account in applicant's name) or real estate deed. ~PYG 50M (~USD 9,150) historically; retirement income ~PYG 7,015,600/mo (~USD 1,300). ^[https://residencies.io/residency/paraguay/permanent-residency/py1]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years temporary → permanent residency. Presence: 1 visit every 12 months (temporary).
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years of permanent residency.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gov.py
- **Community verdict**: Works well (no minimum stay between visits; easiest "paper residency" in the region).

### digital_nomad_visa
Not available. No dedicated digital nomad visa. Remote workers commonly obtain standard temporary residency (no income verification beyond solvency) and work remotely.

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporal — Trabajo
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with Paraguayan employer.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years → permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years PR.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gov.py
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (local job market small; residency not tied to employment for most applicants).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Investor Pass (business route via SUACE)
- **Conditions**: Business investment from USD 70,000 (productive track). Company incorporation via SUACE.
- **Duration / renewal**: Direct permanent residency.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years PR.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gov.py
- **Community verdict**: Works well (SUACE workflow streamlined; legal fees USD 3,000–6,000).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporal — Trabajo (Law 6984/2022)
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with Paraguayan employer.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years → permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years PR.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gov.py
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporal — Estudiante
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in Paraguayan institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student time counts toward PR clock (case-by-case).
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gov.py
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Residencia — Reunificación Familiar
- **Conditions**: Family tie to Paraguayan citizen or resident. No nationality restrictions.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years → permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years PR.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gov.py
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporal — Rentista (retirement)
- **Conditions**: Monthly income ~PYG 7,015,600 (~USD 1,300). Import tariff exemptions on personal effects up to certain quantities. ^[https://residencies.io/residency/paraguay/permanent-residency/py1]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years → permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years PR.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gov.py
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. No direct CBI. The Investor Pass provides permanent residency, after which citizenship is available after 3 years (language + civics required).

## Citizenship
- **Naturalization (Constitution Art. 148)**: After 3 years of permanent residency. Temporary residency does NOT count. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/paraguay-residency-citizenship]
- **Language**: Spanish or Guaraní at A1–A2 level.
- **Civics exam**: 30-question civics/history exam, 70% pass mark.
- **Arraigo**: Demonstrated genuine ties (presence, integration) required.
- **Standard timeline**: 5–6 years total (2 yr temp + 3 yr PR + 1–2 yr processing). Investor track: 3.5–4 years total (direct PR + 3 yr + processing).
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted (Constitution Art. 149, reciprocity basis). In practice, renunciation of prior nationality not enforced.
- **Passport**: ~145 visa-free destinations including all Schengen.

## Tax
- **Tax system**: Territorial. Foreign-source income taxed at 0% permanently. Paraguay-source income up to 10%. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/paraguay-residency-visa]
- **Residency**: No minimum stay between visits to maintain residency. Visit once every 3 years (permanent) or 12 months (temporary).
- **Dividends/capital gains**: Foreign-source not taxed. Local-source dividends ~8% (recently reduced from 15%).
- **Wealth tax**: None.

## IT / Digital Nomad
- No dedicated digital nomad visa. Remote workers use standard temporary residency (no income verification beyond basic solvency). Territorial tax = 0% foreign income makes Paraguay attractive for location-independent workers.
- Internet: improving but uneven outside Asunción. Coworking scene emerging in Asunción.

## Yacht / Maritime
Paraguay is landlocked but is a major flag state for commercial river vessels (Paraná/Paraguay rivers). Not a yachting hub. No special yacht/captain residency program.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian citizens: visa-free for tourism up to 90 days. Residency applications are nationality-blind (no restricted nationalities officially).
- Documents: apostilled background check, birth certificate, civil status, all translated into Spanish by registered public translator.
- Banking: cédula + RUC (tax ID) needed; DNIT registration 1–2 weeks after cédula.
- Paraguay is extremely popular among RU citizens for "paper residency" + 0% foreign-income tax. Demand surged in 2024–2025.
- Physical presence: only 1 visit every 3 years to maintain permanent residency.

## Community pulse
- **Overall**: Paraguay is the fastest/cheapest residency in South America for investors. No minimum stay, territorial tax, and a 3-year PR citizenship clock.
- **Processing**: Investor CIE in ~5 business days; residency carnet 1–3 months (investor) or 6–9 months (standard).
- **Rejection risk**: Low for investors with complete files. Standard route is bureaucratic but reliable.
- **Government fees**: ~USD 100. Legal fees: USD 3,000–6,000.
- **Warning**: Naturalization still requires genuine presence/arraigo — the "1 visit every 3 years" residency-maintenance rule does NOT satisfy the citizenship clock.

## Status

## Country Card

### General description
Paraguay is a landlocked South American country with a unique profile: it offers one of the fastest investment residencies in the region (Investor Pass from $70,000 → direct permanent residency), a 0% territorial tax on foreign income, and the lowest physical-presence requirement (1 visit every 3 years maintains PR). Citizenship requires 3 years of permanent residency, but genuine presence (arraigo) is required. The Investor Pass (Resolution 0283/2026) has driven a surge in applications (~47,000 in 2025). Paraguay is trilingual (Spanish, Guaraní, and increasingly English in business) and a MERCOSUR member.

### Well-known strong points
- Territorial tax: 0% on foreign-source income — one of the best tax regimes for location-independent earners
- Fastest investment residency in South America: Investor Pass from $70,000 → direct PR in ~5 business days
- Minimal physical presence: 1 visit every 3 years to maintain permanent residency
- Low cost of living (Numbeo index 26.0) and low government fees (~$100 for residency)
- MERCOSUR mobility for permanent residents and citizens
- No nationality restrictions — open to Russian, Chinese, and all other passport holders

### Well-known problems
- Citizenship requires genuine presence (arraigo) — "1 visit every 3 years" residency-maintenance rule does NOT satisfy the citizenship clock
- Weak rule of law (WJP 0.47, rank 100/142) and corruption concerns
- No dedicated digital nomad visa — remote workers use standard residency
- Landlocked — no coastline, limited international flight connections
- Small economy with limited professional opportunities for expats
- Infrastructure outside Asunción is basic; internet quality variable in rural areas
