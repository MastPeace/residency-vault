## Overview
Guatemala has one of Central America's most attractive retiree residency programmes: the Rentista/Pensionado route grants PERMANENT residence from day one — not an annually-renewed temporary permit. Under the 2025 residency regulation (Acuerdo IGM-016-2025), the income threshold is US$1,250/month + US$300 per dependent. Guatemala operates a territorial tax system: foreign-source income is generally not taxed. Citizenship requires 10+ years of permanent residence. ^[https://livinginguatemala.com/tramites/igm-residencia-rentista]

## Per-Program Detail

### passive_income_residency / retired_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia de Rentista / Residencia de Pensionado
- **Conditions**: Under Acuerdo IGM-016-2025 (October 2025): US$1,250/month minimum verified passive or pension income for principal applicant; +US$300/month per dependent. ^[https://livinginguatemala.com/tramites/igm-residencia-rentista]
  - Income sources: pensions, Social Security, investment dividends, rental income from foreign properties, retirement fund distributions, annuities.
  - No guarantor required (one of few Latin American programmes without a local sponsor).
  - Income re-verified every 5 years.
- **Duration / renewal**: PERMANENT residency from day one — not temporary, not annually renewed. One-time application fee: US$400 (plus US$25 form). ^[https://livinginguatemala.com/tramites/igm-residencia-rentista]
- **Path to citizenship**: 10+ years of permanent residence under Guatemalan law. Spanish proficiency assessed informally during application.
- **Official source**: https://igm.gob.gt (Instituto Guatemalteco de Migración)
- **Community verdict**: Works well (outstanding value: permanent residency directly, no annual renewals, $1,250/mo threshold, territorial tax, no guarantor; processing 2–4 months).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Temporary Residence (remote worker / digital nomad category)
- **Conditions**: New categories effective October 8, 2025 under IGM-016-2025: remote workers, digital nomads, companions, and foreign entrepreneurs eligible for temporary residence. ^[https://consortiumlegal.com/en/2025/08/22/working-from-guatemala-the-new-immigration-regime-that-will-attract-global-talent]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1-year temporary, renewable up to 5 years maximum temporary stay.
- **Path to citizenship**: Temporary residence years do not count directly — must convert to permanent residence then 10 years.
- **Official source**: https://igm.gob.gt
- **Community verdict**: Too new to assess (effective October 2025; early uptake data lacking).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Temporary Residence (work)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Guatemalan employer. Employer sponsorship required. Standard residency docs (apostilled birth certificate, police clearance, medical exam).
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable to max 5 years temporary.
- **Path to citizenship**: Must convert to permanent residence → 10+ years → naturalization.
- **Official source**: https://igm.gob.gt
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (small formal labour market; employer-driven; not a major expat route compared to rentista/pensionado).

## Citizenship
- **By naturalization**: 10+ years of continuous permanent residence. Spanish proficiency required. Must demonstrate integration into Guatemalan society. ^[https://livinginguatemala.com/tramites/igm-residencia-rentista]
- **By marriage**: Reduced timeline for spouses of Guatemalan citizens (varies by case).
- **Dual citizenship**: Recognized (with some restrictions — Guatemalan nationality is primary for legal purposes within Guatemala).

## Tax
- Territorial tax system: only Guatemala-source income taxed; foreign-source income (including foreign pensions, investment income, rental income) generally exempt.
- Personal income tax: 5–7% on Guatemala-source income above Q 300,000 (~US$39,000).
- VAT (IVA): 12%.
- No capital gains tax for non-business assets (residence sale exempt). No inheritance tax. No wealth tax.

## IT / Digital Nomad
New remote-work residency effective October 2025 (IGM-016-2025). Temporary residence 1–3 years for remote workers with foreign employers/clients. Benefit: legal status + territorial tax = no local tax on foreign income. Still early stage — no track record yet.

## Yacht / Maritime
Not applicable — Guatemala has Caribbean and Pacific coasts but no significant yacht registration framework. Marinas limited.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian nationals require a visa to enter Guatemala (no visa-free). Apply at Guatemalan embassy in Moscow.
- Rentista/Pensionado route accessible: must document US$1,250/mo foreign income. Translation to Spanish required.
- Territorial tax system benefits those with foreign-source income. Spanish proficiency needed for eventual citizenship.
- Smaller RU community compared to DR/Mexico — mostly concentrated in Antigua Guatemala and Guatemala City.

## Community pulse
- Rentista/Pensionado is the standout programme for Guatemala — permanent residency from day one, low threshold, territorial tax, no annual renewals. Widely praised by expat communities.
- New digital nomad regulations (October 2025) modernize the system but lack implementation track record.
- Overall: English not widely spoken outside expat zones/Guatemala City — Spanish competence is a practical necessity.
