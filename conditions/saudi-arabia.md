## Overview
Saudi Arabia launched its [[premium-residency]] programme in 2019 as part of Vision 2030 — the first pathway to long-term residence without an employer sponsor (kafeel). The programme now offers multiple tracks: Permanent (SAR 800,000 one-time), Annual (SAR 100,000/year), Investor (SAR 7M), Real Estate (SAR 4M), Entrepreneur, Special Talent, and Gifted. Saudi Arabia does NOT grant citizenship to expatriates. Personal income tax is 0%, though expatriates pay a dependent levy under the standard Iqama system. ^[https://commoner-law.com/saudi/immigration-pathways/permanent-residency]

## Per-Program Detail

### Premium Residency — General Tiers
Two core tiers open to any qualified applicant regardless of profession:
- **Limited Duration (Annual)**: SAR 100,000/year (~$26,700). 1–5 years, renewable. Fee reduces 2%/year on continuous renewal. ^[https://commoner-law.com/saudi/immigration-pathways/permanent-residency]
- **Unlimited Duration (Permanent)**: SAR 800,000 one-time (~$213,000). Lifetime, no renewal. Permanent tier was reduced from SAR 1M to SAR 800,000 in January 2026. ^[https://themiddleeastinsider.com/2026/05/04/saudi-premium-residency-2026-updated-may]
- **Universal eligibility**: Age 21+, valid passport, clean criminal record, medical exam (free of communicable diseases), proof of financial solvency, valid health insurance. ^[https://pr.gov.sa/en]
- **Key benefits**: No employer sponsor (kafeel), property ownership (including Mecca/Medina for investment), business operation without Saudi partner, independent family sponsorship, unrestricted travel (no exit/re-entry permits), exempt from Nitaqat (Saudization) quota impact, exempt from expatriate dependent levy (SAR 400/dependent/month). ^[https://commoner-law.com/saudi/immigration-rights/premium-residency]
- **Work permit**: Since June 2026, Premium Residency holders must obtain a dedicated work permit via Qiwa platform (SAR 100 fee). ^[https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/662069/saudi-arabia/qiwa-premium-residency-holders-must-obtain-sr100-work-permit]
- **Official source**: https://pr.gov.sa/en
- **Community verdict**: Works well — fully operational, online application, 90-day processing target.

### Premium Residency — Investor Track
- **Official name**: الإقامة المميزة — مسار المستثمر (Premium Residency — Investor Track)
- **Conditions**: SAR 7,000,000+ (~$1.87M) investment in activities inside KSA as determined by the Premium Residency Center. SAR 4,000 non-refundable application fee. ^[https://baticfirm.com/eligibility-criteria-premium-residency-saudi-arabia]
- **Duration / renewal**: Direct permanent residency upon investment.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://pr.gov.sa/en
- **Community verdict**: High threshold but functional for qualifying investors.

### Premium Residency — Real Estate Track
- **Official name**: Premium Residency — Real Estate Owner
- **Conditions**: SAR 4,000,000+ (~$1.07M) completed, mortgage-free residential property in designated zones (including giga-projects). After 7 years of clean ownership, holder qualifies for free conversion to Permanent tier (no SAR 800,000 fee). ^[https://themiddleeastinsider.com/2026/05/04/saudi-premium-residency-2026-updated-may]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 years, renewable. Valid as long as property held.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://pr.gov.sa/en
- **Community verdict**: Newly expanded (2026) — thresholds still settling.

### Premium Residency — Special Talent / Gifted
- **Official name**: Premium Residency — Special Talent / Gifted
- **Conditions**: Special Talent: minimum wage threshold + employment contract in KSA. Gifted: unique gift or specialized award recipient. Residence requirement for permanent conversion: 30 months in KSA during 5-year permit. ^[https://ncar.gov.sa/api/index.php/resource/...]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 years (renewable), convertible to permanent upon meeting conditions. SAR 4,000 application fee.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://pr.gov.sa/en
- **Community verdict**: Operational, niche.

### Premium Residency — Entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Premium Residency — Entrepreneur
- **Conditions**: Category 1: SAR 400,000+ (~$107K) from approved investment entities/funding rounds. Category 2: SAR 15,000,000+ (~$4M) + creation of 10 direct jobs in year 1, additional 10 in year 2. SAR 4,000 application fee. ^[https://baticfirm.com/eligibility-criteria-premium-residency-saudi-arabia]
- **Duration / renewal**: Category 1: 5 years renewable. Category 2: direct permanent residency.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://pr.gov.sa/en
- **Community verdict**: Category 1 accessible for startup founders; Category 2 targets major projects.

### Employment Residency
- **Official name**: Standard Iqama (إقامة)
- **Conditions**: Employer sponsor, valid labour contract, medical exam, criminal record check. Minimum wage varies. Employer-dependent.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable. Tied to employer (kafeel system).
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.absher.sa
- **Community verdict**: Standard GCC employment model, employer-dependent.

### Student Residency
- **Official name**: Student Iqama
- **Conditions**: Enrolment at accredited Saudi institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Duration of study programme.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.moe.gov.sa
- **Community verdict**: Functional, institution-dependent.

### Family Reunification
- **Official name**: Family Visit / Dependent Iqama
- **Conditions**: Sponsor (Iqama or Premium Residency holder) sponsors spouse, children, parents. Standard Iqama holders pay dependent levy (SAR 400/dependent/month). Premium Residency holders exempt.
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to sponsor's permit.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.absher.sa
- **Community verdict**: Standard, levy a cost burden for standard Iqama holders.

## Citizenship
**Not available for expatriates.** Saudi citizenship is almost exclusively by descent (Saudi father). Naturalization is theoretically possible via a rarely-used Prime Minister's decree under Article 9 of the Citizenship Law, but in practice it is not a viable pathway. Premium Residency provides permanent residence only. No investment amount unlocks citizenship. ^[https://commoner-law.com/saudi/immigration-rights/premium-residency]

## Tax
- **0% personal income tax** — no tax on salary, dividends, or capital gains for individuals.
- **Zakat**: 2.5% on qualifying wealth for Saudi/GCC nationals. Non-Saudi residents not subject to zakat.
- **Corporate tax**: 20% on foreign-owned businesses. 2.5% zakat on Saudi/GCC-owned entities.
- **15% VAT** (increased from 5% in 2020, may revert per Vision 2030 milestones).
- **Expatriate dependent levy**: SAR 400/month per dependent for standard Iqama holders (not applicable to Premium Residency holders).
- **No wealth tax, no inheritance tax.**
- Social insurance contributions: 2% employee + 2% employer for non-Saudi workers (GOSI).

## IT / Digital Nomad
- **No dedicated digital nomad visa.** Saudi Arabia does not offer a remote work / nomad visa.
- IT professionals may enter through Premium Residency (Special Talent track), standard employment Iqama, or the Visit Visa for business.
- The Kingdom is investing heavily in tech (NEOM, The Line) and has growing startup funding.
- Saudi Arabia is positioning itself as a regional tech hub; dedicated DN visa may be introduced.
- For now, long-term remote workers must use Premium Residency or employer Iqama.

## Yacht / Maritime
- Red Sea coastline with developing marina infrastructure (Jeddah Yacht Club, NEOM's Sindalah Island).
- NEOM's Sindalah Island (opened 2024): luxury yachting destination, 86-berth marina.
- Saudi Arabia is actively developing its maritime tourism sector under Vision 2030.
- Foreign yacht owners with Premium Residency can register vessels.
- Still early-stage compared to UAE; rapid development ongoing.

## Notes for RU citizens
- **Visa required** for Russian citizens — tourist eVisa available (90-day validity, 30-day stay) via visitsaudi.com.
- **Growing RU community** in Riyadh, Jeddah, and the Eastern Province.
- **Premium Residency accessible** to Russian nationals who meet financial and clean-record requirements.
- **Banking**: Russian nationals face enhanced due diligence but Saudi banks generally remain accessible. No specific sanctions regime targeting RU nationals in KSA.
- **Limited direct flights**: Moscow–Jeddah/Riyadh via Saudia and Aeroflot (few weekly frequencies).
- Saudi Arabia has not adopted Western sanctions against Russia; remains neutral/partner position.

## Community pulse
Mixed. Premium Residency is functioning but expensive (SAR 800K permanent or SAR 100K/yr). The programme is still maturing. Standard Iqama remains the default for most expats but its employer-dependency and dependent levy are friction points. Cultural conservatism is decreasing but remains significant compared to UAE. Quality of life in Riyadh/Jeddah improving rapidly.
