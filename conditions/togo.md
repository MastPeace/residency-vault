## Overview
Togo is a small Francophone West African country on the Gulf of Guinea, with Lomé as a regional port and logistics hub. Immigration is governed by the 1978 Nationality Code and the Foreigners' regime. There is no digital-nomad visa. Citizenship by naturalisation requires 5 years of habitual residence. Dual nationality is formally restricted (naturalised persons must renounce prior nationality). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Togolese_nationality_law]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency — Work Permit + Resident Permit
- **Official name**: Autorisation de Travail (Work Permit) + Titre de Séjour (Resident Permit)
- **Conditions**: Employment contract; employer sponsorship; employer must justify hiring a foreigner.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable, tied to the contract. Ordinary resident stay permit valid 3 years. ^[https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/sites/www.un.org.development.desa.pd/files/unpd_ws_201509_togo_migration_fact_sheet.pdf]
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years habitual residence. ^[https://makerwebstudios.github.io/nationalitylaws/countries/togo]
- **Official source**: https://www.gouv.tg (Direction Générale de la Documentation Nationale)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — French essential; Lomé has a modest expat community (ports, banks, NGOs).

### investment_residency — Investor route
- **Official name**: Investor residence via API-ZF / Free Zone
- **Conditions**: Registered investment; Togo's free-trade zone (Lomé) offers fiscal incentives; facilitation of permits.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.zonefranche.tg
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — free-zone regime is well-established; practical experience varies.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 5 years of habitual residence for naturalisation. ^[https://makerwebstudios.github.io/nationalitylaws/countries/togo]
- **Language**: French expected; no formal test.
- **Dual citizenship**: Formally prohibited under the 1978 Nationality Code; naturalised persons must renounce prior nationality. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Togolese_nationality_law]
- **Passport**: ~61 destinations visa-free (ECOWAS free movement).

## Tax
- Residence-based; tax resident if >183 days or habitual abode.
- Personal income tax: progressive up to 35%.
- Corporate tax: 27% standard (lower in the free zone). VAT: 18%.
- CFA franc (XOF) pegged to EUR.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Lomé has a growing tech/startup scene (e.g. Togolese fintech) and reliable 4G/LTE. Remote workers generally need an employer or investor route. French is critical.

## Yacht / Maritime
Gulf of Guinea coastline (~56 km) with the deep-water Port of Lomé — a major West African transhipment hub. Limited leisure-yacht infrastructure.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa required. Togo maintains neutral relations with Russia. French is the working language. Small expat community, concentrated in Lomé. CFA franc zone provides EUR-pegged currency stability.

## Community pulse
Limited English-language expat forum data. The expat presence is small, concentrated in Lomé (port logistics, banks, NGOs). No significant digital-nomad base; French language is the main practical barrier.

## Country Card

### General description
Togo is a small Francophone West African country on the Gulf of Guinea, whose deep-water port at Lomé makes it a regional trade and logistics hub. Its economy is anchored by the port, phosphates and agriculture. Immigration is employment- and investment-led. Citizenship requires only 5 years of residence, but dual nationality is formally restricted.

### Well-known strong points
- Short 5-year path to citizenship
- Deep-water Port of Lomé — regional trade hub
- Free-trade zone with fiscal incentives for investors
- CFA franc peg to EUR — currency stability
- ECOWAS free movement

### Well-known problems
- Dual nationality formally restricted for naturalised citizens
- No digital-nomad or passive-income visa
- French language effectively mandatory
- Long-standing single-family political dominance (FH 41/100)
- Low median incomes and small market
