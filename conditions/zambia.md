## Overview
Zambia is an English-speaking, landlocked country in Southern Africa known for copper mining and Victoria Falls. Immigration is managed by the Department of Immigration under the Immigration and Deportation Act, with a well-defined permit system (Investor's Permit, Employment Permit, Spouse Permit, Study Permit). Citizenship by registration requires 10 years of residence. Dual citizenship is permitted (Citizenship of Zambia Act No. 33, 2016). ^[https://www.zambiaimmigration.gov.zm/the-citizenship-of-zambia-act-no-33-2016]

Related: [[zimbabwe]], [[south-africa]], [[investment-residency]].

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency — Employment Permit
- **Official name**: Employment Permit
- **Conditions**: Job offer; employer sponsorship; employer must demonstrate no suitable Zambian available. ^[https://www.zambiaimmigration.gov.zm/permit-types]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years, renewable, tied to employment.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years continuous residence (registration). ^[https://www.zambiaimmigration.gov.zm/the-citizenship-of-zambia-act-no-33-2016]
- **Official source**: https://www.zambiaimmigration.gov.zm
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — bureaucracy but English-medium process is accessible; Lusaka has a solid expat community.

### investment_residency — Investor's Permit
- **Official name**: Investor's Permit
- **Conditions**: Foreigner aged 18+ establishing or investing in a Zambian business, or joining an existing company. No fixed public minimum widely published; economic contribution assessed. ^[https://www.zambiaimmigration.gov.zm/permit-types]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 5 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.zambiaimmigration.gov.zm/permit-types
- **Community verdict**: Works well — Investor's Permit is a well-trodden route for small-business founders.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 10 years continuous lawful residence for registration as a citizen (Citizenship of Zambia Act No. 33, 2016). ^[https://media.zambialii.org/media/legislation/20802/source_file/a0318ed730b542f4/2016-33.pdf]
- **Language**: English (official); no formal test.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted — the 2016 Act (Part VI) and Constitution (Art. 39) allow dual citizenship. ^[https://www.zambiaimmigration.gov.zm/the-citizenship-of-zambia-act-no-33-2016]
- **Passport**: ~70 destinations visa-free or on arrival.

## Tax
- Residence-based; tax resident if ordinarily resident or >183 days.
- Personal income tax (PAYE): progressive 0%–37.5%.
- Corporate tax: 30% standard. VAT: 16%.
- No special expat flat-tax regime.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Lusaka has a growing tech scene, reliable 4G/LTE and fibre, and several coworking spaces. Remote workers typically use Investor's Permits or employer sponsorship. English-medium administration is a major advantage.

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked. No coastline. Water recreation centres on Lake Kariba and the Zambezi; Victoria Falls/Livingstone is a major tourism hub.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa policy has liberalised (Zambia waived visa requirements for 53 nationalities in Dec 2024 to boost tourism — check current RU status). Zambia maintains neutral relations with Russia. English is the working language. Banking is functional. Dual citizenship is available — attractive for Russians who do not want to renounce.

## Community pulse
Lusaka has a well-established, English-speaking expat community (mining, agriculture, NGOs). The Investor's Permit is frequently discussed on expat forums as a straightforward path for small-business owners. No significant digital-nomad base; immigration processing is reported as bureaucratic but manageable.

## Country Card

### General description
Zambia is a landlocked, English-speaking Southern African country whose economy is anchored by copper mining and agriculture. It offers one of the more accessible immigration systems in the region, with a clear Investor's Permit and Employment Permit framework. Citizenship requires 10 years of residence, but dual citizenship is permitted — a key differentiator in Africa.

### Well-known strong points
- Dual citizenship permitted (2016 Act)
- English is the official language — very accessible for expats
- Clear, published permit types including Investor's Permit
- Political stability and regular multiparty elections (FH 53/100)
- Victoria Falls and world-class safari tourism

### Well-known problems
- Landlocked — no coastline
- 10-year path to citizenship (long)
- No digital-nomad or passive-income visa
- Copper-price-driven economy; debt distress episodes
- Low median incomes; small consumer market
- Rule of law middling (WJP rank 103/142)
