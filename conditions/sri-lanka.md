## Overview
Sri Lanka's main residence route for investors is the Golden Paradise Residence Visa (10 years, US$200,000 deposit) alongside a standard Investor Visa (US$100,000 for 5 years / US$200,000 for 10). There is no digital-nomad or passive-income program. Naturalization is discretionary and dual citizenship is available to those with a prior Sri Lanka link (resumption/retention) — often involving an investment or fee. ^[https://eservices.immigration.gov.lk/golden-paradise-visa.html]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Golden Paradise Residence Visa
- **Conditions**: Deposit US$200,000 in a Golden Paradise Foreign Currency Account at a licensed bank (50% withdrawable after year 1); OR Investor Visa US$100,000 (5 yr) / US$200,000 (10 yr). Police clearance + medical report. ^[https://eservices.immigration.gov.lk/golden-paradise-visa.html]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 or 10 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence (discretionary naturalization).
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.gov.lk/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — new-ish program, moderate threshold, limited uptake/track record.

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Residence visa (employment basis)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from a Sri Lankan employer; work/residence permit via Department of Immigration & Emigration.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.gov.lk/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for corporate postings.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Employment visa (entry permit)
- **Conditions**: Employer-sponsored job offer.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.gov.lk/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student visa
- **Conditions**: Admission to recognized institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.gov.lk/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Dependant/spouse residence visa
- **Conditions**: Family tie to a visa holder or Sri Lankan citizen.
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to sponsor.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.gov.lk/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalization**: Discretionary, requiring a period of residence (commonly cited as 5 years) and renunciation of prior nationality; the Minister has broad discretion. ^[https://www.immigration.gov.lk/]
- **Dual citizenship**: Available via resumption (former citizens) or retention, often requiring an investment/deposit (e.g., US$25,000+ fixed deposit or treasury bonds held 3 years). ^[https://www.immigration.gov.lk/pages_e.php?id=4]
- **Practical reality**: Mainly relevant to the Sri Lankan diaspora; not a mainstream route for new expats.

## Tax
- **Individual income tax**: Progressive 6%–36% (PAYE). ^[https://www.ird.gov.lk/]
- **Tax residency**: 183 days in a tax year.
- **Residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Sri Lanka-source income.**
- **No special nomad regime.**

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Remote workers for foreign employers lack a formal residence route. Sri Lanka has a growing but small tech sector (Colombo) and moderate internet. Not a major nomad destination.

## Yacht / Maritime
Island nation with commercial ports at Colombo, Galle, Trincomalee. Recreational yachting is limited; Galle and Trincomalee have some moorings. Not a mainstream yachting hub.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Visa required (ETA for tourism). No visa-free entry. Golden Paradise and Investor visas are open in principle. Banking for RU nationals is restricted. Small Russian-speaking presence; not a primary RU destination.

## Community Pulse
Sri Lanka is a low-cost, beautiful base but not a mainstream immigration destination. The Golden Paradise Visa exists but has limited community track record; most long-stayers are on employment or dual-citizenship routes. The 2022 economic crisis still shapes perceptions.

## Country Card

### General description
Sri Lanka is an island nation south of India known for its beaches, tea, and Buddhist heritage. It offers an investor residence visa (Golden Paradise, US$200,000 / 10 years) and dual citizenship mainly for its diaspora, but no nomad or passive-income program. It remains a low-cost, off-the-beaten-path option recovering from the 2022 economic crisis.

### Well-known strong points
- Very low cost of living (Numbeo index ~25)
- Golden Paradise investor visa with a moderate US$200,000 threshold
- Dual citizenship available to the diaspora (resumption/retention)
- Warm tropical climate and extensive beaches
- English widely understood in urban areas

### Well-known problems
- No digital-nomad or passive-income visa
- Weak passport (39 visa-free destinations)
- Economic fragility since the 2022 crisis
- Naturalization discretionary and slow
- Limited yachting and IT infrastructure
