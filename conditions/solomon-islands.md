## Overview
The Solomon Islands is a Melanesian archipelago (population ~724,000) with a small, aid- and logging-dependent economy. Residency is administered by the Immigration Division; permanent residence requires 5 years of prior lawful stay. Citizenship by naturalisation (Citizenship Act 2018) requires 5 years total residence in the last 10 years, and dual citizenship is effectively barred (citizens who acquire another nationality lose Solomon Islands citizenship). There is no investment-migration program. ^[https://solomons.gov.sb/ministry-of-home-affairs/essential-services/obtain-solomon-islands-citizenship]

## Per-Program Detail

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Residents (Long Term) Visa / Permanent Resident
- **Conditions**: Foreign individuals who have lawfully resided in Solomon Islands for at least 5 years on other dependency visas and seek permanent residency. Visa fee USD $1,500, valid 5 years, extendable; no automatic work rights. ^[https://immigration.gov.sb/migrate]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 years, extendable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years total residence in the last 10 years for naturalisation.
- **Official source**: https://immigration.gov.sb/migrate
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (requires long prior stay; no true retiree-arrival route).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work Permit / Employment Visa
- **Conditions**: Employer-sponsored; work rights granted separately.
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to contract.
- **Path to citizenship**: Counts toward the 5-year residence clock.
- **Official source**: https://immigration.gov.sb/migrate
- **Community verdict**: Mixed.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Family Relationship Visa
- **Conditions**: Dependent of a Solomon Islands citizen/resident; USD $300; 2 years, extendable; no automatic work rights. ^[https://immigration.gov.sb/migrate]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, extendable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Residence counts toward naturalisation.
- **Official source**: https://immigration.gov.sb/migrate
- **Community verdict**: Works well for family ties.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. The Solomon Islands does not sell citizenship; naturalisation is residence-based (a 2020 proposal to consider selling citizenship was not enacted). See [[citizenship-by-investment]]. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solomon_Islands_nationality_law]

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 5 years total residence within the 10 years immediately before applying (Citizenship Act 2018); 18+, good character, intend to continue residing. ^[https://solomons.gov.sb/ministry-of-home-affairs/essential-services/obtain-solomon-islands-citizenship]
- **Language**: Knowledge of English, Pijin, or a Solomon Islands vernacular. ^[https://solomonislandsembassy.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Solomon-Islands-Citizenship-Application.pdf]
- **Dual citizenship**: Not permitted — a citizen acquiring another nationality ceases to be a Solomon Islands citizen. ^[https://www.constituteproject.org/constitution/Solomon_Islands_2018]
- **Fee**: SBD $10,000 for adult naturalisation.

## Tax
Small progressive personal income-tax regime; no special non-dom or digital-nomad regime. Verify current rates with the Inland Revenue Division.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa and limited connectivity; Solomon Islands is not an IT-hub market.

## Yacht / Maritime
Archipelagic nation with scattered islands; Honiara is the main port. No yacht-registration hub or maritime-residency program.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Standard consular visa processing; no dedicated agreements or restrictions reported. [[fiji]] is a more practical Pacific residence option for Russian applicants.

## Community Pulse
Very low expat volume. Practical reports emphasise the 5-year prior-stay requirement for permanent residence, the ban on dual citizenship, and limited infrastructure. Naturalisation is rare and discretionary.

## Country Card

### General description
The Solomon Islands is a poor, aid-dependent Melanesian state with weak rule of law and occasional urban unrest. Its immigration system is thin: permanent residence requires 5 years of prior lawful stay, naturalisation 5 years in the last 10, and dual citizenship is prohibited. The passport is surprisingly strong for the region (131 destinations).

### Well-known strong points
- Relatively strong passport (131 visa-free destinations, best in the Pacific)
- Statutory, residence-based naturalisation (5 years in 10)
- English official language

### Well-known problems
- No investment-migration or digital-nomad routes
- Dual citizenship prohibited
- Weak rule of law, corruption, and occasional unrest
- Very low GDP per capita (~$2,600 PPP)
