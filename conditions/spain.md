## Overview
Spain is a large EU/Schengen economy with a strong passport. It offers several residence routes: [[non-lucrative-visa]] (passive income), [[digital-nomad]] visa, and work permits. The former [[golden-visa]] (investor visa) closed on April 3, 2025. Citizenship requires 10 years of residence (2 years for Ibero-American nationals). ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/spain-non-lucrative-visa]

## Residence
Key routes for non-EU nationals in 2026:

**[[Non-lucrative visa]] (NLV)**: for individuals with sufficient passive income who do NOT work in Spain. No remote work permitted. ^[https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/sydney/en/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/Consular/Visado-de-residencia-no-lucrativa.aspx]
- **Income**: 400% of IPREM — ~€2,400/month (€28,800/year) for main applicant in 2026
- **Per dependent**: +100% IPREM (~€600/month)
- **Initial validity**: 1 year; then 2-year renewals
- **Permanent residence**: after 5 years
- **Citizenship**: after 10 years
- **No work of any kind** (including remote work for foreign employers)

**[[Digital-nomad]] visa**: introduced 2023 under the Startup Law. For remote workers employed by foreign companies or freelancing with foreign clients. Income requirement: ~€2,646/month (200% of minimum wage).

**[[Golden-visa]] (former)**: closed for new applications since April 3, 2025. Existing holders retain their permits and can renew.

**Entrepreneur visa**: for innovative business projects, with fast-track processing.

## Citizenship
- 10 years of legal continuous residence (standard)
- 2 years for nationals of Ibero-American countries, Andorra, Philippines, Equatorial Guinea, Portugal
- 1 year for marriage to Spanish citizen (after 1 year of legal residence)
- Spanish language (DELE A2) and cultural knowledge (CCSE) tests
- Spain generally requires renunciation of previous citizenship (exceptions for Ibero-American countries)

## Tax
Progressive income tax: 19%–47% (varies by autonomous community). Wealth tax in some regions. Spain has a special tax regime for impatriates (Beckham Law): 24% flat tax on Spanish-source income up to €600,000 for the first 5 years + 1 year prior to arrival.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Spain's [[digital-nomad]] visa under the Startup Law (2023) allows remote workers to:
- Stay for 1 year initial (renewable for up to 5 years total)
- Work for foreign employers/clients
- Income threshold: ~€2,646/month
- Access the Beckham Law tax regime (24% flat)
- Leading to permanent residence after 5 years

## Yacht / Maritime
Spain has an extensive Mediterranean and Atlantic coastline. Yacht registration under the Spanish flag is available (Registro de Buques). The 12% matriculation tax on yacht purchases was reduced in some autonomous communities. Major yachting hubs: Barcelona, Palma de Mallorca, Valencia, Málaga, and the Balearic/Canary Islands.

## Notes for RU citizens
Spain is popular with Russian nationals. The [[non-lucrative-visa]] and [[digital-nomad]] visa are accessible. The former Golden Visa was heavily used by Russian investors before its closure. Russian consulate applications may face administrative delays. The Spanish "arraigo" mechanism (roots-based regularization after 3 years of undocumented presence) has historically been used by some but is not a recommended pathway. Spain has a significant Russian-speaking community on the Costa del Sol and Costa Blanca.
