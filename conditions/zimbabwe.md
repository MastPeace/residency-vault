## Overview
Zimbabwe is a landlocked Southern African country recovering from decades of economic crisis and hyperinflation. Immigration is governed by the Immigration Act (permits issued by the Department of Immigration). There is no digital-nomad or retirement visa. Citizenship by registration requires 10 years of residence. Dual citizenship is permitted for citizens by birth under the 2013 Constitution. ^[http://zimparis.gov.zw/index.php/consular/registration/zimbabwean-citizenship]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency — Employment Permit
- **Official name**: Employment Permit + Residence Permit
- **Conditions**: Job offer; employer sponsorship; employer must justify hiring a foreigner (scarce-skills orientation).
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years, renewable, tied to employment.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years legal ordinary residence (registration). ^[http://zimparis.gov.zw/index.php/consular/registration/zimbabwean-citizenship]
- **Official source**: https://www.zimbabweimmigration.gov.zw
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — bureaucratic; economy-dependent; English-medium but processing slow.

### investment_residency — Investor's Residence Permit
- **Official name**: Investor's Residence Permit
- **Conditions**: Registered investment or business; Zimbabwe Investment and Development Agency (ZIDA) facilitation for approved projects.
- **Duration / renewal**: Renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.zidainvest.com
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — ZIDA provides one-stop facilitation, but currency and repatriation risks persist.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 10 years legal ordinary residence for registration as a citizen (Citizenship of Zimbabwe Act). ^[http://zimparis.gov.zw/index.php/consular/registration/zimbabwean-citizenship]
- **Language**: English (official); no formal test.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted for citizens by birth (2013 Constitution); those acquiring by registration/descent may be restricted. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwean_nationality_law]
- **Passport**: ~61 destinations visa-free or on arrival.

## Tax
- Residence-based; tax resident if ordinarily resident or >183 days.
- Personal income tax (PAYE): progressive, top rate 40% (USD-denominated economy).
- Corporate tax: 24.72%. VAT: 15%.
- Multi-currency regime (primarily USD) complicates planning; no special expat regime.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Harare has a small but active tech scene and reasonable mobile/fibre internet. Remote workers generally need employer sponsorship or an investor permit. Economic instability (currency, power cuts) is the main practical obstacle.

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked. No coastline. Water recreation centres on Lake Kariba and the Zambezi; Victoria Falls is a major tourism draw (shared with Zambia).

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa on arrival for many nationalities (check current RU status). Zimbabwe maintains friendly relations with Russia. English is the working language. Economic instability and cash/currency difficulties are the main practical problems; banking is functional but USD-based.

## Community pulse
Limited English-language expat forum data. The expat presence is small and concentrated in mining, agriculture and NGOs. Harare has a modest international community. No significant digital-nomad base; economic volatility dominates practical concerns.

## Country Card

### General description
Zimbabwe is a landlocked Southern African country with a well-educated, English-speaking population, renowned for Victoria Falls and its wildlife. After two decades of economic crisis it has stabilised into a multi-currency (largely USD) economy. Immigration is employment- and investment-led. Citizenship requires 10 years of residence; dual citizenship is permitted for citizens by birth.

### Well-known strong points
- English is the official language
- Highly educated workforce; Victoria Falls tourism
- Dual citizenship permitted for citizens by birth
- ZIDA one-stop investor facilitation
- High sunshine hours and pleasant highland climate

### Well-known problems
- 10-year path to citizenship (long)
- Freedom House 26/100 (Not Free); weak rule of law (WJP rank 123/142)
- Chronic currency instability and cash shortages
- Frequent power cuts and infrastructure strain
- No digital-nomad or passive-income visa
- Emigration (brain drain) and limited consumer market
