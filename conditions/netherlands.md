## Overview
The Netherlands is a wealthy EU/Schengen member with a strong passport. It offers a unique residence route for US citizens via the Dutch-American Friendship Treaty (DAFT). The [[blue-card]] (EU Blue Card) is available for highly skilled workers. Citizenship requires 5 years of residence, passing the civic integration exam (inburgering), and renunciation of previous citizenship. ^[https://dutchreview.com/expat/daft-treaty-explainer]

## Residence
Key routes for non-EU nationals:

**DAFT (Dutch-American Friendship Treaty)**: exclusive to US citizens. ^[https://dutchreview.com/expat/daft-treaty-explainer]
- Register a Dutch business (BV or sole proprietorship)
- Deposit ≥ €4,500 in a Dutch business bank account (must remain throughout)
- Permit valid: 2 years, renewable
- Path to permanent residency after 5 years
- Family reunification available
- The 30% ruling (tax benefit) may apply if structured with a BV pre-arrival

**EU Blue Card**: for highly qualified workers. ^[https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/european-blue-card-residence-permit]
- Higher education degree
- Employment contract with Dutch employer
- Salary threshold applies
- Valid: duration of contract + 3 months (max 4 years)
- Family members can join with work authorization
- After 5 years: permanent residence

**Highly Skilled Migrant (Kennismigrant)**: employer-sponsored permit with a reduced salary threshold for graduates and those under 30.

## Citizenship
- 5 years of uninterrupted legal residence
- Civic integration exam (inburgering) — Dutch language A2 + knowledge of Dutch society
- **Renunciation of previous citizenship generally required** (exceptions: marriage to Dutch citizen, loss would cause serious harm)
- Dutch citizenship grants an EU passport

## Tax
Progressive income tax: 36.93% (up to €73,031) / 49.5% above. **30% ruling**: qualifying expat employees can receive 30% of their salary tax-free for up to 5 years (reduced from 8). 2026 30% ruling salary minimum: €69,000. Corporate tax: 19% (up to €200K) / 25.8% above. No wealth tax but a deemed-return wealth tax (Box 3) on assets.

## IT / Digital Nomad
The Netherlands does not have a dedicated [[digital-nomad]] visa. Remote workers primarily use:
- DAFT (US citizens only)
- ZZP (sole proprietorship) for EU/EEA nationals
- Highly Skilled Migrant visa for employer-sponsored workers
Amsterdam, Eindhoven, and Rotterdam are major tech hubs with strong startup ecosystems and excellent digital infrastructure.

## Yacht / Maritime
The Netherlands has one of the world's most advanced maritime industries and is a global leader in superyacht building. Major yacht-building centers: Flevoland, Rotterdam, and the Amsterdam area. Yacht registration under the Dutch flag is available. Recognized captain and maritime licensing.

## Notes for RU citizens
The Netherlands maintains EU sanctions against Russia. Visa processing for Russian citizens may be severely restricted. The DAFT route is unavailable (US citizens only). The Highly Skilled Migrant route requires employer sponsorship in a difficult job market environment. The inburgering language requirement is moderate (A2). The renunciation-of-citizenship requirement is a significant barrier for Russian nationals.
