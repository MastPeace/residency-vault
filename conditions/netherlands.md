## Overview
The Netherlands is a wealthy EU/Schengen member with a strong passport. Its immigration system is managed by the IND (Immigratie- en Naturalisatiedienst). The flagship work permit is the Highly Skilled Migrant (Kennismigrant) scheme, joined by the EU Blue Card and the unique DAFT (Dutch-American Friendship Treaty) route for US citizens. Citizenship requires 5 years of continuous legal residence, passing the civic integration exam (Inburgering, A2 Dutch), and renunciation of previous citizenship (exceptions apply). The 30% tax ruling offers significant expat benefits but was reduced from 8 to 5 years. ^[https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/highly-skilled-migrant]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: The Netherlands has no Golden Visa or investor residence program. Foreign investors may use the Self-Employment Permit (ZZP/BV route) or the Start-up Visa (if innovative). No minimum investment threshold; assessed on business viability and Dutch economic interest. DAFT: US citizens only, €4,500 capital.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–5 years depending on route.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; Inburgering A2; renunciation generally required.
- **Official source**: https://ind.nl/
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (no clear investor pathway; DAFT serves US citizens but is not an investment program).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No passive income or retirement residence route. DAFT requires active business operation. Wealthy individuals may theoretically use the EU long-term residence route after obtaining residence in another EU country first.
- **Duration / renewal**: N/A.
- **Path to citizenship**: N/A.
- **Official source**: https://ind.nl/
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (no passive income route; Netherlands is not a retirement destination for non-EU).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No digital nomad visa. Remote workers cannot use tourist visas for work. Options: DAFT (US citizens, requires active business setup); Self-Employment Permit (requires innovative/dutch-economic-interest business); Highly Skilled Migrant (requires Dutch employer).
- **Duration / renewal**: N/A.
- **Path to citizenship**: N/A.
- **Official source**: https://ind.nl/
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (gap vs. Portugal/Spain/Italy; DAFT is US-only workaround).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Kennismigrant (Highly Skilled Migrant — HSM) / Europese Blauwe Kaart (EU Blue Card)
- **Conditions — HSM**: Employer must be recognized sponsor by IND. Salary thresholds 2026 (gross monthly, excl. 8% holiday allowance): €5,942/mo (age 30+), €4,357/mo (under 30), €3,126/mo (orientation year graduates). No degree required if salary met. ^[https://www.deloitte.com/nl/en/services/tax/perspectives/highly-skilled-migrant-and-european-blue-card-2026-salary-thresholds.html]
- **Conditions — EU Blue Card**: Higher education degree required; salary ≥ €5,331/mo (2026). EU mobility rights after 12 months.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–5 years (contract + 3 months). Permanent residence after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; Inburgering A2; renunciation generally required.
- **Official source**: https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/highly-skilled-migrant
- **Community verdict**: Works well (fast processing 2–8 weeks; recognized sponsor system smooth; 30% ruling is major tax benefit).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Verblijfsvergunning voor Start-up
- **Conditions**: Innovative business with step-by-step plan; recognized facilitator (RVO-listed); sufficient financial resources; registration with Dutch Trade Register. After 1 year: can switch to Self-Employment Permit if business is viable. ^[https://ind.nl/en/forms/7558.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, non-renewable as start-up (must transition to self-employment).
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; Inburgering A2.
- **Official source**: https://ind.nl/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (structured program; facilitator requirement ensures support but limits flexibility).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Gecombineerde Vergunning voor Verblijf en Arbeid — GVVA (Single Permit)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Dutch employer (not recognized sponsor); labor market test (employer must prove no suitable EU/EEA candidate); salary ≥ statutory minimum. Processing up to 90 days. ^[https://www.nlcompass.com/guides/work-permits-netherlands]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years, tied to employer.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; Inburgering A2.
- **Official source**: https://ind.nl/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (labor market test is hurdle; HSM route vastly preferred).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Verblijfsvergunning voor Studie
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Dutch institution; sufficient financial means (€1,300/mo living + tuition); study progress requirement (50% credits annually). Part-time work: max 16 hrs/week or full-time in summer.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years (orientation year counts fully); orientation year (zoekjaar) after graduation — 1 year to find HSM job at reduced salary threshold (€3,126/mo).
- **Official source**: https://ind.nl/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (orientation year is excellent bridge to HSM).

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Verblijf bij Familie- of Gezinslid / Partner Visa
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; income ≥ €2,337/mo (partner), lower for children; relationship proof (marriage, registered partnership, or durable relationship). HSM family: expedited processing, spouse gets free labor market access immediately. ^[https://www.nlcompass.com/guides/work-permits-netherlands]
- **Duration / renewal**: Aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; Inburgering A2.
- **Official source**: https://ind.nl/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (HSM partner route is particularly smooth).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No retirement visa. Non-EU retirees generally cannot obtain Dutch residence solely on pension income unless qualifying through other routes (family reunification, prior residence).
- **Duration / renewal**: N/A.
- **Path to citizenship**: N/A.
- **Official source**: https://ind.nl/
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (Netherlands is not a retirement destination for non-EU nationals).

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. The Netherlands does not offer direct citizenship by investment.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 5 years uninterrupted legal residence. ^[https://www.leideninternationalcentre.nl/just-arrived/official-procedures/dutch-citizenship]
- **Language**: Inburgering (Civic Integration) exam — A2 Dutch (B1 under proposed reform, not yet in force). KNM (knowledge of Dutch society) + ONA (orientation on Dutch labor market).
- **Dual citizenship**: Generally NOT permitted — must renounce previous citizenship. Exceptions: marriage to Dutch citizen; loss would cause serious financial harm; country of origin does not allow renunciation. ^[https://ind.nl/en/dutch-citizenship/dual-nationality]
- **Processing**: Up to 12 months from application.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive Box 1 (income): 36.93% up to €73,031 / 49.5% above (2026). Box 2 (substantial interest): 24.5%/31%. Box 3 (wealth): deemed-return tax (~36% on assumed returns, assets > €57,000).
- **30% ruling**: Qualifying expat employees recruited from abroad can receive 30% of salary tax-free for up to 5 years (reduced from 8). 2026 salary minimum: ~€69,000 (or €46,000 for those under 30 with a master's). Partial non-resident status for Box 2/3. ^[https://www.nlcompass.com/guides/work-permits-netherlands]
- **DAFT holders**: Generally not eligible for 30% ruling unless employed via own BV pre-arrival.
- **Corporate tax**: 19% up to €200K, 25.8% above.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital nomad visa. IT professionals should use the Highly Skilled Migrant route (most common) — recognized sponsors include most major tech companies (Booking.com, Adyen, ASML, Uber Amsterdam, etc.). The 30% ruling is a significant net-income boost. No freelancer/ZZP visa for non-EU: DAFT (US citizens) is the closest workaround but requires active business. Amsterdam, Eindhoven, and Rotterdam are major tech hubs. The Netherlands has world-class digital infrastructure and near-universal English proficiency, making it one of the easiest countries to operate in without Dutch language.

## Yacht / Maritime
The Netherlands is a global leader in superyacht building (Feadship, Oceanco, Amels, Heesen). Dutch flag yacht registration available. Major yacht-building centers: Flevoland, Rotterdam, Amsterdam area. The maritime sector is a major economic pillar. Recognized captain and maritime licensing under Dutch flag.

## Notes for RU Citizens
The Netherlands maintains strict EU sanctions against Russia. Visa processing for Russian citizens is severely restricted. The HSM route requires employer sponsorship — challenging in current geopolitical climate. DAFT is US-exclusive. The Start-up Visa requires a recognized facilitator. The Inburgering A2 language bar is moderate. The renunciation-of-citizenship requirement is a major barrier. The 30% ruling and excellent English-speaking environment are attractive, but the geopolitical headwinds are substantial.

## Community Pulse
The Netherlands has an efficient, employer-driven immigration system: HSM is fast (2–8 weeks) and the recognized sponsor scheme is mature. The 30% ruling reduction (8→5 years) and proposed further tightening are points of expat anxiety. The lack of passive-income, digital-nomad, investor, and retiree routes means non-working or self-employed non-EU individuals have few options (DAFT for US citizens only). The housing crisis (extreme shortages, especially in Amsterdam) is a practical barrier dwarfing immigration policy issues. Dutch directness and English proficiency make cultural integration relatively smooth.
