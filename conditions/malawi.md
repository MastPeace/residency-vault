## Overview
Malawi is a landlocked southeastern African country bordering Lake Malawi (pop. ~20.9M). Known as the "Warm Heart of Africa," its immigration is governed by the Immigration Act (Cap. 15:03) and the Citizenship Act of 1966. Naturalization: 7 years residence. Passport: 41 visa-free destinations. ^[https://data.worldbank.org/country/MW]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Temporary Employment Permit (TEP)
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from Malawian employer; employer sponsorship; labor market preference for locals.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 7 years ordinary residence. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malawian_nationality_law]
- **Official source**: Department of Immigration and Citizenship Services (https://www.immigration.gov.mw).
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data (modest expat community, mainly NGO/agriculture sectors).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student visa
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Malawian institution; proof of financial means.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: Residence time counts toward 7-year clock.
- **Official source**: Malawian embassies / consulates.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Dependent's Pass
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; proof of relationship and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 7 years residence.
- **Official source**: Immigration authorities.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

## Citizenship
- **Residence**: 7 years ordinary residence; adequate knowledge of a prescribed vernacular language (Chichewa) required. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malawian_nationality_law]
- **Language**: English or Chichewa proficiency required by law.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted (since 2019 constitutional amendment).
- **Processing**: Application to Ministry of Home Affairs. Police character certificate required.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive income tax; corporate tax ~30%.
- **Remote workers**: No special regime. Tax enforcement limited but improving.
- **General**: Malawian kwacha (MWK). Developing tax administration.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. Internet is developing (4G in major cities; Starlink available since 2024). Coworking is nascent (Blantyre, Lilongwe). Malawi is not a recognized nomad destination. [[south-africa]] and [[kenya]] are regional alternatives.

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked but features Lake Malawi (~580 km long), one of Africa's largest freshwater lakes. Recreational boating on the lake is a niche tourism sector. No ocean access or yacht relevance.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Visa required (obtainable at Malawian embassies or on arrival). No special RU programs. Small Russian tourism and NGO presence. Passport is modest (41 destinations).

## Community Pulse
Malawi has a modest expat community centered on development aid, NGOs, and agriculture. The "Warm Heart of Africa" reputation attracts some retirees and adventure travelers. Not discussed on nomad or investor-immigration forums. 7-year naturalization is mid-range for Africa.

## Country Card

### General description
Malawi is a landlocked southeastern African country with a densely populated agricultural economy. Known as the "Warm Heart of Africa," it has an English-speaking tradition, a modest passport (41 destinations), and a 7-year naturalization period. Immigration infrastructure is modest but functional, with work permits and dependent passes as the primary routes.

### Well-known strong points
- English as official language (accessible for internationals)
- "Warm Heart of Africa" — safe, welcoming reputation
- Lake Malawi: unique freshwater coastline; niche tourism
- Dual citizenship permitted (since 2019)

### Well-known problems
- Landlocked; poor infrastructure; low GDP per capita (~$1,700 PPP)
- High poverty rate; limited formal employment
- Rule of law challenges; infrastructure underdeveloped
- Reliance on single agricultural exports (tobacco, tea); cyclical food insecurity
