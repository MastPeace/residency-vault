## Overview
Belize is an English-speaking Caribbean/Central American nation with a well-established retirement programme — the Qualified Retired Persons (QRP) programme — created in 1999 under the Retired Persons Incentive Act and operated by the Belize Tourism Board. QRP grants indefinite residency (not permanent residence, but a "Permanent Tourist" status) with minimal physical presence requirements. No dedicated digital nomad visa. Path to citizenship requires standard naturalization (5+ years permanent residence). ^[https://www.belizetourismboard.org/programs-events/retirement-program]

## Per-Program Detail

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Qualified Retired Persons (QRP) Programme
- **Conditions**: Age 40+. Beneficial recipient of retirement income ≥ US$2,000/month (US$24,000/year) from FOREIGN sources in approved currency (USD, GBP, EUR, CAD). ^[https://www.belizetourismboard.org/programs-events/retirement-program]
  - Eligible income sources: pension, annuity, Social Security benefits, investment accounts, inheritance, reverse mortgage, personal savings, retirement contribution plans, other legitimate retirement income.
  - Must deposit the required income into a Belizean bank, credit union, or licensed financial institution.
  - Must pass security clearance from Ministry of National Security.
  - Qualifying nationalities: all Commonwealth (excl. Belize), USA, EU member states, Asia-Pacific countries (Australia, Bangladesh, China, India, Indonesia, Japan, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, New Zealand, Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, Vietnam), all South American countries.
- **Duration / renewal**: Indefinite — QRP card must be renewed annually but status is on-going as long as requirements met. Must spend minimum 30 consecutive days per year in Belize.
- **Path to citizenship**: QRP is NOT permanent residence — does NOT count toward citizenship years. To naturalize: first obtain permanent residence (1 year legal residence + application), then 5+ years permanent residence → apply for naturalization.
- **Official source**: https://www.belizetourismboard.org/programs-events/retirement-program
- **Community verdict**: Works well (lowest-threshold Caribbean retirement programme; $2,000/mo is accessible for many retirees; 30-day/annual presence is very light; English-speaking; NO tax on foreign income — QRP specifically exempts foreign-source income).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Belize Work Permit
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from Belizean employer; employer must demonstrate no suitable local candidate. Annual renewal.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: After 1 year continuous legal residence → permanent residence; after 5 years permanent residence → naturalization. ^[https://belize.com/residency-and-retirement-in-belize]
- **Official source**: Ministry of Immigration, Belize
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (small labour market; mostly in tourism, agriculture, offshore services).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Self-Employed Work Permit / QRP Investor
- **Conditions**: Under QRP, a qualified person may carry on business if: approved by BTB, has minimum BZD 1,000,000 (~US$500,000) investment capital, employs minimum 5 Belizean citizens, has resided 90 non-consecutive days in Belize. ^[https://www.belizetourismboard.org/programs-events/retirement-program]
- **Duration / renewal**: Annual business approval.
- **Path to citizenship**: Permanent residence → naturalization (5+ years).
- **Official source**: https://www.belizetourismboard.org/programs-events/retirement-program
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (high capital threshold; strong employment requirement; limited to QRP holders who already qualify).

## Citizenship
- **By naturalization**: 5 years as permanent resident (or 1 year if married to Belizean). Must demonstrate good character, English proficiency, intent to reside. ^[https://belize.com/residency-and-retirement-in-belize]
- **Dual citizenship**: Fully recognized.
- **By descent**: Available to children born abroad to Belizean parents.
- Note: Permanent residence first requires 1 year of continuous legal residence in Belize on any valid permit.

## Tax
- Territorial tax system: foreign-source income NOT taxed for residents.
- QRP members specifically exempted by law from taxes on foreign-source income. ^[https://belize.com/residency-and-retirement-in-belize]
- No capital gains tax. No inheritance tax. No wealth tax.
- Local income tax: 25% on income up to BZD 26,000; 0% beyond (effectively a cap).

## IT / Digital Nomad
Not available. No dedicated digital nomad visa. Remote workers can enter as tourists (30-day stamp, extendable to 90 days). For longer stays, QRP requires retirement/passive income sources — active remote employment income may not qualify as "retirement income." No stable legal framework for digital nomads beyond tourist visa runs.

## Yacht / Maritime
Belize operates an international ship registry (IMMARBE). Yacht registration available. Offshore banking and IBCs historically significant, though tightened under OECD pressure.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian nationals are NOT listed among qualifying countries for QRP (qualifying list: Commonwealth excl. Belize, USA, EU, Asia-Pacific named countries, South America). Likely ineligible for QRP.
- Standard work permit/permanent residence routes available but not tailored for non-English-speaking applicants.
- No visa-free access; Russian passport holders require visa.

## Community pulse
- QRP is the standout programme — accessible ($2,000/mo), English-speaking, territorial tax, minimal presence requirement.
- Work permits and permanent residence are standard but small-market.
- Expats praise low cost of living and lifestyle; healthcare limited outside Belize City.
- QRP widely recommended for North American retirees.

## Country Card

### General description
Belize is a small English-speaking nation on the Caribbean coast of Central America, bordered by Mexico and Guatemala. It uniquely combines Caribbean culture with Central American geography, featuring the world's second-largest barrier reef. Belize's flagship residency programme is the Qualified Retired Persons (QRP) programme, established in 1999: it grants indefinite "Permanent Tourist" status with a US$2,000/month foreign retirement income requirement, age 40+, and just 30 days/year of physical presence. QRP holders are specifically exempt from taxes on foreign-source income. Belize operates a territorial tax system and offers a moderate 5-year permanent residence + naturalization path (plus 1 year of legal residence first). Dual citizenship is recognized.

### Well-known strong points
- QRP programme: indefinite residency, $2,000/mo, age 40+, 30 days/year presence — low friction
- English-speaking with British common-law legal system
- Territorial tax: no tax on foreign-source income; QRP specifically exempt
- Freedom House score 86/100 (Free) — stable democracy
- World-class diving and barrier reef; Caribbean lifestyle at Central American prices
- Dual citizenship fully recognized

### Well-known problems
- QRP does NOT count toward citizenship years — must convert to permanent residence separately
- QRP income must be "retirement-type" — active remote employment may not qualify
- Russian nationals NOT listed among QRP-qualifying nationalities
- Small economy; limited healthcare infrastructure outside Belize City
- Higher cost of living than neighbouring Central American countries (Numbeo 48.5)
- No digital nomad visa; QRP is the only accessible long-stay route
