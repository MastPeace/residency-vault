## Overview
Moldova is a small, EU-candidate country between Romania and Ukraine, with Romanian as its state language. Its headline attraction for the tech sector is the **Moldova IT Park (MITP)**, a virtual regime where resident IT companies pay a single **7% tax on turnover** (state-guaranteed to 2035). Citizenship is attainable after 10 years of residence, and dual citizenship is permitted (roughly a third of Moldovans also hold Romanian passports). ^[https://mitp.md/]

## Per-Program Detail

### IT Visa
- **Official name**: IT Visa (Moldova IT Park programme)
- **Conditions**: Employment/engagement with a MITP resident company; fast-tracked residence for foreign IT professionals; the Park streamlines immigration for international talent. ^[https://mitp.md/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Time counts toward the 10-year clock.
- **Official source**: https://mitp.md
- **Community verdict**: Works well — the standard entry for foreign developers into Moldova's 3,000+ IT companies.

### Employment residence
- **Official name**: Permis de ședere provizoriu (temporary residence)
- **Conditions**: Employment contract, business registration, study, or family grounds; renewable; permanent residence available after legal residence (5 years standard, shorter for some categories).
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years residence (see below).
- **Official source**: https://igm.gov.md
- **Community verdict**: Works well — straightforward, low bureaucracy by regional standards.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 10 years lawful, habitual residence (8 years for stateless/refugees; 3 years if married to a citizen). ^[https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/112630/MDA-112630.pdf]
- **Language**: Romanian (national language) + knowledge of the Constitution.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted.
- **Citizenship by investment**: a Moldova CBI programme (€100,000 contribution) launched in 2018 was suspended in 2019–2020 and has not reopened. ^[https://legislationline.org/taxonomy/term/12733]

## Tax
- **Personal income tax**: flat **12%**. ^[https://www.invest.gov.md/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Investor-Guide-EN-2026.pdf]
- **Corporate income tax**: 12% (deferral on reinvested profits for SMEs).
- **MITP single tax**: **7% of turnover** replaces corporate income tax, employee income tax, social/medical contributions, and local taxes; state-guaranteed to 2035. ^[https://mitp.md/]
- **VAT**: 20% (8% reduced); social contributions 24%.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Moldova's MITP is Europe's first virtual IT park, with 3,000+ resident companies and ~26,000 specialists, generating over 5% of GDP. The 7% single tax and the IT Visa make Chișinău a genuine, low-cost base for tech companies and remote-friendly firms. No standalone digital-nomad visa for freelancers outside the Park.

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked — no coastline (only a small Danube port at Giurgiulești). Not applicable.

## Notes for RU citizens
Large Russian-speaking population (Russian is widely spoken though not official). Visa and residence rules are not specifically hostile to Russian nationals, but post-2022 Moldova has aligned with EU policies; banking and long-stay permits for RU citizens require standard due diligence. Many RU-speaking IT professionals already work through MITP.

## Community pulse
The IT Park is consistently praised for its 7% tax and low bureaucracy; the wider bureaucracy is seen as improving but corruption remains a complaint (FH 60/100 Partly Free). Cost of living is among the lowest in Europe. Emigration is heavy, which keeps a labour pool of developers affordable.

## Country Card

### General description
Moldova is a landlocked Eastern European country of ~2.5 million, an EU accession candidate with Romanian as its official language and a heavy emigration profile. Its standout feature is the Moldova IT Park's 7% single turnover tax, one of Europe's lowest for tech, anchored in a low-cost, Russian-and-Romanian-speaking environment. Citizenship takes 10 years but dual nationality is permitted, and many Moldovans hold Romanian (EU) passports.

### Well-known strong points
- MITP 7% single tax on turnover (guaranteed to 2035) for IT
- 12% flat personal income tax
- Very low cost of living (Numbeo ~30)
- Dual citizenship permitted; Romanian-language country with EU candidacy
- Cheap, well-educated developer talent pool

### Well-known problems
- Poorest economy in Europe; heavy emigration
- Corruption and weak institutions (FH 60/100 Partly Free)
- No golden visa or passive-income residence; CBI suspended
- Landlocked with limited infrastructure
- 10-year citizenship path
