## Overview
Vanuatu (Republic of Vanuatu, South Pacific) runs the fastest citizenship-by-investment programme in the world — the Development Support Program (DSP), a non-refundable government contribution granting citizenship in roughly 30–60 days with **no residence requirement**. It is administered by the Vanuatu Citizenship Commission under the Citizenship Act (Cap 112). It is the only CBI programme in the Asia-Pacific region. ^[https://vanuatucitizenshipbyinvestment.com/citizenship-by-investment]

## Per-Program Detail

### citizenship_by_investment
- **Official name**: Development Support Program (DSP); alternatives: Capital Investment Immigration Plan (CIIP), Real Estate Option (REO)
- **Conditions (DSP donation)**: US$130,000 single; US$150,000 married couple; US$165,000 couple + 1 child <18; US$180,000 couple + 2 children <18; +US$10,000 per additional child. FIU due-diligence fee US$5,000–5,500/application. DSP excludes China/Hong Kong (served by the identical Contribution Program, VCP). CIIP: US$100,000 donation + US$50,000 redeemable investment (4-yr). REO: US$100,000 donation + property. ^[https://vanuatucitizenshipbyinvestment.com/citizenship-by-investment]
- **Duration / renewal**: Citizenship permanent and hereditary; 10-year biometric passport. No residence requirement.
- **Path to citizenship**: Direct grant; processing ~30–60 days (fastest CBI globally). ^[https://www.vanuatupassport.vu/vanuatu-citizenship-program/vanuatu-passport-cost]
- **Official source**: https://vanuatucitizenshipbyinvestment.com/citizenship-by-investment
- **Community verdict**: Works well (fast, cheap, but passport is weak — no Schengen/UK/US access; EU partially suspended Vanuatu's visa-free deal in 2022–2025 over due-diligence concerns).

## Citizenship
Direct citizenship by investment (DSP/VCP/CIIP/REO). Naturalisation by residence also available. Dual citizenship permitted since the 2013 constitutional amendment. ^[https://vanuatucitizenshipbyinvestment.org/vanuatu-citizenship-by-investment-program]

## Tax
**No income tax, no capital gains tax, no inheritance tax, no wealth tax** — one of the world's few zero-direct-tax jurisdictions. Citizenship holders who do not reside owe nothing locally.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated nomad visa, but citizenship (via CBI) allows living and working freely. Vanuatu is remote and timezone-shifted from Europe/US, so few digital nomads settle there.

## Yacht / Maritime
Vanuatu is one of the world's largest open ship registries (Vanuatu International Shipping Registry) — popular flag-of-convenience for commercial vessels and yachts.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Vanuatu DSP is open to Russians and historically popular with RU applicants for a fast, cheap second passport; some agents accept payment in crypto. Note: the passport gives no Schengen access (EU suspended Vanuatu's visa-waiver 2022; partial reinstatement discussions ongoing into 2026).

## Community Pulse
Long regarded as the cheapest and fastest "Plan B" passport. The EU's 2022 full suspension of the Vanuatu visa-waiver (over weak due diligence) sharply reduced its value for Europe-bound applicants and is the dominant community complaint. Russia, China, Hong Kong, Singapore access remain the main draws.

## Country Card

### General description
Vanuatu is a South Pacific archipelago nation east of Australia, economically small and heavily reliant on tourism, agriculture, and its citizenship-by-investment and ship-registry revenue. It is best known in the CBI world for the fastest and one of the cheapest second passports available.

### Well-known strong points
- Fastest CBI globally (~30–60 days)
- Lowest single-applicant donation ($130,000) of any passport programme
- Zero income/capital-gains/inheritance tax
- No residence requirement; dual citizenship permitted
- Large open ship registry (yacht/commercial flag)

### Well-known problems
- Weak passport (86 destinations; no Schengen/UK/US)
- EU suspended Vanuatu's visa-waiver (2022) over due-diligence concerns
- Remote location, high disaster exposure (cyclones, earthquakes)
- Small, low-income economy (GDP PPP ~$3,200)
