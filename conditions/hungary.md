## Overview
Hungary is an EU/Schengen/NATO member in Central Europe with Budapest as a major tech and expat hub. It offers the **Guest Investor Programme** (Golden Visa, from €250,000), the **White Card** (digital nomad, €3,000/mo), and standard work/Blue Card routes. Citizenship is one of the EU's longest paths: 11 years total (3yr TR + 8yr PR). Hungary permits dual citizenship. ^[https://oif.gov.hu/]

## Per-Program Detail

### Investment Residency — Guest Investor Programme (Golden Visa)
- **Official name**: Vendégbefektetői tartózkodási engedély (Guest Investor Residence Permit)
- **Conditions**: Three investment options: ^[https://oif.gov.hu/factsheets/residence-permit-for-guest-investor]
  1. **€250,000**: acquisition of investment fund shares in a real estate fund registered by the Hungarian National Bank
  2. **€500,000**: purchase of residential real estate in Hungary
  3. **€1,000,000**: charitable donation to a Hungarian public trust
  Note: Direct real estate option was cancelled before 2025; currently two options (fund or donation). Source of funds must be documented. Clean criminal record. ^[https://www.ibanet.org/the-latest-updates-to-the-Guest-Investor-Residency-Programme]
- **Duration / renewal**: Guest Investor Visa valid 6 months → Residence Permit **10 years**, renewable for another 10 years. ^[https://imigrant-hungary.com/en/blog/citizenship-hungary]
- **Path to citizenship**: 11 years total (3yr TR + 8yr PR) → citizenship by naturalization. Golden Visa counts as TR. Hungarian language required.
- **Official source**: https://oif.gov.hu/factsheets/residence-permit-for-guest-investor
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — newer program (launched July 2024), valid pathway but citizenship timeline is long (11 years).

### Digital Nomad Visa — White Card
- **Official name**: Fehér Kártya (White Card)
- **Conditions**: Age ≥18. Remote work for employer registered OUTSIDE Hungary (no Hungarian income, no Hungarian company shareholding). **€3,000/month net income** for preceding 6 months. Health insurance. Accommodation in Hungary (min. 12-month rental). ^[https://imigrant-hungary.com/en/hungary-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable **once only** (max 2 years total). ^[https://lottalingo.com/research/visas/hungary-white-card]
- **Path to citizenship**: Does NOT lead to permanent residence. White Card time does NOT count toward citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://oif.gov.hu/factsheets/white-card
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — limited to 2 years total, no PR path, family not included. Good as a temporary solution.

### Highly Skilled Employment — EU Blue Card
- **Official name**: Kék Kártya EU
- **Conditions**: University degree. Job offer in Hungary. Salary ≥ threshold (set annually).
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 4 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years → PR → 8 years PR → citizenship (11 years total).
- **Official source**: https://oif.gov.hu/factsheets/eu-blue-card
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard EU Blue Card route.

## Citizenship
- **Standard naturalization**: **11 years total** — 3 years temporary residence + 8 years permanent residence ^[https://imigrant-hungary.com/en/blog/citizenship-hungary]
- **Refugees**: 3 years residence
- **Stateless persons / children of Hungarian citizens**: 5 years
- **Language**: Hungarian proficiency (demanding — Finno-Ugric language, unrelated to Indo-European)
- **Constitutional studies exam** required
- Dual citizenship: **permitted** ^[https://imigrant-hungary.com/en/blog/citizenship-hungary]
- No citizenship-by-investment program

## Tax
- Flat income tax: **15%** (one of EU's lowest)
- Social contribution: 18.5% (capped)
- Corporate tax: **9%** (lowest in EU)
- IP income: 50% deduction for qualifying R&D
- Tax residency: 183 days/year or center of vital interests

## IT / Digital Nomad
The White Card (see above) is the dedicated digital nomad route — €3,000/mo net, 1 year renewable once. No path to permanent residence from White Card. Budapest is a thriving tech hub with major companies and a growing startup scene. For long-term IT professionals, the Guest Investor Programme (€250K) or EU Blue Card are better pathways.

## Yacht / Maritime
Not applicable — Hungary is landlocked.

## Notes for RU Citizens
The Guest Investor Programme (Golden Visa, €250K) is formally open to Russian nationals but faces enhanced source-of-funds scrutiny. The White Card (€3,000/mo) is also accessible. Hungarian language is one of Europe's hardest for Russian speakers. Budapest has a small but established Russian-speaking community.

## Country Card

Hungary is an EU/Schengen/NATO member in Central Europe, with Budapest as a major tech and expat hub. It offers the Guest Investor Programme (Golden Visa) from €250,000 and the White Card digital nomad permit (€3,000/mo net). Hungary imposes the EU's lowest personal income tax (15% flat) and corporate tax (9%), making it highly tax-competitive. However, its citizenship path is the EU's longest at 11 years, and it is the only EU country designated Partly Free by Freedom House (65/100). Hungarian is a Finno-Ugric language — one of Europe's hardest for outsiders. The rule of law has been under sustained EU scrutiny, with €1 billion in EU funding frozen over rule-of-law breaches.

### Well-known strong points
- EU's lowest flat income tax (15%) and corporate tax (9%)
- Guest Investor Programme (Golden Visa) from €250,000 — affordable EU investor residence
- Budapest is a major tech and startup hub with a thriving expat scene
- Low cost of living (63% below NYC); Budapest rent among EU's cheapest for a capital
- Dual citizenship permitted
- Strong passport (186 visa-free destinations)

### Well-known problems
- Longest citizenship path in the EU: 11 years (3yr TR + 8yr PR)
- Hungarian language (Finno-Ugric) — extremely difficult for Indo-European speakers
- Only 'Partly Free' EU country (Freedom House 65/100); rule-of-law index lowest among EU peers (0.51)
- White Card limited to 2 years total; no path to permanent residence or citizenship
- €1 billion in EU funding frozen over rule-of-law breaches; political risk for long-term residents
- Landlocked — no coastline
