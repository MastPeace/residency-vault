## Overview
France is the EU's second-largest economy with one of the world's strongest passports. Its flagship immigration instrument is the **Talent Passport** (Passeport Talent — officially a multi-year "Talent" residence permit), covering highly skilled workers, EU Blue Card holders, investors, researchers, and artists. France has no dedicated digital nomad visa — remote work under the Visitor visa (VLS-TS Visiteur) was confirmed banned in April 2026. Citizenship is available after 5 years of residence with B1 French; dual citizenship is permitted. ^[https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F16922?lang=en]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Passeport Talent — Investisseur Économique (Talent Passport — Economic Investor)
- **Conditions**: Direct investment ≥ €300,000 in a French business (equity or loan); must personally hold ≥ 30% of capital or be the managing director; project must create or safeguard jobs. No real estate or passive investment. ^[https://welcome.businessfrance.fr/en/setting-up-a-business/recruiting-international-talent/visas-and-residence-permits/status-of-foreign-company-directors/talent-economic-investor-status]
- **Duration / renewal**: 4-year permit, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence; B1 French; dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (high bar: active management + job creation required; suitable only for genuine business investors).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: VLS-TS Visiteur (Long-Stay Visitor Visa)
- **Conditions**: Passive income ≥ SMIC gross annual (€22,404.20/yr in 2026, ~€1,843/mo). Private health insurance. Signed undertaking not to engage in ANY professional activity in France. Remote work is explicitly prohibited (confirmed April 2026). ^[https://remoteworkeurope.eu/news/2026/france-bans-remote-work-on-visitor-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable annually. Permanent residence after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; B1 French; dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/sejour-touristique-de-plus-de-3-mois
- **Community verdict**: Works well (low income threshold but zero work tolerance; remote workers must avoid this route).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: France does not offer a digital nomad visa. The VLS-TS Visiteur explicitly bans remote work as of April 2026. Alternative: Talent Passport — Profession Artistique et Culturelle (for independent creative professionals) or French Tech Visa for startup founders. ^[https://remoteworkeurope.eu/news/2026/france-bans-remote-work-on-visitor-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: N/A.
- **Path to citizenship**: N/A.
- **Official source**: https://france-visas.gouv.fr/
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (lack of DN route is a major gap; alternatives require employer/sponsorship or business setup).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Passeport Talent — Salarié Qualifié / Carte Bleue Européenne (Talent Passport — Qualified Employee / EU Blue Card)
- **Conditions**: University degree (Bac+3 or higher) or 5 years professional experience; employment contract ≥ 6 months; salary ≥ 1.5× SMIC for EU Blue Card (≈€33,600/yr). French Tech Visa variant for employees of certified innovative companies. ^[https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F16922?lang=en]
- **Duration / renewal**: 4 years (Talent Passport); EU Blue Card: duration of contract + 3 months (max 4 years). Permanent residence after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; B1 French; dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (fast processing, spouse gets immediate work rights; French Tech Visa variant is excellent).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: French Tech Visa for Founders / Passeport Talent — Projet Innovant
- **Conditions**: Innovative startup project recognized by French Tech ecosystem (incubator, accelerator, or public body). Financial means ≥ SMIC (~€22,404/yr). French Tech Visa for Investors: €300,000+ direct investment in a French startup. ^[https://welcome.businessfrance.fr/en/setting-up-a-business/recruiting-international-talent/visas-and-residence-permits/status-of-foreign-company-directors/french-tech-visa-for-founders]
- **Duration / renewal**: 4 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; B1 French; dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://welcome.businessfrance.fr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (French Tech ecosystem is mature; incubator endorsement is key enabler).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Carte de Séjour — Salarié (Standard Employee Residence Permit)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from French employer; labor market test (employer proves no suitable EU candidate); work permit from DIRECCTE.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable. Multi-year (2–4 years) after first renewal.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; B1 French; dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (labor market test is hurdle; Talent Passport is vastly preferred).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: VLS-TS Étudiant
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized French institution; financial means ≥ €615/mo; health insurance.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student years count toward 5-year residence if subsequently employed. Reduced to 2 years for graduates of French higher education (master's+).
- **Official source**: https://france-visas.gouv.fr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Regroupement Familial / Talent — Famille Accompagnante
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit ≥ 18 months; income ≥ SMIC; adequate accommodation. Talent Passport family: simplified procedure, immediate work rights for spouse.
- **Duration / renewal**: Aligned with sponsor (1–4 years).
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; B1 French; dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (Talent Passport family route is particularly smooth).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: VLS-TS Visiteur (retirement route) — same program as passive_income_residency
- **Conditions**: Pension/passive income ≥ ~€1,843/mo (SMIC gross annual); private health insurance; no work permitted.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable. Permanent residence after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years; B1 French; dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://france-visas.gouv.fr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (popular retirement destination with affordable threshold).

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. France does not offer direct citizenship by investment.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 5 years continuous legal residence (standard); 2 years for graduates of French higher education (master's+); 4 years if married to French citizen (after marriage + residence). ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/french-citizenship]
- **Language**: B1 French (CEFR); raised from A2 as of 1 January 2026.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted.
- **Processing**: 12–18 months in practice.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive 0%–45% (2026 bands: 0% up to €11,497; 11% to €29,315; 30% to €83,523; 41% to €178,145; 45% above). Social charges (CSG/CRDS) add ~9.2% on certain income.
- **Impatriate regime**: 30% exemption on total remuneration (or actual additional costs) for up to 8 years for employees recruited abroad by French companies.
- **Wealth tax**: Impôt sur la Fortune Immobilière (IFI) on real estate assets > €1.3M.
- **No flat-tax regime** comparable to Italian €300K or Spanish Beckham Law. France is a high-tax jurisdiction.

## IT / Digital Nomad
France does NOT offer a digital nomad visa, and remote work under the VLS-TS Visiteur is explicitly banned (confirmed April 2026). IT professionals should use:
- **Talent Passport — Qualified Employee**: if hired by a French employer.
- **French Tech Visa**: for startup founders, investors, or employees of certified innovative companies.
- **Profession Artistique et Culturelle**: for independent creative/artistic professionals (some IT freelancers quality).
Paris, Lyon, and Toulouse are major tech hubs. La French Tech ecosystem is world-class. ^[https://remoteworkeurope.eu/news/2026/france-bans-remote-work-on-visitor-visa]

## Yacht / Maritime
France has extensive Mediterranean and Atlantic coastlines (French Riviera, Corsica, Brittany, Normandy). Yacht registration under the French flag (Registre International Français — RIF) is available with favorable tax and crewing conditions. Major yachting hubs: Antibes, Cannes, Monaco (via French entry), Saint-Tropez, La Rochelle. Captain licensing: French-issued credentials recognized under international conventions.

## Notes for RU Citizens
French consulates in Russia may have reduced operations. The VLS-TS Visiteur route is accessible for passive-income holders, but remote work is banned. Talent Passport routes require either a French employer or business investment — feasible but higher barrier. The 5-year citizenship path is among the shortest in Europe, and B1 French is moderate. France has a significant Russian-speaking community, particularly on the Côte d'Azur and in Paris. No official restrictions beyond EU sanctions. Dual citizenship permitted.

## Community Pulse
France's lack of a digital nomad visa is a notable gap compared to Spain, Portugal, and Italy. The confirmed remote-work ban under VLS-TS Visiteur (April 2026) closed a widely used loophole. Talent Passport is excellent for highly skilled employees and entrepreneurs — fast processing (90 days max, 30 for intra-EU Blue Card holders), spouse gets immediate work rights. The 5-year citizenship path is competitive. France remains attractive for retirees on passive income (low threshold, ~€1,843/mo).
