## Overview
Honduras offers two income-based permanent residence routes: the Rentista (US$2,500/month) and the Pensionado (US$1,500/month pension). Both grant PERMANENT residence directly and either counts toward naturalization after 3–5 years. Honduras has a territorial tax system (no tax on foreign income). Residency is administered by the Secretaría de Gobernación, Justicia y Descentralización, with presence requirements minimal (1 visit per year to maintain). Processing takes 3–6 months. ^[https://mylatinlife.com/honduras-rentista-pensionado-visas/]

## Per-Program Detail

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia de Rentista (permanent residence by investment of rentier income)
- **Conditions**: Stable, ongoing income of US$2,500/month from any source (investments, rental, dividends, remote salary, business distributions). Income may come from abroad or within Honduras. Must document 12 months consistency. ^[https://mylatinlife.com/honduras-rentista-pensionado-visas/]
- **Duration / renewal**: PERMANENT residence directly (recognized under Immigration and Foreigners Law rentier category). Maintain by visiting Honduras at least once per year (1 day sufficient). Absence over 12 consecutive months breaks residency.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years continuous legal residence (3 years for Central Americans and Spaniards; 1 year for spouses of Hondurans). ^[https://mylatinlife.com/honduras-citizenship-naturalization]
- **Official source**: https://inm.gob.hn (Instituto Nacional de Migración)
- **Community verdict**: Works well (permanent residence directly; ~$2.5K threshold is moderate; minimal presence; popular with expats seeking territorial tax + low-key lifestyle).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia de Pensionado (retiree permanent residence)
- **Conditions**: Pension income of US$1,500/month from a pension source only (state pension, corporate pension, annuity). Age not required, but income must be pension-type. ^[https://mylatinlife.com/honduras-rentista-pensionado-visas/]
- **Duration / renewal**: PERMANENT residence directly. Same minimal presence (1 visit/yr) requirement.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years (3 for Central Americans/Spaniards; 1 for spouses).
- **Official source**: https://inm.gob.hn
- **Community verdict**: Works well (lower threshold than rentista; permanent from day one; attractive for retirees; regular pension deposits required).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work permit / temporary residence (worker category)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Honduran employer. Employer must demonstrate compliance with Article 11 Labor Code: 90% of workforce must be Honduran nationals. ^[https://garciabodan.com/en/honduras-eases-immigration-requirements-with-new-decree]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable (up to 5 years per Ministerial Agreement No. 374-2025).
- **Path to citizenship**: Temporary residence years may count after converting to permanent; 3 years total temp+perm → permanent; then naturalization.
- **Official source**: https://inm.gob.hn
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (90% local staff rule is restrictive; sponsorship mostly for specialized/technical roles).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Investor residence (Inversionista)
- **Conditions**: Minimum US$50,000 investment in a Honduran business. ^[https://thelatinvestor.com/blogs/news/honduras-expat-guide]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent-path residency.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years (3 Central American/Spanish; 1 spouse).
- **Official source**: https://inm.gob.hn
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (low threshold of $50K but operational complexity in practice).

## Citizenship
- **By naturalization**: 5 years continuous legal residence for most foreign nationals; 3 years for citizens of other Central American countries + Spain; 1 year for spouses of Honduran citizens. ^[https://mylatinlife.com/honduras-citizenship-naturalization]
  - Requirements: clean record (Honduras + home country police clearance), basic Spanish + Honduran history/culture knowledge (interview-based, no standardized test), continuous presence expected.
- **By marriage**: 1 year after being legally married to Honduran.
- **Dual citizenship**: Recognized (naturalized citizens may retain original nationality).

## Tax
- Territorial tax system: only Honduras-source income taxed. Foreign income (including all rentista/pensionado foreign income) exempt.
- Personal income tax: progressive up to 25% on Honduras-source income above L 400,000 (~US$16,000).
- No capital gains tax (for individuals). No inheritance tax. No wealth tax.
- No digital nomad tax regime.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Not available — no dedicated digital nomad visa. Remote workers with foreign income often qualify for Rentista (if income ≥$2.5K/mo) or operate informally via tourist visas (90 days + 30-day extension). Honduras does not formally forbid or tax remote foreign work within the territory, but no specific framework exists. ^[https://thelatinvestor.com/blogs/news/honduras-expat-guide]

## Yacht / Maritime
Honduras has an international ship registry. Roatán (Bay Islands) is a modest Caribbean sailing destination. Yacht registration possible. No dedicated yacht-residence programme.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian nationals require a visa to enter Honduras (no visa-free). 
- Rentista/Pensionado routes technically open but less commonly used by RU expats (smaller RU community than DR/Costa Rica).
- Spanish useful for day-to-day life and citizenship interview; Bay Islands (Roatán, Útila) are English-speaking pockets.
- Territorial tax benefit attractive for those with offshore passive income.

## Community pulse
- Rentista and Pensionado are the headline programmes: permanent residence directly, low presence requirements, territorial tax.
- Honduras has a reputation for bureaucratic inconsistency — using a local attorney recommended.
- Healthcare limited outside Tegucigalpa/San Pedro Sula; expats cluster in Bay Islands.
- October 2025 decree (No. 374-2025) created a "special category" and eased criteria for certain applicants (50% of standard income threshold).
