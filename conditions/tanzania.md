## Overview
Tanzania offers two main residence permit classes: Class A (investors/self-employed) and Class B (employees). The Tanzania Investment Centre (TIC) facilitates investor permits. There is no dedicated digital nomad visa, no retirement visa, and no citizenship-by-investment programme. Citizenship is by presidential grant only — extremely rare for foreigners. Dual citizenship is not permitted. ^[https://www.immigration.go.tz/index.php/residence-permit-class-a]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency — Residence Permit Class A
- **Official name**: Residence Permit Class A
- **Conditions**: Registered company in Tanzania. Through TIC: minimum capital USD 100,000 (varies by sector). Non-TIC route: bank proof of assets valued at USD 300,000+ required. Certificate of incorporation, TIN, tax clearance, business license, lease/proof of occupancy, employment contracts for local employees. Physical verification visit may be required. ^[https://www.us.tzembassy.go.tz/services/residence-and-work-permits-class-a-b-and-c]
- **Duration**: 2 years, renewable. Fee: USD 2,000–3,050 (varies by source/route), plus 10% facilitation fee to TIC/TISEZA. EAC citizens pay USD 1,550. Diaspora investors: USD 1,000. ^[https://www.tic.go.tz/pages/resident-permit]
- **Path to citizenship**: None practical — citizenship is by presidential grant only. Naturalisation is NOT a standard right. Dual citizenship is not permitted. ^[https://www.immigration.go.tz]
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.tz; https://www.tic.go.tz
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — permits are obtainable but bureaucracy is significant. Zanzibar has a separate immigration system.

### employment_residency — Residence Permit Class B
- **Official name**: Residence Permit Class B
- **Conditions**: Job offer. Employer sponsorship. Qualifications in fields not available locally. Online application via OWAIS system (kazi.go.tz). ^[https://www.us.tzembassy.go.tz/services/residence-and-work-permits-class-a-b-and-c]
- **Duration**: 2 years, renewable. Fee: USD 2,050 (EAC citizens: USD 550). ^[https://www.tic.go.tz/pages/resident-permit]
- **Path to citizenship**: None practical.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.tz; https://www.kazi.go.tz
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — functional but processing times vary. Employer sponsorship is mandatory.

## Citizenship
- By presidential grant only (naturalisation is not a right).
- Requirements: generally 10+ years continuous residence, good character, Swahili/English proficiency, renunciation of original citizenship.
- Dual citizenship: NOT permitted. ^[https://www.immigration.go.tz]
- Passport: visa-free access to ~70 destinations.
- **Honest assessment**: Tanzania is NOT a citizenship-by-residence destination. Even long-term residents rarely obtain citizenship.

## Tax
- Residence-based taxation. Tax resident if >183 days or permanent home in Tanzania.
- Personal income tax: progressive 9%–30% (top rate on TZS 8,400,000+/month).
- Corporate tax: 30%.
- VAT: 18%.
- Zanzibar has separate tax treatment in some respects.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated DN visa. Dar es Salaam is the commercial hub; Arusha is the safari/tourism centre; Zanzibar (Stone Town) is popular for remote work lifestyle. Internet: 4G/LTE widely available, fibre in urban areas. Co-working spaces in Dar (Regus, Colab). Cost of living: low-moderate. Remote workers typically enter on tourist visas (90 days, extendable) — this is a grey area, not formally authorised for remote work.

## Yacht / Maritime
Tanzania has an Indian Ocean coastline (Dar es Salaam, Zanzibar). Zanzibar is a popular cruising destination. The Tanzania Shipping Agencies Corporation (TASAC) handles vessel registration. Dar es Salaam Yacht Club and Zanzibar Yacht Club exist. Limited superyacht infrastructure.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa on arrival or eVisa for Russian citizens (90 days tourist). Tanzania is a popular tourist destination for Russians (Zanzibar). Banking: functional but limited. Direct flights from Moscow (seasonal charters). Tanzania maintains neutral relations with Russia.

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-19
- **Recent changes**: Online Work Permit Application System (OWAIS) integrated with e-Permit system. TISEZA (Tanzania Investment Special Economic Zone Authority) facilitating permits. No major policy shifts.
- **Watch items**: Monitor any introduction of DN visa (regional trend).

## Country Card

The United Republic of Tanzania, formed by the union of Tanganyika and Zanzibar, is East Africa's largest country by land area and one of its most politically stable. It's a premier safari destination (Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Kilimanjaro) with a long Indian Ocean coastline including the semi-autonomous Zanzibar archipelago. Dar es Salaam is the commercial hub. The immigration system is functional but citizenship is virtually closed to foreigners.

### Well-known strong points
- Political stability — one of Africa's most peaceful nations; regular peaceful transitions of power
- World-class safari tourism and Zanzibar's cultural appeal
- Swahili is a unifying national language spoken across the population
- Low cost of living by global standards
- Class A investor permits available (USD 100,000+ via TIC)
- Zanzibar operates a separate immigration regime, offering additional flexibility

### Well-known problems
- Citizenship is by presidential grant only — effectively closed to foreigners; dual citizenship not permitted
- No digital nomad visa, no retirement visa — limited residency options
- Bureaucracy can be slow and opaque; Zanzibar's separate system adds complexity
- Personal income tax up to 30% for high earners
- Internet connectivity and infrastructure are improving but still below East African leaders
- Limited pathway for long-term residence beyond employer-sponsored or investor permits
