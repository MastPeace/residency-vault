## Overview
Indonesia has rapidly built out its residence offering: the Golden Visa (5–10 year residence-by-investment, launched July 2024), the Second Home Visa (non-working long-stay, IDR 2 billion deposit), a retirement KITAS, and standard work KITAS. It is one of Southeast Asia's most popular bases for remote workers and retirees, especially Bali. Naturalization requires 5 years of continuous residence (10 non-continuous) and renunciation of prior nationality — dual citizenship is not recognized for adults. ^[https://www.imigrasi.go.id/]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Golden Visa (Visa Emas)
- **Conditions**: Individual investor — US$350,000 (5 yr) or US$700,000 (10 yr) in government bonds, listed shares, or mutual funds; or a flat/apartment ≥ US$1,000,000. Company founder (PT PMA) — US$2.5M (5 yr) / US$5M (10 yr). Corporate — US$25M/50M. ^[https://kemlu.go.id/files-service/storage/childmenu/additional_file/1729524087671671775b15a_Indonesian_Golden_Visa.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 or 10 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years continuous residence (10 non-continuous).
- **Official source**: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/
- **Community verdict**: Works well — new but growing; ~1,274 visas issued by mid-2026.

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Second Home Visa (index E33 / E33F)
- **Conditions**: Non-working long-stay; IDR 2,000,000,000 (~US$125,000) deposit in a state-owned bank OR qualifying property. No age or pension requirement. ^[https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 or 10 years; no local sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years continuous residence.
- **Official source**: https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for high-net-worth long-stayers; no work rights.

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Remote Worker Visa (index E33G)
- **Conditions**: Proof of employment/income from foreign sources.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year.
- **Official source**: https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — exists but many remote workers still use the Second Home or tourism/B211A routes; enforcement of local-work rules is uneven.

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Work KITAS / Golden Visa (global talent category)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Indonesian employer; specialist skills/qualifications.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–5 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Investor KITAS (E28 series)
- **Conditions**: Qualifying investment in an Indonesian (PMA) company with defined ownership stake.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–5 years (Golden Visa route 5–10).
- **Official source**: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work KITAS (ITAS)
- **Conditions**: Job offer; employer obtains work permit (RPTKA) and KITAS.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable; KITAP (permanent) after 5 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard route, employer-heavy process.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student KITAS
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in accredited institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Official source**: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Family/dependant KITAS
- **Conditions**: Spouse/child of KITAS or KITAP holder.
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to sponsor.
- **Official source**: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Retirement KITAS (Silver Hair visa)
- **Conditions**: Age 55+; proof of pension/income and accommodation (lease), plus domestic helper if desired. ^[https://www.imigrasi.go.id/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable up to 5.
- **Official source**: https://www.imigrasi.go.id/
- **Community verdict**: Works well — popular in Bali; the Second Home Visa is the richer alternative.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalization**: 5 years continuous residence OR 10 years non-continuous; aged 18+; speak Bahasa Indonesia; accept Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution; no criminal sentence ≥1 year; renounce prior nationality. ^[https://bphn.go.id/data/documents/lawoncitizenship.pdf]
- **Dual citizenship**: Not recognized for adults (children may hold dual until age 18–21, then choose).
- **Timeline**: KITAS → KITAP (after 5 yrs) → naturalization.

## Tax
- **Individual income tax**: Progressive 5%–35% (top rate on income > IDR 5 billion/yr). ^[https://www.pajak.go.id/]
- **Tax residency**: 183 days or intent to reside; residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents 20% withholding on Indonesia-source income.
- **No special nomad regime**; Digital Nomad Visa holders are expected to comply with Indonesian tax rules on locally-sourced income.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Bali (Canggu, Ubud) is one of the world's largest digital-nomad hubs. Formal options: Remote Worker Visa (E33G, 1 yr) and Second Home Visa (E33, 5–10 yr, no work). Note that local work without a work permit is technically prohibited; enforcement is inconsistent. Strong coworking and internet in Bali/Jakarta.

## Yacht / Maritime
World's largest archipelago (~17,000 islands). Major cruising grounds: Raja Ampat, Komodo, Bali. Marinas: Benoa (Bali), Marina Del Rey. Foreign-flagged yachts can obtain a temporary import permit; Indonesian flag registration available. Growing but still-developing yachting infrastructure.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Russian nationals can access the Golden Visa, Second Home, and Retirement visas. Banking for RU nationals in Indonesia is more restricted post-2022. Bali has a large Russian-speaking community. Some RU citizens use the Second Home or Investor KITAS route.

## Community Pulse
Indonesia is a top-3 global nomad/retirement destination. The Golden Visa and Second Home Visa are new and well-received but the Second Home IDR 2bn deposit is a high bar. Consistent community complaints: frequent visa rule changes and uneven enforcement of remote-work rules.

## Country Card

### General description
Indonesia is the world's fourth-most-populous country and largest archipelago, spanning ~17,000 islands between the Indian and Pacific Oceans. It is a major economy and the world's most popular tropical nomad/retirement base (especially Bali), with a fast-expanding residence menu: Golden Visa (5–10 yr), Second Home Visa, Remote Worker Visa, and retirement KITAS — plus a 5-year path to citizenship.

### Well-known strong points
- Golden Visa and Second Home Visa give 5–10 year stays without a local sponsor
- Bali is a world-class digital-nomad and retirement hub
- Low cost of living (Numbeo index ~35)
- Warm climate, rich culture, huge coastline and diving/cruising grounds
- Growing economy and improving infrastructure

### Well-known problems
- No dual citizenship; naturalization requires renunciation and Indonesian language
- Second Home Visa's IDR 2 billion deposit is a high bar for many
- Frequent and sometimes unclear immigration rule changes
- Bureaucracy (KITAS/KITAP processes) and uneven local-work enforcement
- Infrastructure gaps (traffic, flood risk in Jakarta)
