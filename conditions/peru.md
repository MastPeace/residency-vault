## Overview
Peru's immigration authority is the Superintendencia Nacional de Migraciones. The system distinguishes temporary and resident visas. Peru adopted a **digital nomad visa in law** (Legislative Decree 1582, November 2023) but it has **never been implemented** — no TUPA regulations, no fees, no application route as of 2026. Naturalization was significantly tightened by **Law No. 32421** (August 2025), raising the residency requirement from 2 to 5 years and adding a minimum income threshold of 10 UIT (~S/ 55,000 / ~USD 15,000/yr). ^[https://legalclarity.org/how-to-get-peruvian-citizenship-requirements-and-steps]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residente — Inversionista
- **Conditions**: Investment ≥ PEN 500,000 (~USD 150,000) in a new Peruvian company, or ~USD 30,000 in an existing company. Investment must be registered with ProInversión or appropriate agency. ^[https://www.bizlatinhub.com/types-visa-peru]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable. Permanent residency after 3 years of continuous legal residence.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years of continuous legal residency (Law 32421). Dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gob.pe
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (two different thresholds in circulation; USD 30,000 vs PEN 500,000 discrepancy reflects different investment types).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residente — Rentista
- **Conditions**: Passive income ≥ USD 1,000/mo from abroad (pensions, rentals, dividends). Additional USD 500/mo per dependent. Must be deposited into a Peruvian bank account. ^[https://www.expatfocus.com/peru/guide/peru-residency]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years (Law 32421). Was 2 years under prior law.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gob.pe
- **Community verdict**: Works well (low threshold; straightforward documentation).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Nómada Digital (Decreto Legislativo 1582, Art. 29.1(i))
- **Conditions**: **NOT OPERATIONAL**. Created in law November 2023. Both the 2024 and 2025 TUPA updates failed to include the category. No application procedure, fees, or requirements published. ^[https://www.riotimesonline.com/peru-digital-nomad-visa-not-available-2026]
- **Duration / renewal**: On paper: 365 days, renewable. In practice: cannot apply.
- **Path to citizenship**: Unknown (law not implemented).
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gob.pe
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (law exists but visa is a phantom — remote workers use tourist stamps or independent-worker visa instead).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Visa de Residente — Trabajador
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with a Peruvian company, approved by the Ministry of Labor. Contract minimum 1 year. ^[https://www.bizlatinhub.com/types-visa-peru]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years (Law 32421).
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gob.pe
- **Community verdict**: Works well (standard employment route; Ministry of Labor approval required).

### startup_entrepreneur
Subsumed under Inversionista. Business investment from ~USD 30,000 in existing or new Peruvian company.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residente — Trabajador
- **Conditions**: Binding employment contract, approved by Ministry of Labor. Must apply from within Peru (cambio de calidad migratoria) unless already abroad.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gob.pe
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residente — Estudiante
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in a Peruvian educational institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student time counts toward residency requirement.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gob.pe
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Visa de Residente — Familiar
- **Conditions**: Certified family relationship (spouse, minor child, dependent parent) with a Peruvian citizen or resident.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 4 years of marriage + residency (under Law 32421). Previously 2 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gob.pe
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residente — Pensionista / Rentista
- **Conditions**: Lifetime pension ≥ USD 1,000/mo from abroad. Must be deposited in Peruvian bank. ^[https://www.expatfocus.com/peru/guide/peru-residency]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years. Note: retirees may be affected by the new income threshold (10 UIT ≈ USD 15,000/yr) under Law 32421 for naturalization.
- **Official source**: https://www.migraciones.gob.pe
- **Community verdict**: Works well for residency; citizenship path now harder due to income requirement.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. A brief CBI scheme existed in 1992 (USD 25,000) but was abolished after 1 year due to public outcry.

## Citizenship
- **Standard naturalization (Law 32421, August 2025)**: 5 years of continuous legal residency, up from 2 years. ^[https://legalclarity.org/how-to-get-peruvian-citizenship-requirements-and-steps]
- **Marriage-based**: 4 years of marriage to a Peruvian citizen + legal residency (doubled from 2).
- **Income threshold**: Annual income ≥ 10 UIT (~S/ 55,000 / ~USD 15,000 in 2026), from lawful activities declared to SUNAT.
- **Language**: Spanish or indigenous language proficiency. Tests on history, geography, and civics.
- **Processing time**: Up to 18 months, final decision by the President.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted. No renunciation required.
- **Passport**: ~150+ visa-free destinations, including Schengen.

## Tax
- **Residency trigger**: 183 days in Peru in any 12-month period.
- **Tax system**: Worldwide income. Progressive rates 0%–30%. ^[https://www.expatfocus.com/peru/guide/peru-residency]
- **Territorial option**: None — Peru taxes worldwide income of tax residents.
- **Digital nomads**: Staying 183+ days triggers tax residency.

## IT / Digital Nomad
- **Digital nomad visa is a phantom**: law exists (DL 1582) but cannot be applied for. Remote workers use (a) tourist entry — up to 183 days/year, no work rights; (b) Trabajador Independiente visa — for freelancers with documented income. ^[https://www.riotimesonline.com/peru-digital-nomad-visa-not-available-2026]
- Lima has a growing coworking scene and reasonable internet. Cusco/Arequipa popular for shorter stays on tourist stamps.

## Yacht / Maritime
Not a major yachting hub. No special yacht/captain residency program.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian citizens: visa-free for tourism up to 90 days (since 2011 bilateral agreement). However, RU nationals can enter visa-free only for tourism — residency applications require consular processing.
- Banking: possible with Carné de Extranjería; post-2022 compliance checks for RU nationals are standard.
- No specific restrictions targeting Russian applicants for residency.
- Residency path is straightforward but the new citizenship income threshold (10 UIT) may be a barrier for retirees.

## Community pulse
- **Overall**: Peru was one of the fastest citizenship paths in South America (2 years) — that ended with Law 32421. Now 5 years, in line with regional average.
- **Processing**: Residency ~30–60 days for Rentista, longer for Investor. Citizenship up to 18 months.
- **Physical presence**: 183 days/year required to maintain residency status.
- **Cost**: Change of status fee ~PEN 163 (~USD 45).

## Country Card

### General description
Peru is South America's fourth-most populous country, stretching from the Pacific coast through the Andes to the Amazon basin. It is best known for Machu Picchu and Inca heritage, but also has a growing economy driven by mining, agriculture, and tourism. The Rentista visa ($1,000/mo passive income) and Investor visa (~$150,000) are the main residency routes. The digital nomad visa, created by law in November 2023, has never been implemented — a persistent frustration. Peru significantly tightened citizenship requirements in August 2025 (Law 32421), raising the residency requirement from 2 to 5 years and adding an income threshold.

### Well-known strong points
- Rentista visa has one of the lowest income thresholds in the region ($1,000/mo)
- Rich cultural and archaeological heritage (Machu Picchu, Nazca Lines, 12 UNESCO sites)
- Low cost of living (Numbeo index 28.5) — Lima is affordable, provinces cheaper
- Growing gastronomy scene — Lima is a global culinary capital
- MERCOSUR associate member; ~143 visa-free destinations
- Foreigners can own real estate with no restrictions (except within 50 km of borders)

### Well-known problems
- Digital nomad visa is a "phantom" — law exists but cannot be applied for (2+ years unimplemented)
- Citizenship requirement doubled from 2 to 5 years (Law 32421, Aug 2025) + income threshold of ~$15,000/yr
- Political instability — 6 presidents in 5 years (2018–2023); protests and unrest frequent
- Lima's winter grey (garúa): June–October brings persistent coastal fog, minimal sunshine
- Bureaucratic inefficiency — Migraciones processes are slow and opaque
- High crime rates in Lima and major cities; petty theft and express kidnappings are common
