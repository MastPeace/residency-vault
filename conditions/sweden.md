## Overview
Sweden is an EU/Schengen member with a strong passport and a world-class tech ecosystem (Spotify, Klarna, Ericsson). Immigration is managed by the Swedish Migration Agency (Migrationsverket). Sweden offers a self-employed residence route but no golden visa or dedicated digital nomad visa. In June 2026, Sweden enacted stricter citizenship rules: language requirement, civic knowledge test, self-sufficiency, and longer residence periods. Permanent residency typically after 4 years of work-based residence. ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English]

## Per-Program Detail

### Highly Skilled Employment
- **EU Blue Card / Work permit** — for qualified professionals
- **Conditions**: higher education degree (180 ECTS credits minimum) or 5+ years relevant professional experience; binding job offer from Swedish employer; salary ≥SEK 39,900/month (~€3,450/mo, 2026 threshold); employment terms per Swedish collective agreements; employer provides insurance (health, life, occupational injury, pension). ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Working-in-Sweden/Employed.html]
- **EU Blue Card specific**: higher education degree required; salary ≥1.5× average gross salary (~SEK 57,000/mo in 2026)
- **Duration**: max 2 years initial, renewable
- **Path to permanent residency**: after 4 years of work-based residence permits within 7 years
- **Path to citizenship**: 8 years of habitual residence (new 2026 rules)
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Working-in-Sweden/Employed.html]
- **Community verdict**: Works well for qualified professionals; processing times at Migrationsverket can be long

### Startup Entrepreneur
- **Self-employed residence permit** — for entrepreneurs and self-employed persons
- **Conditions**: valid passport; significant experience in the field and previous business ownership; documented knowledge of Swedish and/or English; own ≥50% of the business; business sells goods/services in Sweden; sufficient support funds for first 2 years: SEK **200,000** main applicant + SEK **100,000** per spouse + SEK **50,000** per accompanying child; reliable budget documentation; established customer contacts/network in Sweden; after 2-year probation, business must be financially viable and support the applicant. ^[https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/self-employed-worker-sweden_en]
- **Duration**: 2 years initial (probationary), then permanent possible
- **Path to citizenship**: 8 years
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Working-in-Sweden/Self-employed.html]
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — substantial documentation burden; SEK 200K capital requirement is high; business viability assessment can be unpredictable

### Employment Residency
- **Work permit** — standard employment route for non-EU nationals
- **Conditions**: job offer from Swedish employer; salary ≥SEK 28,000/month (2026 general requirement); employment terms must match Swedish collective agreements or industry standards; employer provides insurance. Labour market test: employer must advertise the position in Sweden/EU for ≥10 days (via Swedish Public Employment Service). ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Working-in-Sweden/Employed.html]
- **Duration**: max 2 years initially (or contract duration), renewable
- **Path to permanent residency**: 4 years of work-based residence
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Working-in-Sweden/Employed.html]
- **Community verdict**: Works well for qualified roles; processing delays are a known issue

### Student Residency
- **Student residence** — for non-EU students
- **Conditions**: acceptance at Swedish university; proof of sufficient funds (SEK 102,684/year for 2026 — 12 months of living costs); tuition for non-EU varies (typically SEK 80,000–295,000/year). ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Studying-in-Sweden.html]
- **Duration**: duration of studies (max 1 year if course <1 year)
- **Path to citizenship**: student years count partially
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Studying-in-Sweden.html]
- **Community verdict**: Works well; high tuition for non-EU; strong university system

### Family Reunification
- **Family reunification** — for nuclear family of legal residents
- **Conditions**: sponsor must hold valid permanent or temporary residence; maintenance requirement (income sufficient to support family — ≥SEK 6,000/month remaining after housing costs for a couple); adequate housing (own/rent). New rules from 1 October 2026: stricter requirements for family ties immigration. ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Moving-to-someone-in-Sweden.html]
- **Duration**: tied to sponsor's permit
- **Path to citizenship**: 8 years (independent application)
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Moving-to-someone-in-Sweden.html]
- **Community verdict**: Works well; new 2026 rules may tighten requirements

## Citizenship
- **Standard**: 8 years of habitual residence in Sweden (new rules from 6 June 2026 — previously 5 years)
- **Shortened**: 3 years if married/cohabiting with Swedish citizen (previously 2 years); 4 years for stateless persons
- **Language**: Swedish language proficiency (level to be specified; expected at least B1)
- **Civic knowledge test**: required (new 2026)
- **Self-sufficiency**: must demonstrate ability to support oneself (new 2026)
- **Good conduct**: enhanced requirements from July 2026 — serious crimes, debts, or security concerns bar naturalisation
- **Dual citizenship**: permitted since 2001
- **EU passport**: yes ^[https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Becoming-a-Swedish-citizen.html]

## Tax
- **Progressive income tax**: municipal tax ~32% (flat per municipality) + state tax 20% on income above SEK 643,100 (~€55,600) in 2026. Top effective rate ~52% (municipal + 20% state on income above ~SEK 643,100; additional 5% above ~SEK 733,300).
- **Expert tax relief**: 25% of gross salary tax-free for foreign experts, researchers, and key personnel for first 7 years. Conditions: recruited from abroad; salary ≥SEK 114,000/month (for tax year 2026); employer applies to Taxation of Research Workers Board (Forskarskattenämnden). ^[https://www.bdo.se/sv-se/artiklar-insikter/sweden%E2%80%99s-expert-tax-regime-current-rules,-proposed-changes-and-practical-implications]
- **Corporate tax**: 20.6%.
- **VAT**: 25% standard.
- **No wealth tax** (abolished 2007).

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated [[digital-nomad]] visa. Sweden is one of Europe's strongest tech ecosystems. IT professionals use:
- Work permit (employer-sponsored); very active startup/scale-up scene (Stockholm = "unicorn factory")
- Self-employed route for freelance IT consultants
- Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö are major tech/startup hubs. Remote work from Sweden for foreign employers generally not permitted under a work permit unless the employer is Swedish.

## Yacht / Maritime
Sweden has one of the world's longest coastlines with extensive archipelagos (Stockholm archipelago has 30,000+ islands). Yacht registration under Swedish flag available. Major yachting hubs: Stockholm, Gothenburg, Strömstad, Marstrand. Strong sailing tradition and recognised maritime licensing.

## Notes for RU citizens
Sweden is among the stricter EU states for Russian applicants. Full EU sanctions apply. The June 2026 citizenship tightening (8 years, language, civic test, self-sufficiency) makes long-term planning difficult. The self-employed route is accessible in principle but SEK 200K capital and the "customer network in Sweden" requirement are substantial barriers. Swedish consulates in Russia operate at reduced capacity. No meaningful Russian-speaking community.

## Country Card

Sweden is the largest Nordic country by population and economy, with a world-class tech ecosystem that has produced global companies like Spotify, Klarna, and Ericsson. It offers a high standard of living, strong social protections, and progressive values, but its immigration system tightened significantly in 2026: citizenship now requires 8 years of habitual residence, a language test, and a civic knowledge exam. No golden visa, passive-income route, or digital nomad visa exists — entry is almost entirely employment-based. Stockholm is a major European tech hub, and the self-employed route provides a narrow path for entrepreneurs with Swedish business ties.

### Well-known strong points
- World-class tech ecosystem — Stockholm produces more unicorns per capita than any region outside Silicon Valley
- High quality of life, excellent public services, and strong social safety net
- Expert tax relief: 25% of salary tax-free for foreign experts (up to 7 years)
- Strong worker protections, generous parental leave (480 days), and work-life balance
- Clean environment, vast nature access, and extensive archipelagos
- Dual citizenship permitted since 2001

### Well-known problems
- Citizenship tightened to 8 years (2026) + language test + civic exam — a moving target for long-term planners
- High personal tax burden (~32% municipal + 20% state on upper incomes)
- No digital nomad, passive-income, or golden visa routes — limited entry options
- SEK 200,000 capital requirement for self-employed route + customer-network-in-Sweden test
- Housing shortage in Stockholm and major cities with long rental queues
- Long, dark winters with limited daylight in much of the country
