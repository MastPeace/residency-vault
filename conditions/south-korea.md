## Overview
South Korea has a strong passport (top 3 in many rankings) and a structured immigration system with several residence pathways: the D-8 investor visa, E-7 skilled worker visa, F-2 points-based residence, and F-5 permanent residence. Korea also formally launched its Digital Nomad (Workation) visa (F-1-D) on June 30, 2026, with eased income requirements. Korea's super-aged society (21.2% over 65) is driving pro-immigration policy. Dual citizenship is restricted. ^[https://workvisa.guide/country/south-korea]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: D-8 Corporate/Investor Visa (기업투자)
- **Conditions**: Minimum investment of KRW 100,000,000 (approx. USD $75,000) in a Korean business. Higher investment tiers: F-2-8 real estate investment, F-2-12 public business investment deposit (principal-guaranteed, via Korea Development Bank). D-8 investors qualify for F-5 permanent residence after investing KRW 500,000,000+ and creating 5+ Korean jobs. ^[https://lottalingo.com/research/how-to-move-to-south-korea]
- **Duration / renewal**: D-8: 2 years, renewable; F-2: 3 years renewable; F-5: permanent (10-year renewal cycle).
- **Path to citizenship**: F-5 PR after 5 years (or via investment criteria); citizenship after 5 years residence + TOPIK 4 + renunciation of prior nationality.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.kr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (low entry threshold vs. EU Golden Visas; clear F-5 upgrade path via KRW 500M + jobs).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No passive income residence. F-2-8 (real estate investment) and F-2-12 (public fund deposit) require significant capital but are investment-based, not passive-income-based.
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (no passive income route; investment routes exist but require active capital placement).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Digital Nomad (Workation) Visa — F-1-D
- **Conditions**: Age 18+; employed by a foreign company (or own a foreign business) for 1+ year; work remotely for foreign employer only (no Korean employer). Income: 2× per-capita GNI standard (pilot rule); from June 30, 2026, eased to 1–2× GNI for under-34s and non-metropolitan residents (GNI per capita ~KRW 52.41M in 2025 → 2× = ~KRW 104.8M; 1× for eligible under-34 outside Seoul = ~KRW 52.4M). Medical insurance ≥ USD $75,000. Spouses and children can accompany. ^[https://eiglaw.com/south-korea-officially-launches-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable up to max 3 years (extended from 2 years).
- **Path to citizenship**: No direct path; must transition to a work/investor visa for longer-term residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.kr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (formally permanent since June 2026; eased income tiers; no direct PR path).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: E-7 Specially Designated Activities (특정활동) / E-7-1 points-based
- **Conditions**: E-7 covers 80+ designated professional occupations (IT, engineering, natural sciences, design). E-7-1 is a points-based variant rewarding qualifications, experience, Korean ability. Salary: no fixed minimum (varies by occupation). E-7 holders can progress to F-2 (points) and F-5 PR. ^[https://workvisa.guide/country/south-korea]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2–3 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: F-5 PR after 5 years continuous residence; citizenship after 5 years + TOPIK 4.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.kr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (E-7-4 points track is the realistic path for skilled trades; F-5 endpoint reachable in ~5 years).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: D-8-4 Technology Startup Visa (OASIS program)
- **Conditions**: For tech entrepreneurs; requires investment of KRW 100M+ OR a qualifying patent/IP; endorsement via OASIS (startup incubator) program; must establish a startup in designated fields. D-8-4 can convert to D-8 (investor) or F-2/F-5.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: D-8-4 → D-8/F-2 → F-5 → citizenship (5+ years).
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.kr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (dedicated tech startup path; incubator endorsement is the key gate).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: E-7 (skilled) / E-9 (non-professional, EPS) → F-2 → F-5
- **Conditions**: E-7: job offer in a designated occupation. E-9 (Employment Permit System): non-professional workers under government quota, employer-tied. E-7-4: points-based conversion for experienced workers (work experience in Korea, income, Korean language). TOPIK level required for upgrades. ^[https://workvisa.guide/blog/south-korea-worker-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: E-7: 2–3 years; E-9: short, employer-tied; E-7-4: 1–3 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: E-7-4 → F-2 → F-5 (5 years) → citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.kr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (E-7-4 is the realistic settlement route for skilled workers; E-9 alone does not lead to PR).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: D-2 Student Visa → D-10 Job Seeking Visa
- **Conditions**: Enrollment at a Korean university/language program; part-time work permitted (limited hours). D-10 job-seeking visa for graduates: 6 months (extendable; raised to 3 years max in Oct 2025 with 1-year renewals). Korean degrees earn F-2 points.
- **Duration / renewal**: D-2 for course duration; D-10 6 months–3 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: D-10 → E-7 → F-2 → F-5 → citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.kr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (Korean degrees + TOPIK strongly favor F-2 points).

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: F-6 Marriage Migrant Visa (결혼이민) / F-3 Dependent
- **Conditions**: F-6: marriage to Korean national; work permitted. F-3: dependent of a long-term visa holder. F-6 holders qualify for F-5 after 2 years (simplified) or standard 5 years. F-6 also enables simplified naturalization (3 years residence).
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: F-6: simplified naturalization after 2–3 years (varies); standard 5 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.kr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (marriage to Korean national is the fastest residence/citizenship route).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: F-5-13 Overseas Pension Permanent Residence (은퇴이민)
- **Conditions**: Age 60+; foreign pension income of at least 2× Korean GNI per capita (approx. USD $76,000+/yr using April 2026 GNI benchmark). Grants DIRECT permanent residence (F-5) — open to foreigners without Korean heritage. ^[https://lottalingo.com/research/how-to-move-to-south-korea]
- **Duration / renewal**: F-5 permanent (10-year renewal cycle).
- **Path to citizenship**: F-5 → citizenship after 5 years residence + TOPIK 4 + renunciation.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.kr/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (one of the few direct-to-PR retirement routes in Asia; high pension threshold).

## Citizenship
- **Years of residence**: 5 years continuous lawful residence (general naturalization). Simplified: 3 years for spouse of Korean national; 2–3 years for special cases. ^[https://legalclarity.org/how-to-become-a-citizen-of-south-korea]
- **Language**: TOPIK Level 4+ (B2) OR completion of KIIP (Korean Immigration and Integration Program) — full KIIP exempts the written test and interview.
- **Exam/Interview**: Naturalization aptitude test (Korean language, culture, history) + interview; both waived with full KIIP completion.
- **Dual citizenship**: Generally NOT permitted — naturalized citizens must renounce prior nationality within 1 year. Exceptions: marriage to Korean national, special contributions, age 65+. ^[https://legalclarity.org/does-south-korea-give-citizenship-to-foreigners]
- **Fee**: KRW 300,000 application; processing 12–18 months.
- **Military service**: Mandatory for male citizens (~18–21 months); applies to naturalized men.

## Tax
- **Residency-based taxation**: Residents (domicile or 183+ days) taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Korea-source income only.
- **Income tax**: Progressive rates: 6%, 15%, 24%, 35%, 38%, 40%, 42%, 45% (top rate above KRW 1B). + 10% local income tax surcharge.
- **Capital gains**: 22% (real estate higher, up to 46.2% for short-term/multi-property).
- **Inheritance/gift tax**: Progressive up to 50%. No wealth tax.
- **Corporate tax**: 10%–24% (progressive).
- **Tax treaty**: Extensive network; foreign-source income excluded for first 5 years for foreign residents under certain conditions.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Korea has a formal [[digital-nomad]] visa (F-1-D) — permanent since June 30, 2026 — with eased income tiers and 3-year max stay. Strong tech ecosystem (Seoul, Pangyo). E-7 covers IT professionals; D-8-4 serves tech startups. TOPIK language ability materially improves residence and citizenship outcomes.

## Yacht / Maritime
Korea has a developed coastline with marinas in Busan, Incheon, and Jeju. Foreign-flagged yachts may visit Korean ports. Korea Coast Guard oversees maritime safety. No special yacht investor pathway.

## Notes for RU citizens
Korea maintains sanctions against Russia and suspended most visa services for Russian nationals after 2022. Processing is severely restricted. The D-8 investor route is theoretically open but practically very difficult for RU nationals due to banking sanctions and enhanced due diligence. A small Russian-speaking community exists in Seoul and Busan, concentrated in Dongdaemun area. F-5-13 retirement route is theoretically nationality-neutral but pension sourcing from Russia faces sanctions hurdles.
