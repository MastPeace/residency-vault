## Overview
Saint Lucia runs a citizenship-by-investment (CIP) programme administered by the Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU). The headline route is a non-refundable contribution to the Saint Lucia National Economic Fund (NEF), with real-estate, enterprise, and government-bond alternatives. Processing is the slowest of the major Caribbean CBI programmes (15–16 months). ^[https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/citizenship-by-investment]

## Per-Program Detail

### citizenship_by_investment
- **Official name**: Citizenship by Investment Programme (National Economic Fund / Approved Real Estate / Enterprise / Government Bonds)
- **Conditions (NEF donation)**: US$240,000 main applicant + up to 3 dependants; +US$10,000 per additional dependant under 18; +US$20,000 per additional dependant 18+; newborn (≤12 mo) US$5,000; spouse add-on US$35,000. Approved Real Estate: US$300,000. Enterprise: US$3,500,000 (solo). Government Bonds (NAB): US$300,000 non-interest-bearing + US$50,000 admin fee, 5-yr hold. Mandatory interview. ^[https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/citizenship-by-investment]
- **Duration / renewal**: Citizenship is permanent; no residence requirement.
- **Path to citizenship**: Direct grant on approval; processing ~15–16 months. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/saint-lucia-citizenship-by-investment]
- **Official source**: https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/citizenship-by-investment
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (strong passport, but long processing and higher minimum than Dominica/St Kitts).

## Citizenship
Direct citizenship by investment (above). Naturalisation by residence also possible at discretion. Dual citizenship permitted. The Citizenship of Saint Lucia Act was amended (Jun 2024) to extend citizenship by descent to diaspora descendants. ^[https://www.govt.lc/news/about/citizenship-residency]

## Tax
No capital gains, inheritance, or wealth taxes. Non-resident CIP citizens pay no local income tax; residents taxed on local income only. No minimum stay means CBI holders typically owe nothing locally.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. CIP citizens may live and work freely but the programme imposes no residency requirement.

## Yacht / Maritime
Yacht registration under the St Lucia flag is available; Rodney Bay Marina (Gros Islet) is a Caribbean yachting hub.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Open to Russian applicants subject to enhanced due diligence. No Schengen access. Mandatory interview applies. Use via authorised agents; long processing is the main friction.

## Community Pulse
St Lucia's CIP is respected but slower and pricier than peers. The 2024 Caribbean MoU pushed the NEF floor to $240,000. Complaints centre on processing time (15–16 months) and stricter due-diligence scrutiny.

## Country Card

### General description
Saint Lucia is a Windward Islands nation known for the Pitons, volcanic beaches, and a tourism-driven economy. Its citizenship-by-investment programme is a significant state revenue source, and the island positions itself as an upscale resort and yachting destination.

### Well-known strong points
- Respectable passport (Henley 143, Schengen/UK access)
- Strong rule of law for the region (WJP 0.62)
- Multiple investment routes (fund, real estate, bonds, enterprise)
- No residence requirement for citizenship
- Stable democracy (FH 91/100)

### Well-known problems
- Longest CBI processing in the Caribbean (15–16 months)
- Higher minimum ($240,000) than Dominica/St Kitts
- Mandatory interview; strict due diligence
- Small economy, tourism-dependent

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-11
- **Recent changes**: NEF floor raised to $240,000 under the 2024 Caribbean MoU; Citizenship Act amended Jun 2024 (descent for diaspora).
- **Watch items**: Processing-time reduction; further regional CBI price harmonisation.
