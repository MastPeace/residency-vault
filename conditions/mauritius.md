## Overview
Mauritius is a premier Indian Ocean relocation destination with one of Africa's most developed residency frameworks. The Economic Development Board (EDB) administers Occupation Permits (combined work+residence), the Premium Visa (long-stay/digital nomad), and the Retired Non-Citizen Residence Permit. Mauritius offers a flat 15% personal/corporate tax, no CGT, no inheritance tax, English as an official language, and a stable democratic government. Permanent residence (20-year) is achievable after 5 years on an OP/RP; citizenship via naturalisation requires ~6 years. ^[https://passport.govmu.org/passport?page_id=595]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency — Occupation Permit (Investor)
- **Official name**: Occupation Permit — Investor
- **Conditions**: USD 100,000 initial investment (of which min USD 25,000 transferred to MAU company account; remainder in equipment/R&D). MUR 5M turnover from year 3, MUR 8M from year 5 for renewal. ^[https://edbmauritius.org/newsletter-july-2026-op]
- **Duration**: 10 years, renewable with turnover compliance.
- **Path to citizenship**: Permanent Residence Permit (20-year) after 5 years on OP with MUR 15M annual turnover or MUR 75M over 5 years. Citizenship via naturalisation: 12 months continuous + 5/7 years aggregate residence. USD 500K investment reduces to 2 years continuous residence. ^[https://dha.govmu.org/Documents/services/MAURITIUS%20CITIZENSHIP%20ACT(2).pdf]
- **Official source**: https://passport.govmu.org/passport?page_id=626
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — established framework but frequent threshold revisions (2025 Finance Act raised salary/turnover requirements significantly); bureaucracy manageable via agencies. Source: expat forums, KPMG alerts.

### digital_nomad_visa — Premium Visa
- **Official name**: Premium Visa
- **Conditions**: USD 1,500/month income per adult applicant, USD 500/month per dependent child. Health/travel insurance. Main income source must be outside Mauritius. No entry into local labour market. No fee (free application). ^[https://passport.govmu.org/passport?page_id=595]
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: None — Premium Visa time does NOT count toward permanent residence or citizenship. Must switch to OP/RP for that path. ^[https://edbmauritius.org]
- **Official source**: https://passport.govmu.org/passport?page_id=595
- **Community verdict**: Works well — easy online application, no fees, quick processing. Main limitation: no PR path.

### highly_skilled_employment — Occupation Permit (Professional)
- **Official name**: Occupation Permit — Professional
- **Conditions**: Minimum basic monthly salary MUR 50,000 (harmonised across all sectors as of 2025). Employer sponsorship required. ^[https://edbmauritius.org/newsletter-july-2026-op]
- **Duration**: Maximum 3 years or employment contract duration, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR after 5 years on OP with monthly salary of at least MUR 400,000 for 5 consecutive years. ^[https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/za/pdf/2025/Tax%20Alert%2095.pdf]
- **Official source**: https://passport.govmu.org/passport?page_id=626
- **Community verdict**: Works well for finance/tech professionals in Mauritius's growing IFC sector.

### retired_residency — Residence Permit (Retired Non-Citizen)
- **Official name**: Residence Permit as Retired Non-Citizen
- **Conditions**: Age 50+. Initial transfer of USD 2,000 to local bank within 60 days, then USD 24,000/year or USD 2,000/month. ^[https://passport.govmu.org/passport?mdocs-file=887]
- **Duration**: 10 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR after 5 years with USD 200,000 aggregate transfer. ^[https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/za/pdf/2025/Tax%20Alert%2095.pdf]
- **Official source**: https://passport.govmu.org/passport?page_id=626
- **Community verdict**: Works well — popular retirement destination, especially with French/UK/South African retirees.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalisation**: 12 months continuous residence immediately preceding application + 5 years aggregate out of last 7 years. USD 500,000 investment reduces requirement to 2 years continuous residence. Language: English or any language current in Mauritius. ^[https://dha.govmu.org/Documents/services/MAURITIUS%20CITIZENSHIP%20ACT(2).pdf]
- **Dual citizenship**: Allowed for citizens by birth since 1995. Prohibited for naturalised persons — naturalised citizens may be deprived of citizenship if ordinarily resident abroad for 5+ consecutive years. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauritian_nationality_law]
- **Passport strength**: Visa-free access to ~150 destinations, including EU/Schengen, UK, Singapore.

## Tax
- Personal income tax: flat 15% (with exemptions and deductions).
- Corporate tax: 15% (3% for certain export-oriented companies).
- No capital gains tax, no inheritance tax, no wealth tax.
- Territorial-based taxation remittance rules for Premium Visa holders (income earned abroad, remitted to Mauritius, may be taxable — seek advice).
- Double taxation agreements with 40+ countries.

## IT / Digital Nomad
The Premium Visa is the dedicated route for digital nomads and remote workers. Mauritius has fibre-optic internet, co-working spaces in Grand Baie/Tamarin/Port Louis, and a growing tech scene. English and French widely spoken. The 15% flat tax is competitive. Time on Premium Visa does NOT count toward PR. Switch to OP-Investor or OP-Professional for residency clock.

## Yacht / Maritime
Mauritius is a growing Indian Ocean yachting hub. Port Louis Harbour and Grand Baie offer marina facilities. The Mauritius Shipping Division handles vessel registration. Recognised international captain credentials. Strategic location for Indian Ocean cruising (Seychelles, Madagascar, Maldives).

## Notes for RU citizens
Mauritius offers visa-free entry for 90 days for Russian citizens (visa-waiver extension to 180 days under discussion). The Premium Visa is accessible. Local banking has become more cautious post-2022 but remains workable. The Russian-speaking community is small but present. No direct sanctions impact. Mauritius's non-aligned foreign policy is a plus.
