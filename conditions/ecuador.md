## Overview
Ecuador's immigration is governed by the Human Mobility Law (Ley Orgánica de Movilidad Humana) and managed by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Human Mobility (Cancillería) and the Ministry of Government. Ecuador offers five main residency pathways: Pensioner, Investor, Professional, Rentista, and Digital Nomad (Rentista Trabajador Nómada). Its digital nomad visa is one of the longest in Latin America: 2 years, renewable once (4 years total), with one of the lowest income thresholds (~USD 1,410/mo). The Ecuadorian passport provides ~95 visa-free destinations. Ecuador dollarized in 2000 (uses USD). ^[https://visa-digital-nomad.com/ecuador]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residencia Temporal — Inversionista
- **Conditions**: Investment ≥ 100× Ecuadorian minimum wage (~USD 48,200 at 2026 minimum wage of USD 482). Options: real estate purchase (~$46,000+), certificate of deposit, or business investment. Additional ~$500 per dependent. ^[https://www.expatfocus.com/ecuador/guide/ecuador-visas]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years temporary, renewable once. Permanent residency after ~21 months of temporary residence (max 90 days outside Ecuador during prior 2 years).
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years of permanent residency (total ~5 years from arrival). Dual citizenship permitted since 2008.
- **Official source**: https://www.gob.ec
- **Community verdict**: Works well (moderate threshold; dollarized economy simplifies finances).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residencia Temporal — Rentista
- **Conditions**: Passive income ≥ 3× minimum wage (~USD 1,410/mo as of 2025) from abroad: dividends, rental income, trust distributions. Must be deposited into Ecuadorian bank. ^[https://thewanderinginvestor.com/services/residency-and-citizenship-by-investment/how-to-obtain-residency-in-ecuador]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, renewable once.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years of permanent residency after temp → PR conversion (~21 months).
- **Official source**: https://www.cancilleria.gob.ec
- **Community verdict**: Works well (low threshold; straightforward for retirees and passive-income earners).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Visa de Residencia Temporal Rentista para Trabajo Remoto (Visa Nómada Digital)
- **Conditions**: Remote work for foreign employer/clients/own company. Income ≥ 3× Ecuadorian minimum wage (~USD 1,410/mo). **Open to ALL nationalities** — no visa-exempt requirement. Bank statements for last 3 months. Employment contract ≥ 2 years. Health insurance required. ^[https://guide.genki.world/ecuador-digital-nomad-visa-requirements-and-how-to-apply]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, renewable once (4 years total). Get cédula (Ecuadorian ID), open bank accounts.
- **Path to citizenship**: Counts as temporary residency — can convert to permanent after ~21 months, then citizenship after 3 years PR.
- **Official source**: https://serviciosdigitales.cancilleria.gob.ec
- **Community verdict**: Works well (long duration, low cost ~$320 govt fees, open to all nationalities; some teething issues with the new platform).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Visa de Residencia Temporal — Profesional
- **Conditions**: University degree (apostilled and registered with SENESCYT) + proof of professional practice or employment contract. ^[https://thewanderinginvestor.com/services/residency-and-citizenship-by-investment/how-to-obtain-residency-in-ecuador]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Standard path via permanent residency.
- **Official source**: https://www.gob.ec
- **Community verdict**: Works well (degree validation via SENESCYT can be time-consuming).

### startup_entrepreneur
Subsumed under Inversionista. Business investment from ~USD 48,200.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residencia Temporal — Trabajo
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with Ecuadorian employer.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Standard path.
- **Official source**: https://www.gob.ec
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (Ecuadorian labor market is small; employer sponsorship needed).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residencia Temporal — Estudiante
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in Ecuadorian educational institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student time counts.
- **Official source**: https://www.gob.ec
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Visa de Residencia Temporal — Dependiente / Familiar
- **Conditions**: Certified family tie to Ecuadorian citizen or resident. All visa types allow sponsoring spouse and children. Parents can be included once sponsor has permanent residency.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 2 years of legal residency while married to an Ecuadorian.
- **Official source**: https://www.gob.ec
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de Residencia Temporal — Jubilado (Pensionado)
- **Conditions**: Lifetime pension ≥ 3× minimum wage (~USD 1,410/mo). Most popular route for foreign retirees. ^[https://www.expatfocus.com/ecuador/guide/ecuador-visas]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, renewable once. Permanent residency after ~21 months.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years PR (total ~5 years from arrival).
- **Official source**: https://www.gob.ec
- **Community verdict**: Works well (most popular residency category; Ecuador is a top retirement destination).

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available.

## Citizenship
- **Standard naturalization**: 3 years of permanent residency, uninterrupted (max 90 days abroad per year). ^[https://www.expatfocus.com/ecuador/guide/ecuador-citizenship]
- **Marriage-based**: 2 years of legal residency while married to Ecuadorian citizen.
- **Refugees/stateless**: 2 years of residency.
- **Language/civics**: 20-question computerized multiple-choice exam in Spanish (90% pass mark). Interview explaining reasons for wanting Ecuadorian nationality. Exempt for 65+ from knowledge test (still need Spanish writing/speaking).
- **Processing time**: 6–12 months after filing.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted under Article 8 of 2008 Constitution.
- **Physical presence**: Max 90 days outside Ecuador in any year during qualifying period.
- **Total timeline**: ~5 years (2 years temp + 3 years PR + 6–12 months processing).

## Tax
- **Residency trigger**: 183 days in Ecuador in a calendar year.
- **Tax system**: Worldwide income for residents. Progressive rates 5%–35%. ^[https://visa-digital-nomad.com/ecuador]
- **Digital nomads**: Foreign-source income exempt from Ecuadorian income tax while on the digital nomad visa (non-resident status preserved if under 183 days).
- **Dividends**: 0% for residents, 10% for non-residents.
- **Wealth tax**: None.

## IT / Digital Nomad
- One of the best digital nomad programs in Latin America: open to all nationalities, 2+2 years, low income threshold (~$1,410/mo), low fees (~$320), counts toward permanent residency.
- Internet: 102 Mbps average, good in major cities (Quito, Cuenca). Coworking scene growing.
- Pro tip: applicants over 65 pay 50% fee discount; disability 30%+ pays nothing.

## Yacht / Maritime
Not a major yachting hub. Guayaquil and Manta have marinas. No special yacht/captain residency program.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian citizens: visa-free for tourism up to 90 days. Digital nomad visa is open to ALL nationalities including RU.
- Residency applications processed through Cancillería e-VISAS platform (serviciosdigitales.cancilleria.gob.ec).
- Documents must be apostilled and translated into Spanish.
- Banking: cédula + residency visa opens accounts. Dollarized economy removes currency risk.
- Ecuador has a small but established Russian-speaking community in Quito and Cuenca.

## Community pulse
- **Overall**: Ecuador is one of the most accessible residency destinations in Latin America. Low financial thresholds, dollarized economy, and the digital nomad visa counting toward PR make it attractive.
- **Processing**: Temporary residency processed via e-VISAS platform. Permanent residency requires physical presence (max 90 days absence in 2 years).
- **Rejection risk**: Low if documentation is complete.
