## Overview
South Africa is the continent's most developed economy with a recently launched Remote Work Visitor Visa (October 2024) and a reformed points-based work visa system. Cape Town, Johannesburg, and Durban host significant expat communities. The Department of Home Affairs (DHA) manages immigration. Permanent residence is achievable after 5 years on a work visa + PR; citizenship requires 5 years of permanent residence. Dual citizenship is permitted with prior retention approval. ^[https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/notices/1824-remote-work-visa-requirements-9-oct-2024]

## Per-Program Detail

### digital_nomad_visa — Remote Work Visitor Visa
- **Official name**: Visitor's Visa Section 11(1)(b)(iv) for Remote Work
- **Conditions**: Gross salary ≥ R650,796/year (~USD 35,000 at mid-2026 rates). Valid employment contract with foreign employer. Bank statements (3 months) proving income. Medical report, police clearance, proof of accommodation. MUST register with SARS (tax) if present >183 days in 12-month period. Cannot take up SA employment. No in-country change of status. ^[https://dirco.gov.za/toronto/nomad-visa]
- **Duration**: Exceeding 3 months up to 3 years. ^[https://www.dha.gov.za/images/notices/8october24/Remote_Work_Visa_-_requirements_-_9_Oct_2024.pdf]
- **Path to citizenship**: None — this is a visitor visa. Does NOT count toward PR or citizenship. Cannot switch to another visa type in-country.
- **Official source**: https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/notices/1824-remote-work-visa-requirements-9-oct-2024
- **Community verdict**: Works well — finally operational after long delay (gazetted Oct 2024). Key limitation: no PR path, no in-country conversion.

### highly_skilled_employment — Critical Skills Visa
- **Official name**: Critical Skills Work Visa (Points-Based System)
- **Conditions**: Degree + 5 years experience in critical skills list occupation. Points scored on qualification, experience, salary offer (R650,796+), language. ^[https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/department-announces-gazetting-remote-work-visitor-visa]
- **Duration**: Up to 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR after 5 years on work permit + PR grant. Citizenship: 5 years permanent residence + 4/8 years physical presence. ^[https://www.saembassy.org/visas/civic_immigration/citizenship]
- **Official source**: https://www.dha.gov.za
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — slow DHA processing is the primary complaint. The new points system aims to reduce discretion/corruption.

## Citizenship
- Naturalisation: 5 years of permanent residence + 4 years physical presence out of last 8 years + 1 year continuous residence immediately before application. ^[https://www.saembassy.org/visas/civic_immigration/citizenship]
- Marriage to SA citizen: 2 years PR + 2 years marriage.
- Language: basic command of one of 11 official languages (English/Afrikaans typical).
- Good character; police clearance; SA citizenship test (knowledge of SA).
- Dual citizenship: Permitted BUT South African citizens must apply for retention of citizenship BEFORE acquiring another citizenship. Naturalised citizens should check if home country permits dual. ^[https://www.saembassy.org/visas/civic_immigration/citizenship]
- Passport: visa-free to ~105 destinations including EU/Schengen (visa-free), UK, Russia (visa-free).
- **Honest path**: Realistic but slow — expect 10+ years from first work visa to passport. DHA backlogs are significant.

## Tax
- Residence-based taxation. Tax residents taxed on worldwide income.
- Progressive rates: 18%–45% (top bracket kicks in at ZAR 1.8M+).
- Foreign employment income exemption: up to ZAR 1.25M if >183 days abroad (12-month period, 60+ continuous).
- Capital gains tax: 18% (effective rate at 45% bracket).
- No wealth tax. 15% VAT.
- Remote workers: IRS registration mandatory if >183 days in-country (even on visitor visa) OR if no DTA with home country.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Cape Town is Africa's premier digital nomad hub: world-class internet, co-working spaces (Workshop17, Inner City Ideas Cartel), vibrant startup scene. Johannesburg and Durban also viable. The Remote Work Visa finally provides a legal framework. Cost of living: moderate by global standards, favourable with foreign currency. Load-shedding (power cuts) remains an intermittent issue but has improved.

## Yacht / Maritime
South Africa is a world-class yachting destination:
- Cape Town (V&A Waterfront, Royal Cape Yacht Club), Durban, Port Elizabeth.
- Yacht registration under SA flag via South African Maritime Safety Authority (SAMSA).
- Recognised international yachtmaster credentials (RYA, MCA).
- Superyacht repair/maintenance facilities in Cape Town.
- Strategic location: Atlantic-Indian Ocean transition, gateway to Southern Ocean.

## Notes for RU citizens
South Africa is visa-free for Russian citizens (90 days tourist). The Remote Work Visa pathway is accessible. Banking is available but has become more stringent for Russian nationals since 2022 (enhanced KYC). Cape Town and Johannesburg have notable Russian-speaking communities. South Africa's non-aligned BRICS membership is a positive. Direct flights are limited; typically via Dubai/Doha/Istanbul.

## Country Card

South Africa is Africa's most industrialised economy and the continent's premier relocation destination. With three capital cities, 11 official languages, and a diverse geography from Mediterranean Cape Town to subtropical Durban and the highveld Johannesburg, it offers unmatched variety. The October 2024 launch of the Remote Work Visitor Visa has positioned it as a serious digital nomad hub. Permanent residence and citizenship are achievable but slow (realistically 10+ years). Dual citizenship is permitted with prior retention approval.

### Well-known strong points
- Africa's strongest passport (~105 visa-free) and most developed infrastructure
- New Remote Work Visitor Visa (2024) — legal framework for up to 3 years, income threshold R650,796/yr
- Cape Town is a world-class city for lifestyle, tech, and nomad community (Workshop17, Inner City Cartel)
- Rule of Law 0.57, Freedom House 81/100 — Africa's strongest democratic institutions
- Moderate cost of living with foreign currency; excellent quality-to-price ratio
- World-class yachting infrastructure (Cape Town V&A, Royal Cape YC); strategic Atlantic-Indian Ocean gateway

### Well-known problems
- Remote Work Visa does NOT lead to PR or citizenship; no in-country change of status
- DHA processing backlogs are significant — work visas can take months/years
- Load-shedding (scheduled power cuts) remains an intermittent issue, though improving
- High crime rates in certain areas; personal security awareness essential
- Top income tax rate of 45% applies to high earners; worldwide income taxed if resident >183 days
- Inequality and unemployment are among the world's highest
