## Overview

Bosnia and Herzegovina (BiH) is an EU candidate country with a complex two-entity structure (Federation of BiH and Republika Srpska). Passport ranks ~72nd globally with visa-free access to ~135 destinations. BiH has **no formal golden visa, digital nomad visa, or citizenship-by-investment program**. Residence is primarily obtained through employment, business, family ties, education, or property ownership with demonstrated connection. Citizenship requires ~8 years of residence (5 years temporary + 3 years permanent). ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/bosnia]

## Per-Program Detail

### Property Ownership (de facto pathway)

- **Conditions**: Owning real estate in BiH may support a temporary residence application, but property ownership alone is insufficient. Applicant must demonstrate an "effective connection" to BiH — local investment, family connections, children attending school, or other established ties. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/bosnia]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable while requirements continue to be met.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years continuous temporary residence → 3 years permanent residence → citizenship. Total: ~8 years. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/bosnia]
- **Official source**: [Service for Foreigners' Affairs (SPS)](https://sps.gov.ba/?lang=en&page_id=2491)
- **Community verdict**: Problematic — property alone is not enough. Other ties are needed. No formal investment threshold. Process varies between entities (FBiH vs. RS).

### Employment Residency / Highly Skilled Employment

- **Official name**: Privremeni boravak po osnovu zaposlenja (Temporary residence based on employment)
- **Conditions**: Valid employment contract with a BiH employer. Work permit issued by the entity-level employment authority. Employer must justify hiring a foreign national. ^[https://sps.gov.ba/?lang=en&page_id=2491]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable with continued employment.
- **Path to citizenship**: ~8 years (5 temp + 3 PR).
- **Official source**: [SPS — Stay of Aliens](https://sps.gov.ba/?lang=en&page_id=2491) | [MSB — Entry and Stay](https://msb.gov.ba/inf_za_strance/default.aspx?id=14555&langTag=en-US)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — work permit process is bureaucratic, entity-level variations add complexity. Limited job market for foreign professionals.

### Startup / Entrepreneur

- **Official name**: Privremeni boravak po osnovu osnivanja privrednog društva
- **Conditions**: Register a company in BiH. Entity-level registration (FBiH or RS). No formal minimum investment. Must demonstrate business activity.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: ~8 years.
- **Official source**: [SPS](https://sps.gov.ba/?lang=en&page_id=2491)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — company registration is possible but slow. Entity-level differences create friction.

### Student Residency

- **Conditions**: Enrollment in an accredited BiH educational institution.
- **Duration**: Duration of studies.
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### Family Reunification

- **Conditions**: Spouse or minor child of a BiH resident or citizen.
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

## Citizenship

- **By naturalization**: 5 years of continuous temporary residence + 3 years of permanent residence = ~8 years total. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/bosnia]
- **Permissible absences**: Total ≤ 10 months or single absence ≤ 6 months during the 5-year period. ^[https://sps.gov.ba/?lang=en&page_id=2491]
- **Language**: Bosnian/Croatian/Serbian proficiency may be required.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted in some cases (bilateral agreements).
- **By descent**: Available if parent is BiH citizen.
- **CBI**: Not available. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/bosnia]
- **Timeline**: ~8 years minimum.

## Tax

- **Personal income tax**: Flat 10% (FBiH) / 10% (RS) — varies slightly by entity.
- **Corporate tax**: 10% (FBiH and RS).
- **Social security**: ~41.5% total (among the highest in Europe).
- **No wealth tax**.
- **Double taxation treaties**: ~40 countries.

## IT / Digital Nomad

Bosnia and Herzegovina does not have a [[digital-nomad]] visa. No formal remote work pathway exists. The business registration or employment routes are the only practical options. Internet infrastructure is adequate in urban areas (Sarajevo, Banja Luka, Mostar). Not a significant nomad destination.

## Yacht / Maritime

Bosnia has a tiny Adriatic coastline (~20 km) at Neum. No significant yachting infrastructure. Not relevant.

## Notes for RU citizens

Russian citizens enjoy visa-free entry to BiH (up to 30 days, extendable to 90 days within 180-day period). Banking is limited for RU citizens. Russian is understood to varying degrees. BiH is not an EU member — no sanctions. However, BiH is not a primary relocation destination for Russian citizens due to limited economic opportunities, high social security burden, bureaucratic process, and complex entity structure. Property ownership is permitted for foreign citizens.

## Community Pulse

BiH is not a popular expat destination. Those who relocate report slow bureaucracy, entity-level fragmentation, high social charges, limited job market. Positive aspects: low cost of living, natural beauty, cultural richness. Residence process is functional but not streamlined.
