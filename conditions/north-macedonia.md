## Overview

North Macedonia is an EU candidate country (NATO member since 2020). Passport ranks ~45th globally with visa-free access to ~125 destinations. Residence permits are available through employment, business, education, and family ties. Citizenship requires **8 years** of legal residence. No formal golden visa, digital nomad visa, or citizenship-by-investment program exists. The country uses the Macedonian denar (MKD). ^[https://investnorthmacedonia.gov.mk/work-visas-and-permits]

## Per-Program Detail

### Employment Residency / Highly Skilled Employment

- **Official name**: Привремен престој врз основа на вработување (Temporary residence based on employment)
- **Conditions**: Valid employment contract with a North Macedonian employer. Work permit issued by the Employment Service Agency. Employer must demonstrate need for foreign worker. ^[https://investnorthmacedonia.gov.mk/work-visas-and-permits]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable with continued employment.
- **Path to citizenship**: 8 years legal residence. ^[https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2024-05/1_law_on_citizenship_2022_consolidated_eng.pdf]
- **Official source**: [Invest North Macedonia — Work Visas and Permits](https://investnorthmacedonia.gov.mk/work-visas-and-permits) | [MFA — Visa Types](https://mfa.gov.mk/en-GB/konzularni-uslugi/vidovi-vizi-za-vlez-vo-rsm)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — work permit process is bureaucratic. Limited job market for foreign professionals. Low wages by European standards.

### Startup / Entrepreneur

- **Official name**: Привремен престој врз основа на основање трговско друштво
- **Conditions**: Register a company in North Macedonia. No minimum capital requirement specified for residence purposes.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 8 years.
- **Official source**: [Invest North Macedonia](https://investnorthmacedonia.gov.mk/work-visas-and-permits)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — company registration is possible but process is slow. Low local market limits business viability.

### Student Residency

- **Conditions**: Enrollment in an accredited North Macedonian educational institution.
- **Duration**: Duration of studies.
- **Community verdict**: Works well for enrolled students.

### Family Reunification

- **Conditions**: Spouse or minor child of a North Macedonian resident or citizen. Also available for family members of citizens by origin. ^[https://investnorthmacedonia.gov.mk/work-visas-and-permits]
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

## Citizenship

- **By naturalization**: 8 years of legal residence in North Macedonia. ^[https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2024-05/1_law_on_citizenship_2022_consolidated_eng.pdf]
- **Language**: Macedonian language proficiency required.
- **Dual citizenship**: Generally not recognized; renunciation usually required (exceptions exist).
- **By descent**: Available if at least one parent is a citizen.
- **By marriage**: 3 years of marriage + residence.
- **CBI**: Not available.
- **Timeline**: 8 years minimum.

## Tax

- **Personal income tax**: Flat **10%**. ^[https://investnorthmacedonia.gov.mk/]
- **Corporate tax**: 10%.
- **Social security**: ~28% total contributions.
- **No wealth tax**.
- **Double taxation treaties**: ~50 countries.
- **Tax residency**: 183 days physical presence.

## IT / Digital Nomad

North Macedonia does **not** have a [[digital-nomad]] visa. No formal remote work pathway exists. Internet infrastructure is adequate in Skopje. Not a significant nomad destination. Low cost of living is the main draw for the few remote workers who choose it.

## Yacht / Maritime

North Macedonia is landlocked. Not applicable.

## Notes for RU citizens

Russian citizens enjoy visa-free entry to North Macedonia (up to 90 days within 180-day period). Banking access for RU citizens is limited. Russian is not widely spoken. North Macedonia is a NATO member and EU candidate — geopolitical alignment with the West may affect RU applicants. Not a significant relocation destination for Russian citizens.

## Community Pulse

North Macedonia is not a popular expat destination. Low cost of living is the primary draw. Main complaints: limited economic opportunities, bureaucratic residence process, low wages, limited international community. Those who relocate for employment typically come through multinational companies or diplomatic missions.
