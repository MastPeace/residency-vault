## Overview
The Cayman Islands is a British Overseas Territory and one of the world's leading offshore financial centres. It imposes **zero direct taxation** (no income, capital gains, corporate, property, or inheritance tax). There is no citizenship-by-investment programme, but a "Person of Independent Means" certificate grants residence for a substantial investment, and permanent residence (with a points system) is available after 8 years' lawful residence. ^[https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/cayman_tax_residency.pdf]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Certificate of Permanent Residence for Persons of Independent Means
- **Conditions**: Minimum CI$2,000,000 (~US$2,439,000) invested in developed real estate; demonstrate sufficient resources to maintain self and dependants. No right to work. ^[https://www.bedellcristin.com/imported-media/2886/guide-to-residency-options-in-ci-for-persons-of-independent-means-sept-2020.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: Lifetime grant (no expiry).
- **Path to citizenship**: Eligible for naturalisation as a British Overseas Territories Citizen after 5 years' legal+ordinary residence.
- **Official source**: https://caymanresident.com/move/immigration/extended-residence
- **Community verdict**: Works well for high-net-worth retirees (expensive; no work right).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Residency Certificate for Persons of Independent Means
- **Conditions**: Invest ≥CI$1,000,000 (≥CI$500,000 in Grand Cayman residential real estate) AND show CI$120,000/yr income OR CI$400,000 deposit; lower figures for Cayman Brac/Little Cayman. Min 90 days/yr physical presence. ^[https://www.bedellcristin.com/imported-media/2886/guide-to-residency-options-in-ci-for-persons-of-independent-means-sept-2020.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: 25 years, renewable. No right to work.
- **Path to citizenship**: BOTC naturalisation after 5 years.
- **Official source**: https://caymanresident.com/move/immigration/extended-residence
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (popular with retirees; high threshold and 90-day stay).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work Permit → Permanent Residence (8+ years)
- **Conditions**: Employer-sponsored work permit; after 8 years' legal+ordinary residence, apply for permanent residence under the points system. ^[https://caymanresident.com/move/immigration/extended-residence]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permit tied to job; PR indefinite.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR → BOTC naturalisation → (optional) full British citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://caymanresident.com/move/immigration/extended-residence
- **Community verdict**: Works well for finance professionals (long 8-year clock; term limits apply to some permits).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Global Citizen Concierge Programme
- **Conditions**: Remote worker/family earning ≥US$100,000/yr (couple US$150,000), employed outside Cayman. ^[https://caymanresident.com/move/immigration/extended-residence]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: Does not count toward the residence clock.
- **Official source**: https://caymanresident.com/move/immigration/extended-residence
- **Community verdict**: Works well but niche (high income bar; only ~a few hundred issued).

## Citizenship
Caymanians hold British Overseas Territories Citizenship (BOTC) and, since 2002, are typically also full British citizens. Naturalisation as BOTC is possible after 5 years' residence; full "Caymanian status" (right of abode) takes ~15 years. Dual citizenship is permitted. ^[https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/british-overseas-territories-citizens/british-overseas-territories-citizens-accessible-version]

## Tax
**0% income tax, 0% capital gains, 0% corporate, 0% property, 0% inheritance** — no direct taxes of any kind, for residents and non-residents. No double-tax treaties (nothing to double-tax). No annual tax filing. ^[https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/cayman_tax_residency.pdf]

## IT / Digital Nomad
The Global Citizen Concierge (see above) is Cayman's only remote-work route. Otherwise, remote workers must qualify via work permit or the Independent Means certificate (no work right).

## Yacht / Maritime
Cayman is a major yacht-registration flag (Cayman Islands Shipping Registry, part of the UK Red Ensign group). George Town is a yachting and offshore-finance hub.

## Notes for RU Citizens
No CBI; all routes require substantial capital (CI$1,200,000+ for Independent Means) or a sponsored job. 0% tax is the main draw. Enhanced compliance/source-of-funds scrutiny applies in this financial centre.

## Community Pulse
Expat finance workers and wealthy retirees dominate discussion: the zero-tax environment and rule-of-law stability are praised; the 8-year work-permit clock and CI$1,200,000+ Independent Means threshold are the main barriers. Cost of living is very high.

## Country Card

### General description
The Cayman Islands is a wealthy British Overseas Territory and a top-five global offshore financial centre, with a sophisticated common-law legal system and zero direct taxation. It is home to a large expatriate financial-services workforce and a smaller wealthy-retiree community attracted by residency-for-investment certificates.

### Well-known strong points
- Zero direct taxation (income, capital gains, corporate, inheritance)
- British legal system and BOTC/British citizenship route
- World-class offshore finance and funds industry
- Stable, safe, English-speaking

### Well-known problems
- Very high cost of living and property prices
- High residency thresholds (CI$1,200,000–2,000,000) and 8-year PR clock
- Work-permit term limits; no general self-employment right
- Hurricane exposure
