## Overview
Czechia is an EU/Schengen member with a strong economy and low unemployment. The primary residence route for non-EU workers is the Employee Card, with the [[blue-card]] available for highly qualified professionals. Citizenship requires 5 years of permanent residence (10 years total residence typical). ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## Residence
**Employee Card (Zaměstnanecká karta)**: the basic residence permit for employment. Dual-purpose document combining residence and work authorization. Valid for the duration of the employment contract, typically up to 2 years, renewable. Requires a job offer and labor market test. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

**EU Blue Card**: for highly qualified workers with a university degree or 3+ years of professional experience. Faster processing, no labor market test, and greater mobility within the EU.

**Digital Nomad Programme**: Czechia has introduced a specific programme for IT specialists, allowing remote workers to reside in Czechia while working for foreign employers. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

Applications are submitted at Czech embassies abroad or (in specific cases) from within Czechia at the Ministry of Interior. Processing times vary from 60–90 days for Employee Cards up to 12+ months in heavily loaded regions. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## Citizenship
- 5 years of permanent residence (or 3 years for EU citizens)
- Czech language proficiency (B1)
- Clean criminal record
- Proof of income and accommodation
- Dual citizenship permitted since 2014

## Tax
Flat income tax rate of 15% (23% above ~CZK 1.9M/year). Social and health insurance contributions total ~11% for employees. Czechia has double taxation treaties with most countries.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Czechia has launched a [[digital-nomad]] programme specifically for IT specialists. Key features:
- Targets non-EU IT professionals working remotely for foreign companies
- Part of the broader "Digital Nomad" initiative alongside the Employee Card and [[blue-card]] routes
- Prague and Brno are thriving tech hubs with strong startup ecosystems

## Yacht / Maritime
Not applicable. Czechia is landlocked.

## Notes for RU citizens
Russian citizens need a visa to enter the Schengen area. The Employee Card and Blue Card routes are open but applications at Czech consulates in Russia may face processing delays. Czech language requirement for citizenship is a significant hurdle. The Russian-speaking community exists primarily in Prague and Karlovy Vary.
