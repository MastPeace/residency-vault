## Overview
Croatia joined the EU in 2013, the Eurozone and Schengen in 2023. It has a strong passport (EU) and one of Europe's most mature [[digital-nomad]] visa frameworks, introduced in 2021. In 2026, Croatia was ranked #8 globally for digital nomads by U.S. News & World Report. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## Residence
Temporary residence permits are valid up to 1 year (2 years for family reunification, 18 months for digital nomads). The Aliens Act (OG 133/20, amended through 55/26) governs all permits. Applications are made at MUP (Ministry of Interior) police administrations. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

**2026 Law on Foreigners amendments** (effective June 4, 2026): introduced a Croatian language requirement for work permit extensions (mandatory from June 2027), easier employer switching after 6 months, multi-year seasonal work permits, and a 90-day statutory deadline for residence and work permit decisions. New housing rules: minimum 14 m² per person, rent capped at 30% of net salary. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

EU/EEA citizens register temporary residence with police; after 5 years, they may apply for long-term residence. Non-EU citizens need a residence permit before arrival. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## Citizenship
By naturalization: 8 years of continuous residence (can be reduced for ethnic Croats). Language proficiency in Croatian required. Croatia permits dual citizenship.

## Tax
Income tax is progressive (20%/30%). Digital nomads on the DN residence permit are exempt from Croatian income tax on foreign-sourced income — a key advantage. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## IT / Digital Nomad
Croatia's [[digital-nomad]] residence permit (introduced 2021) allows non-EU remote workers to stay for up to 18 months. Key details:
- **Income requirement (2026)**: ~€3,622.50/month (2.5× average Croatian net salary), or €43,470 in savings for 12 months / €65,205 for 18 months ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]
- **Application cost**: ~€100–200
- **Processing time**: officially 30–60 days, practice 2–12 months
- **Tax**: 0% on foreign income
- **Extension**: No direct renewal; 6-month mandatory break before reapplication
- **Family**: Allowed, income +10% per dependent
- **No path to permanent residency from this permit** ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

The [[blue-card]] (EU Blue Card) is also available for highly qualified workers. Croatia ranked #8 globally for digital nomads in 2026. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## Yacht / Maritime
Croatia has over 1,000 islands along the Adriatic coast and an extensive yachting infrastructure. The country issues ICC (International Certificate of Competence) recognized boat licences. Yacht registration under the Croatian flag is available for vessel owners with Croatian residence or a Croatian-registered company. Major marinas in Split, Dubrovnik, Zadar, and Pula.

## Notes for RU citizens
Russian citizens need a visa to enter Croatia (Schengen). Work permits and digital nomad permits are available. No special RU-specific programs. Russian is not widely spoken outside tourist areas.
