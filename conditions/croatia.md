## Overview
Croatia joined the EU in 2013, the Eurozone and Schengen in 2023. It has one of Europe's most mature **digital nomad** visa frameworks (since 2021) and was ranked #8 globally for digital nomads in 2026. The Aliens Act was amended in June 2026 with new language requirements and housing rules. Citizenship requires 8 years of continuous residence. Croatia permits dual citizenship. ^[https://www.expatincroatia.com/apply-citizenship-croatia]

## Per-Program Detail

### Digital Nomad Visa
- **Official name**: Boravak digitalnih nomada (Digital Nomad Residence Permit)
- **Conditions**: Non-EU/EEA citizen. Remote work using ICT for foreign-registered companies or freelancing for foreign clients. **Income ≥ ~€3,622.50/month** (2.5× Croatian average net salary, 2026) OR proof of savings: **€43,470** for 12 months / **€65,205** for 18 months. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json] Health insurance. Accommodation in Croatia. Clean criminal record. Family: income +10% per dependent. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to **18 months** (not a standard 1-year). No direct renewal — **6-month mandatory break** before reapplication. **Crackdown on visa-hopping (2026)**: switching to another residence category (spouse of DN holder, "other purposes") without leaving Croatia is no longer permitted. ^[https://dispatcheseurope.com/croatia-making-alterations-to-its-digital-nomad-visa-which-isnt-actually-a-visa]
- **Path to citizenship**: Does NOT lead to permanent residence or citizenship. Must switch to work permit route for long-term path.
- **Official source**: https://mup.gov.hr
- **Community verdict**: Works well — mature DN visa, generous 18-month duration, 0% tax on foreign income. The 6-month break rule and new restrictions on in-country status switching are significant limitations.

### Tax note for digital nomads
Digital nomads on the DN residence permit are **exempt from Croatian income tax** on foreign-sourced income — a key advantage. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

### Highly Skilled Employment — EU Blue Card
- **Official name**: Plava karta EU
- **Conditions**: University degree. Job offer from Croatian employer. Salary ≥ 1.5× Croatian average.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence → PR → citizenship at 8 years total.
- **Official source**: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard Blue Card route.

### Employment Residency
- **Official name**: Boravišna i radna dozvola (Residence and work permit)
- **Conditions**: Job offer. Labor market test. 2026 Aliens Act amendments: Croatian language requirement for work permit extensions (mandatory from June 2027), easier employer switching after 6 months, multi-year seasonal permits, **90-day statutory deadline** for decisions. New housing: min 14 m²/person, rent ≤30% net salary. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 1 year (2 years for family reunification), renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence → PR → citizenship at 8 years.
- **Official source**: https://mup.gov.hr
- **Community verdict**: Works well — robust legal framework but 2026 amendments add new complexity (language requirement).

## Citizenship
- **Standard**: 8 years continuous residence + permanent residence status + Croatian language B1 + culture test ^[https://www.expatincroatia.com/apply-citizenship-croatia]
- **Marriage to Croatian**: permanent residence required, live together in Croatia
- **Croatian descent/emigrants**: shorter path (Article 11)
- **Special interest**: Article 12 — can bypass standard requirements
- **Language**: Croatian proficiency + knowledge of Croatian culture and social order
- **Dual citizenship**: Croatia permits dual citizenship BUT naturalization under Article 8 requires renunciation of previous citizenship ^[https://www.expatincroatia.com/apply-citizenship-croatia]

## Tax
- Progressive income tax: 20% / 30% (varies by municipality)
- DN permit holders: **0% tax on foreign income** ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]
- Corporate tax: 18% (10% for small businesses)
- Social contributions: ~20% employee
- No wealth tax

## IT / Digital Nomad
Croatia's DN permit is one of the most generous: up to 18 months, 0% tax on foreign income, and the Adriatic lifestyle is a major draw. Ranked #8 globally for digital nomads in 2026. Major tech hubs: Zagreb, Split. Fast internet in urban areas. The 6-month mandatory break after 18 months is the key downside — no continuous path to PR from the DN permit alone.

## Yacht / Maritime
Croatia has over 1,000 Adriatic islands and is a premier yachting destination. ICC-recognized boat licenses available. Yacht registration under Croatian flag available for vessel owners with Croatian residence or a Croatian-registered company. Major marinas: Split, Dubrovnik, Zadar, Pula. Extensive marina infrastructure — one of Europe's best yachting regions.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Russian citizens need a Schengen visa (Croatia joined Schengen 2023). The DN visa and work permits are open. Croatian language (South Slavic) is moderately accessible for Russian speakers. Russian is not widely spoken outside tourist areas. Croatian public sentiment toward Russia is generally neutral/EU-aligned.
