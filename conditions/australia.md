## Overview
Australia has a strong passport and a large, points-based skilled migration system. The General Skilled Migration program (subclasses 189, 190, 491) is the primary skilled route. The Business Innovation and Investment Program (BIIP, subclass 188) closed to new applications on July 31, 2024, but existing 188 holders continue to transition to PR (subclass 888). The Significant Investor Visa (SIV) required AUD $5M in complying investments. Australia permits dual citizenship. ^[https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Business Innovation and Investment Program (BIIP) — Significant Investor stream (subclass 188C → 888C)
- **Conditions**: CLOSED TO NEW APLICATIONS (July 31, 2024). Previously: AUD $5,000,000 in complying investments (managed funds, ASX-listed, government bonds, etc.) held for 4 years. Residence requirement: 40 days per year average (cumulative 160 days over 4 years). State/territory nomination required. Existing 88C holders continue to transition to 888C PR. ^[https://migrationalliance.com.au/en/siv/significant-investor-visa-for-permanent-resident-visa-australia.html]
- **Duration / renewal**: Subclass 188C: 4 years and 3 months provisional → subclass 88C: permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 4 years lawful residence including 12 months as PR; max 12 months absence in 4 years, max 90 days in final year; English + citizenship test.
- **Official source**: https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/geting-a-visa/visa-listing/business-inovation-and-investment-188
- **Community verdict**: Closed (program shut to new applicants; no replacement for pure investment residency announced).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No passive income / independent means route.
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (no pathway).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No DN visa. Working holiday makers (subclass 417/462) and visitor visas allow short stays; remote work for a foreign employer is permitted on a visitor visa but does not constitute residence.
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (visitor route works informally; no dedicated DN pathway planned).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189) / Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) / Skilled Work Regional visa (subclass 491)
- **Conditions**: Points test — minimum 65 to enter pool, competitive invitations require 85+. Age 25–32: 30 pts max; Superior English (IELTS 8): 20 pts; PhD: 20 pts; Australian work experience: up to 20 pts. Occupation must be on MLTSSL (189), MLTSSL/STSOL (190), or MLTSSL/STSOL/ROL (491). Positive skills assessment required. State nomination for 190 (+5 pts) and 491 (+15 pts). ^[https://www.globalselect.com.au/points-calculator]
- **Duration / renewal**: 189/190: permanent residence on grant. 491: 5-year provisional → PR (subclass 191) after 3 years regional residence.
- **Path to citizenship**: 4 years lawful residence including 12 months as PR; max 12 months total absence and max 90 days in final year; English + citizenship test.
- **Official source**: https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/working-in-australia/skillselect
- **Community verdict**: Works well (transparent points system; competitive cutoffs are the main challenge — most invites require 85+).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: BIIP Entrepreneur stream (188E → 888E) — CLOSED
- **Conditions**: Closed to new applications. Previously required state/territory endorsement and a funding agreement from a specified third party.
- **Duration / renewal**: N/A.
- **Path to citizenship**: N/A (program closed).
- **Official source**: https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/
- **Community verdict**: Closed (no current entrepreneur pathway outside employer-sponsored or points-tested routes).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) / Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482 → 186 TRT)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from an Australian employer; occupation on relevant list; 3 years of relevant work experience for 186 DE; 2 years with the same employer on 482 for 186 TRT. SID 482: skills assessment and English requirement.
- **Duration / renewal**: 186: permanent on grant. 482: 2–4 years → 186 TRT pathway to PR after 2 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: Standard 4 years lawful residence + 12 months PR.
- **Official source**: https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (reliable PR pathway for sponsored workers; occupation-list dependency is the main risk).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student visa (subclass 500) → Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in a CRICOS-registered course; financial capacity; health insurance. 485 visa: Post-Study Work stream for 2–4 years (longer for regional study). Study in Australia adds 5 points toward skilled migration, regional study adds 5 more.
- **Duration / renewal**: 500 for course duration; 485 for 2–4 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 485 → 189/190/491 or 482/186 → PR → citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/geting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500
- **Community verdict**: Works well (strong study-to-PR pipeline; post-study work rights are generous).

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Partner visa (subclass 820/801 onshore, 309/100 offshore) / Parent visa / Child visa
- **Conditions**: Sponsr is Australian citizen/PR/eligible NZ citizen. Partner: genuine relationship; sponsor income threshold applies. Partner processing: ~12–24 months. Parent visas have a very long queue (contributory parent 143: ~12+ years). ^[https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/]
- **Duration / renewal**: Temporary → permanent (2-stage for partner); permanent on grant for child.
- **Path to citizenship**: Standard after PR: 4 years lawful residence + 12 months PR.
- **Official source**: https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for partners; problematic for parents (extreme wait times).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No retirement visa. The Investor Retirement visa (subclass 405) is closed to new applicants. Options: BIIP investor streams (closed) or temporary visitor visas.
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (no retirement pathway).

## Citizenship
- **Years of residence**: 4 years lawful residence immediately before applying, including 12 months as a permanent resident. ^[https://oneplanetmigrationlaw.com.au/immigration-blog/australian-citizenship-by-conferral-2026]
- **Absence limits**: Max 12 months total in 4 years; max 90 days in the 12 months before applying.
- **Language**: English proficiency (citizenship test conducted in English).
- **Exam**: Australian citizenship test (values, history, government).
- **Ceremony**: Required; pledge of commitment.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted.
- **Fee**: AUD $595 (from July 1, 2026).

## Tax
- **Residency-based taxation**: Tax residents pay on worldwide income. Non-residents pay on Australian-source income only.
- **Income tax**: Progressive rates: 0% (up to AUD $18,200), 19%, 30%, 37%, 45% (above AUD $190,000). Medicare levy: 2%.
- **Capital gains**: Taxed as income; 50% CGT discount for assets held >12 months by individuals.
- **No wealth tax, no inheritance tax**. Superannuation (retirement savings) at 15% concessional rate.
- **Corporate tax**: 25% (base rate entities) / 30% (others).

## IT / Digital Nomad
No [[digital-nomad]] visa. The tech ecosystem in Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane is strong. Skilled migration points test favours STEM and IT occupations with faster invitations in some categories. Working Holiday Maker visas are popular for younger nomads but are temporary and age-limited.

## Yacht / Maritime
Australia has a massive coastline with world-class marinas (Sydney, Gold Coast, Whitsundays). Foreign-flagged yachts can cruise with a Coastal Cruising Permit. Australian Maritime Safety Authority (AMSA) handles vessel registration and licensing. No special yacht investor pathway.

## Notes for RU citizens
Australia maintains sanctions against Russia. Visa processing for Russian nationals continues but faces enhanced security screening and longer processing times. Skilled migration remains open in principle — nationality is not a bar — but obtaining police clearances and financial documents from Russia is logistically difficult. Small but established Russian-speaking community in Sydney and Melbourne. BIIP closure means investor route is no longer available.

## Country Card

### General description
Australia is an island continent and a federal parliamentary democracy in the Southern Hemisphere, known for its high standard of living, strong economy, and outdoor lifestyle. It operates a transparent points-based skilled migration system (subclasses 189, 190, 491) that is the primary pathway for professionals — no job offer is required for the Skilled Independent (189) visa, though competitive cutoffs (85+ points) are high. The Business Innovation and Investment Program (BIIP) closed to new applicants in July 2024. Australia permits dual citizenship, has a strong passport (182 visa-free destinations), and major cities (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane) rank consistently among the world's most liveable.

### Well-known strong points
- Transparent points-based skilled migration — no job offer needed for subclass 189
- High salaries and strong worker protections (minimum wage ~A$24/hr)
- Generous post-study work rights (2–4 years) with clear study-to-PR pipeline
- Excellent healthcare (Medicare) and education systems
- Very high quality of life: beaches, climate, outdoor culture
- Strong rule of law (WJP 0.80) and stable parliamentary democracy

### Well-known problems
- Very high cost of living (Numbeo 71.4), especially housing in Sydney/Melbourne
- Competitive skilled migration points cutoff (85+ for invitation) — many qualified applicants wait years
- No digital nomad or passive income visa; investor visa (BIIP) closed to new applicants
- Geographic isolation — long flights to most of the world
- Strict absence limits for citizenship (max 90 days in final year)
- Parent visa queue measured in decades (12+ years for contributory parent)
