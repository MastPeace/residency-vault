## Overview
Qatar offers several residency pathways: property-linked residence (temporary or permanent), entrepreneur/executive residence (Jusour programme), and standard employment sponsorship. Personal income tax is 0%. Qatar does NOT offer citizenship by investment and citizenship by naturalization is extremely restricted. ^[https://uglobal.com/immigration/qatar-investor-residency-overview]

## Per-Program Detail

### Property Owner Residency
- **Official name**: Real Estate Residency for Property Owners
- **Conditions**:
  - **Temporary residence**: Property purchase ≥ QAR 730,000 (~$200,000) in designated freehold/usufruct zones. Valid title deed, clean criminal record, medical fitness, health insurance. No minimum stay requirement. ^[https://uglobal.com/immigration/qatar-investor-residency-overview]
  - **Permanent residence**: Property ≥ QAR 3,650,000 (~$1,000,000) in designated zones. Requires Arabic language fluency. Annual quota of ~100 permanent permits — qualifying investment does not guarantee approval. State processing fee ~$824. ^[https://www.qatarliving.com/en/article/how-to-apply-qatar-residency-permit-rp]
- **Duration / renewal**: Temporary: 1 year, renewable. Permanent: permanent residency card, no fixed expiry.
- **Path to citizenship**: None. Permanent residency is not citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://portal.moi.gov.qa
- **Community verdict**: Works well for temporary residency; permanent tier limited by annual quota.

### Entrepreneur Residency (Jusour)
- **Official name**: Invest Qatar Residency Program — Entrepreneur Residency
- **Conditions**: Minimum age 18. Official endorsement from recognized business incubator (QBIC, Scale7, Qatar FinTech Hub, QSTP). Bank statement ≥ QAR 36,500 (~$10,000) over past 3 months. CV, passport, QID (if residing). ^[https://www.invest.qa/en/residency-program]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.invest.qa/en/residency-program
- **Community verdict**: Works well — strong incubator ecosystem, practical pathway for startup founders.

### Executive Residency (Jusour)
- **Official name**: Invest Qatar Residency Program — Executive Residency
- **Conditions**: Must already hold Qatari QID. Minimum salary: QAR 50,000/mo (~$13,700) for Chairman/COO/CTO/CFO/CEO; QAR 80,000/mo (~$22,000) for Executive Director. Employer nomination required. ^[https://www.invest.qa/en/residency-program]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 10 years, renewable. Self-sponsored (no employer tie).
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.invest.qa/en/residency-program
- **Community verdict**: Functional for existing senior executives.

### Employment Residency
- **Official name**: Work Residency Permit (QID)
- **Conditions**: Employer sponsor, valid labour contract, medical exam, biometrics. Minimum wage varies. Employer-dependent.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years, renewable. Tied to employer.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://portal.moi.gov.qa
- **Community verdict**: Standard GCC employment model.

### Student Residency
- **Official name**: Student Residency Permit
- **Conditions**: Enrolment at accredited Qatari institution (e.g., Qatar University, Education City).
- **Duration / renewal**: Duration of programme.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://portal.moi.gov.qa
- **Community verdict**: Functional for enrolled students.

### Family Reunification
- **Official name**: Family Residency Visa
- **Conditions**: Sponsor must hold valid QID, meet minimum salary (varies, typically QAR 10,000+). Covers spouse, children, parents.
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to sponsor's QID.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://portal.moi.gov.qa
- **Community verdict**: Standard, salary threshold may be a barrier.

## Citizenship
**Not available for expatriates.** Qatari citizenship is almost exclusively by descent (Qatari father). Naturalization is theoretically possible under Article 5 of Qatar Nationality Law (25 years continuous residence + Arabic fluency + good conduct + self-sufficiency) but approvals are exceedingly rare — typically a handful per year, if any. Permanent residence (property route) does not lead to citizenship. No citizenship-by-investment programme. ^[https://uglobal.com/immigration/qatar-investor-residency-overview]

## Tax
- **0% personal income tax** — no tax on salary, dividends, capital gains, or rental income.
- **10% corporate tax** on foreign-owned entities. 0% on Qatari/GCC-owned entities.
- **0% VAT** (no value-added tax currently levied).
- **No wealth tax, no inheritance tax.**
- **Excise tax**: 100% on tobacco/energy drinks, 50% on sugary drinks.
- Double taxation treaty network (~80 treaties).
- Common Reporting Standard (CRS) participant.

## IT / Digital Nomad
- **No dedicated digital nomad visa.** Qatar does not offer a remote work visa.
- Technology hub aspirations: Qatar Science & Technology Park (QSTP), Qatar FinTech Hub, Education City.
- Digital infrastructure: excellent (fibre nationwide, 5G coverage).
- Entrepreneurs may use Jusour programme (see above) with incubator endorsement.
- For standard remote workers, tourist visa (90 days, visa-free for 95+ nationalities) is the primary option.

## Yacht / Maritime
- The Pearl Marina, Lusail Marina — premium berthing in Doha.
- Qatar-flag registration available.
- Marinas expanding as part of tourism development (post-World Cup infrastructure).
- Strategic Persian Gulf location.

## Notes for RU citizens
- **Visa-free entry** (90 days) for Russian passport holders.
- **No sanctions**, Qatari banks generally accessible to Russian nationals.
- **0% tax** and high quality of life (Doha).
- **Limited RU community** — smaller than UAE but growing.
- **Direct flights**: Doha–Moscow with Qatar Airways (multiple daily).
- Jusour Entrepreneur Residency accessible for startup founders.
- Banking KYC enhanced but manageable for clean-source funds.

## Community pulse
Qatar provides high quality of life, safety, and 0% tax. Residency pathways are expanding (Jusour programme, property ownership) but remain more limited than UAE. The main friction points: limited permanent residency quota, no citizenship path, hot climate, and high cost of living. The Jusour programme has been well-received by the startup community.

## Country Card

Qatar has the world's highest GDP per capita (PPP ~$112,000) thanks to enormous natural gas reserves. The country invested heavily in infrastructure for the 2022 FIFA World Cup, leaving Doha with world-class airports, metro, and hotels. Personal income tax is 0%, and the cost of living is moderate for a high-income Gulf state. Expatriates make up roughly 85% of the population. Residency options are more limited than the UAE — property investment ($200K+) and QFC-based freelancer visas are the main self-sponsored routes. Citizenship is almost never granted to foreigners.

### Well-known strong points
- Highest GDP per capita in the world (PPP); 0% personal income tax
- World-class infrastructure: Hamad International Airport, Doha Metro, modern healthcare
- Extremely safe with very low crime rates
- Strong rule of law by regional standards (WJP 0.63, best in Gulf after UAE)
- Strategic global hub: Qatar Airways connects to 160+ destinations; central time zone
- Generous state benefits for nationals; expatriates benefit indirectly from high-quality public services

### Well-known problems
- No path to citizenship for expatriates — permanent residency exists but naturalization is almost never granted
- Limited self-sponsored residency options compared to UAE; most expats tied to employer sponsorship
- Extreme summer heat (May–September, 45°C+) with high humidity
- High cost of housing in Doha, especially since World Cup
- Restrictive political environment: no political parties, limited press freedom (FH 25/100)
- 6-month maximum absence rule — stricter than UAE's no-minimum-stay Golden Visa
