## Overview
Montenegro is an EU candidate country that uses the Euro. It offers residence by property investment from €150,000 — informally called the "Montenegro [[golden-visa]]." The former citizenship-by-investment programme closed in December 2022. Citizenship requires 10 years of residence and Montenegrin language proficiency. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/montenegro]

## Residence
**Property-based residence permit** (Law on Foreigners, effective January 17, 2026): ^[https://worldcompanysetup.com/en/getting-a-golden-visa-in-montenegro]
- Minimum property tax value: **€150,000**
- Property must be free of encumbrances (no mortgage, valid building permits)
- Temporary residence permit: 1 year, renewable annually while property is maintained and taxes paid
- No minimum stay requirement to maintain the annual permit
- After 5 years of continuous residence: eligible for permanent residence
- After 10 years of residence (total): potential citizenship eligibility
- Property taxes: 3% on sale/second-hand (varies)
- Family reunification available for spouse and children under 18

**Other residence grounds**: employment, business registration, marriage to Montenegrin, study.

## Citizenship
- 10 years of legal continuous residence
- Montenegrin language proficiency
- Renunciation of previous citizenship generally required
- Citizenship by investment programme CLOSED since December 2022
- By descent: available if parent is Montenegrin citizen

## Tax
Flat income tax: 9% (up to €720/month net) and 11% above. Corporate tax: 9%–15%. Property tax: ~0.1%–1% of market value. No wealth tax. Montenegro has a favorable tax environment by European standards.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Montenegro does not have a dedicated [[digital-nomad]] visa. The property-based residence permit or business registration route works for remote workers. Internet infrastructure is reasonable in urban areas. Low cost of living and Euro denomination are attractive for nomads.

## Yacht / Maritime
Montenegro has an Adriatic coastline (Bay of Kotor, Budva, Bar). Porto Montenegro (Tivat) is a luxury superyacht marina — one of the Mediterranean's premier yachting destinations. Yacht registration under the Montenegrin flag is available. Captain licensing recognition is developing.

## Notes for RU citizens
Montenegro is popular with Russian citizens. Property purchases by RU nationals are allowed. Russian is somewhat understood in tourist areas. The €150,000 property route is accessible and presents a low entry barrier compared to EU [[golden-visa]] analogues. Banking for RU citizens: Sberbank and other Russian-linked financial presence existed historically but situation may have changed. Visa-free entry for Russian citizens (up to 90 days).
