## Overview

Montenegro is an EU candidate country that uses the Euro (unilaterally). Passport ranks ~44th globally with visa-free access to ~125 destinations. It offers multiple residency pathways: property investment (€150,000), a dedicated digital nomad visa (2+2 years), and standard employment/family routes. The former citizenship-by-investment program closed in December 2022. Citizenship requires 10 years of residence. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/montenegro]

## Per-Program Detail

### Investment / Property Residence (investment_residency / passive_income_residency / retired_residency)

- **Official name**: Privremeni boravak po osnovu vlasništva nad nepokretnostima (Temporary residence based on real estate ownership)
- **Conditions**: Real estate in Montenegro with tax value ≥ **€150,000**. Property must be free of encumbrances (no mortgage, valid building permits). No minimum stay requirement. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/montenegro]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable annually while property is maintained and taxes paid.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years continuous temporary residence → permanent residence → 10 years total residence → citizenship. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/montenegro]
- **Official source**: [Government of Montenegro — Law on Foreigners (effective Jan 17, 2026)](https://www.gov.me/en)
- **Community verdict**: Works well — straightforward property-based route. €150,000 threshold is low by European standards. The 10-year citizenship path is the main drawback. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/montenegro]

### Digital Nomad Visa

- **Official name**: Privremeni boravak za digitalne nomade (Temporary residence for digital nomads) — created via 2022 amendments to the Law on Foreigners, implementing rules by Ministry of Interior. ^[https://www.imidaily.com/montenegro-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Conditions**: Regular income of at least **3× the Montenegrin minimum wage** (~€2,010/month as of June 2025). Employment contract with a foreign employer or proof of remote work for non-Montenegrin clients. Valid passport (3+ months). Proof of accommodation. Health insurance. Police clearance certificate. ^[https://wise.com/gb/blog/montenegro-digital-nomad-visa] ^[https://www.visaverge.com/digitalnomads/montenegro-launches-digital-nomad-visa-for-remote-workers]
- **Duration / renewal**: Initial validity **2 years**, extendable once for another 2 years (maximum **4 years** total). ^[https://www.imidaily.com/montenegro-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Path to citizenship**: DN visa time counts toward residence. Total 10 years needed for citizenship.
- **Official source**: [Digital Nomads Montenegro — Ministry of Interior](https://digitalnomads.gov.me/article/legal-status-for-nomads)
- **Application fee**: ~€67 ^[https://www.euronews.com/travel/2026/01/10/want-to-move-to-europe-here-are-all-the-digital-nomads-visas-available-for-remote-workers]
- **Tax exemption**: Qualifying freelancers earning above the specified multiple of average salary may be exempt from Montenegrin personal income tax. ^[https://www.imidaily.com/montenegro-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Community verdict**: Works well — one of the more generous DN visas in Europe (2-year initial, 4-year max, tax exemption, family reunification). Euro economy, low cost of living. ^[https://www.visaverge.com/digitalnomads/montenegro-launches-digital-nomad-visa-for-remote-workers]

### Employment Residency / Highly Skilled Employment

- **Official name**: Privremeni boravak po osnovu zaposlenja (Temporary residence based on employment)
- **Conditions**: Valid employment contract with a Montenegrin employer. Work permit required.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable with continued employment.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years total residence.
- **Official source**: [Government of Montenegro — Ministry of Interior](https://www.gov.me/en)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — work permit process can be slow. Limited job market.

### Startup / Entrepreneur

- **Official name**: Privremeni boravak po osnovu osnivanja privrednog društva
- **Conditions**: Register a company in Montenegro. No minimum capital requirement for LLC (DOO) — €1 nominal.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years total residence.
- **Official source**: [Government of Montenegro](https://www.gov.me/en)
- **Community verdict**: Works well — company registration is straightforward. Useful when property investment route is not desired.

### Student Residency

- **Conditions**: Enrollment in an accredited Montenegrin educational institution.
- **Duration**: Duration of studies.
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### Family Reunification

- **Conditions**: Spouse or minor child of a Montenegrin resident or citizen.
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

## Citizenship

- **By naturalization**: 10 years of legal continuous residence in Montenegro. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/montenegro]
- **Language**: Montenegrin language proficiency required.
- **Dual citizenship**: Generally requires renunciation of previous citizenship.
- **By descent**: Available if parent is Montenegrin citizen.
- **CBI**: Citizenship-by-investment program CLOSED since December 2022.
- **Timeline**: 10 years minimum. Processing: 12+ months.

## Tax

- **Personal income tax**: Flat 9% (up to €720/month net), 11% above. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/montenegro]
- **Corporate tax**: 9%–15%.
- **Property tax**: ~0.1%–1% of market value.
- **No wealth tax**.
- **DN tax perk**: Qualifying digital nomads may be exempt from Montenegrin personal income tax. ^[https://www.imidaily.com/montenegro-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Double taxation treaties**: ~45 countries.

## IT / Digital Nomad

Montenegro has a **dedicated digital nomad residence permit** since 2022. See [[#digital-nomad-visa]] for full details. The 2+2 year structure, tax exemption possibility, Euro-denominated economy, and Adriatic lifestyle make it one of Europe's more attractive DN destinations. Internet infrastructure is good in urban areas (Podgorica, Budva, Kotor, Tivat). Growing coworking scene.

## Yacht / Maritime

Montenegro has a significant Adriatic coastline (Bay of Kotor, Budva, Bar). Porto Montenegro (Tivat) is a luxury superyacht marina — one of the Mediterranean's premier yachting destinations. Yacht registration under the Montenegrin flag is available. Captain licensing recognition is developing. Relevant for yacht-captain considerations.

## Notes for RU citizens

Montenegro is popular with Russian citizens. Visa-free entry (up to 90 days). Russian is somewhat understood in tourist areas. The €150,000 property route is accessible. Banking for RU citizens: historically present but situation evolving. The DN visa provides an alternative non-investment pathway. No sanctions — Montenegro is not in the EU.

## Community Pulse

Expats report Montenegro as a friendly, affordable destination. Property-based residence is well-established. DN visa is relatively new but functioning. Main complaints: slow bureaucracy, 10-year citizenship timeline, limited job market for employment-based residence.

## Status

- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-12
- **Recent changes**: New Law on Foreigners effective Jan 17, 2026 — formalized €150,000 property route. DN visa operating since 2022 amendments. CBI remains closed.
- **Watch items**: EU accession progress (will affect property values). Potential changes to DN visa thresholds as minimum wage rises.

## Country Card

Montenegro is a small Adriatic country of ~623,000 people, an EU candidate that unilaterally uses the Euro. It punches above its weight in residency options: a €150,000 property-investment route, a dedicated 2+2-year digital nomad visa (tax-exempt possible), and a superyacht marina (Porto Montenegro, Tivat). The 10-year citizenship timeline is the main structural drawback, balanced by flat 9–11% income tax and a Mediterranean lifestyle at moderate cost.

### Well-known strong points
- Euro-denominated economy — no currency risk for Euro-earners
- Formal DN visa: 2 years initial, renewable once (4 yr total), income ~€2,010/mo
- Property residence at €150,000 — low threshold by European standards
- Flat 9–11% PIT; DN visa holders may qualify for tax exemption
- Adriatic coastline with world-class yachting (Porto Montenegro, Bay of Kotor)
- Russian-friendly: visa-free, some Russian spoken, no sanctions

### Well-known problems
- Very long citizenship path: 10 years minimum
- Dual citizenship generally requires renunciation of previous citizenship
- Coastal tourism surge (Jul–Aug) drives up short-term rents dramatically
- Slow bureaucracy; limited job market outside tourism and real estate
- Small country — limited urban amenities outside Podgorica and coastal strip
- EU accession slow; political fragmentation in parliament
