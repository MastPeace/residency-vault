## Overview

Georgia offers one of the easiest residence regimes globally: visa-free entry for 100+ nationalities (including Russia) for up to **1 year**. Residence permits are available via business registration, employment, marriage, or investment ($300,000+). The Individual Entrepreneur (IE) regime with **1% turnover tax** on income up to GEL 500,000 (~$185,000) makes it a global hub for IT freelancers and remote workers. Citizenship is difficult: **10 years** of residence + language + history exam + renunciation of existing citizenship. Georgia is not in the EU but has an Association Agreement. Major immigration tightening occurred September 1, 2025, with further amendments March and September 2026. ^[https://globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0456-georgia-to-introduce-new-work-authorization-and-visa-and-residence-permit-requirements-for-foreign-workers]

## Per-Program Detail

### Startup / Entrepreneur (IE Route)

- **Official name**: Individual Entrepreneur (IE) registration
- **Conditions**: Register as an Individual Entrepreneur in Georgia. Simple online process (1 day). No requirement for Georgian client base. ^[https://pbservices.ge/services/individual-entrepreneur-georgia]
- **Tax**: **1% turnover tax** on annual income up to GEL 500,000 (~$185,000). 3% above that threshold. 20% flat income tax alternative. ^[https://pbservices.ge/services/individual-entrepreneur-georgia]
- **Duration / renewal**: IE registration is indefinite. Residence permit based on IE: up to 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years legal residence.
- **Official source**: [Public Service Hall — Business Registration](https://psh.gov.ge/)
- **Community verdict**: Works well — the IE + 1% tax regime is the primary reason Georgia attracts IT freelancers and remote workers. Registration is fast, banking is accessible. ^[https://pbservices.ge/services/individual-entrepreneur-georgia]

### Investment Residency

- **Official name**: Investment residence permit
- **Conditions**: ≥ **$300,000** investment in real estate or business in Georgia. ^[https://pbservices.ge/blog/residency-rules-work-residence-permit-in-georgia]
- **Duration**: 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years legal residence.
- **Official source**: [Public Service Hall](https://psh.gov.ge/)
- **Community verdict**: Works well — 5-year permit is generous. $300K threshold is high relative to local property prices but moderate by international standards.

### Employment Residency / Highly Skilled Employment

- **Official name**: Work residence permit
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with Georgian employer. **September 2026 changes**: work permits now required for many categories previously exempt. Stricter enforcement. ^[https://globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0456-georgia-to-introduce-new-work-authorization-and-visa-and-residence-permit-requirements-for-foreign-workers]
- **Duration**: Up to 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years.
- **Official source**: [Public Service Hall](https://psh.gov.ge/)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — tightening since 2025 has made this route less attractive. IE route is preferred.

### Student Residency

- **Conditions**: Enrollment in an accredited Georgian higher education institution. **From September 1, 2026**: school students excluded from eligibility; government monitoring of physical presence for student residence permit holders introduced; educational institutions must notify authorities in writing of student status changes; suspended students must restore status by **January 1, 2027** — otherwise residence right annulled. ^[https://expathub.ge/georgias-immigration-rules-2026]
- **Duration**: Duration of studies.
- **Community verdict**: Tightened — school-level students excluded, ongoing presence monitoring added.

### Family Reunification

- **Official name**: Marriage/family residence permit
- **Conditions**: **From September 1, 2026**: direct path to permanent residence via marriage abolished. New temporary spousal residence: 1 year (extendable up to 5 years). PR only after 5 years of marriage + residence. Inter-agency commission created to verify marriage authenticity (joint interviews, home visits). Criminal liability for fictitious marriage introduced (Art. 344³ Criminal Code). ^[https://en.justadvisors.ge/blog/residence-permit-marriage-georgia]
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable up to 5 years.
- **Community verdict**: Hardened significantly — marriage route no longer a fast track to PR. Genuine marriages will face more scrutiny.

## Citizenship

- **By naturalization**: **10 years** of legal residence in Georgia. ^[https://pbservices.ge/blog/residency-rules-work-residence-permit-in-georgia]
- **Language**: Georgian language proficiency required.
- **History/law exam**: Knowledge of Georgian history and law.
- **Dual citizenship**: Generally **not recognized** — renunciation of existing citizenship is typically required. Dual citizenship only by Presidential grant (rare).
- **By marriage**: 5 years of marriage to Georgian citizen (faster path).
- **By exception**: For individuals with special merit.
- **Timeline**: 10 years minimum. Very difficult path.

## Tax

- **IE (Small Business Status)**: **1% turnover tax** on annual income up to GEL 500,000 (~$185,000). ^[https://pbservices.ge/services/individual-entrepreneur-georgia]
- **Standard income tax**: 20% flat on Georgian-source income.
- **Foreign-source income**: **0% tax** for individuals earning less than GEL 500,000 from foreign sources (provided income is not from Georgian sources).
- **Corporate tax**: 15% — Estonian model (tax only on distributed profits).
- **Property tax**: 0% on primary residence.
- **No wealth tax, no inheritance tax**.

## IT / Digital Nomad

Georgia functions as a major global hub for remote workers and IT freelancers, now with a **formal digital nomad visa (C5)**. The C5 visa (adopted April 16, 2026 by parliament): multiple entry, up to 12 months continuous stay, visa valid up to 5 years. Requires: confirmed income from foreign companies, citizenship from a "safe countries" list, fee $20–$500. ^[https://werty.tech/en/blog/digital-nomads-in-georgia-visa-c5-rules-and-benefits-for-expats]

The practical alternative remains: enter visa-free (1 year for 100+ nationalities including Russia), register as an Individual Entrepreneur (IE) online in 1 day, pay **1% turnover tax** on income up to GEL 500,000. Banking (Bank of Georgia, TBC) is accessible. Tbilisi has a large expat/relocant community. The IE regime is more tax-advantageous than the C5 visa for most remote workers. ^[https://pbservices.ge/services/individual-entrepreneur-georgia]

## Yacht / Maritime

Georgia has a Black Sea coastline (Batumi, Poti). Yacht infrastructure is underdeveloped. Batumi port primarily handles commercial shipping. Not a significant yachting destination.

## Notes for RU citizens

Georgia is the #1 destination for Russian relocants. Russian citizens can enter visa-free and stay up to **1 year** without a permit. Business registration takes 1 day. Banking is accessible. Russian is widely spoken in Tbilisi and Batumi. The IE 1% tax regime is ideal for IT professionals. Note: the 2025–2026 immigration tightening (work permits, stricter rules, new D6 visa) directly affects RU nationals who previously relied on informal arrangements. Political tensions exist but have not materially affected residence procedures. ^[https://globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0456-georgia-to-introduce-new-work-authorization-and-visa-and-residence-permit-requirements-for-foreign-workers]

## Community Pulse

Georgia remains one of the most accessible countries globally for independent relocants. The IE + 1% tax regime is world-class. Main complaints: immigration tightening (2025–2026), difficulty of citizenship (10 years + renunciation), occasional bureaucratic hurdles. Banking for RU citizens has become more selective post-2022 but remains accessible.
