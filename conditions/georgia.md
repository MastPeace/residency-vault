## Overview
Georgia offers one of the easiest residence regimes globally: visa-free entry for 100+ nationalities (including Russia) for up to 1 year. Residence permits are straightforward via business registration, employment, marriage, or property ownership. Citizenship is difficult (10 years of residence + language + renunciation). Georgia is not in the EU but has an Association Agreement. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## Residence
Georgia overhauled its immigration framework effective September 1, 2025, with further amendments on March 1, 2026, and September 1, 2026. Key residence permit types: ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

| Category | Basis | Duration |
|----------|-------|----------|
| Temporary residence (work) | Employment contract | Up to 1 year, renewable |
| Temporary residence (business) | Registered business with turnover | Up to 1 year |
| Temporary residence (marriage) | Marriage to Georgian citizen | 1 year |
| Permanent residence | 6 years of legal temporary residence | 5 years |
| Investment residence | ≥ $300,000 in real estate or business | 5 years |

**September 2026 changes**: Work permits now required for many categories previously exempt. Fines and deportation procedures strengthened. New D6 visa introduced. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

**Marriage residence**: From September 1, 2026, a new temporary residence permit introduced for spouses of Georgian citizens with stricter requirements. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## Citizenship
- 10 years of legal residence in Georgia
- Georgian language proficiency
- Knowledge of Georgian history and law
- Typically requires renunciation of existing citizenship (Georgia generally does not recognize dual citizenship except by Presidential grant)
- By exception: citizenship may be granted faster for individuals with special merit or those married to Georgians (5 years)

## Tax
Georgia has one of the most attractive tax regimes for individuals:
- 0% income tax for individuals earning less than GEL 500,000 (~$185,000) from foreign sources, provided the income is not from Georgian sources
- 20% flat income tax on Georgian-source income
- 15% corporate tax (Estonian model — only on distributed profits)
- 0% property tax on primary residence
- No wealth tax, no inheritance tax ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## IT / Digital Nomad
Georgia is a major hub for remote workers (functioning as a de-facto [[digital-nomad]] destination without a formal visa), and IT freelancers, especially from Russia and Belarus. No formal [[digital-nomad]] visa exists, but most can enter visa-free for 1 year and operate via:
- Individual Entrepreneur (IE) registration: simple online process, 1% turnover tax for small businesses (< GEL 500,000)
- No requirement for a Georgian client base
- Banking (Bank of Georgia, TBC) is accessible with passport

## Yacht / Maritime
Georgia has a Black Sea coastline (Batumi, Poti). Yacht infrastructure is underdeveloped. The Batumi port handles primarily commercial shipping.

## Notes for RU citizens
Georgia is the #1 destination for Russian relocants. Russian citizens can enter visa-free and stay up to 1 year without a permit. Business registration takes 1 day. Banking is accessible. Russian is widely spoken in Tbilisi and Batumi. Note: the 2026 immigration tightening (work permits, stricter rules) directly affects RU nationals who previously relied on informal arrangements. Political tensions between Georgia and Russia occasionally create uncertainty but have not materially affected residence procedures. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]
