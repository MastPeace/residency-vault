## Overview
Seychelles is an Indian Ocean archipelago of 115 islands offering a Workcation Retreat Program (digital nomad visa) for up to 1 year, and a Permanent Residence by Investment Program for those investing USD 1M+ in a local business. Citizenship is extremely restricted — the country does not readily naturalise foreigners. The tax system is territorial: no personal income tax for non-citizens on foreign-sourced income. ^[https://www.seychelles.govtas.com]

## Per-Program Detail

### digital_nomad_visa — Workcation Retreat Program
- **Official name**: Seychelles Workcation Retreat Program
- **Conditions**: Remote worker employed by foreign company OR business owner/freelancer with foreign clients. Proof of income/funds sufficient for stay. Valid health/travel insurance covering COVID-19. Clean criminal record. Proof of accommodation. Fee: €45 (approx. USD 50). ^[https://esim.holafly.com/visas/seychelles-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Duration**: 12 months. Extension possible upon meeting conditions.
- **Path to citizenship**: None — this is a visitor program, not a residence route.
- **Official source**: https://www.seychelles.govtas.com
- **Community verdict**: Works well — straightforward application, low fee. Main limitation: short duration, no residency credit. Source: nomad forums.

### investment_residency — Permanent Residence by Investment
- **Official name**: Seychelles Permanent Residence Program
- **Conditions**: USD 1M+ direct investment in a new or existing business. At least 5 years of business association/affiliation in Seychelles. Clean criminal record. ^[https://www.imidaily.com/seychelles-investor-pr]
- **Duration**: Permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: Extremely rare. Seychelles does not have a standard naturalisation process for investors. Citizenship is by descent, marriage (to Seychellois, 10+ years), or presidential discretion.
- **Official source**: https://www.imi.gov.sc (Immigration and Civil Status)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — PR is achievable for serious investors, but citizenship is effectively closed. Source: IMI Daily.

## Citizenship
- Citizenship by descent only (born to Seychellois parent).
- Marriage to Seychellois citizen: 10+ years of marriage, requires presidential approval.
- No standard naturalisation-by-residence route for foreigners.
- Dual citizenship: Seychelles generally does not permit dual citizenship.
- Passport: visa-free to ~150 destinations including EU/Schengen, UK, Singapore, Russia.
- **Honest assessment**: Citizenship is NOT a realistic goal for most expats.

## Tax
- **Territorial tax system**: income sourced in Seychelles is taxed; foreign-sourced income is generally not taxed for non-citizens.
- No personal income tax on foreign income for digital nomads.
- 15% business tax for local companies.
- 15% VAT (Goods and Services Tax).
- No capital gains tax, no inheritance tax.
- Digital nomads working for foreign employers: generally not subject to Seychelles income tax on foreign salary.

## IT / Digital Nomad
The Workcation Retreat Program provides a 1-year stay. Internet connectivity has improved significantly with the Seychelles East Africa System (SEAS) submarine cable. Co-working spaces exist on Mahé (Victoria, Eden Island). Cost of living is high — Seychelles imports most goods. Best suited for short-term (3–12 month) remote work stints, not long-term residency planning.

## Yacht / Maritime
Seychelles is a major Indian Ocean yachting destination. Port Victoria (Mahé) handles superyachts. Seychelles Maritime Safety Administration (SMSA) manages vessel registration. Popular cruising ground for Indian Ocean (Maldives, Mauritius, Madagascar). International captain credentials recognised.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa-free entry for 90 days for Russian citizens. Direct flights from Moscow resumed in 2024/2025. No sanctions issues. Russian tourism has grown significantly. Banking: more limited than Mauritius. Seychelles has a notable Russian-speaking expat community.

## Country Card

Seychelles is a stunning Indian Ocean archipelago of 115 islands with Africa's highest GDP per capita PPP (~$35,000) and one of the continent's strongest passports (~150 visa-free). Victoria (Mahé) is one of the world's smallest capitals. The Workcation Retreat Program offers a 1-year stay for remote workers at minimal cost (€45). Permanent residence is available via USD 1M+ investment. The tax system is territorial — foreign-sourced income is generally not taxed for non-citizens. Citizenship is effectively closed to foreigners.

### Well-known strong points
- Africa's highest GDP per capita (~$35,000 PPP); strong passport (~150 visa-free destinations)
- Territorial tax system — no personal income tax on foreign income for non-citizens
- Workcation Retreat Program: 1-year remote work stay, only €45 fee
- Rule of Law 0.63 (rank 43/142), Freedom House 79/100 — strong democratic governance
- English and French are both official; trilingual population
- World-class yachting destination with growing superyacht infrastructure

### Well-known problems
- Citizenship is effectively closed — no standard naturalisation route for foreigners
- Very high cost of living — most goods imported; expat living is expensive
- Tiny population (~130,000) and economy — extremely limited domestic market
- Workcation Retreat Program does not lead to PR or citizenship; no long-term DN pathway
- Permanent residence requires USD 1M+ investment — high entry barrier
- Infrastructure is limited outside Mahé; internet is improving but can be inconsistent
