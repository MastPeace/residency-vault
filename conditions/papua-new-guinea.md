## Overview
Papua New Guinea (PNG) is the largest Pacific Island state (population ~11.8 million) and a resource-export economy (LNG, gold, copper). Residency and citizenship are administered by the Immigration & Citizenship Authority (ICA). Permanent Residence requires 5 years of lawful residence plus falling into a specific category (CEO, business owner, skilled professional, missionary, retiree 55+, or spouse of a citizen); citizenship by naturalisation requires 8 years continuous residence. No investor-visa or CBI shortcut exists. ^[https://ica.gov.pg/permanent-residence/permanent-residence-classes]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Permanent Residence — business/investor categories
- **Conditions**: 5 years lawful residence AND one of: CEO of a company with ≥250 employees and ≥K10M annual turnover; majority owner of a business with ≥5 years operation, net assets/turnover >K2 million; or property investment with net investment ≥K10 million. ^[https://ica.gov.pg/permanent-residence/permanent-residence-classes]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent (subject to continuing compliance).
- **Path to citizenship**: 8 years continuous residence for naturalisation.
- **Official source**: https://ica.gov.pg/permanent-residence/permanent-residence-classes
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (high thresholds; long 5-year pre-qualification).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Permanent Residence — Retired Person (55+)
- **Conditions**: Over 55, demonstrable income of at least K50,000/year, and at least 5 years working in PNG. ^[https://ica.gov.pg/permanent-residence/permanent-residence-classes]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 8 years continuous residence.
- **Official source**: https://ica.gov.pg/permanent-residence/permanent-residence-classes
- **Community verdict**: Limited uptake (requires prior 5 years working in PNG — not a true retiree-arrival visa).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work Permit / Employment Visa
- **Conditions**: Employer-sponsored; police certificate; employer demonstrates role need.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years, tied to employer.
- **Path to citizenship**: Counts toward 8-year residence clock.
- **Official source**: https://ica.gov.pg/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. PNG has no citizenship-by-investment program; naturalisation is residence-based. See [[citizenship-by-investment]].

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 8 years continuous residence (Constitution s.67/69). ^[https://ica.gov.pg/citizenship/application-for-citizenship-by-naturalization]
- **Language / character**: English (or Tok Pisin) knowledge; good character; four PNG citizen referees.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted only with a limited list of ~8 prescribed countries. ^[https://ica.gov.pg/uploads/media/post_file_3195072-visa-information-booklet-2025.pdf]
- **Fees**: Naturalisation K10,000; naturalisation + dual citizenship K15,000.

## Tax
Resident individuals taxed on PNG-source and (for residents) worldwide income; progressive personal rates. No special non-dom or digital-nomad regime. Verify current rates with the Internal Revenue Commission.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Remote workers would need an employment or business category. PNG is not an IT-hub market.

## Yacht / Maritime
Extensive coastline and islands; Port Moresby and Lae are main ports. PNG is not a yacht-registration hub; no dedicated maritime-residency program.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Standard consular visa processing; no dedicated agreements. PNG's security situation and high thresholds make it a niche destination for Russian applicants. [[fiji]] is a far more accessible Pacific option.

## Community Pulse
Very low expat-investor volume. Practical reports emphasise high thresholds (K2M/K10M), a mandatory 5-year residence pre-qualification for PR, and 8-year citizenship clock. Security concerns (Port Moresby crime) are the dominant community theme.

## Country Card

### General description
PNG is a large, resource-rich but poor and institutionally weak Melanesian state. Its residency system is employer- and category-driven with very high thresholds for permanent residence and an 8-year citizenship clock. It is not a mainstream destination for investment migration or digital nomads.

### Well-known strong points
- Large resource economy (LNG, gold, copper)
- English official language and common-law heritage
- Clear (if demanding) statutory PR and citizenship criteria
- Regular elections and an independent judiciary

### Well-known problems
- Very high PR thresholds (K2M–K10M) and mandatory 5-year pre-residence
- 8-year citizenship clock and restricted dual citizenship
- Crime and security concerns in Port Moresby
- Weak rule of law and widespread corruption (FH 61/100 Partly Free)
