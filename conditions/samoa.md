## Overview
Samoa is a small, stable parliamentary democracy in the central South Pacific. It has no visa requirement for most visitors (90-day Visitor's Permit on arrival; 60 days for Russians). Citizenship is acquired mainly by descent or by registration after holding a Permanent Resident Permit for 5 years; the Citizenship Investment Act 2015 shortens this to 3 years for qualifying investors. There is no active direct citizenship-by-investment program. ^[https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Citizenship-Act-2004.pdf]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Citizenship Investment Act 2015 — investor permanent residence
- **Conditions**: Permanent Resident Permit granted on investment/business benefit to Samoa; assessment covers age, qualifications, funds, Samoan language, planned activity, and character. A small annual quota applies. ^[https://mpmc.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Permanent-Residence-application-MPMC105.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent once granted; conditional on continued benefit to Samoa.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years holding the PR permit under the Citizenship Investment Act 2015 (vs 5 years standard). ^[https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Citizenship-Act-2004.pdf]
- **Official source**: https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Citizenship-Act-2004.pdf
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (small quota, high discretion, low uptake).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Permanent Resident Permit (discretionary, non-investment)
- **Conditions**: Assessed on age, health, character, funds, and benefit to Samoa; principal residence required (>50% of year in Samoa). Absence >12 months may cancel the permit. ^[https://mpmc.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Permanent-Residence-application-MPMC105.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: Indefinite, subject to continued residence and good standing.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years holding PR → citizenship by permanent residence.
- **Official source**: https://mpmc.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Permanent-Residence-application-MPMC105.pdf
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (quota pool up to 3 years; high discretion).

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available as direct citizenship. Only the 3-year investor naturalisation route under the Citizenship Investment Act 2015. See [[citizenship-by-investment]].

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 5 years continuous Permanent Resident Permit (standard); 3 years for investors under the Citizenship Investment Act 2015. ^[https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Citizenship-Act-2004.pdf]
- **Language**: Samoan language skills are part of the PR assessment.
- **Dual citizenship**: Generally not permitted for naturalised citizens (renunciation expected).
- **By marriage**: 5 years marriage to a Samoan citizen (residing together).

## Tax
Samoa operates a territorial taxation system for residents; foreign-source income is generally not taxed. Personal income tax is low; consult the Samoa Ministry of Revenue for current rates. ^[https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Citizenship-Act-2004.pdf]

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. A free 90-day Visitor's Permit covers short stays; longer remote work requires a work or permanent-residence permit.

## Yacht / Maritime
Samoa (Apia harbour) is a stop on South Pacific sailing routes; a national shipping registry exists. No special yacht-residency program.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Russian citizens receive a 60-day visa-exempt entry (bilateral agreement, 2017). Longer residence requires a work or PR permit. No dedicated restrictions. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_Samoa]

## Community Pulse
Very low expat volume; no significant backlog or rejection reporting. The PR quota system (pool up to 3 years) and high ministerial discretion are the main practical obstacles. [[fiji]] is a more mainstream Pacific retiree alternative.

## Country Card

### General description
Samoa is a small, politically stable Pacific island state with a Christian heritage and strong communal culture. Its residency system centres on a discretionary, quota-based Permanent Resident Permit and a 3-year investor naturalisation fast-track, but uptake is low and there is no direct citizenship-by-investment product.

### Well-known strong points
- Stable democracy (Freedom House 84/100 Free)
- 3-year investor naturalisation fast-track (shortest in the region)
- Visa-free entry for many nationalities (90 days; 60 for Russians)
- Territorial taxation (foreign income generally untaxed)

### Well-known problems
- Small annual PR quota with multi-year pooling and high discretion
- No digital-nomad visa or direct citizenship-by-investment
- Dual citizenship generally not permitted for naturalised citizens
- Very small economy and thin expat infrastructure
