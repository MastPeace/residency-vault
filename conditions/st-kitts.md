## Overview
St Kitts & Nevis operates the world's oldest Citizenship by Investment (CBI) programme, established in 1984. It is often called the "platinum standard" of CBI. The programme grants lifetime citizenship with no residency requirement, no interview or visit obligation, and full dual citizenship permitted. The passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 157+ countries, including the Schengen Area, UK, and Russia. There is no income tax on worldwide income. ^[https://ciu.gov.kn/cbi-options]

## Per-Program Detail

### citizenship_by_investment
- **Official name**: St Kitts & Nevis Citizenship by Investment Programme
- **Conditions**: Four investment routes:
  - **SISC Donation** (Sustainable Island State Contribution): US$250,000 non-refundable contribution (covers family of up to 4). ^[https://ciu.gov.kn/cbi-options]
  - **Developer's Real Estate**: US$325,000 minimum in government-approved development; must hold 7 years. ^[https://ciu.gov.kn/cbi-options]
  - **Private Real Estate**: US$600,000 minimum for private home purchase; cannot sell for 5 years. ^[https://ciu.gov.kn/cbi-options]
  - **Public Benefit Option (PBO)**: US$250,000 minimum in approved public benefit project. ^[https://ciu.gov.kn/cbi-options]
  - Due diligence fees: ~US$7,500 main applicant; ~US$4,000 per dependent 16+.
- **Duration / renewal**: Citizenship is immediate and irrevocable once granted. Passport renewal every 10 years. No physical presence required at any stage.
- **Path to citizenship**: Direct citizenship upon investment — no prior residence needed. No language exam. No interview (mandatory virtual/consular interview introduced per 2024 OECS MoA).
- **Official source**: https://ciu.gov.kn/cbi-options
- **Community verdict**: Works well (oldest and most established CBI; price increase to $250,000 in 2024 aligned with OECS MoA; no residence, no visit — pure passport acquisition).

## Citizenship
- **By investment**: Immediate upon qualifying investment + due diligence. No residence required.
- **By naturalization**: Available after 14 years of legal residence (rarely used given CBI exists).
- **Dual citizenship**: Fully recognized; no restrictions.

## Tax
- No personal income tax. No capital gains tax. No inheritance/estate tax. No wealth tax. No worldwide income taxation. Territorial system does not apply — simply no income tax at all. ^[https://ciu.gov.kn]
- Corporate tax: 33% (reduced rates for small businesses).

## IT / Digital Nomad
Not available. No dedicated digital nomad visa. Remote workers may enter as tourists (visa-free for many nationalities, typically 90 days).

## Yacht / Maritime
St Kitts & Nevis operates an international ship registry. Yacht registration available under St Kitts & Nevis flag.

## Notes for RU citizens
- CBI is open to Russian nationals (no blanket ban unlike some Caribbean programmes post-2022). Due diligence enhanced.
- Passport provides visa-free access to Russia.
- No requirement to visit or reside — fully remote acquisition.

## Community pulse
- CBI: Mature, well-understood, predictable. Price increase to $250,000 (SISC) and $325,000 (real estate) since July 2024 under OECS MoA.
- Work permits: employer-driven; small local labour market — limited demand.
- No significant backlogs or systemic rejections reported.

## Country Card

### General description
St Kitts & Nevis is the smallest sovereign state in the Western Hemisphere and operates the world's oldest Citizenship by Investment (CBI) programme, established in 1984. Often called the "platinum standard" of CBI, the programme grants lifetime citizenship with zero residency or physical presence requirements. Four investment routes are available from US$250,000 (SISC donation) or US$325,000+ (real estate). The passport provides visa-free/visa-on-arrival access to ~157 destinations, including Schengen, UK, and Russia — among the strongest in the Caribbean CBI market. The country has no personal income tax of any kind. Naturalization without investment takes 14 years (rarely used). Dual citizenship is fully recognized.

### Well-known strong points
- Oldest and most established CBI programme globally (since 1984)
- Strongest CBI passport in the Caribbean: ~157 visa-free destinations
- Zero personal income tax — no income tax, capital gains, inheritance, or wealth tax
- No physical presence required at any stage; fully remote acquisition
- CBI open to Russian nationals (no blanket ban); passport has visa-free Russia access
- Strong rule of law (WJP 0.66) and stable democracy (FH 89/100)

### Well-known problems
- CBI is the most expensive in the Caribbean: US$250,000 SISC donation minimum
- Investment thresholds raised July 2024 under OECS MoA
- No digital nomad visa; no mid-tier residence options — CBI or work permit only
- Very small country with limited infrastructure and healthcare
- Mandatory virtual/consular interview introduced per 2024 OECS MoA
- Naturalization without CBI takes 14 years — impractical for most
