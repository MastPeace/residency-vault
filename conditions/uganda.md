## Overview
Uganda's Directorate of Citizenship and Immigration Control (DCIC) operates a detailed Entry Permit system with classes A through H covering diplomatic, investment, employment, and independent-means residence. The system is fully online via the e-visa portal. Uganda permits dual citizenship since 2005. There is no dedicated digital nomad visa or retirement visa. Citizenship is attainable but requires a long residence period. ^[https://www.immigration.go.ug/permit/entry-permit]

## Per-Program Detail

### passive_income_residency — Class H Ordinary Residents
- **Official name**: Class H — Ordinary Residents Entry Permit
- **Conditions**: Assured income derived from sources outside Uganda. Undertaking not to accept any employment in Uganda. ^[https://www.immigration.go.ug/permit/entry-permit]
- **Duration**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Theoretically after 20+ years continuous residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.ug/permit/entry-permit
- **Community verdict**: Limited data — very few applicants use this route. Uganda is not a mainstream passive-income residency destination.

### employment_residency — Class G2 Expatriate Employees
- **Official name**: Class G2 — Expatriate Employment Entry Permit
- **Conditions**: Job offer from registered Ugandan employer. Employer sponsorship (organisation code required). Certified qualifications. Police clearance. ^[https://www.immigration.go.ug/permit/entry-permit]
- **Duration**: 1–2 years, renewable. EAC citizens (Kenya, Rwanda): exempted from fees (still must apply). Tanzanian citizens: USD 1,550/year. ^[https://www.immigration.go.ug/permit/entry-permit]
- **Path to citizenship**: 20+ years continuous residence (constitutional requirement).
- **Official source**: https://www.immigration.go.ug/permit/entry-permit
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — functional online system (visas.immigration.go.ug), 2–3 working days processing for permits.

## Citizenship
- By registration/naturalisation: constitution requires a long period of residence — typically 20+ years of continuous legal residence.
- Marriage to Ugandan citizen: reduced timeline possible.
- Dual citizenship: Permitted since 2005 Constitutional Amendment. ^[https://www.immigration.go.ug]
- Passport: visa-free to ~65 destinations. EAC free movement.
- **Honest assessment**: Uganda citizenship is a very long-term proposition. Dual citizenship permitted is a plus, but the 20-year timeline makes it impractical for most.

## Tax
- Residence-based taxation. Tax resident if >183 days or permanent home in Uganda.
- Personal income tax: progressive 10%–40% (top rate on UGX 120M+/yr, ~USD 32,000).
- Corporate tax: 30%.
- VAT: 18%.
- Rental income tax: 12%–30%.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated DN visa. Kampala is the primary hub. Internet: 4G/LTE (Airtel, MTN), fibre in business districts. Co-working spaces emerging (Hive Colab, Outbox). Cost of living: low. Remote workers typically use tourist visas or the Class H (assured income) permit. Kampala has a lively expat scene (NGOs, tech, startups).

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked country. Lake Victoria offers inland water activities (Entebbe, Jinja). Not a maritime yachting destination.

## Notes for RU citizens
eVisa for Russian citizens (single entry, 90 days). Uganda maintains neutral relations with Russia. Small Russian-speaking community. Kampala is safe by regional standards. Banking: functional.
