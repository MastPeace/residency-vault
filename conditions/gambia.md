## Overview
The Gambia is a small West African country (pop. ~2.8M) almost entirely surrounded by Senegal, with a short Atlantic coastline. English is the official language. Naturalization requires 15 years of continuous residence under the 1997 Constitution — one of the longest in Africa. Passport: 62 visa-free destinations (relatively strong for the region). ^[https://gid.gov.gm/citizenship]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work permit / Residence permit
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from Gambian employer; labor market approval.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 15 years continuous residence for naturalization. ^[https://gid.gov.gm/citizenship]
- **Official source**: Gambia Immigration Department (https://gid.gov.gm).
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data (small expat community, mainly NGO/diplomatic/tourism).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student visa
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Gambian institution; proof of financial means.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: Residence time counts toward 15-year clock.
- **Official source**: Gambian embassies / consulates.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Family residence
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; proof of relationship and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 15 years; spouse of Gambian citizen: 7 years marriage + residence. ^[https://gid.gov.gm/citizenship]
- **Official source**: Gambia Immigration Department.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

## Citizenship
- **Residence**: 15 years continuous ordinary residence for naturalization by registration. Spouse of Gambian citizen: 7 years marriage + residence. ^[https://gid.gov.gm/citizenship]
- **Language**: English proficiency expected.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted (since 1997 Constitution).
- **Processing**: Application to Ministry of Interior / Office of the President. Police character certificate required.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive income tax; corporate tax ~27%.
- **Remote workers**: No special regime. Tax enforcement is limited but improving.
- **General**: Dalasi (GMD) currency. Developing tax administration.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. Internet in the Greater Banjul Area is adequate (4G); limited elsewhere. Coworking is nascent. The Gambia is a minor tourism destination (beach resorts) but not a recognized nomad hub. [[senegal]] is the dominant regional alternative.

## Yacht / Maritime
Short Atlantic coastline (~80 km) with the Gambia River and port of Banjul. Minor yacht tourism exists (winter season). No flag-of-convenience registration. Limited maritime infrastructure.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Visa required (obtainable on arrival for some nationalities; check current policy). No special RU programs. Small Russian expat/tourism presence. The Gambian passport is relatively strong in the region (62 destinations) but offers limited global mobility.

## Community Pulse
The Gambia has a modest expat community centered on tourism, NGOs, and diplomacy. Not a major immigration destination. The 15-year naturalization requirement is among the longest in Africa. Not discussed on nomad or investor-immigration forums.

## Country Card

### General description
The Gambia is a small, English-speaking West African country straddling the Gambia River, almost entirely surrounded by Senegal. It has a modest tourism-based economy, a relatively strong passport for the region (62 destinations), and dual citizenship is permitted. However, the 15-year naturalization requirement is among the longest in Africa.

### Well-known strong points
- English as the official language (rare in francophone-dominated West Africa)
- Relatively strong passport (62 destinations) for the region
- Dual citizenship permitted
- Modest beach tourism infrastructure

### Well-known problems
- Very long naturalization period (15 years — among Africa's longest)
- High poverty rate; limited economic opportunities
- Small, river-shaped geography limits domestic markets
- Rule of law challenges (FH 50/100, WJP rank 87/142)

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-12
- **Recent changes**: No known recent changes. Democratic transition since 2017 (post-Jammeh era) ongoing.
