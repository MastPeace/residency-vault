## Overview
Cyprus is an EU member state with a strong passport. It offers permanent residency by investment from €300,000 and a non-domiciled tax regime that makes it a premier tax-planning jurisdiction. Citizenship requires 8 years of residence (or 5/4 for highly skilled workers with Greek language). ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## Residence
**Permanent residency by investment (Category F fast-track)**: minimum €300,000 investment in real estate or business (plus VAT). Processed within ~6 months. The permit does not expire; holders need not renew. Covers the whole family. Income requirement: €9,568/year for main applicant + €4,613 per dependent. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

**Temporary residence (Pink Slip)**: for employed workers, students, and visitors staying beyond 90 days.

**Yellow Slip (MEU1)**: for EU citizens registering residence; leads to permanent residence (MEU3) after 5 years. From August 2026, all older paper-format documents must be replaced with biometric cards per EU Regulation 2019/1157. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## Citizenship
- **Naturalization (M127)**: 8 years of residence (2555 days) within a 10-year period, including 12 continuous months before application.
- **Highly skilled workers**: 5 years (A2 Greek) or 4 years (B1 Greek).
- **Marriage to Cypriot**: Previously 3 years of residence; a September 2026 bill proposes reducing this to 6 months for spouses living in Cyprus (marriage 3+ years) and allowing spouses abroad to apply with 5+ years of marriage. ^[raw/articles/analyzed-2026-09-13.json]

## Tax
Cyprus is a premier tax jurisdiction due to its **non-domiciled (non-dom) regime**:
- 0% tax on dividends and interest for non-domiciled residents (for 17 years)
- 0% tax on foreign-sourced income unless remitted
- Corporate tax: 12.5%
- Personal income tax: progressive up to 35%
- Effective January 1, 2026: applicants no longer need to prove non-tax-residence elsewhere; dual tax residency now permitted. ^[raw/articles/monitor-snapshot-2026-09-13.json]

## IT / Digital Nomad
Cyprus does not have a dedicated [[digital-nomad]] visa, but its [[nhr]]-like non-dom tax regime (0% on dividends for 17 years) attracts remote business owners, but the temporary residence permit (Pink Slip) and the immigration permit routes work for remote workers and IT professionals. The 12.5% corporate tax and non-dom benefits are strong draws for tech entrepreneurs.

## Yacht / Maritime
Cyprus has one of the largest ship registries in the EU. It offers competitive tonnage tax for qualifying shipowners, yacht leasing schemes, and recognized captain licensing. Yacht registration under the Cypriot flag is available.

## Notes for RU citizens
Cyprus is popular with Russian high-net-worth individuals. The €300,000 permanent residency route is widely used. Russian is spoken in Limassol (large RU community). The 2026 non-dom change (dual tax residency allowed) is particularly beneficial for RU citizens who maintain ties to Russia. Bank account opening in Cyprus may require enhanced due diligence for RU passport holders.
