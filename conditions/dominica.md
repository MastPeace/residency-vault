## Overview
Dominica (Commonwealth of Dominica, not the Dominican Republic) runs one of the world's oldest and cheapest citizenship-by-investment (CBI) programmes, administered by the Citizenship by Investment Unit (CBIU). Citizenship is granted directly for a non-refundable contribution with **no physical presence required**. It is the lowest single-applicant entry point in the Caribbean CBI market. ^[https://www.cbiu.gov.dm/dominica-citizenship]

## Per-Program Detail

### citizenship_by_investment
- **Official name**: Citizenship by Investment Programme (Economic Diversification Fund / Approved Real Estate)
- **Conditions (EDF donation)**: US$200,000 single applicant; US$250,000 main applicant + up to 3 dependants; +US$25,000 per additional dependant under 18; +US$40,000 per additional dependant 18+. Approved Real Estate: min US$200,000, held ≥3 years. Due diligence US$7,500 main applicant, US$4,000 per dependant 16+. Mandatory interview for applicants 16+. ^[https://www.cbiu.gov.dm/investment-options/economic-diversification-fund]
- **Duration / renewal**: Citizenship is permanent and hereditary; no residence or renewal requirement.
- **Path to citizenship**: Direct grant on approval (typically ~3–4 months processing). ^[https://www.cbiu.gov.dm/news/dominica-citizenship-cost-and-fees]
- **Official source**: https://www.cbiu.gov.dm/dominica-citizenship
- **Community verdict**: Works well (lowest Caribbean entry price; mature, well-established programme; mandatory interview added for all 16+ applicants).

## Citizenship
Direct citizenship by investment (see above). Naturalisation by residence is also available at ministerial discretion after long residence, but CBI is the standard route. Dual citizenship is permitted.

## Tax
No capital gains, inheritance, or wealth taxes. Non-resident CBI citizens pay no Dominican income tax; residents are taxed on local income only. No minimum-stay requirement means CBI holders typically owe nothing.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. CBI holders with Dominican citizenship can live and work there if they choose, but the programme imposes no residency.

## Yacht / Maritime
Yacht registration under the Dominica flag is available. Caribbean yachting hub with hurricane-season exposure (Jun–Nov).

## Notes for RU Citizens
Dominica CBI is open to Russian applicants subject to enhanced due diligence (same as all applicants). No EU visa-free access (Dominica passport ≠ Schengen); use for mobility to China, Singapore, Russia, Hong Kong. Apply via authorised agents.

## Community Pulse
Widely regarded as the cheapest and most reliable Caribbean CBI. The July 2024 Caribbean MoU aligned prices upward ($200,000 floor), and the mandatory-interview requirement (2023+) slowed but did not halt approvals. Few residency complaints since no stay is required.

## Country Card

### General description
Dominica is a mountainous Eastern Caribbean island nation between Guadeloupe and Martinique, with a small population and an economy built on agriculture, eco-tourism, and — centrally — its citizenship-by-investment programme, which is a major revenue source. It is the "Nature Isle" of the Caribbean, marketed for eco-tourism and offshore/economic citizenship.

### Well-known strong points
- Lowest-cost Caribbean CBI entry point ($200,000 single)
- No physical-residence requirement for citizenship
- Established, government-run programme since 1993
- Visa-free access to China, Russia, Singapore, Hong Kong
- Stable parliamentary democracy (FH 92/100)

### Well-known problems
- Small, hurricane-prone island (Hurricane Maria 2017 heavily damaged it)
- No Schengen/EU visa-free access
- Mandatory interview + strict due diligence slow some applications
- Limited economy and local job market
