## Overview
Poland is a large EU/Schengen economy with a fast-growing tech sector and the EU's 5th-largest population. The primary residence routes are employment-based: the EU [[blue-card]], standard work permits, and sole proprietorship (JDG). Poland does NOT offer a Golden Visa, a passive-income residence, or a dedicated digital nomad visa. Citizenship tightened significantly in 2026: 10 years residence (up from 8), C1 Polish language, and civic exam. ^[https://www.visaverge.com/news/polands-ministry-of-the-interior-tightens-citizenship-rules-to-curb-immigration]

## Per-Program Detail

### Highly Skilled Employment — EU Blue Card (Niebieska Karta UE)
- **Official name**: Niebieska Karta UE (EU Blue Card)
- **Conditions**: University degree (min. 3 years) OR 5 years documented professional experience at degree-equivalent level. Employment contract (umowa o pracę) ≥6 months (preferably ≥1 year). **Salary threshold (2026)**: PLN 13,355.34/month gross (150% of GUS 2025 average of PLN 8,903.56). ^[https://latwy-start.pl/en/service/blue-card] B2B/JDG contracts do NOT qualify. No labor market test.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years, renewable. After 2 years on Blue Card in Poland: job change only requires notification, not re-approval. Blue Card time in other EU countries partially counts toward EU long-term residence. ^[https://mojafirma.org/eu-blue-card-in-poland]
- **Path to citizenship**: EU long-term residence after 5 years → permanent residence → citizenship (total ~10 years).
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc-en/eu-blue-card
- **Community verdict**: Works well — straightforward for qualified IT professionals; fast processing via MOS online system. B2B contractors excluded.

### Employment Residency — Single Permit
- **Official name**: Zezwolenie na pobyt czasowy i pracę (Single Permit)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Polish employer. Salary ≥ minimum wage (PLN 4,806/mo gross in 2026). Labor market test (opinia starosty) usually required. ^[https://migrant.poznan.uw.gov.pl/en/procedury/temporary-residence-permit-high-qualified-workers-eu-blue-card]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years, renewable. Applications via MOS online system since April 27, 2026. ^[https://migrant.poznan.uw.gov.pl/en/procedury/temporary-residence-permit-high-qualified-workers-eu-blue-card]
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years → EU long-term residence or permanent residence → citizenship (~10 years total).
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc-en/temporary-residence-permit
- **Community verdict**: Works well — but processing times can be 6–12 months in major cities (Warsaw, Kraków).

### Startup/Entrepreneur — Sole Proprietorship (JDG)
- **Official name**: Działalność gospodarcza (JDG) → self-sponsored TRC
- **Conditions**: Register sole proprietorship in Poland. Demonstrate stable income, accommodation, health insurance. Common route for IT freelancers.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Same as employment — ~10 years total.
- **Official source**: https://www.biznes.gov.pl/en
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — popular among IT freelancers but scrutiny increasing. Requires demonstrated business activity, not shell registration.

### Student Residency
- **Official name**: Zezwolenie na pobyt czasowy — studia
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in a Polish university or educational institution. Proof of sufficient funds and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: Duration of study program (max 3 years per permit), renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student years count partially toward permanent residence (50% rate).
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc-en/temporary-residence-permit
- **Community verdict**: Works well — straightforward for enrolled students.

### Family Reunification
- **Official name**: Zezwolenie na pobyt czasowy — połączenie z rodziną
- **Conditions**: Sponsor holds valid Polish residence permit ≥2 years (or Blue Card with no waiting period). Stable income and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years, renewable. Family members receive work authorization immediately with Blue Card sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: Same as sponsor's path.
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc-en/family-reunification
- **Community verdict**: Works well — Blue Card family reunification especially smooth (no 2-year wait).

## Citizenship
- **Standard**: 10 years of legal residence (2026 tightened rules — up from previous 8-year benchmark) ^[https://www.visaverge.com/news/polands-ministry-of-the-interior-tightens-citizenship-rules-to-curb-immigration]
- **Marriage to Pole**: 3 years in marriage + 2 years residence ^[https://progressholding.pl/en/polish-citizenship-2025-how-many-years-to-live]
- **Pole's Card (Karta Polaka)**: 1 year residence after obtaining the card
- **Language**: B1 Polish (rumored increase to C1 under 2026 reform) ^[https://www.visaverge.com/news/polands-ministry-of-the-interior-tightens-citizenship-rules-to-curb-immigration]
- **Civic exam**: Mandatory as of 2026 ^[https://www.visaverge.com/news/polands-ministry-of-the-interior-tightens-citizenship-rules-to-curb-immigration]
- Dual citizenship permitted

## Tax
- Progressive PIT: 12% (up to PLN 120,000) / 32% above
- Flat 19% linear rate for self-employed (JDG)
- IP Box: 5% on qualifying IP income
- Social security: ~13.7% employee (capped at ~PLN 208,000 base in 2026)
- Corporate tax: 9% (small taxpayers) / 19% (standard)

## IT / Digital Nomad
Poland has NO dedicated digital nomad visa. IT professionals use:
- **EU Blue Card** — strongest route, no labor market test, salary ≥ PLN 13,355.34/mo
- **JDG (sole proprietorship)** — self-sponsored, flat 19% tax
- **Standard work permit** — lower salary threshold, labor market test
Major tech hubs: Warsaw, Kraków, Wrocław, Gdańsk.

## Yacht / Maritime
Baltic Sea coastline (Gdańsk, Gdynia, Szczecin). Yacht registration under Polish flag available. Growing marina infrastructure. Not a primary yachting destination.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Polish consulates in Russia have severely reduced operations. Visa issuance for Russian citizens is among the most restricted in the EU. Residence permit applications face highest scrutiny of any EU country. B1/C1 Polish language is demanding. Pole's Card route viable for those with documented Polish ancestry. Some Russian nationals have successfully used JDG self-employment route.
