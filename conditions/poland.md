## Overview
Poland is a large EU/Schengen economy with a fast-growing tech sector. The primary residence route for skilled workers is the [[blue-card]], with a salary threshold of PLN 13,355.34/month (2026). Citizenship requires 8 years of permanent residence (or 3 years for permanent residents with Polish ancestry through the Pole's Card). ^[https://latwy-start.pl/en/service/blue-card]

## Residence
**EU Blue Card (Niebieska Karta UE)**: for highly qualified non-EU workers. ^[https://latwy-start.pl/en/service/blue-card]
- University degree or equivalent qualification
- Employment contract ≥ 12 months
- **Salary threshold (2026)**: PLN 13,355.34/month gross (150% of 2025 average salary of PLN 8,903.56)
- Valid: up to 3 years
- No labor market test
- Family members get residence + work authorization immediately (no 2-year wait)
- After 5 years: EU long-term residence

**Other residence permits**:
- Temporary residence and work permit (standard, ≤ 3 years)
- Single Permit (work + residence combined)
- All applications submitted online via the MOS (Case Handling Module) portal since April 27, 2026 ^[https://migrant.poznan.uw.gov.pl/en/procedury/temporary-residence-permit-high-qualified-workers-eu-blue-card]

## Citizenship
- 8 years of permanent residence (standard)
- 3 years of permanent residence for Pole's Card holders
- **B1 Polish language proficiency**
- Clean criminal record
- Stable income and accommodation
- Poland permits dual citizenship

## Tax
Progressive income tax: 12% (up to PLN 120,000) / 32% above. A flat 19% linear rate for self-employed. Social security: ~13.7% employee (capped at ~PLN 208,000 base). IP Box regime: 5% tax on qualifying IP income.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Poland does not have a dedicated [[digital-nomad]] visa. IT professionals primarily use:
- The [[blue-card]] — strong route with no labor market test
- Standard work permit
- Sole proprietorship (JDG) for freelancers (self-sponsored residence permit)
Warsaw, Kraków, Wrocław, and Gdańsk are major tech hubs.

## Yacht / Maritime
Poland has a Baltic Sea coastline (Gdańsk, Gdynia, Szczecin). Yacht registration is available under the Polish flag. Growing marina infrastructure but not a primary yachting destination.

## Notes for RU citizens
Polish consulates in Russia have reduced operations. Visa issuance for Russian citizens is severely restricted. Residence permit applications from Russian nationals face the highest scrutiny of any EU country due to political tensions. The B1 Polish language requirement is demanding. Historically, some Russian nationals have used the Pole's Card route if they have Polish ancestry.
