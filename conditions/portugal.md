## Overview
Portugal is one of Europe's most popular immigration destinations, offering multiple residence paths: the [[golden-visa]] (ARI), the [[d7-visa]] (passive income), the [[d8-visa]] (digital nomad), and work permits. The immigration system is managed by AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo), which replaced SEF in 2023. Portugal grants EU citizenship after 5 years of legal residence. ^[https://aima.gov.pt/pt]

**May 2026 nationality law change**: Most nationals now require 10 years of legal residency for citizenship (7 years for EU and CPLP citizens). This is a significant increase from the previous 5-year benchmark. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/portugal-golden-visa-changes]

## Residence
Key residence routes for non-EU nationals in 2026:

| Route | Visa | Basis | Income/Investment |
|-------|------|-------|-------------------|
| [[golden-visa]] | ARI | Qualifying investment | Fund: €500K; no real estate since Oct 2023 |
| [[d7-visa]] | D7 | Passive income | ≥ Portuguese minimum wage (€920/mo in 2026) |
| [[d8-visa]] | D8 | Digital nomad/remote work | ≥ 4× minimum wage (~€3,680/mo) |
| Work visa | D1/D2 | Employment contract | Job offer |
| Family reunification | D6 | Family tie | Sponsor must hold valid permit; 2-year rule now applies |

**AIMA renewal portal**: As of July 10, 2026, residence permit renewals are done via AIMA's Portal de Renovações. Ordinary permits expiring Jul 2025–Oct 2026 and ARI cards expired Feb 2020–Oct 2026 are within the current window. ARI renewal fee: €632.10 + €3,157.80 = €3,789.90 per person. ^[https://movingto.com/pt/portugal-residence-permit-renewal]

## Citizenship
- **Pre-May 2026**: 5 years of legal residence (standard naturalization)
- **Post-May 2026 (Law 61/2025)**: 10 years for most nationals; 7 years for EU/CPLP citizens
- Portuguese language proficiency: A2 (basic)
- Dual citizenship permitted
- Golden Visa minimum stay (7 days year 1, 14 days per 2-year period) counts toward citizenship clock

## Tax
Portugal's [[nhr]] (Non-Habitual Resident) regime offers:
- 20% flat tax (or 0%, depending on profession) on qualifying Portuguese-source professional income for 10 years
- 0% tax on most foreign-source income (pensions taxed at 10% under 2024 NHR reforms)
- NHR 2.0 (IFICI — Tax Incentive for Scientific Research and Innovation) introduced 2024 for qualifying R&D professionals

Standard progressive rates: 14.5%–48%.

## IT / Digital Nomad
The [[d8-visa]] is the dedicated route for remote workers and digital nomads. Requirements:
- Income ≥ 4× Portuguese minimum wage (~€3,680/mo in 2026)
- Remote work contract or freelance engagement with foreign entities
- Initial permit 1 year, renewable 2+2 years
- After 5 years: permanent residence / citizenship eligibility

## Yacht / Maritime
Portugal has an extensive Atlantic coastline and a significant maritime industry. Yacht registration under the Portuguese flag is available. Recognized yacht licensing and captain credentials. Major ports: Lisbon, Cascais, Portimão, Vilamoura. The Algarve is a key yachting hub.

## Notes for RU citizens
Portuguese consulates in Russia may have reduced operations. Applications are typically routed through third-country consulates or directly in Portugal. The D7 (passive income) and D8 (remote work) routes are accessible. Portuguese language requirement (A2) is one of the lowest in the EU. No official restrictions against Russian nationals, but enhanced due diligence for Golden Visa applicants.
