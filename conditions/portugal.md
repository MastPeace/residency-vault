## Overview
Portugal is one of Europe's most popular immigration destinations. Its immigration system is managed by AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo), which replaced SEF in 2023. Portugal offers multiple residence paths: D7 (passive income), D8 (digital nomad), ARI/Golden Visa (investment), and work permits. EU citizenship is available after 10 years of legal residence (7 for EU/CPLP nationals) under Lei Orgânica 1/2026, in force since 19 May 2026. ^[https://aima.gov.pt/pt]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: ARI — Autorização de Residência para Atividade de Investimento (Golden Visa)
- **Conditions**: €500,000 in qualifying investment funds; €250,000 cultural/artistic production; €500,000 scientific research; €500,000 business + 5 jobs (or €250,000 + maintain 5 jobs). Real estate route closed since October 2023. ^[https://getgoldenvisa.com/portugal-golden-visa-program]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year permit, renewable every 2 years. Minimum stay: 7 days year 1, 14 days per subsequent 2-year period.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years legal residence (7 for EU/CPLP); A2 Portuguese. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/portugal-golden-visa-changes]
- **Official source**: https://aima.gov.pt/pt
- **Community verdict**: Works well (fund route mature; real estate abolition reduced options but fund route clean).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: D7 — Visto de Residência para Reformados e Titulares de Rendimentos
- **Conditions**: Passive income ≥ €920/mo in 2026 (Portuguese minimum wage); +50% for spouse (€460), +30% per dependent child (€276). Savings buffer ~€11,040 for single applicant. Income must be passive: pensions, dividends, rental income, royalties. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/portugal-d7-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year initial, renewable for 3 years. Permanent residence after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years (7 EU/CPLP); A2 Portuguese.
- **Official source**: https://aima.gov.pt/pt (Art. 77, Law 23/2007)
- **Community verdict**: Works well (lowest income threshold in Western Europe; AIMA backlogs are main friction).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: D8 — Visto para Nómadas Digitais / Trabalhadores Remotos
- **Conditions**: Remote work income ≥ €3,680/mo (4× minimum wage); employment contract or freelance engagement with foreign entities. ^[https://www.pearlsofportugal.com/visa-portugal/portugal-digital-nomad-d8-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year initial, renewable 2+2 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years (7 EU/CPLP); A2 Portuguese.
- **Official source**: https://aima.gov.pt/pt
- **Community verdict**: Works well (high threshold but straightforward; 6–9 month processing times common).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: EU Blue Card (Cartão Azul UE) / Tech Visa
- **Conditions**: University degree + binding job offer; salary ≥ 1.5× national average gross annual salary. Tech Visa offers fast-track for certified tech companies.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 4 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years (7 EU/CPLP); A2 Portuguese.
- **Official source**: https://aima.gov.pt/pt
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: D2 — Visto para Empreendedores
- **Conditions**: Viable business plan; proof of sufficient funds; incorporation or intent to incorporate in Portugal; job creation expected.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years (7 EU/CPLP); A2 Portuguese.
- **Official source**: https://aima.gov.pt/pt
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (subjective business plan evaluation; consular discretion high).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: D1 — Visto de Trabalho por Conta de Outrem
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from Portuguese employer; employer must demonstrate no suitable EU/EEA candidate available.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, tied to employment contract.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years (7 EU/CPLP); A2 Portuguese.
- **Official source**: https://aima.gov.pt/pt
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (labor market test can be hurdle; easier for shortage occupations).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: D4/D5 — Visto de Estudo
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Portuguese institution; proof of financial means (~€920/mo); health insurance.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: Time counts toward 10-year clock (7 EU/CPLP).
- **Official source**: https://aima.gov.pt/pt
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: D6 — Reagrupamento Familiar
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid residence permit; proof of accommodation; sufficient financial means. 2-year rule for some categories since 2026.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years (7 EU/CPLP); A2 Portuguese.
- **Official source**: https://aima.gov.pt/pt
- **Community verdict**: Works well (but AIMA processing delays).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: D7 (retirement route) — same as passive income
- **Conditions**: Pension income ≥ €920/mo (Portuguese minimum wage); +50% spouse, +30% child. D7 is the umbrella covering both passive-income and retired applicants.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year initial, renewable for 3 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years (7 EU/CPLP); A2 Portuguese.
- **Official source**: https://aima.gov.pt/pt
- **Community verdict**: Works well (popular retirement destination).

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. Portugal does not offer direct citizenship by investment.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 10 years legal residence (standard); 7 years for EU/CPLP nationals; 3 years if married to Portuguese citizen. Pre-May 2026 applications: 5 years under old law. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/portugal-golden-visa-changes]
- **Language**: A2 Portuguese (basic).
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted.
- **Golden Visa pathway**: Minimum-stay days (7/14 per period) count toward citizenship clock.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive 14.5%–48%.
- **NHR**: Closed to new applicants (ended 31 Dec 2023). ^[https://www.portutax.com/news/nhr-20-in-portugal-what-the-ifici-regime-means-for-expats-in-2026-81]
- **IFICI (NHR 2.0)**: 20% flat tax on qualifying Portuguese-source professional income; exemptions on much foreign income. Requires EQF Level 6 qualification + 3 years relevant experience, or EQF Level 8 doctorate, in designated high-value activities. Retirees and passive-income earners are excluded. ^[https://imin-portugal.com/blog/portugal-ifici-tax-regime]
- **D7 holders**: Generally subject to standard progressive taxation; IFICI rarely accessible.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Portugal's D8 visa is the dedicated remote-work route. Income threshold: €3,680/mo (4× minimum wage). Lisbon, Porto, and Madeira are major nomad hubs with strong coworking infrastructure. No separate "IT specialist" visa; D8 + EU Blue Card cover the market.

## Yacht / Maritime
Extensive Atlantic coastline; major ports: Lisbon, Cascais, Portimão, Vilamoura. Portuguese flag yacht registration available. Algarve is a key yachting hub. Recognized captain licensing under international conventions.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Consulates in Russia may have reduced operations — apply via third-country consulates or in Portugal. D7 (passive income) and D8 (remote work) accessible. A2 Portuguese is one of the lowest EU language bars. Enhanced due diligence for Golden Visa applicants. No official restrictions beyond EU sanctions screening.

## Community Pulse
AIMA backlogs are the dominant complaint across forums: 6–9 months for initial D7/D8 processing, renewal queues. The 10-year citizenship reform (May 2026) was a shock but still among the shortest in Western Europe. Golden Visa fund route is smooth and well-established. D7 income threshold (€920/mo) is the lowest in Western Europe — heavily used by retirees and passive-income holders.

## Country Card

### General description
Portugal is a southern European country on the Iberian Peninsula, bordered by Spain and the Atlantic Ocean. It has a service-based economy with strong tourism, a growing tech sector, and one of the lowest costs of living in Western Europe. Portugal is among the most popular EU destinations for expats and remote workers, offering multiple residence pathways (D7 passive income, D8 digital nomad, Golden Visa investment fund route) with moderate income thresholds and a clear — if recently lengthened — path to citizenship.

### Well-known strong points
- Lowest passive-income residence threshold in Western Europe (D7: €920/mo)
- High visa-free passport strength (188 destinations)
- Safe, politically stable parliamentary democracy (FH 96/100)
- Very high sunshine hours (2,799h Lisbon) and mild climate
- Moderate cost of living (Numbeo index 41.2) with good quality of life
- A2 Portuguese language requirement for citizenship — lowest in EU

### Well-known problems
- Citizenship now requires 10 years of residence (up from 5, reformed May 2026)
- AIMA immigration agency backlogs: 6–9 month processing times common
- Bureaucratic inefficiency and slow public administration
- Rising housing costs in Lisbon, Porto, and Algarve driven by foreign demand
- Low median salary (~€1,150/mo net) relative to EU average
- NHR tax regime closed to new applicants (replaced by narrower IFICI)

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-12
- **Recent changes**: Nationality law 10-year clock (Lei Orgânica 1/2026, 19 May 2026). AIMA Portal de Renovações operational (Jul 2026). NHR closed, IFICI active. Golden Visa real estate closed since Oct 2023.
- **Watch items**: AIMA backlog clearance. Judicial challenges to 10-year citizenship. IFICI eligibility expansion.
