## Overview
Sierra Leone is an English-speaking West African country on the Atlantic coast. Immigration is governed by the 1973 Citizenship Act (amended 2006, 2017) and the Foreigners' regime. There is no digital-nomad visa. Naturalisation is unusually demanding: 15 years of residence (8 for persons of Negro-African descent). Dual citizenship has been permitted since 2006. ^[https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/en/region/sierra-leone]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency — Work Permit
- **Official name**: Work Permit + Residence Permit
- **Conditions**: Employment contract; employer sponsorship; employer must justify hiring a foreigner.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable, tied to the contract.
- **Path to citizenship**: 15 years residence (8 for persons of Negro-African descent). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Leonean_nationality_law]
- **Official source**: https://mfaic.gov.sl (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — English-medium but bureaucracy slow; small expat community in Freetown.

### investment_residency — Investor route
- **Official name**: Investor residence (SLIEPA facilitation)
- **Conditions**: Registered investment; Sierra Leone Investment and Export Promotion Agency (SLIEPA) facilitates business setup and permits.
- **Duration / renewal**: Renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 15 years (8 Negro-African).
- **Official source**: https://www.sliepa.org
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — facilitation exists but practical experience varies.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 15 years continuous residence for non-Negro-African applicants; 8 years for persons of Negro-African descent. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Leonean_nationality_law]
- **Language**: English (official); no formal test.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted since the 2006 amendment (granting dual citizenship and equal rights). ^[https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/en/region/sierra-leone]
- **Passport**: ~60 destinations visa-free or on arrival (ECOWAS free movement).

## Tax
- Residence-based; tax resident if ordinarily resident or >183 days.
- Personal income tax (PAYE): progressive up to 30%.
- Corporate tax: 30% standard. GST: 15%.
- Leone (SLL) currency; no special expat regime.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Freetown has a small but growing tech/startup scene and improving 4G/fibre. Remote workers generally need an employer or investor route. English is an advantage, but infrastructure remains a constraint.

## Yacht / Maritime
Atlantic coastline (~400 km) with the port of Freetown — one of West Africa's best natural harbours. Limited leisure-yacht infrastructure; some beach tourism on the Freetown peninsula.

## Notes for RU citizens
Visa required. Sierra Leone maintains neutral relations with Russia. English is the working language — an advantage over Francophone neighbours. Small expat community. Banking is limited; infrastructure weak.

## Community pulse
Limited English-language expat forum data. The expat presence is small, concentrated in Freetown (mining, NGOs, UN). No significant digital-nomad base. Naturalisation is rarely sought given the 15-year standard requirement.

## Country Card

### General description
Sierra Leone is an English-speaking West African country on the Atlantic coast, with one of the region's best natural harbours at Freetown. Its economy is driven by mining (iron ore, diamonds, rutile) and agriculture. Immigration is employment- and investment-led. Naturalisation is among the most demanding in the region (15 years standard), though dual citizenship is permitted and English eases access.

### Well-known strong points
- English is the official language — accessible for expats
- Dual citizenship permitted (since 2006)
- Freetown natural harbour; mining-driven economy
- ECOWAS free movement
- Stable multiparty politics since 2018 (FH 59/100)

### Well-known problems
- 15-year naturalisation (8 years only for Negro-African descent)
- No digital-nomad or passive-income visa
- Weak infrastructure — power, roads, healthcare
- Low median incomes and high poverty
- Limited banking and financial services

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-11
- **Recent changes**: No major immigration-policy shifts known; 1973 Citizenship Act (amended 2006/2017) remains the framework.
- **Watch items**: Any naturalisation-period reform; digital-nomad visa in the ECOWAS region.
