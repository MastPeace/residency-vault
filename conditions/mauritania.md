## Overview
Mauritania is a vast Saharan/Sahelian country on West Africa's Atlantic coast (pop. ~4.9M). Islamic Republic with Arabic as the official language. Immigration governed by the Mauritanian Nationality Code. Naturalization: 5 years residence. Passport: 25 visa-free destinations. ^[https://data.worldbank.org/country/MR]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Permis de travail / Carte de sejour
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from Mauritanian employer; employer sponsorship required.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years legal residence. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauritanian_nationality_law]
- **Official source**: Direction Generale de la Surete Nationale (no comprehensive online portal).
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data (very few non-African expats outside mining/diplomatic).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Visa d'etude
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Mauritanian institution; proof of financial means.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: Residence time counts toward 5-year clock.
- **Official source**: Mauritanian embassies / consulates.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Regroupement familial
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; proof of family relationship and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence.
- **Official source**: Immigration authorities.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

## Citizenship
- **Residence**: 5 years legal residence for naturalization. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauritanian_nationality_law]
- **Language**: Arabic proficiency expected (practical requirement).
- **Dual citizenship**: Not generally recognized (renunciation required).
- **Processing**: Discretionary, by decree. Historical ethnic discrimination in nationality law.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive income tax; corporate tax ~25%. Mining sector has special regimes.
- **Remote workers**: No special regime. Limited enforcement outside formal sector.
- **General**: Ouguiya (MRU) currency. Tax administration developing.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. Internet in Nouakchott is adequate (4G); limited elsewhere. Coworking is virtually nonexistent. Mauritania is not on the nomad map. [[senegal]] and [[morocco]] are regional alternatives.

## Yacht / Maritime
Extensive Atlantic coastline (~754 km) with the port of Nouakchott and Nouadhibou. Limited yacht tourism; no dedicated flag-of-convenience. Fishing is the main maritime industry.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Visa required. No special RU programs. Russia has some mining/fishing interests. Passport is weak (25 destinations); limited mobility benefit.

## Community Pulse
Virtually no expat immigration community. The country is rarely discussed on nomad or investor forums. Bureaucratic procedures, limited English, and restrictive social environment deter foreign residents.

## Country Card

### General description
Mauritania is a large, sparsely populated Saharan/Sahelian country on West Africa's Atlantic coast. Islamic Republic with Arabic as the official language and an economy anchored in mining (iron ore, gold), fishing, and limited agriculture. Immigration infrastructure is minimal. Naturalization requires 5 years residence but is discretionary.

### Well-known strong points
- Extensive Atlantic coastline with port access
- Relatively short naturalization period (5 years)
- CFA-zone alternative (own currency MRU)
- Mining-sector employment opportunities

### Well-known problems
- Very weak rule of law (WJP rank 132/142)
- Slavery and ethnic discrimination issues (Haratine population)
- Harsh Saharan climate; sandstorms frequent
- No formal residency programs; restrictive social environment

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-26
- **Recent changes**: No known recent changes. Political stability under President Ghazouani.
- **Watch items**: Nationality-law reforms. Security situation (Sahel spillover). Slavery eradication progress.
