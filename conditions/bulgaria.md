## Overview
Bulgaria is an EU member (since 2007), entered the Eurozone waiting room (ERM II) in 2020, and joined **full Schengen in January 2025**. It offers the EU's lowest income tax (10% flat) and cost of living. Residence routes: **Golden Visa by investment** (€512K fund → permanent residence; €312K property → temporary), **digital nomad visa** (launched Dec 2025), and standard work permits. Citizenship requires 5 years of permanent residence (or 3 years PR + Bulgarian origin). ^[https://www.bulgarian-citizenship.com/permanent-residence-in-bulgaria]

## Per-Program Detail

### Investment Residency — Golden Visa
- **Official name**: Постоянно пребиваване чрез инвестиция (Permanent residence by investment, Art. 25 Foreigners Act)
- **Conditions**: Two routes: ^[https://www.bulgarian-citizenship.com/permanent-residence-in-bulgaria]
  1. **€512,000** (1,000,000 BGN) in Bulgarian AIF (Alternative Investment Fund) or ETFs → **permanent residence** from day one. Fund must invest primarily in Bulgarian assets. Processing ~6–8 months.
  2. **€312,000** (610,000 BGN) in Bulgarian real estate → **temporary residence**. Can apply for PR after 5 years.
  Clean criminal record. Source of funds documentation.
- **Duration / renewal**: Fund route: permanent residence (no renewal). Property route: temporary residence, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Fund route: 5 years PR → eligible for citizenship (fast-track). Property route: 5 years TR → PR → then citizenship. Bulgarian language B1 required for naturalization. ^[https://www.bulgarian-citizenship.com/bulgarian-citizenship-investment]
- **Official source**: https://www.mvr.bg/migration
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — fund route provides immediate PR but investment is substantial. No minimum stay requirement for maintaining PR.

### Digital Nomad Visa
- **Official name**: Разрешение за пребиваване на цифрови номади (Digital Nomad Residence Permit)
- **Conditions**: Launched **December 20, 2025**. Non-EU citizens earning income from OUTSIDE Bulgaria. Three applicant categories: ^[https://etias.com/articles/bulgaria-launches-digital-nomad-visa-for-remote-workers]
  1. Remote employees of non-EU/EEA/CH companies
  2. Business owners/shareholders (≥25%) of foreign company
  3. Freelancers with ≥1 year of foreign clients
  **Income**: 50× Bulgarian monthly minimum wage for previous year ≈ **~€27,500/year** (≈€2,300/month). Health insurance. Accommodation. Clean criminal record. ^[https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/09/bulgaria-digital-nomad-visa.html]
- **Duration / renewal**: D visa (valid 90 days) → residence permit 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Does NOT directly lead to permanent residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.mvr.bg/migration
- **Community verdict**: New program — launched Dec 2025, limited track record. Income requirement is the lowest among EU DN visas.

### Highly Skilled Employment — EU Blue Card
- **Official name**: Синя карта на ЕС
- **Conditions**: University degree. Job offer from Bulgarian employer. Salary ≥ 1.5× Bulgarian average.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 4 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence → PR → citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card/eu-blue-card-bulgaria_en
- **Community verdict**: Works well — low salary threshold compared to Western EU.

## Citizenship
- **Standard**: 5 years of permanent residence + B1 Bulgarian language + clean record ^[https://www.bulgarian-citizenship.com/bulgarian-citizenship-naturalization]
- **Investment route**: 5 years PR from Golden Visa fund route → fast-track eligibility
- **Marriage to Bulgarian**: 3 years PR
- **Bulgarian origin**: 3 years PR
- Dual citizenship: **permitted** for natural-born Bulgarians; for naturalized citizens, renunciation of previous citizenship may be required

## Tax
- Flat income tax: **10%** (EU's lowest personal rate)
- Social contributions: ~14% employee, ~19% employer (capped)
- Corporate tax: **10%** (with 5% dividend tax = 15% total)
- No wealth tax, no inheritance tax (direct line)
- Tax residency: 183 days/year

## IT / Digital Nomad
Bulgaria launched its dedicated digital nomad visa in December 2025 — lowest income threshold in EU (~€2,300/mo). Full Schengen since Jan 2025. Low cost of living. Major tech hubs: Sofia, Plovdiv, Varna. Strong IT sector with growing startup scene. The 10% flat tax rate is a major draw for high-earning remote workers who become tax residents.

## Yacht / Maritime
Black Sea coastline (Varna, Burgas). Danube River. Yacht registration under Bulgarian flag available. Growing marina infrastructure but not a premier yachting destination.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Bulgaria is one of the most RU-friendly EU countries: Cyrillic alphabet, cultural/historical ties, significant Russian-speaking community (especially in coastal areas and Sofia). The Golden Visa fund route (€512K) is accessible. The new DN visa (€2,300/mo) is also open. Bulgarian language (Slavic) is relatively accessible for Russian speakers. Banking for RU nationals may face enhanced due diligence but is generally more accessible than in Western EU.

## Country Card

Bulgaria is an EU member (since 2007) and full Schengen member since January 2025. It offers the EU's joint-lowest income tax (10% flat) and cost of living, with one of the fastest citizenship paths: 5 years of permanent residence. Its Golden Visa fund route (€512,000) grants immediate permanent residence, while a €312,000 property route provides temporary residence. The digital nomad visa launched in December 2025 has the EU's lowest income threshold (~€2,300/month). Bulgarian uses the Cyrillic script — a barrier for Latin-alphabet users but accessible for Russian speakers. The country ranks among the EU's weakest on rule of law (0.56) and Freedom House (77).

### Well-known strong points
- Joint-lowest EU income tax (10% flat) and corporate tax (10%); 5% dividend tax
- Fastest EU citizenship path: 5 years PR (via Golden Visa fund route)
- Golden Visa fund route (€512K) grants immediate permanent residence from day one
- Digital Nomad visa with EU's lowest income threshold (~€2,300/month)
- Lowest cost of living in the EU (65% below NYC); Sofia rent among Europe's cheapest
- Cyrillic script and Slavic language — accessible for Russian/Ukrainian/Belarusian speakers

### Well-known problems
- Lowest GDP per capita in the EU ($41,520 PPP); lowest average wages
- Weak rule of law (WJP 0.56, rank 59) and corruption concerns (Freedom House 77)
- Bulgarian language (Cyrillic alphabet) challenging for non-Slavic speakers; B1 required for citizenship
- DN visa launched December 2025 — very limited track record; no PR path from DN alone
- Dual citizenship for naturalized citizens may require renunciation
- Golden Visa fund route (€512K) is more expensive than comparable programs
