## Overview
Andorra is a microstate in the Pyrenees between France and Spain. It is not an EU member, but its low taxes (max 10% personal income tax) make it a leading European relocation target. Its signature program, **passive residence** (*residència sense activitat lucrativa*), was reworked by the "Omnibus 2" law (Act 2/2026, in force 12–13 Feb 2026): the minimum investment rose from €600,000 to **€1,000,000**, and the AFA deposit became non-refundable. Citizenship is among Europe's hardest: 20 years. ^[https://www.advantia.ad/en/living-in-andorra/omnibus-2-law-new-requirements-residence-andorra]

## Per-Program Detail

### Passive residence
- **Official name**: Residència sense activitat lucrativa (passive / by-investment residence)
- **Conditions**: €1,000,000 in Andorran assets (authorised funds, local company shares, insurance) OR €400,000 in the Fons d'Habitatge housing fund; non-refundable €50,000 AFA contribution (+€12,000/dependent); 90+ days/year presence; private health insurance; clean criminal record. 2026 quota: 163 permits. ^[https://www.relotus-relocation.com/en/journal/andorra-residence-permit-for-russians]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year initial, then renewals of 2+2+10 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 20 years residence; Catalan B1 + history/geography exam.
- **Official source**: https://www.impostos.ad (Andorran government)
- **Community verdict**: Works well for high-net-worth profiles; the €1M threshold (up from €600K) sharply narrowed eligibility in 2026.

### Digital Nomad Visa
- **Official name**: D.3 Residència per a nòmada digital
- **Conditions**: Remote work for foreign clients/employers; income ≥ 300% of the minimum interprofessional wage (€4,342–4,576/mo in 2026); +50% per adult dependent, +30% per child; no AFA deposit required; 90+ days/year. ^[https://legalclarity.org/andorra-digital-nomad-visa-requirements-tax-and-costs]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year initial, renewable (2+3+10 ladder).
- **Path to citizenship**: 20 years residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.govern.ad
- **Community verdict**: Works well — lower bar than passive residence (no €1M investment); capped at 100 permits/year.

### Self-employed residence
- **Official name**: Residència per compte propi (self-employed / company owner)
- **Conditions**: Incorporate and own ≥20% of an Andorran company; AFA deposit €15,000; monthly self-employed social contribution (≈€450–500).
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 20 years residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.govern.ad
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard route for business owners.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 20 years (standard); 10 years if educated in Andorra; 5 years if married to a citizen (raised from 3 in 2026). ^[https://www.expatfocus.com/andorra/guide/andorra-citizenship]
- **Language**: Catalan — B1 (mandatory for under-70s) plus a history & geography exam. ^[https://europeanconservative.com/articles/news-corner/andorra-tightens-nationality-rules-new-catalan-language-residence-requirements]
- **Dual citizenship**: NOT permitted — must renounce prior nationality.

## Tax
- **Personal income tax (IRPF)**: 0% up to €24,000; 5% €24,000–40,000; 10% above €40,000 — **10% maximum**. ^[https://legalclarity.org/andorra-digital-nomad-visa-requirements-tax-and-costs]
- **Corporate tax**: 10% maximum (reduced rates for new companies).
- **IGI (VAT equivalent)**: 4.5% general rate.
- **No** wealth tax, inheritance tax, or capital-gains tax on most assets.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Andorra runs a dedicated D.3 digital-nomad permit with no large deposit, but the income bar (~€4,400/mo) is high relative to the low tax. Andorra la Vella has a modest coworking scene; most nomads live there or in Escaldes-Engordany. 0–10% tax is the main draw for high-earning remote workers.

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked — no coastline or yachting infrastructure. Not applicable.

## Notes for RU citizens
No blanket ban on Russians, but three practical hurdles: needing Schengen access to reach Andorra, last place in the quota queue, and the EU sanctions cap on new deposits above €100,000 in Andorran banks. The €1M investment route and the €50K non-refundable contribution are the entry cost. Andorran banks run enhanced KYC for RU passport holders. ^[https://www.relotus-relocation.com/en/journal/andorra-residence-permit-for-russians]

## Community pulse
Long-standing reputation as Europe's "low-tax Switzerland alternative" has held, but the Feb 2026 Omnibus-2 reform (€600K → €1M, non-refundable €50K, quota cut to 163) triggered broad criticism from advisors and a shift toward the D.3 nomad permit and self-employed routes. Processing is fast (~6 weeks for passive) once the file is complete.

## Country Card

### General description
Andorra is a tiny, mountainous principality of ~83,000 people wedged between France and Spain, with Catalan as its official language and a service/tourism economy built on duty-free retail and winter sports. It is outside the EU but inside the euro area and Schengen-adjacent. Its defining attraction is a 10%-maximum personal income tax, which, combined with a high quality of life and low crime, draws high-net-worth expats and remote workers.

### Well-known strong points
- Personal income tax capped at 10% (0–10% brackets)
- Very safe, politically stable parliamentary co-principality (FH 93/100)
- Strong passport (170 visa-free, Henley 2026)
- High quality of life, mountain/ski environment, excellent healthcare
- No wealth, inheritance, or capital-gains tax

### Well-known problems
- Citizenship requires 20 years and renunciation of prior nationality
- Feb 2026 reform raised passive-residence investment to €1M and made the €50K contribution non-refundable
- Tiny 2026 quota (163 passive permits)
- No coastline; small, expensive housing market
- High income threshold (~€4,400/mo) for the digital-nomad permit
