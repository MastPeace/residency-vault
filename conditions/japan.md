## Overview
Japan has the world's strongest passport (typically #1–2 in rankings) and a well-structured but demanding immigration system. There is no investor Golden Visa, no passive-income visa, and no retirement visa — residence is granted for what you do (employment, business management, family, study), not what you own. The Highly Skilled Professional (HSP) points system offers a fast track to permanent residency (1–3 years). Japan tightened naturalization screening to ~10 years effective April 1, 2026. Dual citizenship is generally not permitted. ^[https://japan-visa.com/visas/highly-skilled]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Not available (Business Manager Visa, 経営・管理, is the closest route)
- **Conditions**: No dedicated investor visa. Buying property in Japan grants NO residence. The Business Manager Visa (reformed 2025) requires ¥30,000,000 capital, a full-time employee (Japanese national or specific status holder), a business plan confirmed by a Japanese-qualified SME consultant/CPA/tax accountant, and active management in Japan. ^[https://rumavi.com/en/property-guides/japan-residency-visas]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–5 year status of residence, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR after 10 years residence (5 years on work status); naturalization after ~10 years; single nationality required.
- **Official source**: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (property purchase does NOT give residency — a common misconception; Business Manager Visa is demanding and costly).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No passive income / independent means residence. Residence status is activity-based (work, business, study, family).
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (no pathway for passive-income or remote retirees).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Digital Nomad Visa (Designated Activities status, 特定活動)
- **Conditions**: Annual income of ¥10,000,000+ from non-Japanese sources; private health insurance covering ¥10M+; restricted to nationals of visa-exempt countries with tax treaties with Japan. Not a residence status — no zairyu card, no dependents. Max 6 months, non-renewable; re-apply possible after 6 months outside Japan. ^[https://japan-visa.com/visas/digital-nomad]
- **Duration / renewal**: 6 months, non-renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: None directly; must switch to a work/Business Manager status for longer stays.
- **Official source**: https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/long/visa16.html
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (launched 2024; short duration and no residence status limit its usefulness; income threshold is high).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Highly Skilled Professional (HSP, 高度専門職), Types 1 and 2
- **Conditions**: 70+ points under the HSP points system (academic background — PhD 30 pts, annual salary, age, Japanese language ability, top-university degrees). Three categories: (i) academic research, (ii) specialized/technical (engineers), (iii) business management. 70 points → eligible for PR after 3 years; 80 points → after 1 year (points must be maintained throughout). HSP Type 2 (after 3 years on Type 1) grants almost unrestricted activity. ^[https://english.visajapan.jp/koudo.html]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5-year period of stay (maximum); HSP Type 2 unlimited.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR: 1 year (80 pts) or 3 years (70 pts). Naturalization: ~10 years residence; basic Japanese (3rd-grade elementary reading/writing); single nationality required.
- **Official source**: https://www.isa.go.jp/en/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (fastest path to PR in Japan; high bar but transparent points system).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Business Manager Visa (経営・管理)
- **Conditions**: ¥30,000,000 capital (raised from ¥5M in 2025 reform); full-time employee; business plan confirmed by Japanese-qualified professional; physical office in Japan; active management. For high earners: master's degree + ¥20M annual income, or 10 years experience + ¥20M, or 5 years management + ¥40M — grants 5-year status and PR in 1 year. ^[https://rumavi.com/en/property-guides/japan-residency-visas]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–5 year status, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR after 10 years (standard) or 1 year (J-Skip high-earner); naturalization ~10 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (2025 reform sharply raised the bar; now difficult for small startups; J-Skip route benefits only high earners).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services (技術・人文知識・国際業務)
- **Conditions**: Bachelor's degree or 10 years of relevant work experience; job offer from Japanese employer; salary commensurate with Japanese workers. Covers engineers, IT professionals, marketers, translators. Standard work visa for most foreign professionals. ^[https://www.isa.go.jp/en/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1, 3, or 5-year status, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR after 10 years (5 years on work status); naturalization ~10 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (standard route; employer sponsorship and 10-year PR timeline are the main constraints).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student Visa (留学)
- **Conditions**: Enrollment at a recognized university, vocational school, or language school; proof of funds (~¥1.5M/yr); part-time work up to 28 hours/week (with permission). Graduates may transition to work visa (change of status) or HSP.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration (up to 4 years for universities).
- **Path to citizenship**: Graduate → work visa → PR (10 years) → naturalization.
- **Official source**: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (education is a common entry route; job-hunting support for graduates).

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Spouse/Child of Japanese National (日本人の配偶者等) / Dependent (家族滞在)
- **Conditions**: Spouse of Japanese national: no work restrictions, PR after 3 years residence + 1 year marriage (simplified). Dependent visa: tied to working parent's status; limited work (28 hr/wk with permission). Spouse/child of PR holder also eligible.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–3 years (spouse of Japanese national), 1–5 years for long-term residents.
- **Path to citizenship**: Spouse of Japanese national: naturalization after 3 years residence; PR after 3 years. Dependent: 10-year standard route.
- **Official source**: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (spouse of Japanese national is the fastest route to PR/citizenship).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Not available.
- **Conditions**: No retirement visa. No route for passive retirees without family ties.
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (no retirement pathway; the largest gap in Japan's system).

## Citizenship
- **Years of residence**: Statutory minimum 5 years; new screening standard (April 1, 2026) effectively requires ~10 years continuous residence. ^[https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/03/27/japan/politics/japan-new-naturalization-requirements]
- **Additional requirements**: Stable income; good conduct; 2 years of tax and social insurance payment records + 5 years of tax certificates (from April 2026); Japanese language at 3rd-grade elementary reading/writing level; renunciation of prior nationality.
- **Dual citizenship**: Generally NOT permitted — naturalized citizens must renounce their previous nationality (principle of single nationality). ^[https://english.visajapan.jp/qa/qa_kika11.html]
- **Note**: Naturalization screening now matches PR's 10-year standard, closing the loophole where citizenship was "easier" than PR.

## Tax
- **Residency-based taxation**: "Resident" (domicile or 1+ year residence) taxed on worldwide income; "non-permanent resident" (non-Japanese, <5 of 10 years) taxed on Japan-source income only unless remitted; "non-resident" taxed on Japan-source income only. ^[https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/individual/12006.htm]
- **Income tax**: Progressive national rates: 5%, 10%, 20%, 23%, 33%, 40%, 45% (above ¥40M). + 2.1% reconstruction surtax.
- **Resident (inhabitant) tax**: 10% flat + per-capita levy (¥5,000).
- **Capital gains**: Securities 20.315%; real estate varies. Inheritance/gift tax: progressive up to 55%. Exit tax for high-net-worth residents.
- **Corporate tax**: ~29.74% effective (national + local).

## IT / Digital Nomad
Japan has a [[digital-nomad]] visa (6 months, ¥10M+ income) but it is short, non-renewable, and not a residence status. The tech ecosystem (Tokyo, Osaka, Fukuoka) is large; HSP category (ii) is tailored to engineers and IT professionals. J-Skip (special HSP for very high earners/researchers) offers 5-year status and PR in 1 year.

## Yacht / Maritime
Japan has extensive coastlines and marinas (Tokyo Bay, Yokohama, Okinawa). Foreign-flagged yachts may visit Japanese ports with proper clearance. Japan Coast Guard oversees maritime matters. No special yacht investor pathway.

## Notes for RU citizens
Japan imposed sanctions on Russia after 2022. Visa issuance to Russian nationals is significantly restricted; many categories are effectively paused. Long-term residence applications from RU nationals face very high hurdles. The Japanese diaspora and Russian-speaking community in Tokyo is small. Business and HSP routes remain theoretically open but practically difficult. Renunciation of Russian citizenship is a hard requirement for naturalization, which complicates the path for RU applicants.

- **Recent changes**: Naturalization screening tightened to ~10 years (April 1, 2026). PR application fee rising to ¥200,000 (from Oct 1, 2026). Business Manager capital requirement raised to ¥30M (2025). Potential PR revocation for unpaid taxes from April 1, 2027.
- **Watch items**: Digital Nomad visa extension under consideration. PR fee increase implementation.
