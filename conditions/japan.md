## Country Card

### General description
Japan is an East Asian island nation with the world's second-strongest passport (188 visa-free), a large high-tech economy, and a demanding but well-structured immigration system. Residence is granted for what you do (employment, business, family, study), not what you own — there is no investor Golden Visa, no retirement visa, and no passive-income route. The Highly Skilled Professional (HSP) points system offers the fastest path to permanent residency (1 year at 80+ points), and the Business Manager visa serves entrepreneurs. Japan tightened naturalization screening to an effective ~10 years in April 2026 and does not permit dual citizenship.

### Well-known strong points
- World's 2nd-strongest passport: 188 visa-free destinations
- HSP points system: PR in 1 year at 80 points, 3 years at 70 points — fastest in Asia
- Relatively moderate cost of living (Numbeo index 45.6) compared to Singapore/US/Australia
- Excellent public safety, healthcare, infrastructure; WJP Order & Security rank 8 globally
- Unique culture, world-class cuisine, efficient public transit, high quality of life
- Moderate personal tax compared to Western Europe; non-permanent residents taxed only on Japan-source income

### Well-known problems
- No investor Golden Visa, no passive-income route, no retirement visa — activity-based only
- Citizenship requires ~10 years + renunciation of prior nationality — dual citizenship not permitted
- Business Manager Visa capital raised to ¥30M (2025) — sharply limits small-scale entrepreneurship
- Digital Nomad Visa is only 6 months, non-renewable, ¥10M/yr income threshold
- Language barrier: Japanese is difficult and essential for daily life outside expat bubbles
- Aging, shrinking population; highest public debt/GDP ratio among developed nations
