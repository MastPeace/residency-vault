## Overview
Kenya launched its Class N Digital Nomad Permit in 2024, making it one of Africa's more accessible remote work destinations. The Directorate of Immigration Services (eFNS portal) administers permits. Nairobi is East Africa's business hub with a growing tech scene (Silicon Savannah). Kenya permits dual citizenship. The eTA (Electronic Travel Authorization) replaced the visa system for short stays. ^[https://fns.immigration.go.ke/infopack/permits/classN]

## Per-Program Detail

### digital_nomad_visa — Class N Digital Nomad Permit
- **Official name**: Class N Permit — Digital Nomads (KEP/N)
- **Conditions**: Remote work for company registered outside Kenya; OR shareholder/conducting business for foreign company; OR self-employed with foreign clients. Proof of income (bank statements/payslips, 3 months). Proof of accommodation. Letter of no objection from home country embassy. Cover letters. Clean record. Processing fee: USD 200 (non-refundable). Issuance fee: USD 1,000/year. ^[https://fns.immigration.go.ke/infopack/permits/classN]
- **Duration**: 1 or 2 years, renewable. ^[https://fns.immigration.go.ke/infopack/permits/classN]
- **Path to citizenship**: Unclear — the permit is new (launched 2024) and the citizenship path from Class N specifically is not yet tested. General Kenyan citizenship requires 7 years continuous lawful residence. ^[https://kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2011/12/eng@2022-12-31]
- **Official source**: https://fns.immigration.go.ke/infopack/permits/classN
- **Community verdict**: Works well — permit is operational on eFNS portal. USD 1,200/yr total is moderate. Nairobi and Mombasa are popular bases. Source: BAL Immigration News, BI East Africa.

### employment_residency — Class D Employment Permit
- **Official name**: Class D — Employment Permit
- **Conditions**: Job offer from registered Kenyan employer. Employer must prove no qualified Kenyan available. Academic/professional qualifications. ^[https://fns.immigration.go.ke]
- **Duration**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR after holding work permit for 7 years + 3 years continuous residence. Citizenship: 7 years continuous lawful residence. ^[https://kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2011/12/eng@2022-12-31]
- **Official source**: https://fns.immigration.go.ke
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — work permits face scrutiny; employer sponsorship essential.

## Citizenship
- By registration/naturalisation: 7 years continuous lawful residence. ^[https://kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2011/12/eng@2022-12-31]
- Marriage to Kenyan citizen: 7 years (if applying under general registration).
- Language: Swahili or English.
- Good character; no imprisonment of 3+ years.
- Dual citizenship: Permitted since 2010 Constitution. Dual citizens must disclose other citizenship within 3 months. Kenyan-by-birth citizens may hold dual. Naturalised citizens may retain original citizenship (if home country permits). ^[https://kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2011/12/eng@2022-12-31]
- Passport: visa-free to ~75 destinations. EAC partner states free movement. Not strong by EU standards.
- **Honest assessment**: 7-year path is moderate by African standards, but still a significant commitment.

## Tax
- Residence-based taxation for tax residents (183+ days or permanent home in Kenya).
- Personal income tax: 10%–30% progressive (top rate at KES 9.6M+/yr, ~USD 75,000).
- Digital services tax: 1.5% on digital marketplace income.
- Corporate tax: 30% (resident); 37.5% (non-resident).
- VAT: 16%.
- Digital nomads: if resident >183 days, worldwide income becomes taxable. Seek professional advice.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Nairobi is East Africa's leading tech hub (Silicon Savannah). Reliable internet, co-working spaces (Nairobi Garage, iHub), and a vibrant startup ecosystem. Mombasa offers coastal lifestyle. The eTA system enables visa-free entry for many nationalities (90-day tourist). The Class N permit now provides a legal framework for longer stays. Cost of living: moderate — USD 1,000–2,000/month for comfortable lifestyle.

## Yacht / Maritime
Kenya has a coastline on the Indian Ocean (Mombasa, Malindi, Lamu). Limited yacht facilities compared to South Africa or Mauritius. Mombasa Yacht Club and Kilifi offer basic mooring. Kenya Maritime Authority handles vessel registration. International captain credentials recognised.

## Notes for RU citizens
Kenya offers eTA for Russian citizens (tourist, up to 90 days). The Class N permit is accessible. Banking for Russian nationals: available but enhanced due diligence. Nairobi has a small but established Russian-speaking community. Direct flights limited; typically via Dubai/Istanbul/Addis Ababa.

## Country Card

East Africa's largest economy and a regional tech hub ("Silicon Savannah"), Kenya offers a diverse landscape from Indian Ocean beaches to savannah and highlands. Nairobi is the political and economic centre with a growing startup scene. The 2010 Constitution introduced a devolved system of government. Kenya is an EAC member with free movement within the bloc, and dual citizenship is permitted.

### Well-known strong points
- Class N Digital Nomad Permit provides a legal remote-work framework (USD 1,200/yr)
- Nairobi is East Africa's leading tech and startup hub with reliable internet
- English and Swahili are both official — English is widely spoken in business
- Dual citizenship permitted (since 2010 Constitution)
- Major safari tourism destination (Masai Mara, Amboseli) with strong expat infrastructure
- Moderate cost of living (USD 1,000–2,000/month for a comfortable lifestyle)

### Well-known problems
- Bureaucracy can be significant — work permits require employer sponsorship
- Political instability during election cycles; sporadic unrest
- Traffic congestion in Nairobi is severe; infrastructure gaps remain
- Personal income tax up to 30% applies to tax residents (183+ days)
- Class N permit path to citizenship is unclear (new programme, untested)
- Security concerns in some border regions and parts of Nairobi
