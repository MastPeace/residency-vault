## Overview
Belarus is an authoritarian state (Freedom House 7/100) whose residence system is employer- and family-driven, with no golden visa or digital-nomad visa. Its one distinctive feature for tech is the **High-Tech Park (HTP)**, whose residents are exempt from most taxes and whose employees pay a reduced **9%** income tax (vs 13%). Citizenship requires 7 years of uninterrupted residence and is decided personally by the president; dual citizenship is not permitted. ^[https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2025-08/5b0663474.pdf]

## Per-Program Detail

### Permanent residence
- **Official name**: Вид на жительство (permanent residence permit)
- **Conditions**: Granted to close relatives of citizens, refugees, and — after 5 years' continuous temporary residence (3 years for highly-qualified workers), or for investments of ≥15,000 base units. Absence of ≤90 days/year preserves continuity. ^[https://eng.polessu.by/node/885]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 7 years uninterrupted residence after permanent status.
- **Official source**: https://mvd.gov.by
- **Community verdict**: Works well but discretionary and politically sensitive.

### Employment residence
- **Official name**: Разрешение на временное проживание (temporary residence)
- **Conditions**: Work permit + employer sponsorship, study, or family grounds; renewable annually.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: leads to permanent residence, then 7-year citizenship clock.
- **Official source**: https://mvd.gov.by
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — bureaucratic and subject to state discretion.

### HTP
- **Official name**: Парк высоких технологий (High-Tech Park)
- **Conditions**: HTP resident companies are exempt from income tax, VAT, and most duties (until 2049); their employees pay **9%** personal income tax instead of 13%. ^[https://economy.gov.by/en/pvt-en/]
- **Duration / renewal**: Company regime, not an individual permit.
- **Path to citizenship**: via standard residence route.
- **Official source**: https://economy.gov.by/en/pvt-en/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for IT companies (1,000+ residents, 60,000+ staff), though many firms relocated after 2020–2022.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 7 years uninterrupted residence after a permanent residence permit. ^[https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2025-08/5b0663474.pdf]
- **Language**: Belarusian or Russian; knowledge of the Constitution.
- **Approval**: personal decision of the president.
- **Dual citizenship**: NOT permitted — must renounce prior nationality.

## Tax
- **Personal income tax**: flat **13%** (employees); **9%** for HTP employees. ^[https://economy.gov.by/en/pvt-en/]
- **Corporate tax**: 20% (HTP residents exempt from most taxes).
- **VAT**: 20% (10% reduced).
- No wealth or inheritance tax; modest property tax.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. The HTP is Belarus's flagship IT regime — a "regulatory sandbox" that once anchored a major software-outsourcing sector (EPAM, Wargaming), with 9% employee tax and company tax exemptions. Post-2020 repression and 2022 sanctions pushed many developers and firms to relocate (notably to [[poland]], [[georgia]], and [[armenia]]).

## Yacht / Maritime
Landlocked — no coastline or yachting. Not applicable.

## Notes for RU citizens
Belarus and Russia have a Union State with mutual recognition of many residence/employment rights; Russian citizens enjoy simplified entry, work, and residence. For non-RU third-country nationals (including many post-2022 RU-speaking relocators), permits are bureaucratic and discretionary. Political risk is the dominant concern: arbitrary detention and exit controls.

## Community pulse
Expat and relocator forums report functional but arbitrary bureaucracy, surveillance, and a politically repressive climate (Freedom House 7/100) as the overriding factors. The IT sector's best talent largely left after 2020–2022. Residence is generally pursued only for family or employer ties, not as a lifestyle choice.

## Country Card

### General description
Belarus is a landlocked Eastern European country of ~9 million with a heavily state-directed economy and an authoritarian government. Its IT sector, anchored by the High-Tech Park's generous tax exemptions and 9% employee tax, was a genuine regional strength before 2020–2022 repression and sanctions prompted a mass exodus of developers. Residence and citizenship are technically possible but discretionary, politicised, and unattractive to most international relocators.

### Well-known strong points
- HTP tax regime: company tax exemptions + 9% employee income tax
- 13% flat personal income tax; low cost of living (Numbeo ~30)
- Skilled, low-cost STEM workforce (legacy of HTP)
- Simplified entry/work for Russian citizens (Union State)
- Relatively short 7-year citizenship clock (on paper)

### Well-known problems
- Authoritarian state; Freedom House 7/100 Not Free
- No dual citizenship; presidential discretion over naturalisation
- No golden visa, DN visa, or passive-income residence
- Political repression, surveillance, and arbitrary detention risk
- Sanctions and capital controls; banking restrictions for foreigners
