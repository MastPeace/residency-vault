## Overview
Malaysia's flagship residence program is Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H), restructured in 2024 into three tiers (Platinum/Gold/Silver) based on fixed-deposit size, plus the DE Rantau Nomad Pass for digital nomads and standard Employment Passes. Naturalization is legally possible after 10 years of residence but is rarely granted in practice, and Malaysia does not recognize dual citizenship (must renounce). ^[https://www.mm2h.gov.my/]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) — Platinum / Gold / Silver
- **Conditions**: Fixed deposit in a Malaysian bank — Platinum US$1,000,000 (20-yr pass, RM200k fee), Gold US$500,000 (15-yr), Silver US$150,000 (5-yr). Age ≥25. 50% of FD withdrawable after approval. ^[https://motac.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Terms-And-Regulations-For-New-Participants-Under-The-Malaysia-My-Second-Home-MM2H-.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: 5/15/20 years, renewable; minimum 90 days/year stay for under-50s.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years residence (rarely granted).
- **Official source**: https://www.mm2h.gov.my/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — 2024 tiers raised deposits and added fees; Silver is the accessible entry.

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: MM2H (FD-based long-stay)
- **Conditions**: Same as above — no monthly income requirement (offshore income requirement dropped in 2024); residence secured by the fixed deposit.
- **Duration / renewal**: 5–20 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.mm2h.gov.my/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — deposit is capital (not spent), but 50% withdrawal restriction and the 90-day stay rule are friction.

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: DE Rantau Nomad Pass (Professional Visit Pass)
- **Conditions**: IT/digital professional (developer, designer, marketer, etc.); ≥ US$24,000/yr income (non-tech roles US$60,000); contracts ≥3 months old. ^[https://www.mdec.my/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 3–12 months, renewable up to another year; sponsored by a Malaysian entity (MDEC-endorsed).
- **Path to citizenship**: Does not lead to PR.
- **Official source**: https://www.mdec.my/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for digital freelancers; the sponsor/endorsement step adds friction.

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Employment Pass (EP) Category I / II
- **Conditions**: Job offer; Category I ≥ RM10,000/mo (2-yr), Category II RM5,000–10,000 (2-yr), Category III RM3,000–5,000 (1-yr, limited).
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–5 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.imi.gov.my/
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard route.

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: MTEP (Malaysia Tech Entrepreneur Programme)
- **Conditions**: Founder of a tech startup endorsed by MDEC.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Official source**: https://www.mdec.my/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — endorsement-based.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Employment Pass (PVP)
- **Conditions**: Job offer; employer sponsorship.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–5 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.imi.gov.my/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Student Pass
- **Conditions**: Admission to recognized institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Official source**: https://www.imi.gov.my/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Dependant Pass / MM2H dependant
- **Conditions**: Spouse, children <21 (or <34 unmarried/unemployed), parents.
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to sponsor.
- **Official source**: https://www.imi.gov.my/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: MM2H (Silver tier most common for retirees)
- **Conditions**: FD US$150,000+; age ≥25 (no separate retirement age floor); 90 days/year stay.
- **Duration / renewal**: 5 years (Silver).
- **Official source**: https://www.mm2h.gov.my/
- **Community verdict**: Works well for retirees wanting a low-cost tropical base.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalization**: 10 years of residence (10 of the 12 preceding years); Malay language proficiency; renunciation of prior nationality. ^[https://www.mha.gov.my/]
- **Dual citizenship**: Not recognized.
- **Practical reality**: Naturalization is very rarely granted to non-Malay applicants; most expats remain on passes indefinitely.

## Tax
- **Individual income tax**: Resident progressive 0%–30%; non-resident flat 30%. ^[https://www.hasil.gov.my/]
- **Tax residency**: 182 days in a calendar year.
- **MM2H benefit**: Tax exemption on foreign-sourced income remitted into Malaysia (per program terms).
- **No capital gains tax for individuals (except real property gains).**

## IT / Digital Nomad
DE Rantau Nomad Pass (US$24,000/yr income) is the formal route; Penang and Kuala Lumpur are growing nomad hubs. Malaysia combines low cost, English proficiency, and good internet. Note the DE Rantau pass requires an MDEC endorsement/sponsor.

## Yacht / Maritime
Strategic location on the Strait of Malacca. Marinas: Port Klang, Penang (Straits Quay), Langkawi (Royal Langkawi Yacht Club), Johor (Puteri Harbour). Langkawi is a major duty-free cruising base; foreign-flagged vessels welcome.

## Notes for RU Citizens
MM2H and DE Rantau are open to Russian nationals (subject to security screening). Visa-on-arrival/e-visa generally available. Banking for RU nationals is more restrictive post-2022. Langkawi and Penang have Russian-speaking communities.

## Community Pulse
MM2H's 2024 restructuring (higher deposits, tiers, fees) was the dominant story — Silver (US$150,000) is the practical entry. DE Rantau is praised for its low income bar. Citizenship is widely understood to be effectively closed.

## Country Card

### General description
Malaysia is a Southeast Asian federation straddling the Malay Peninsula and northern Borneo, with a diversified upper-middle-income economy. It offers a tiered MM2H residence program (fixed-deposit based), the DE Rantau nomad pass, and strong English-language infrastructure at low cost — a leading retirement and nomad base, though citizenship is effectively closed.

### Well-known strong points
- Very strong passport (181 visa-free destinations)
- Low cost of living with high quality of life (Numbeo index ~30)
- English widely spoken; good healthcare and infrastructure
- MM2H fixed deposit is capital (not spent) and offers tax exemption on foreign income
- DE Rantau nomad pass with a low US$24,000 income bar

### Well-known problems
- Citizenship practically closed; no dual citizenship
- MM2H 2024 restructuring raised deposits and added fees
- 90-day minimum stay rule and 50% FD withdrawal restriction
- Bureaucratic MM2H approval process (through licensed agents)
- Moderate political freedoms (Freedom House 52)
