## Overview
Oman has recently reformed its residency framework for foreign property owners and investors (Decision No. 87/2026), introducing sponsor-free residency through property ownership. Personal income tax is 0%. Oman does NOT offer citizenship to expatriates. ^[https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1191803/oman/rop/oman-updates-property-linked-visas-residency-rules-for-foreign-investors]

## Per-Program Detail

### Property Owner Residency
- **Official name**: Property Owner Visa / Residency (تأشيرة مالك عقار)
- **Conditions**: Foreign national owning a registered residential unit, or property with incomplete registration (under-construction), certificate from the competent authority. No local sponsor required. Entry within 3 months of visa issuance. Under Decision No. 87/2026 (June 2026). ^[https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1191803/oman/rop/oman-updates-property-linked-visas-residency-rules-for-foreign-investors]
  - For unregistered properties: visa valid 6 months to 1 year, renewable, up to 3 months stay per visit.
  - For registered properties: full residency without sponsor, based on ownership certificate.
- **Duration / renewal**: Linked to property ownership. Residency automatically terminates upon sale/transfer of property (including family members' residency). ^[https://gulfnews.com/story/world/gulf/oman/oman-eases-residency-rules-grants-sponsor-free-visas-for-foreign-property-owners-2-1.500583051]
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: Royal Oman Police Decision No. 87/2026, Official Gazette No. 1653
- **Community verdict**: Newly reformed (Jun 2026) — promising, no established track record yet.

### Investor Residency
- **Official name**: Investor Residency for Licensed Foreign Investors
- **Conditions**: Licensed by the competent authority to invest funds in Oman. No sponsor required under 2026 reforms. First-degree family members and legal representatives of property-owning entities also eligible. ^[https://gulfnews.com/story/world/gulf/oman/oman-eases-residency-rules-grants-sponsor-free-visas-for-foreign-property-owners-2-1.500583051]
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to investment/license validity.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: Royal Oman Police Decision No. 87/2026
- **Community verdict**: Reformed framework, expanding.

### Employment Residency
- **Official name**: Employment Visa / Labour Card
- **Conditions**: Employer sponsor, valid labour contract, medical exam. Minimum wage: varies by sector.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable. Tied to employer.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.rop.gov.om
- **Community verdict**: Standard GCC model, employer-dependent.

### Student Residency
- **Official name**: Student Visa
- **Conditions**: Enrolment at accredited Omani institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Duration of study.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.rop.gov.om
- **Community verdict**: Functional.

### Family Reunification
- **Official name**: Family Joining Visa
- **Conditions**: Expanded under 2026 reforms — sponsors include Omani citizens, GCC nationals, licensed foreign investors, foreign property owners, and foreign government employees. Age-related exemptions for dependants introduced. ^[https://www.muscatdaily.com/2026/06/21/oman-eases-residency-rules-for-foreign-property-owners]
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to sponsor's permit.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: Royal Oman Police Decision No. 87/2026
- **Community verdict**: Improved under 2026 reforms — broader sponsor eligibility.

## Citizenship
**Not available for expatriates.** Omani citizenship is by descent (Omani father) or by rare Royal Decree. Naturalization is theoretically possible after 20+ years of residence + Arabic fluency + integration but approvals are exceedingly rare. No citizenship-by-investment programme. ^[https://www.fm.gov.om]

## Tax
- **0% personal income tax** — no tax on salary, dividends, capital gains.
- **15% corporate tax** on most businesses (3% for SMEs meeting criteria).
- **5% VAT** introduced April 2021.
- **No wealth tax, no inheritance tax, no capital gains tax for individuals.**
- **Excise tax**: 100% on tobacco/energy drinks, 50% on sugary drinks.
- Social insurance: 11.5% employer + 7% employee (Omani nationals only; expats exempt).

## IT / Digital Nomad
- **No dedicated digital nomad visa.** Oman does not offer a remote work visa.
- IT infrastructure is developing (4G nationwide, 5G in Muscat).
- Oman is investing in tech parks (Knowledge Oasis Muscat, ITA).
- For remote workers: tourist visa (10–30 days, many nationalities visa-free or eVisa) is the primary short-term option.
- Long-term remote workers: property owner route or investor route.

## Yacht / Maritime
- Marina Bandar Al Rowdha, Al Mouj Marina (Muscat) — modern berthing.
- Oman's coastline is one of the most scenic in the Gulf (Musandam fjords).
- Oman-flag registration available.
- Strategic position on Indian Ocean and Arabian Sea — access to East Africa, South Asia.

## Notes for RU citizens
- **eVisa or visa on arrival** for Russian citizens (10–30 days, depending on type).
- **Small but stable RU community** — mostly in Muscat.
- **No sanctions**, Omani banks accessible to Russian nationals (though enhanced KYC).
- **Property ownership** in Integrated Tourism Complexes (ITCs) open to foreigners — this is the main route to Property Owner Residency.
- **Limited direct flights**: Muscat–Moscow via Oman Air (seasonal/few weekly). Connections via Dubai/Doha common.
- Oman has maintained neutrality and positive relations with Russia.

## Community pulse
Oman is quiet, safe, and culturally more relaxed than Saudi Arabia. The 2026 residency reforms are a significant liberalization, making property-linked residence sponsor-free. The expat community is smaller and more low-key than UAE/Qatar. Quality of life is high, cost of living moderate. Main limitation: narrow residency pathway options, no citizenship path, small job market.

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-26
- **Recent changes**: Decision No. 87/2026 (Jun 2026) — major reform: sponsor-free property visas, expanded family sponsorship, unregistered property visas, investor residency without sponsor.
- **Watch items**: Implementation of 2026 reforms in practice. Potential expansion to digital nomad or retirement visas.

## Country Card

Oman is the quiet outlier of the Gulf: slower-paced, more traditional, and less glitzy than its neighbors. With 0% personal income tax, stunning coastal and mountain landscapes, and the lowest cost of living among GCC states, it appeals to those seeking authentic Gulf life without Dubai's intensity. The 2026 residency reforms (Decision No. 87/2026) significantly liberalized the system, introducing sponsor-free property visas, expanded family sponsorship, and investor residency without a local sponsor. Expatriates make up roughly 43% of the population. The Ibadhi Islamic tradition gives Oman a distinct, more moderate religious character.

### Well-known strong points
- 0% personal income tax with the lowest cost of living in the GCC
- Natural beauty: mountains (Jebel Akhdar), deserts, and 3,000 km of coastline
- 2026 residency reforms: sponsor-free property and investor visas, simplified pathways
- Calm, stable political environment with less regional tension exposure
- Authentic Arab culture preserved — no skyscraper overload, traditional architecture enforced
- Friendly and welcoming population; strong expat community in Muscat

### Well-known problems
- No path to citizenship — residence only; naturalization is extraordinarily rare
- Smaller, less diversified economy than UAE/Qatar; fewer job opportunities for professionals
- Bureaucracy is slow and paper-heavy; government services lag behind Dubai/Abu Dhabi
- Limited entertainment and nightlife compared to Dubai/Doha
- Hot and humid summers (May–September) with temperatures exceeding 45°C inland
- Weaker passport (85 visa-free) and less global connectivity than neighboring Gulf states
