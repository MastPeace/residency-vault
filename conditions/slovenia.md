## Overview
Slovenia is an EU/Schengen/Eurozone member in Central Europe at the crossroads of the Alps and the Adriatic. It introduced a **Digital Nomad residence permit** in November 2025. Citizenship requires **10 years** of residence (with various shorter paths). Slovenia generally does NOT permit dual citizenship for naturalized applicants (renunciation required). ^[https://sibiz.eu/digital-nomad-visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### Digital Nomad Visa
- **Official name**: Dovoljenje za začasno prebivanje za digitalne nomade (Temporary residence permit for digital nomads)
- **Conditions**: Non-EU citizen. Remote work using ICT for foreign-registered companies or foreign clients. **Income ≥ €3,377.10/month** (as of April 2026 — linked to Slovenian average salary). ^[https://sibiz.eu/digital-nomad-visa] Valid health insurance. Clean criminal record. No grounds for refusal per Art. 55 Aliens Act (security risk, criminal convictions). ^[https://sibiz.eu/digital-nomad-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable. ^[https://sibiz.eu/digital-nomad-visa]
- **Path to citizenship**: Does NOT directly lead to permanent residence. Must switch to employment or business route for long-term path.
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.si/en/topics/entry-and-residence/
- **Community verdict**: New — launched Nov 2025, very limited track record.

### Startup / Entrepreneur — Company Registration
- **Official name**: Dovoljenje za prebivanje zaradi ustanovitve podjetja (Residence permit for company establishment)
- **Conditions**: Register a Slovenian company (d.o.o. — limited liability company). Minimum share capital: €7,500. Business plan. Proof of funds. ^[https://data.si/en/blog/start-business-in-slovenia-open-a-company-get-residency]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence → PR → citizenship at 10 years total.
- **Official source**: https://spot.gov.si/en/info/how-can-non-eu-nationals-start-a-business-in-slovenia
- **Community verdict**: Works well — popular route for self-employed professionals. EU company in a stable jurisdiction.

### Highly Skilled Employment — EU Blue Card
- **Official name**: Modra karta EU
- **Conditions**: University degree. Job offer from Slovenian employer. Salary ≥ 1.5× Slovenian average.
- **Duration / renewal**: Duration of contract + 3 months, max 2 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence → PR → 10 years total → citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card/eu-blue-card-slovenia_en
- **Community verdict**: Works well — small market but growing demand for skilled workers.

## Citizenship
- **Standard naturalization**: **10 years** of residence (or 1 year continuous before application + 5 years registered residence in some interpretations) ^[https://www.gov.si/en/topics/citizenship]
- **Marriage to Slovenian**: 3 years
- **Slovenian descent**: shorter path
- **Extraordinary naturalization**: for persons of special interest to Slovenia (no minimum residence)
- **Language**: Slovenian proficiency (oral + written) — South Slavic language
- **Renunciation**: Generally required to renounce previous citizenship ^[https://www.refworld.org/legal/legislation/natlegbod/1991/en/13738]
- Dual citizenship: **not generally permitted** for naturalized citizens

## Tax
- Progressive income tax: 16%–50% (top rate from €72,000)
- Social contributions: ~22.1% employee
- Corporate tax: 19% (22% from 2024)
- Capital gains tax: 0%–25% (0% after 15 years holding)
- No wealth tax
- Attractive for business owners: company registration provides path to residence

## IT / Digital Nomad
The digital nomad residence permit (Nov 2025) is Slovenia's newest route — €3,377.10/mo income threshold. Ljubljana is a growing tech hub with a high quality of life. Excellent location — Alps + Adriatic, borders Italy, Austria, Hungary, Croatia. Fast internet, EU digital infrastructure. The company registration route is popular among IT professionals.

## Yacht / Maritime
Short Adriatic coastline (~47 km — one of the smallest). Small marina infrastructure (Portorož, Izola, Koper). Yacht registration under Slovenian flag available but limited. Not a primary yachting destination.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Russian citizens need a Schengen visa. The DN visa and company registration route are formally open but face enhanced scrutiny. Slovenian language (South Slavic, similar to Croatian/Serbian) is moderately accessible for Russian speakers. Small Russian-speaking community. No special RU restrictions beyond standard EU measures.
