## Overview
Nicaragua offers one of the world's lowest income thresholds for a pensionado residency: US$600/month (pensionado) or US$750/month (rentista). However, a major reform in November 2024 (Law No. 1228) eliminated the previous permanent residence pathway for pensioners/rentiers — all are now temporary residents only. Naturalization now requires 7 years of permanent residence (up from 4 years previously). The reform also repealed Law No. 694, removing tax benefits that previously accompanied pensionado status. ^[https://consortiumlegal.com/en/2025/07/29/immigration-reform-in-nicaragua-changes-of-interest-for-2024]

## Per-Program Detail

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia de Pensionado (Temporary — since November 2024 reform)
- **Conditions**: Age 45+. Monthly pension income ≥ US$600 from foreign source; +US$150 per dependent. ^[https://www.liveandinvestoverseas.com/country-hub/nicaragua/nicaragua-visa-and-residency-information]
  - Must provide: US Embassy notarized affidavit of income (for US citizens), apostilled birth certificate, apostilled criminal record, health certificate, passport valid 6+ months with latest entry stamp, 2 passport photos, guarantee deposit (airline ticket equivalent).
- **Duration / renewal**: TEMPORARY residence only (since reform). Annual card fee: US$200. Can be extended; no path to permanent residence via pensionado itself — need alternative route (work, marriage, investment).
- **Path to citizenship**: After obtaining permanent residence via alternative route, 7 years (previously 4). ^[https://consortiumlegal.com/en/2025/07/29/immigration-reform-in-nicaragua-changes-of-interest-for-2024]
- **Official source**: https://www.migob.gob.ni (Dirección General de Migración y Extranjería)
- **Community verdict**: Worsened (pre-reform: best-value LATAM pensionado with $600/mo + permanent residence. Post-Nov 2024: temporary-only, no tax benefits, stricter renewal, path to citizenship deferred to 7 years — major downgrade).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia de Rentista (Temporary)
- **Conditions**: Age 45+. Monthly passive income ≥ US$750 (any source: dividends, interest, rental, investment returns) from foreign source. +US$150 per dependent. ^[https://www.liveandinvestoverseas.com/country-hub/nicaragua/nicaragua-visa-and-residency-information]
- **Duration / renewal**: TEMPORARY only (since reform). Annual renewal.
- **Path to citizenship**: Convert to permanent via alternative route → 7 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.migob.gob.ni
- **Community verdict**: Worsened (same reform impact as pensionado — temporary-only now).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Temporary residence (work)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Nicaraguan employer. Employer must be legally constituted in Nicaragua. Contract, company documents, police clearance, medical certificate, apostilled birth certificate.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Temporary residence for 3+ years → apply for permanent residence → 7 years → naturalization. Or: marriage to Nicaraguan → faster path.
- **Official source**: https://www.migob.gob.ni
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (small formal labour market; 90% local-staff requirement; mostly used by multinationals in Zona Franca/manufacturing sectors).

## Citizenship
- **By naturalization**: 7 years of PERMANENT residence (up from 4 years pre-November 2024). Exceptions: 2 years for Central Americans (CA-4) and Spaniards; reduced for spouses of Nicaraguans.
  - Requirements: Spanish proficiency, basic knowledge of Nicaraguan history/culture, clean criminal record, economic solvency. ^[https://consortiumlegal.com/en/2025/07/29/immigration-reform-in-nicaragua-changes-of-interest-for-2024]
- **By marriage**: Spouse of Nicaraguan citizen.
- **Dual citizenship**: Recognized (de facto — Nicaragua does not require renunciation).

## Tax
- Territorial tax system: foreign-source income generally not taxed for non-domiciled individuals.
- Tax benefits under old Law No. 694 (tax exemptions for pensionados/rentistas) REPEALED by Law No. 1228 in November 2024. ^[https://consortiumlegal.com/en/2025/07/29/immigration-reform-in-nicaragua-changes-of-interest-for-2024]
- Personal income tax on local-source income: progressive up to 30%.
- VAT: 15%.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Not available — no dedicated digital nomad visa. Remote workers may enter as tourists (visa-free for many; purchase $10 tourist card on arrival; 90-day stay extendable). Rentista route requires age 45+ — younger digital nomads may not qualify. Self-employed/investor visas less developed.

## Yacht / Maritime
Nicaragua has both Caribbean and Pacific coasts. Maritime infrastructure limited. Some yachting activity around Corn Islands (Caribbean) and San Juan del Sur (Pacific). No dedicated yacht-residence programme.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian nationals: visa-free entry (90 days, tourist card $10 USD on arrival).
- Rentista/Pensionado thresholds are among the world's lowest ($600–750/mo) — very accessible.
- November 2024 reform downgraded status from permanent to temporary and removed tax benefits — significant impact on attractiveness for RU retirees previously using the programme.
- Spanish proficiency needed; small but existing RU community in Granada and San Juan del Sur.

## Community pulse
- Pre-November 2024: Nicaragua was arguably the world's cheapest pensionado route ($600/mo) with permanent residency, tax benefits, and 4-year citizenship path. Widely praised.
- Post-November 2024 reform (Law No. 1228): Major regression. Permanent residence eliminated for pensionados/rentistas — now temporary only. Tax benefits repealed. Naturalization extended to 7 years. Residency category "no longer permanent" — renewals now annual with document re-submission.
- Expat sentiment: significantly disappointed. Many considering alternatives (Guatemala rentista, Panama pensionado).
- Processing time for initial residency: 2–8 months depending on route.
