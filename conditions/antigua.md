## Overview
Antigua & Barbuda operates a Citizenship by Investment (CBI) programme launched in 2013 under the Citizenship by Investment Act 2014. The programme offers four investment routes with citizenship granted in 3–6 months. A 5-day physical presence requirement applies within the first 5 years. The passport grants visa-free/visa-on-arrival access to ~150 countries including Schengen, UK, Hong Kong, and Singapore. Dual citizenship is recognized. ^[https://cip.gov.ag/citizenship]

A Nomad Digital Residence (NDR) visa was previously available but has ended. ^[https://nomad.gov.ag/ui/ndrVisa.php]

## Per-Program Detail

### citizenship_by_investment
- **Official name**: Antigua & Barbuda Citizenship by Investment Programme (CIP)
- **Conditions**: Four investment routes:
  - **NDF Donation** (National Development Fund): US$230,000 non-refundable contribution (family of up to 4); +$25,000 per additional dependent. ^[https://cip.gov.ag/citizenship]
  - **Real Estate**: Minimum US$300,000 in government-approved real estate; must hold 5 years. ^[https://cip.gov.ag/citizenship]
  - **Business Investment**: US$1,500,000 sole investor, or joint investment of US$5,000,000+ with each investor contributing minimum US$400,000. ^[https://cip.gov.ag/citizenship]
  - **UWI Fund** (University of the West Indies): US$260,000 minimum (family of 6+); includes one-year tuition scholarship. ^[https://cip.gov.ag/citizenship]
  - Processing fee: US$30,000 for family of 4; due diligence: ~US$7,500 main applicant.
- **Duration / renewal**: Citizenship is permanent and irrevocable. Passport renewal every 5 years (now biometric). 5-day physical presence required within first 5 years of citizenship.
- **Path to citizenship**: Direct citizenship upon investment completion. No prior residence needed. No language exam. Mandatory interview (virtual or in-person) per 2024 OECS MoA.
- **Official source**: https://cip.gov.ag/citizenship
- **Community verdict**: Works well (mature programme; price increased August 2024 to $230K NDF and $300K real estate per OECS MoA; 5-day visit requirement is minimal friction).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Antigua & Barbuda Nomad Digital Residence (NDR)
- **Conditions**: Programme has ended. Previously required: age 18+, employed outside Antigua, minimum US$50,000 annual income, clean criminal record. Fees were $1,500 single / $2,000 couple / $3,000 family. ^[https://nomad.gov.ag/ui/ndrVisa.php]
- **Duration / renewal**: Was 2 years. Not currently accepting applications.
- **Path to citizenship**: N/A (programme ended).
- **Official source**: https://nomad.gov.ag/ui/ndrVisa.php
- **Community verdict**: Ended (programme discontinued; no replacement announced).

## Citizenship
- **By investment**: Direct upon qualifying investment + due diligence. 5-day physical presence in first 5 years.
- **By naturalization**: Available after 7 years of legal residence.
- **Dual citizenship**: Fully recognized.

## Tax
- No personal income tax (abolished 2016). No capital gains tax. No inheritance tax. No wealth tax. No worldwide income taxation.
- Corporate tax: 25%.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Nomad Digital Residence programme has ended. No current digital nomad visa. Remote workers may enter as tourists (visa-free for many, up to 6 months).

## Yacht / Maritime
Antigua & Barbuda operates an international ship registry. Yacht registration available. Major yachting hub — English Harbour and Falmouth Harbour are key Caribbean marinas.

## Notes for RU citizens
- CBI is open to Russian nationals but with enhanced due diligence post-2022. Some agents report longer processing.
- No visa-free Russia access with Antigua passport.
- No requirement to visit during application; 5-day visit within 5 years only.

## Community pulse
- CBI: Well-established, predictable. 2024 price increase aligned with OECS MoA. Processing 4–6 months typical.
- NDR programme: Discontinued — no replacement.
- Work permits: Small market; employer-driven.
