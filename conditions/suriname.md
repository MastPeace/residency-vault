## Overview
Suriname is a small, Dutch-speaking country on the northern coast of South America (CARICOM member), with a culturally mixed population (Christian, Hindu, Muslim). Naturalisation requires **5 years' domicile**, and dual nationality is generally **not** permitted (applicants must renounce prior citizenship). There is no citizenship-by-investment programme (a presidentially floated idea, not enacted) and no digital-nomad visa. A special "Persoon van Surinaamse Afkomst" (PSA) status facilitates return for the diaspora. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surinamese_nationality_law]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Temporary residence permit (werkvergunning / verblijfsvergunning)
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from a Surinamese employer; employer sponsorship. A temporary residence permit is obtained first, then longer residence. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/suriname]
- **Duration / renewal**: Temporary residence valid up to 2 years, renewable; after 5 years continuous stay, naturalisation may be pursued. ^[https://www.goldenvisas.com/suriname]
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years' domicile, then naturalisation (renunciation of prior nationality required). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surinamese_nationality_law]
- **Official source**: https://www.goldenvisas.com/suriname
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — small job market; PSA diaspora route more common than fresh naturalisation.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalisation**: 5 years' domicile in Suriname for general naturalisation; applicants must renounce any other nationality. Naturalisation by presidential decree also exists for qualifying persons. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surinamese_nationality_law]
- **PSA status**: Persons of Surinamese origin (parent/grandparent born in Suriname) may acquire nationality via a facilitated route. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surinamese_nationality_law]
- **Dual citizenship**: Generally NOT permitted — naturalisation requires renunciation; nationality is lost on voluntary acquisition of another. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surinamese_nationality_law]
- **Passport**: ~68 visa-free/on-arrival destinations (CARICOM + some Schengen arrangements).

## Tax
- Residence-based taxation. Personal income tax progressive up to ~38%; corporate income tax 36%; VAT 10%. No non-dom or special expat regime.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Paramaribo is a modest business hub; tech/nomad infrastructure thin. Remote workers enter as tourists or via employer sponsorship.

## Yacht / Maritime
Atlantic coastline with the Suriname River port at Paramaribo. Limited leisure-yachting infrastructure; not a yachting destination.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Visa required; Dutch working language. Diaspora-heavy immigration system.
- No residence-by-investment or CBI (only discussed). Residence is employer-driven.
- Renunciation of prior citizenship is a significant deterrent for Russian applicants.

## Community Pulse
Thin inbound expat community, mostly Dutch-diaspora (PSA) returnees and CARICOM business. The 2024 president's announcement on "considering" a CBI programme generated interest but no legislation. No active investor or nomad community.

## Country Card

### General description
Suriname is a small, culturally diverse, Dutch-speaking country on South America's northern coast and a CARICOM member. Its economy depends on mining (gold, oil, bauxite). It offers a 5-year path to citizenship, but the renunciation requirement for dual nationality and the absence of investment or nomad routes make it a niche destination, used mainly by the Surinamese diaspora (PSA status).

### Well-known strong points
- Relatively short 5-year naturalisation timeline
- Democratic and "Free" (FH 80/100); moderate rule of law (WJP 84/143)
- CARICOM free movement and cultural ties to the Netherlands
- Culturally diverse, tolerant society
- Reasonable GDP per capita (~$19,400 PPP)

### Well-known problems
- Dual nationality generally NOT permitted (renunciation required)
- No residence-by-investment, CBI, or nomad visa
- Small economy; limited job market
- Dutch-language administration
- Debt burden and past macroeconomic instability

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-11
- **Recent changes**: No immigration-policy changes confirmed; president floated a CBI idea in a 2024 annual speech (not enacted).
- **Watch items**: Possible CBI legislation; dual-nationality reform; PSA programme updates.
