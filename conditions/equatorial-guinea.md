## Overview
Equatorial Guinea is a small Central African nation (pop. ~1.7M) composed of a mainland region and islands, rich in oil. It is one of Africa's most authoritarian states. Naturalization: 10 consecutive years of residence (Fundamental Law 1982), with renunciation of prior nationality required. Passport: ~25 visa-free destinations. ^[https://data.worldbank.org/country/GQ]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Permiso de trabajo / Tarjeta de residencia
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Equatoguinean employer; employer sponsorship. Spanish is the official language.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 consecutive years residence. ^[https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/Eq_Guinea_Fundamental_Law_extracts_11982_1982.pdf]
- **Official source**: Ministerio de Seguridad Nacional (no online portal).
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data (expat presence: oil industry, diplomatic).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Visado de estudiante
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Equatoguinean institution; proof of financial means.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: Residence time counts toward 10-year clock.
- **Official source**: Equatoguinean embassies / consulates.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Reagrupacion familiar
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; proof of relationship and accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years residence.
- **Official source**: Immigration authorities.
- **Community verdict**: No reliable data.

## Citizenship
- **Residence**: 10 consecutive years (Fundamental Law 1982, Article 10). Renunciation of prior nationality required. ^[https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/Eq_Guinea_Fundamental_Law_extracts_11982_1982.pdf]
- **Language**: Spanish proficiency expected (official language).
- **Dual citizenship**: Not permitted (renunciation required).
- **Processing**: Discretionary, by decree. Highly restrictive in practice.

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive income tax; corporate tax ~35%. Oil sector dominates revenue.
- **Remote workers**: No special regime. Tax enforcement limited outside oil sector.
- **General**: CFA franc (XAF, pegged to EUR). Oil wealth creates GDP per capita paradox (~$19,000 PPP) but extreme inequality.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated digital-nomad visa. Internet is limited and expensive (monopoly provider). Coworking nonexistent. Equatorial Guinea is not a nomad destination. [[gabon]] and [[cameroon]] are regional alternatives.

## Yacht / Maritime
Island and mainland coastline (296 km). Port of Malabo and Bata. Yacht tourism is minimal. No flag-of-convenience registration. Oil dominates maritime activity.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Visa required. No special RU programs. Russian oil interests present. Passport is very weak. One of the least open African passports.

## Community Pulse
No expat immigration community outside oil industry and diplomatic enclaves. One of the world's most closed and authoritarian societies (FH 5/100). Not discussed on any immigration forums.

## Country Card

### General description
Equatorial Guinea is a small, oil-rich Central African nation and one of the world's most authoritarian states. Despite a high GDP per capita (~$19,000 PPP) from oil, wealth is concentrated in the ruling elite and the vast majority live in poverty. Spanish is the official language. Naturalization requires 10 consecutive years and renunciation of prior nationality.

### Well-known strong points
- High GDP per capita (oil-fueled) on paper
- Spanish as official language (unique in Africa)
- CFA-franc-zone member (XAF pegged to EUR)
- Strategic Gulf of Guinea location

### Well-known problems
- One of the world's most repressive regimes (FH 5/100)
- Oil wealth not shared; extreme inequality
- Naturalization requires 10 consecutive years; renunciation required
- Weak passport; one of Africa's least open

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-19
