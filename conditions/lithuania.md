## Overview
Lithuania is an EU/Schengen/NATO/Eurozone member in the Baltic region with a rapidly growing fintech and IT sector. It does **NOT** offer a Golden Visa, a digital nomad visa, or citizenship-by-investment. The primary non-EU routes are the **Startup Visa** (for innovative entrepreneurs), the **EU Blue Card**, and standard work permits. Citizenship requires **10 years** of permanent residence + Lithuanian language + Constitution exam + **renunciation of previous citizenship** (generally). ^[https://www.migration.lt/naturalization]

## Per-Program Detail

### Startup Visa
- **Official name**: Startup Visa (Startuolio viza)
- **Conditions**: Non-EU founder starting an innovative business in Lithuania. Startup Committee approval required. CV, diplomas, business overview, pitch deck. Video interview (30–60 min, pitch + Q&A). ^[https://nomadresidence.com/lithuania]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year (Temporary Residence Permit), renewable for 1 year. After approval: must establish business within 120 days. ^[https://nomadresidence.com/lithuania]
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence → permanent residence → 10 years total → citizenship (Lithuanian language + Constitution exam + renunciation of previous citizenship).
- **Official source**: https://www.startupvisalithuania.com
- **Community verdict**: Works well — transparent process, but limited to genuinely innovative startups. No passive entrepreneur route.

### Highly Skilled Employment — EU Blue Card
- **Official name**: ES Mėlynoji kortelė
- **Conditions**: University degree (min. 3 years). Job offer from Lithuanian employer. Salary ≥ 1.5× Lithuanian average gross wage.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence → PR → 10 years → citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card/eu-blue-card-lithuania_en
- **Community verdict**: Works well — growing ICT sector creates demand.

### Employment Residency — Standard Work Permit
- **Official name**: Leidimas laikinai gyventi darbo pagrindu
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Lithuanian employer. Labor market test may apply. Salary meeting threshold.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 3 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residence → PR → 10 years total → citizenship.
- **Official source**: https://www.migracija.lt
- **Community verdict**: Works well — standard EU process.

## Citizenship
- **Standard**: **10 years** of lawful permanent residence ^[https://www.migration.lt/naturalization]
- **Marriage to Lithuanian**: 7 years permanent residence in marriage ^[https://www.migration.lt/naturalization]
- **Marriage to former deportee/political prisoner**: 5 years
- **Language**: Lithuanian proficiency + Constitution of Lithuania exam ^[https://www.migration.lt/naturalization]
- **Renunciation**: must renounce previous citizenship (generally) or declare intention to do so ^[https://www.migration.lt/naturalization]
- **Dual citizenship**: generally NOT permitted for naturalized citizens (exceptions for birthright and certain special cases)
- Processing: Migration Department reviews in 3 months, full process up to 12 months. Citizenship granted by Presidential decree. ^[https://www.migration.lt/naturalization]

## Tax
- Progressive income tax: 20% (up to ~€101,000) / 32% above
- Social contributions: ~19.5% employee (~7% health + ~12.5% pension)
- Corporate tax: 15% (5% for small businesses meeting criteria)
- No wealth tax

## IT / Digital Nomad
Lithuania does NOT have a dedicated digital nomad visa. Non-EU remote workers cannot legally work in Lithuania without a proper work permit. IT professionals primarily use:
- **Startup Visa** — for founders of innovative startups
- **EU Blue Card** — for employed professionals
- **Standard work permit** — with employer sponsorship
Vilnius and Kaunas are major fintech/IT hubs. Lithuania is one of the EU's top fintech jurisdictions.

## Yacht / Maritime
Baltic Sea coastline (Klaipėda, Palanga). Small yachting infrastructure. Yacht registration under Lithuanian flag available. Not a major yachting destination.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Lithuania has adopted a very restrictive stance toward Russian citizens since 2022. Visa issuance severely limited. Residence permits face enhanced scrutiny. Lithuanian language is demanding. Relatively small Russian-speaking community. The political environment is among the most unwelcoming in the EU for Russian applicants.

## Country Card

### General description
Lithuania is an EU/Schengen/NATO/Eurozone member in the Baltic region with a rapidly growing fintech and IT sector — it is one of the EU's top fintech jurisdictions. It does NOT offer a Golden Visa, digital nomad visa, or citizenship by investment. The primary non-EU routes are the Startup Visa (for innovative founders approved by the Startup Committee) and the EU Blue Card. Citizenship requires 10 years of permanent residence — one of the longest timelines in the EU — plus Lithuanian language proficiency, a Constitution exam, and generally renunciation of previous citizenship. Lithuania has a progressive income tax (20–32%), a 15% corporate tax (5% for small businesses), and major tech hubs in Vilnius and Kaunas.

### Well-known strong points
- One of EU's top fintech jurisdictions — strong, growing IT/startup sector
- High GDP per capita (PPP $58,901) — highest among Baltic states
- Well-run Startup Visa programme with transparent Startup Committee evaluation
- EU/Schengen/NATO/Eurozone membership with strong passport (181 visa-free)
- Moderate cost of living with good quality of life in Vilnius
- Strong rule of law (WJP #18) and improving Freedom House score (90/100)

### Well-known problems
- No Golden Visa, no digital nomad visa — limited entry routes for non-EU nationals
- 10-year citizenship timeline is among the longest in the EU
- Dual citizenship generally not permitted — must renounce original citizenship
- Low median salary (~€1,400–1,600) relative to Western Europe
- Very restrictive stance toward Russian citizens since 2022
- Small population (~2.8M) and modest local market for business
