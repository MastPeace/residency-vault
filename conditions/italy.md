## Overview
Italy introduced its [[digital-nomad]] visa in April 2024, making it an attractive EU destination for remote workers. Citizenship requires 10 years of residence. Italy also offers a flat-tax regime for new residents (€100K/year on foreign income) that appeals to HNWI. The [[golden-visa]] (Investor Visa) is available from €250,000. ^[https://www.romeing.it/how-to-get-italys-digital-nomad-visa-in-2026]

## Residence
Key routes:

**[[Digital-nomad]] visa (Article 27-quater, Legislative Decree 286/1998)**: for non-EU highly skilled remote workers. ^[https://www.romeing.it/how-to-get-italys-digital-nomad-visa-in-2026]
- Two streams: self-employed (digital nomads) and employees (remote workers)
- Income: ≥ ~€24,000–€28,500/year (3× the minimum threshold for healthcare exemption)
- Valid: 1 year, renewable annually
- After 5 years: eligible for EU long-term residence permit
- No quota limitations
- Processing: up to 90 days (remote workers), up to 120 days (digital nomads)
- Apply at Italian consulate, then obtain permesso di soggiorno within 8 days of arrival

**Investor Visa (Golden Visa)**: €250,000 in innovative startups, €500,000 in Italian companies, €1M in philanthropic donations, or €2M in government bonds. 2-year permit, renewable, path to citizenship.

**Elective Residence Visa**: for financially self-sufficient individuals with passive income ≥ €31,000/year. No work permitted.

## Citizenship
- 10 years of legal residence (non-EU)
- 4 years for EU citizens
- B1 Italian language proficiency
- Clean criminal record
- Dual citizenship permitted
- Processing: 2–3 years in practice

## Tax
Italy offers a **flat-tax regime for new residents**: €100,000/year substitute tax on all foreign income (renewable for up to 15 years). This is a major draw for HNWI. Standard progressive rates: 23%–43% plus regional/municipal surcharges.

The **impatriate regime** offers a 50% tax exemption on Italian-source income for qualifying workers moving to Italy (70% in southern regions).

## IT / Digital Nomad
Italy's [[digital-nomad]] visa is specifically designed for highly qualified professionals. The "highly qualified" requirement means either a post-secondary degree or 3+ years of professional training/experience. Visa holders can change clients/employers freely without needing a new permit.

## Yacht / Maritime
Italy has one of the world's largest yachting industries. Yacht registration under the Italian flag is available. The country is a global center for superyacht building and maintenance (Viareggio, Genoa, Ancona). Captain licensing: Italian-issued credentials (patente nautica) are recognized under international conventions. Major marinas in Portofino, Sardinia, Amalfi Coast, and Venice.

## Notes for RU citizens
Italian consulates in Russia may have reduced services. Applications are often routed through third countries. The elective residence visa and investor visa have been popular with Russian HNWI. The €100K flat-tax regime is attractive. No official restrictions against Russian nationals beyond standard EU sanctions screening.
