## Overview
Italy offers a broad range of residence routes: the Digital Nomad Visa (introduced April 2024), Investor Visa (Golden Visa from €250,000), and the Elective Residence Visa (passive income from €31,000/yr). The immigration system is managed by the Ministry of Interior (questure) and consulates. Citizenship requires 10 years of legal residence (4 years for EU citizens). Italy's flat-tax regime for new residents (€300,000/yr on foreign income) is a major draw for HNWI. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/italy-digital-nomad-visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Investor Visa for Italy (Visto per Investitori) — "Golden Visa"
- **Conditions**: €250,000 in innovative startups; €500,000 in Italian limited companies; €1,000,000 in philanthropic donations; €2,000,000 in Italian government bonds. No real estate route. ^[https://getgoldenvisa.com/italy-golden-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, renewable. Permanent residence after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years; B1 Italian; dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://investinitaly.gov.it/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (lowest EU investor threshold at €250K but startup option is risky; limited uptake compared to Portugal/Greece).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Visto per Residenza Elettiva (Elective Residence Visa)
- **Conditions**: Stable passive income ≥ €31,000/yr (€31,160 statutory reference) for single applicant; +~20% for spouse (~€38,000 combined), +~5% per dependent child. Must have secured Italian accommodation (1-year registered lease or property deed) before applying. Private health insurance ≥ €30,000 coverage. NO work of any kind, including remote work. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/italy-elective-residence-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable. Permanent residence after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years; B1 Italian; dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://www.esteri.it/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (high income bar: €31K vs Portugal's €11K; consulates routinely expect more than minimum; "you proved money, not income" is frequent refusal reason).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Visto per Nomadi Digitali / Lavoratori da Remoto (Art. 27-quater, D.Lgs 286/1998)
- **Conditions**: Income ≥ ~€28,000/yr (3× minimum threshold for healthcare exemption). Must be "highly qualified": post-secondary degree OR 3+ years professional experience. Two streams: self-employed (nomad) and employees (remote workers). Foreign employer/clients only. No quota limits. ^[https://consperth.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Digital-Nomads.pdf]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable annually. EU long-term residence after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years; B1 Italian; dual citizenship permitted.
- **Official source**: https://www.esteri.it/ (DM 29/02/2024)
- **Community verdict**: Works well (no quotas, fast processing ~60–90 days; but "highly qualified" definition causes consular variation).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Carta Blu UE (EU Blue Card)
- **Conditions**: University degree (3+ years) or 5 years relevant professional experience (3 for IT); job offer ≥ 6 months; salary ≥ 1.2× average gross annual salary (reduced for shortage occupations).
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years; permanent residence after 5 years (can be reduced).
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years; B1 Italian.
- **Official source**: https://www.esteri.it/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (standard EU Blue Card; Decreto Flussi quotas do not apply).

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Startup Visa (Decreto Crescita 2.0)
- **Conditions**: Innovative business plan; minimum €50,000 seed funding from recognized investor or incubator; no quota limits. Self-employment permit (Lavoro Autonomo) alternative for non-innovative businesses.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years; B1 Italian.
- **Official source**: https://italiastartupvisa.mise.gov.it/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (streamlined; no quotas; but €50K funding requirement is barrier).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Permesso di Soggiorno per Lavoro Subordinato (Standard Work Permit)
- **Conditions**: Job offer from Italian employer; subject to Decreto Flussi annual quotas (heavily oversubscribed); employer must prove no suitable EU candidate.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years depending on contract.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years; B1 Italian.
- **Official source**: https://www.esteri.it/
- **Community verdict**: Problematic (Decreto Flussi quotas are a bottleneck; EU Blue Card and DN visa are vastly preferred).

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Visto per Studio / Permesso di Soggiorno per Studio
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Italian institution; financial means ≥ €6,000/yr; health insurance.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student years count 50% toward 10-year clock.
- **Official source**: https://www.esteri.it/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Ricongiungimento Familiare
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid permit; income ≥ assegno sociale (€6,542/yr for 1 family member, scaled); adequate accommodation.
- **Duration / renewal**: Aligned with sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years; B1 Italian.
- **Official source**: https://www.esteri.it/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (processing delays common at questure level).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Elective Residence (retirement route) — same program as passive_income_residency
- **Conditions**: Pension ≥ €31,000/yr; secured Italian accommodation; health insurance.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable. Permanent residence after 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years; B1 Italian.
- **Official source**: https://www.esteri.it/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (high bar but popular; 7% flat tax regime for foreign pensioners in small southern municipalities is unique incentive).

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. Italy does not offer direct citizenship by investment.

## Citizenship
- **Residence requirement**: 10 years legal residence (non-EU); 4 years for EU citizens; 2 years if married to Italian citizen (halved if residing in Italy). ^[https://www.romeing.it/how-to-get-italys-digital-nomad-visa-in-2026]
- **Language**: B1 Italian (CEFR).
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted.
- **Processing**: 2–3 years in practice (jurisdictional backlog).
- **Jure sanguinis**: Italy has one of the most generous citizenship-by-descent programs (ancestor born in Italy after 1861, with conditions).

## Tax
- **Standard rates**: Progressive 23%–43% (plus regional/municipal surcharges ~1.2%–3.3%).
- **Flat-tax for new residents (Art. 24-bis TUIR)**: €300,000/yr substitute tax on ALL foreign income, renewable up to 15 years. Raised from €200,000 by 2026 Budget Law. Family members: +€25,000 each. Major HNWI draw. ^[https://jsbc.it/news/italy-impatriati-flat-tax-2026-2027-rule-changes]
- **7% flat tax for foreign pensioners**: Pensioners settling in small southern municipalities (<20,000 inhabitants) pay 7% flat tax on foreign pension income for up to 10 years.
- **Impatriate regime**: 50% tax exemption on Italian-source income for qualifying workers (70% in southern regions), up to 5 years.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Italy's Digital Nomad Visa (Art. 27-quater) is the dedicated remote-work route. Income: ≥€28,000/yr. Two streams: self-employed (nomads) and employees (remote workers). Processing: ~60–90 days at consulate, then permesso di soggiorno within 8 days of arrival. Milan, Rome, Turin, and Bologna have strong startup/nomad scenes. Impatriate tax breaks (50%–70% exemption) are available for DN visa holders who meet the requirements.

## Yacht / Maritime
Italy is a global leader in superyacht building and maintenance (Viareggio, Genoa, Ancona). Italian flag yacht registration available. Major marinas: Portofino, Sardinia (Costa Smeralda), Amalfi Coast, Venice. Captain licensing: Italian-issued credentials (patente nautica) recognized internationally. The yachting industry is a major economic sector; crew visas available under specific arrangements.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Italian consulates in Russia may have reduced services — route through third-country consulates. Elective Residence Visa and Investor Visa are accessible to Russian HNWI. The €300K flat-tax regime is a major draw. Digital Nomad Visa is open to Russian applicants but the "highly qualified" bar and source-of-funds scrutiny apply. No official restrictions beyond EU sanctions. Significant Russian community in parts of Tuscany, Liguria, and Sardinia.

## Community Pulse
Italy's DN visa has been well-received — no quotas, clean requirements, and access to impatriate tax breaks. The Elective Residence Visa has a significantly higher income bar (€31K) than Portugal D7 (€11K) or Spain NLV (€28.8K), making it a premium-tier option. The flat-tax regime (€300K/yr) is unique in the EU but the 50% price hike (from €200K) in 2026 cooled some interest. Citizenship processing times (2–3 years) are a frustration point. Investor Visa uptake is low relative to other EU programs.
