## Overview
Venezuela is a South American country undergoing a prolonged economic and humanitarian crisis with mass emigration. Naturalisation requires **10 years of continuous legal residence** (reduced to **5 years** for nationals of Latin American/Caribbean countries, Spain, Portugal and Italy). Dual citizenship is constitutionally permitted (Article 34). There is no citizenship- or residence-by-investment programme and no digital-nomad visa; the country is almost exclusively an emigration, not immigration, destination. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_nationality_law]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Visa de trabajo / residencia temporal (trabajo)
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer; employer sponsorship. In practice the crisis economy has sharply reduced foreign-hire activity.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years continuous residence (5 for Latin American/Caribbean/Spain/Portugal/Italy nationals). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_nationality_law]
- **Official source**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_nationality_law
- **Community verdict**: Problematic — near-zero foreign hiring; most foreigners leave, not enter.

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia de rentista (retirement/rentier)
- **Conditions**: Proof of stable foreign income (pension or rentier income). No published fixed threshold; consular discretion.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 10 years residence (5 for qualifying nationals).
- **Official source**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_nationality_law
- **Community verdict**: No reliable community data — extremely rare route in practice.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalisation**: 10 years continuous legal residence (general); 5 years for nationals of Latin American/Caribbean countries, Spain, Portugal, Italy; spouse of a Venezuelan after 5 years' marriage/residence. Citizenship test required. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_nationality_law]
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted (Article 34 of the Constitution). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_nationality_law]
- **Passport**: ~120 visa-free/on-arrival (historically strong in Latin America).

## Tax
- Territorial-based taxation: Venezuelan-source income taxed; foreign-source income generally not taxed for residents. Progressive PIT up to ~34%. In practice, the economy is heavily de-facto dollarised with weak enforcement. Corporate income tax ~34%.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad visa. Infrastructure (power, internet, banking) has deteriorated sharply; remote workers do not target Venezuela.

## Yacht / Maritime
Extensive Caribbean coastline; islands of Isla Margarita, Los Roques, and Mochima. Historically a yachting/cruising destination, but services and safety have declined with the crisis.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Visa required; some historical visa-free arrangements lapsed. Spanish working language.
- No viable residence pathway in practice; banking and remittances are extremely difficult.
- Dual citizenship is permitted, but Venezuela is a poor value proposition for Russian applicants given crisis conditions.

## Community Pulse
The dominant narrative is emigration (~7–8 million Venezuelans abroad). There is no meaningful inbound expat or investor community. The 2024 election and continued political crisis have further deterred any residence interest.

## Country Card

### General description
Venezuela is a South American country with the world's largest proven oil reserves, now in a prolonged economic, political and humanitarian crisis that has driven ~7–8 million citizens abroad. It is almost exclusively an emigration source, not a destination. Naturalisation exists (10 years, or 5 for Latin American/Caribbean/Hispanic nationals) with dual citizenship permitted, but there is no investment or nomad pathway.

### Well-known strong points
- Dual citizenship constitutionally permitted
- Relatively strong passport (~120 destinations) by regional standards
- 5-year naturalisation for Latin American/Caribbean/Spain/Portugal/Italy nationals
- Rich natural resources and Caribbean coastline

### Well-known problems
- Deep economic crisis; hyperinflation and de-facto dollarisation
- "Not Free" (FH 13/100); rule of law worst in WJP index (143/143)
- Mass emigration; minimal inbound residence interest
- Collapsed infrastructure (power, internet, banking, healthcare)
- No investment-residence or nomad visa

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-11
- **Recent changes**: No immigration-policy changes confirmed; political crisis post-2024 election continues.
- **Watch items**: Any normalisation/opening of residence policy; passport-strength changes as relations shift.
