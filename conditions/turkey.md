## Overview
Turkey operates the world's most popular citizenship-by-investment programme (a [[golden-visa]] that directly yields citizenship, unlike the EU variants). The 2026 tax reform (20-year foreign income exemption) functions as a de-facto [[nhr]] for relocating investors., with real estate investment from $400,000 granting citizenship in 8–12 months. The Turkish passport provides visa-free access to ~110 destinations and E-2 Investor Visa eligibility for the US. Turkey permits dual citizenship. In August 2026, Turkey revoked 6,134 fraudulently obtained citizenships. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Citizenship_by_Investment]

## Residence
**Short-term residence permit**: available for property owners (any value), business owners, students, and tourists applying for citizenship. Valid for 1 year, renewable.

**Investor residence permit** (Article 31(1)(j)): issued as a prerequisite for the citizenship-by-investment process. No physical residence required.

**Work permit**: employer-sponsored; also serves as a residence permit. Turkey has tightened irregular migration controls.

## Citizenship
**Citizenship by Investment (Article 12, Law No. 5901)**: ^[https://www.astons.com/citizenship-by-investment/turkey]
- **Real estate**: ≥ $400,000 (hold 3 years with title-deed annotation)
- **Bank deposit**: ≥ $500,000 (hold 3 years)
- **Government bonds**: ≥ $500,000 (hold 3 years)
- **REIT/fund**: ≥ $500,000
- **Job creation**: 50+ employees
- Processing: 8–12 months
- **No residence requirement, no language test**
- Spouse and children under 18 included
- After 3 years: investment can be divested, citizenship retained

**August 2026 revocation**: 6,134 citizenships revoked for fraud detected in the program. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Citizenship_by_Investment]

**Standard naturalization**: 5 years of continuous residence, Turkish language proficiency, no criminal record.

## Tax
Progressive income tax: 15%–40%. **2026 tax reform**: eligible individuals relocating to Turkey may receive a 20-year exemption on qualifying foreign-source income. Corporate tax: 25%. No wealth tax. Turkey-Russia double taxation treaty in effect.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Turkey does not have a dedicated [[digital-nomad]] visa. However, the short-term residence permit based on property ownership or the business visa covers remote workers. Istanbul and Antalya have growing digital nomad communities. Cost of living is favorable.

## Yacht / Maritime
Turkey has an extensive Mediterranean/Aegean coastline. Yacht registration under the Turkish flag is available. The "Blue Voyage" coast (Bodrum, Marmaris, Göcek, Fethiye) is a world-class yachting destination. Turkey has a large yacht-building industry. Captain licensing under Turkish maritime regulations.

## Notes for RU citizens
Turkey is exceptionally popular with Russian citizens: visa-free entry (60–90 days), direct flights, no sanctions, accessible banking (some Turkish banks serve RU clients), and widespread Russian-speaking communities in Antalya, Istanbul, and Alanya. The $400,000 citizenship-by-investment route is heavily used by Russian HNWIs. The 2026 tax reform (20-year foreign income exemption) is particularly attractive for RU entrepreneurs relocating their operations.
