## Overview

Turkey operates one of the world's most popular **citizenship-by-investment** programs — real estate investment from **$400,000** or financial investment from **$500,000** yields a Turkish passport in **3–4 months** with no residence requirement and no language test. The passport provides visa-free access to ~110 destinations and US E-2 Investor Visa eligibility. Turkey permits dual citizenship. The August 2026 revocation of 6,134 fraudulently obtained citizenships highlights diligence requirements. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Citizenship_by_Investment]

## Per-Program Detail

### Citizenship by Investment

- **Official name**: Türk Vatandaşlık Kanunu'nun 12. Maddesi Kapsamında İstisnai Yoldan Vatandaşlık (Exceptional Acquisition of Citizenship under Article 12, Law No. 5901)
- **Conditions** (choose one): ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/turkey-citizenship-by-investment] ^[https://www.visaverge.com/visa/turkey-citizenship-by-investment-400-500k-routes-and-timeline]
  - **Real estate**: ≥ **$400,000** (purchase price, hold 3 years with title-deed annotation)
  - **Bank deposit**: ≥ **$500,000** (hold 3 years in Turkish bank)
  - **Government bonds**: ≥ **$500,000** (hold 3 years)
  - **REIT/fund shares**: ≥ **$500,000** (hold 3 years)
  - **Job creation**: 50+ employees
- **Processing**: **3–4 months** from complete file to passport. ^[https://www.visaverge.com/visa/turkey-citizenship-by-investment-400-500k-routes-and-timeline]
- **No residence requirement, no language test**.
- **Family**: Spouse and children under 18 included.
- **Exit**: After 3 years, investment can be divested; citizenship is irrevocable (except for fraud).
- **Official source**: [Invest in Turkey — Citizenship](https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/news/news-from-turkey/pages/new-turkish-citizenship-rules-encourage-foreign-investment.aspx) | [Directorate General of Migration Management](https://en.goc.gov.tr/)
- **⚠️ August 2026**: 6,134 citizenships revoked for fraud. Due diligence is critical — use reputable law firms. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Citizenship_by_Investment]
- **Community verdict**: Works well (with caveats) — fast processing, low entry relative to Caribbean CBI programs, real estate market is liquid in Istanbul/Antalya. Fraud revocations highlight need for proper legal representation. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/turkey-citizenship-by-investment]

### Investment Residency / Property Residence

- **Official name**: Kısa Dönem İkamet İzni (Short-term residence permit)
- **Conditions**: Own any residential property in Turkey (no minimum value for residence purposes). ^[https://en.goc.gov.tr/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years continuous residence → standard naturalization (Turkish language required).
- **Official source**: [DGMM — Residence Permits](https://en.goc.gov.tr/)
- **Community verdict**: Works well — straightforward for property owners. Note: CBI is the preferred route for those who can meet $400K threshold.

### Employment Residency / Highly Skilled Employment

- **Official name**: Çalışma İzni (Work permit — also serves as residence permit)
- **Conditions**: Employer-sponsored. Employer must justify hiring a foreign national. ^[https://en.goc.gov.tr/]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year initially, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years continuous residence + Turkish language.
- **Official source**: [DGMM](https://en.goc.gov.tr/)
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — work permits are quota-limited. Employer must demonstrate inability to fill position locally.

### Startup / Entrepreneur

- **Conditions**: Register a business in Turkey. Business visa/residence available.
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Community verdict**: Works well for established businesses.

### Student Residency

- **Conditions**: Enrollment in a Turkish university or educational institution.
- **Duration**: Duration of studies.
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### Family Reunification

- **Conditions**: Spouse or minor child of a Turkish citizen or legal resident.
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

## Citizenship

- **CBI route**: $400K/$500K → citizenship in 3–4 months (see [#citizenship-by-investment](#citizenship-by-investment)).
- **Standard naturalization**: 5 years continuous residence, Turkish language proficiency, no criminal record.
- **By marriage**: 3 years of marriage to a Turkish citizen.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted.
- **Revocation risk**: Fraud in CBI application is grounds for revocation (6,134 cases in Aug 2026).

## Tax

- **Personal income tax**: Progressive, 15%–40%. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/turkey-citizenship-by-investment]
- **2026 tax reform**: Eligible relocating individuals may receive a **20-year exemption** on qualifying foreign-source income (de-facto NHR equivalent). ^[https://www.astons.com/citizenship-by-investment/turkey]
- **Corporate tax**: 25%.
- **No wealth tax**.
- **Double taxation treaties**: ~85 countries including Russia.

## IT / Digital Nomad

Turkey does not have a dedicated [[digital-nomad]] visa. However, the short-term residence permit based on property ownership covers remote workers. Istanbul and Antalya have growing digital nomad communities. Cost of living is favorable. Internet infrastructure is good in major cities.

## Yacht / Maritime

Turkey has an extensive Mediterranean/Aegean coastline. The "Blue Voyage" coast (Bodrum, Marmaris, Göcek, Fethiye) is world-class for yachting. Yacht registration under the Turkish flag is available. Turkey has a large yacht-building industry. Captain licensing under Turkish maritime regulations. Relevant for [[yacht-captain]] considerations.

## Notes for RU citizens

Turkey is exceptionally popular with Russian citizens: visa-free entry (60–90 days), direct flights, no sanctions, accessible banking (some Turkish banks serve RU clients), widespread Russian-speaking communities (Antalya, Istanbul, Alanya). The $400,000 CBI route is heavily used by Russian HNWIs. The 2026 tax reform (20-year foreign income exemption) is attractive for RU entrepreneurs. Payment schemes exist for property purchases using RUB (via third-country conversions).

## Community Pulse

CBI program is well-established and fast. Main risk: fraud in the application chain (use reputable law firms). Expats report generally positive experience. Banking for foreigners can be bureaucratic. The August 2026 mass revocation has not materially slowed the program but increased scrutiny.

## Country Card

Turkey is a transcontinental country spanning Europe and Asia with ~88 million people, operating one of the world's most popular citizenship-by-investment programs: $400,000 in real estate yields a passport in 3–4 months with no residence requirement and no language test. The passport grants visa-free access to ~71 destinations and US E-2 Investor Visa eligibility. A 2026 tax reform introduced a 20-year exemption on foreign-source income for qualifying relocants — a de-facto NHR equivalent. The August 2026 revocation of 6,134 fraudulently obtained citizenships underscores the importance of proper legal representation.

### Well-known strong points
- Fastest CBI globally: citizenship in 3–4 months via $400K real estate
- No residence requirement, no language test for CBI; dual citizenship permitted
- US E-2 Investor Visa eligibility with Turkish passport
- 2026 tax reform: 20-year exemption on foreign-source income for qualifying relocants
- Large, liquid real estate market (Istanbul, Antalya); 3-year hold period
- Massive tourism infrastructure; world-class Mediterranean/Aegean coastline

### Well-known problems
- Rule of Law very weak (WJP 0.41, rank 117/142); Freedom House: Not Free (33/100)
- High inflation (40–50% in recent years); currency depreciation risk
- CBI fraud risk: 6,134 citizenships revoked (Aug 2026) — due diligence is critical
- No formal digital nomad visa; standard naturalization requires 5 years + Turkish language
- Progressive income tax up to 40% for high earners (unless qualifying for new exemption)
- Political authoritarianism; media censorship; internet restrictions
