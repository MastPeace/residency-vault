## Overview
Bahrain operates a [[golden-residency]] programme (permanent, renewed every 10 years for a BD 300 administrative fee) covering: property owners (BD 130K+), employees (5+ years, BD 2K/mo salary), retirees (BD 4K pension or 15+ yrs in Bahrain + BD 2K pension), and talented individuals. Self-sponsorship residence permits are also available for property owners at BD 200 (2yr), BD 400 (5yr), BD 600 (10yr). Personal income tax is 0%. Bahrain does NOT offer citizenship to expatriates. ^[https://www.npra.gov.bh/en/services/visas-residence-permit/residence-permits-services/golden-residency]

## Per-Program Detail

### Golden Residency — Property Owner
- **Official name**: الإقامة الذهبية — مالك عقار (Golden Residency for Foreigners Owning Real Estate)
- **Conditions**: Property ownership in Bahrain with total value ≥ BD 130,000 (~$345,000) as per title deed (NPRA route) or ≥ BD 200,000 (~$530,000) via eGovernment portal. Valid passport (6+ months), health insurance, 6-month bank statement, clean medical report, previous residence permit (if applicable). ^[https://www.npra.gov.bh/en/services/visas-residence-permit/residence-permits-services/golden-residency/golden-residence-permit-owner-of-properties-in-bahrain]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent residency, subject to BD 300 administrative fee every 10 years. Application fee BD 5 (non-refundable). ^[https://www.bahrain.bh/wps/portal/en/BNP/ServicesCatalogue/GSX-UI-PServiceDetails?psID=3021]
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.npra.gov.bh/en/services/visas-residence-permit/residence-permits-services/golden-residency
- **Community verdict**: Works well — straightforward, low threshold, fast processing (5 working days).

### Golden Residency — Employee
- **Official name**: Golden Residency for Foreign Residents (Employees)
- **Conditions**: Working in Bahrain for ≥ 5 years with average salary ≥ BD 2,000/mo (~$5,300) in past 5 years. Valid health insurance, 6-month bank statement. Or retired after 15+ years in Bahrain government/private sector with average pension ≥ BD 2,000/mo in last 5 years. ^[https://www.npra.gov.bh/en/services/visas-residence-permit/residence-permits-services/golden-residency/golden-residence-permit-employedretired-foreigner-residing-in-bahrain]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent residency, BD 300 every 10 years. Application fee BD 5.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.npra.gov.bh
- **Community verdict**: Works well for long-term expat professionals.

### Golden Residency — Retired (Non-Resident)
- **Official name**: Golden Residency for Retired Nonresident Foreigners
- **Conditions**: Basic pension ≥ BD 4,000/mo (~$10,600) from Bahrain or any country. Health insurance in Bahrain, 6-month bank statement, clean medical report. ^[https://www.npra.gov.bh/en/services/visas-residence-permit/residence-permits-services/golden-residency]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent residency, BD 300 every 10 years. Application fee BD 5.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.npra.gov.bh
- **Community verdict**: Niche — high pension threshold limits audience.

### Golden Residency — Talented Individuals
- **Official name**: Golden Residency for Talented Individuals
- **Conditions**: Nomination by government agency in designated fields, OR PhD certificate (academics), OR higher education degree in priority specialization, OR approval from accredited business incubator (entrepreneurs). ^[https://www.npra.gov.bh/en/services/visas-residence-permit/residence-permits-services/golden-residency]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent residency, BD 300 every 10 years. Application fee BD 5.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.npra.gov.bh
- **Community verdict**: Operational but narrow eligibility.

### Self-Sponsorship Residence (Property)
- **Official name**: Self-Sponsorship Residence Permit for Property Owners
- **Conditions**: Property ownership in Bahrain. No minimum value specified at NPRA level (practical floor varies). ^[https://services.bahrain.bh]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years (BD 200), 5 years (BD 400), or 10 years (BD 600). Application fee BD 5.
- **Official source**: https://services.bahrain.bh
- **Community verdict**: Alternative to Golden Residency for lower-value property owners.

### Employment Residency
- **Official name**: Standard Work Permit
- **Conditions**: Employer sponsor, valid labour contract. Medical exam. Minimum wage: BD 400/mo (private sector).
- **Duration / renewal**: 1–2 years, renewable. Tied to employer.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.lmra.bh
- **Community verdict**: Standard GCC employment model.

### Student Residency
- **Official name**: Student Visa
- **Conditions**: Enrolment at accredited Bahraini institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Duration of study.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.npra.gov.bh
- **Community verdict**: Functional.

### Family Reunification
- **Official name**: Family Visa
- **Conditions**: Sponsor must hold valid residence permit, meet minimum salary (varies, typically BD 400+).
- **Duration / renewal**: Tied to sponsor.
- **Path to citizenship**: None.
- **Official source**: https://www.npra.gov.bh
- **Community verdict**: Standard.

## Citizenship
**Not available for expatriates.** Bahraini citizenship is by descent (Bahraini father) or by rare Royal Decree. Naturalization is limited to: Arab nationals with 15+ years residence + Arabic fluency, or women married to Bahraini citizens (5 years). Approvals are exceedingly rare. No citizenship-by-investment programme.

## Tax
- **0% personal income tax** — no tax on salary, dividends, capital gains.
- **0% corporate tax** on most businesses (except oil & gas: 46% on profits).
- **10% VAT** introduced 2022.
- **No wealth tax, no inheritance tax, no capital gains tax.**
- **Excise tax**: 100% on tobacco, 50% on sugary drinks.
- Social insurance: 12% employer + 7% employee (Bahraini nationals); 3% employer + 1% employee (expatriates).
- Extensive double taxation treaty network.

## IT / Digital Nomad
- **No dedicated digital nomad visa.** Bahrain does not offer a remote work visa.
- IT sector is growing: Bahrain FinTech Bay, StartUp Bahrain ecosystem, Amazon Web Services regional hub.
- Digital infrastructure: excellent (fibre nationwide, 5G).
- For remote workers: tourist visa (visa-free/eVisa for many nationalities, 2 weeks to 3 months).
- Long-term: Golden Residency — Employee route after 5 years; property route if purchasing.
- Bahrain is positioning itself as a regional fintech hub.

## Yacht / Maritime
- Amwaj Marina, Bahrain Yacht Club — modest but functional.
- Bahrain-flag registration available.
- Strategic Persian Gulf location with access to all GCC coastlines.
- Smaller yachting scene than UAE but growing.

## Notes for RU citizens
- **eVisa** available for Russian passport holders (2 weeks, extendable). Not visa-free — visa required in advance or on arrival (discretionary).
- **Small RU community** — smaller than UAE but present.
- **No sanctions**, Bahraini banks generally accessible to Russian nationals.
- **Property route to Golden Residency** is the most accessible pathway: BD 200K (~$530K) via eGovernment or BD 130K via NPRA.
- **Direct flights**: Bahrain–Moscow via Gulf Air (limited, ~2–3 weekly).
- Bahrain's social environment is the most liberal in the Gulf (alcohol legal, Western lifestyle), making it a softer landing for RU nationals compared to Saudi/Kuwait/Qatar.
- Awareness low among RU audience; UAE dominates mindshare.

## Community pulse
Bahrain is the most underrated Gulf residency destination. Golden Residency is accessible (BD 200K property + BD 300/10yr), fast (5 working days), and liberal. The main drawbacks: smaller economy, less global connectivity (airport), and lower prestige than UAE. For those prioritizing cost-effective permanent residency in a 0% tax Gulf state, Bahrain is a strong option. Expat quality of life is good, cost of living moderate.

## Country Card

Bahrain is a small island kingdom in the Persian Gulf, connected to Saudi Arabia by the King Fahd Causeway. It was the first Gulf state to diversify beyond oil, developing a financial services hub in Manama. Personal income tax is 0%, and the cost of living is the most affordable among GCC financial centres. The Golden Residency programme offers self-sponsored residence through property purchase (BD 130K–200K), and freelancer permits are available. Approximately half the population are expatriates. Bahrain serves as a weekend destination for Saudi visitors, giving it a more liberal social atmosphere than its neighbors.

### Well-known strong points
- 0% personal income tax; most affordable financial hub in the GCC
- Strong financial services sector — long-established offshore banking centre
- Saudi causeway access: 40-minute drive to the larger Saudi market
- Golden Residency with property threshold from BD 130K — lowest investment residency in the Gulf
- More socially liberal than Saudi Arabia/Qatar — alcohol legal, active nightlife
- Compact and walkable — small size means short commutes and easy lifestyle

### Well-known problems
- No pathway to citizenship — naturalization is exceptionally rare for non-Gulf Arabs
- Severe political repression since 2011; Freedom House 12/100, one of the lowest in the region
- Shia-majority population ruled by Sunni monarchy; persistent sectarian tensions
- Small domestic market (~1.6M) limits business scale
- Government debt is high (~111% of GDP) relative to Gulf peers
- Extreme summer heat and humidity (May–September); limited green space
