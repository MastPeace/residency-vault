## Overview
Ireland is an EU member with one of the world's strongest passports and a thriving tech economy (EU headquarters of Google, Meta, Apple, Microsoft). The main work route is the Critical Skills Employment Permit (Stamp 4 after 2 years). The Immigrant Investor Programme is closed since February 2023. Citizenship requires 5 years of reckonable residence. ^[https://total.law/us-to-ie/residency]

## Residence
**Irish Residence Permit (IRP)**: required for all non-EEA nationals staying > 90 days. Registration is in-person at the Immigration Registration Office (€300 fee). ^[https://total.law/ie/residency]

Key immigration stamps:
- **Stamp 1**: work permit holders (with employment restrictions)
- **Stamp 1G**: graduates/spouses
- **Stamp 4**: unrestricted work. Obtained:
  - After 2 years on Critical Skills Employment Permit
  - After 5 years on General Employment Permit
  - Through family reunification
- **Stamp 5 (WCATT)**: Without Condition As To Time — indefinite residence without renewal. After 8 years of legal residence on qualifying stamps. ^[https://consiliojus.com/irish-residence-permit]

**Stamp 0**: limited permission for financially independent persons (similar to [[non-lucrative-visa]]). Income: €50,000/year minimum + lump sum. Valid 1 year. No work. ^[https://esim.holafly.com/visas/ireland-digital-nomad-visa]

## Citizenship
- 5 years of reckonable residence within the last 9 years (including 1 continuous year before application)
- 3 years if married to an Irish citizen
- No language test (uniquely rare in the EU)
- €1,125 application fee
- Dual citizenship permitted
- Right to Irish = EU passport

## Tax
Progressive income tax: 20% (up to €40,000 single) / 40% above. USC surcharge: 0.5%–4.5%. PRSI: 4%. Corporate tax: 12.5% (15% for large companies under OECD Pillar 2). Ireland's corporate tax is a major draw for multinational tech companies.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Ireland does not offer a dedicated [[digital-nomad]] visa. IT professionals primarily use:
- Critical Skills Employment Permit (2-year path to Stamp 4)
- Stamp 0 (for financially independent, not permitted to work)
- The tech job market is robust but requires employer sponsorship for initial entry

## Yacht / Maritime
Ireland has an extensive Atlantic coastline. Yacht registration under the Irish flag is available. Major marinas: Dublin, Cork (Crosshaven), Dún Laoghaire. Recognized sailing and boating licensing.

## Notes for RU citizens
Irish visas for Russian nationals are available but processing may be delayed. The Critical Skills route is open in principle. No specific restrictions beyond standard EU sanctions screening. Ireland has a small Russian-speaking community. The absence of a language test for citizenship is advantageous.
