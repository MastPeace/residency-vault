## Overview
Ireland is an EU member with one of the world's strongest passports and a thriving tech economy (EU headquarters of Google, Meta, Apple, Microsoft). Immigration is managed by Immigration Service Delivery (ISD). The Immigrant Investor Programme (IIP) is closed since February 2023. Ireland uniquely requires no language test for citizenship. Citizenship: 5 years of reckonable residence in the last 9 years (3 years for spouses of Irish citizens). The Critical Skills Employment Permit is the flagship route: Stamp 4 (unrestricted work) after 2 years. ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie]

## Per-Program Detail

### Investment Residency
- **Immigrant Investor Programme (IIP)** — **CLOSED** since 15 February 2023. No new applications accepted. Existing approved investors retain rights and can renew. Previous thresholds: €1,000,000 enterprise investment (min 3 years), €1,000,000 investment fund, €2,000,000 REIT, or €500,000 endowment (€400,000 for 5+ applicants). ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-live-in-ireland/immigrant-investor-programme]
- **Community verdict**: N/A (closed). Previously heavily used by Chinese nationals (~90% of applications).

### Passive Income Residency
- **Stamp 0** — limited permission for financially independent persons
- **Conditions**: regular income ≥**€50,000/year** (individual); substantial savings/lump sum in addition; comprehensive private health insurance; NO work of any kind permitted (including remote work); no recourse to public funds. This is a permissive/discretionary permission — ISD must be convinced the person will not become a burden. ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/registering-your-immigration-permission/information-on-registering/immigration-permission-stamps]
- **Duration**: 1 year, renewable annually (no path to long-term residence through this stamp alone)
- **Path to citizenship**: Stamp 0 does NOT count as reckonable residence for citizenship
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/registering-your-immigration-permission/information-on-registering/immigration-permission-stamps]
- **Community verdict**: Problematic — no path to permanent residence or citizenship; renewal is not guaranteed; very limited use case

### Highly Skilled Employment
- **Critical Skills Employment Permit** — Ireland's flagship fast-track work route
- **Conditions**: job offer in an occupation on the **Critical Skills Occupations List** (includes most IT, engineering, healthcare, and science roles) with salary ≥**€38,000/year**; OR any occupation with salary ≥**€64,000/year**; degree or equivalent experience required. No labour market test for first application. ^[https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-enterprise-tourism-and-employment/publications/critical-skills-employment-permits]
- **Duration**: 2 years on employment permit → automatic eligibility for **Stamp 4** (unrestricted work permit, no employer tie). After 5 years total reckonable residence → citizenship.
- **Path to permanent residency**: Stamp 5 (WCATT — Without Condition As To Time) after 8 years of legal residence on qualifying stamps. ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/registering-your-immigration-permission/information-on-registering/immigration-permission-stamps]
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-enterprise-tourism-and-employment/publications/critical-skills-employment-permits]
- **Community verdict**: Works well — fastest path to unrestricted work in the EU; strong demand for IT professionals

### Startup Entrepreneur
- **Start-up Entrepreneur Programme (STEP)** — for founders of innovative businesses
- **Conditions**: innovative business with high growth and export potential; funding ≥**€50,000** (from own resources, angel investors, venture capital, or state agency); business must create ≥10 jobs and €1M revenue within 3–4 years; comprehensive business plan. Application to the Irish Naturalisation and Immigration Service. ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-live-in-ireland/i-want-to-start-a-business-in-ireland]
- **Duration**: 2 years, renewable
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years reckonable residence
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-live-in-ireland/i-want-to-start-a-business-in-ireland]
- **Community verdict**: Mixed — funding threshold is moderate but 10 jobs target is ambitious for an early-stage start-up

### Employment Residency
- **General Employment Permit** — standard work route (non-Critical Skills)
- **Conditions**: job offer from Irish employer; salary ≥**€34,000/year** (exceptions for certain occupations at €30,000); labour market test required (advertised for 4+ weeks on EURES + national employment service); valid for 6–24 months initially. ^[https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-enterprise-tourism-and-employment/publications/employment-permits]
- **Path to Stamp 4**: after 5 years on a General Employment Permit
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years reckonable residence
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-enterprise-tourism-and-employment/publications/employment-permits]
- **Community verdict**: Works well; slower path than Critical Skills (5 vs 2 years to Stamp 4)

### Student Residency
- **Stamp 2** — for non-EU students
- **Conditions**: acceptance at recognised Irish institution (university, institute of technology); proof of €10,000/year funds for living costs; tuition ~€9,000–55,000/year (varies hugely by programme and institution); private health insurance. ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-live-in-ireland/i-want-to-study-in-ireland]
- **Duration**: duration of course (max 7 years)
- **Work rights**: 20 hours/week during term, 40 hours during holidays
- **Post-graduation**: Stamp 1G — 2 years to seek employment (graduates of Level 8/9/10)
- **Path to citizenship**: Stamp 2 does NOT count as reckonable residence; Stamp 1G counts
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-live-in-ireland/i-want-to-study-in-ireland]
- **Community verdict**: Works well; moderate cost by English-speaking-country standards; robust graduate employment pathway

### Family Reunification
- **Family reunification** — for nuclear family of legal residents
- **Conditions**: sponsor must hold valid Stamp 1/4/5; gross income ≥€40,000/year (for a family of 2, higher for more dependents); adequate housing; no reliance on public funds. Processing ~12 months. ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-live-in-ireland/i-want-to-join-my-family-in-ireland]
- **Duration**: tied to sponsor's stamp
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years (independent application after Stamp 4/5)
- **Official source**: ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-live-in-ireland/i-want-to-join-my-family-in-ireland]
- **Community verdict**: Works well; income threshold moderate for Irish salaries; long processing times are a known complaint

### Retired Residency
- **Stamp 0** — same as passive income, accessible to retirees with sufficient pension/income
- **Conditions**: pension/income ≥€50,000/year; substantial lump sum savings; private health insurance; no work permitted INCLUDING remote work. No path to permanent residency or citizenship. ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/registering-your-immigration-permission/information-on-registering/immigration-permission-stamps]
- **Community verdict**: Problematic for anyone seeking a long-term solution — no pathway to permanence

## Citizenship
- **Standard**: 5 years of reckonable residence within the last 9 years (including 1 continuous year immediately before application)
- **Shortened**: 3 years if married to or in a civil partnership with an Irish citizen
- **Language**: **NO language test** (uniquely among major EU countries)
- **Dual citizenship**: permitted
- **Application fee**: €1,125; processing ~6–12 months
- **Reckonable residence**: counted on Stamp 1, 1G, 3, 4, 4S, 5. Stamp 0, 2, and 2A do NOT count.
- **EU passport**: yes — Irish passport is exceptionally strong (visa-free to USA, UK CTA rights) ^[https://www.irishimmigration.ie/how-to-become-a-citizen]

## Tax
- **Progressive income tax**: 20% up to €44,000 (single, 2026 estimate) / 40% above. USC surcharge: 0.5%–8%. PRSI: 4%–4.1%. Marginal rate ~52%.
- **Corporate tax**: 12.5% active trading (15% for companies with >€750M revenue under OECD Pillar 2).
- **Non-domiciled remittance basis**: Irish residents who are NOT domiciled in Ireland are taxed on Irish-source income + foreign income ONLY if remitted to Ireland. This is a significant advantage for wealthy non-Irish-domiciled residents. No remittance charge. ^[https://www.rbk.ie/insight/remittance-basis-whats-it-all-about]
- **No wealth tax**.
- **VAT**: 23% standard.
- **Capital gains tax**: 33%.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No dedicated [[digital-nomad]] visa. IT professionals use:
- Critical Skills Employment Permit (fast-track, 2 years to Stamp 4)
- Ireland is Europe's tech capital: Google, Meta, Apple, Microsoft, Stripe, and hundreds of tech companies have major operations in Dublin/Cork/Galway
- Remote work for foreign employers while physically in Ireland requires a work permit or Stamp 0 (which explicitly excludes work). A gap in practical enforcement exists.

## Yacht / Maritime
Ireland has an extensive Atlantic and Irish Sea coastline. Yacht registration under the Irish flag (Mercantile Marine Office). Major marinas: Dún Laoghaire, Crosshaven (Cork), Howth. Strong sailing culture (Royal Cork Yacht Club — world's oldest).

## Notes for RU citizens
Irish visas for Russian citizens are available but processing may be delayed. No specific restrictions beyond EU sanctions screening and enhanced due diligence. The Critical Skills route is open in principle. The non-dom remittance basis is uniquely attractive for wealthy applicants. The absence of a language test is advantageous. Ireland has a small Russian-speaking community. Banking for Russian nationals has tightened significantly.
