## Overview
Brazil's immigration system is administered by the Ministry of Justice (Conselho Nacional de Imigração — CNIg) and Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Brazil offers a well-established Investor Visa (VIPER), a digital nomad visa (VITEM XIV), a retirement route, and standard work/student/family visas. Citizenship is available after 4 years of legal residence (1 year for spouses/parents). Brazil is a MERCOSUR member; permanent residents enjoy mobility rights. ^[https://www.gov.br/mre/]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: VIPER — Visto Permanente para Investidor (Brazil Investor Visa)
- **Conditions**: Real estate: BRL 1,000,000 (~$192,000 USD) in most regions; BRL 700,000 (~$135,000 USD) in North/Northeast. Business investment: BRL 600,000 (~$115,000 USD) in a Brazilian company with local employment. R$150,000 for tech/innovation startups. ^[https://www.imidaily.com/latin-america/unsung-im-programs-part-3-brazils-permanent-residence-investor-visa-viper]
- **Duration / renewal**: Business route: immediate permanent residency (reviewed after 3 years). Real estate: 2-year temporary, renewable while investment maintained. Minimum stay: 30 days/year for real estate; max 6 months consecutive absence for business.
- **Path to citizenship**: 4 years of continuous residence; 14 days minimum stay every 2 years to qualify.
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.br/mre/pt-br
- **Community verdict**: Works well (investment thresholds lower than many European programs; bureaucracy manageable with local counsel).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Retirement Visa (Visto de Aposentado)
- **Conditions**: Monthly pension ≥ ~$2,000 USD (approximately 6× minimum wage). Must be a guaranteed lifetime pension from government or private source.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year temporary, renewable. Permanent residency available after 4 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 4 years of residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.br/mre/
- **Community verdict**: Works well (clear requirements; documentation must be apostilled and translated).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: VITEM XIV — Digital Nomad (Resolução CNIG MJSP No. 45/2021)
- **Conditions**: Income ≥ $1,500/mo USD from foreign source OR bank funds ≥ $18,000 USD. Employment contract or freelance with foreign entities. Health insurance valid in Brazil. No Brazilian employment permitted. ^[https://www.gov.br/mre/pt-br/consulado-sao-francisco/visas/vitem-xiv-digital-nomad]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year initial, renewable once (2 years maximum). In-country conversion from tourist visa possible (apply within 30 days of arrival via MigranteWeb).
- **Path to citizenship**: VITEM XIV time counts toward 4-year naturalization requirement.
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.br/mre/pt-br/consulado-sao-francisco/visas/vitem-xiv-digital-nomad
- **Community verdict**: Works well (one of the cheapest DN visas globally; $1,500/mo threshold among lowest in G20).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: VITEM V — Work Visa
- **Conditions**: University degree + job offer from Brazilian employer; employer must demonstrate labor market need. No separate "Blue Card" equivalent.
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 2 years, tied to employment.
- **Path to citizenship**: 4 years of residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.br/mre/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (employer sponsorship is key bottleneck; CNPJ-registered employer required).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: VITEM V — Work Visa (Trabalho)
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from Brazilian employer; employer must be registered (CNPJ); labor market approval.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 4 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.br/mre/
- **Community verdict**: Mixed.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: VITEM IV — Student Visa
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized Brazilian institution; proof of financial means; health insurance.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student years count toward 4-year clock.
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.br/mre/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: VITEM XI — Family Reunion Visa
- **Conditions**: Family relationship with Brazilian citizen or resident: spouse, minor children, parents. Same-sex unions recognized.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 1 year (spouse/parent of Brazilian).
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.br/mre/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Retirement Visa (same as passive income route)
- **Conditions**: Guaranteed lifetime pension ≥ ~$2,000/mo USD.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2-year temporary → permanent after 4 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 4 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.gov.br/mre/
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. No direct citizenship-by-investment program.

## Citizenship
- **Standard residence requirement**: 4 years of continuous legal residence. ^[https://www.astons.com/blog/how-to-get-brazilian-citizenship]
- **Reduced**: 1 year if married to Brazilian citizen or parent of Brazilian child (max 3 months absence).
- **Language**: Portuguese proficiency required; reading, writing, speaking.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted. Brazil does not require renunciation.
- **Jus soli**: Child born in Brazil automatically receives Brazilian citizenship.
- **Application**: Online via platform "Naturalização"; processed by Ministry of Justice.
- **Mercosur mobility**: Brazilian citizens enjoy residency rights in all MERCOSUR countries.

## Tax
- **Residency trigger**: 183+ days in any 12-month period, or holding permanent visa with intent to reside.
- **Tax residents**: Worldwide income taxed at progressive rates 0%–27.5%.
- **Capital gains**: 15%–22.5% on Brazilian-source gains.
- **No territorial system**: Brazil taxes worldwide income of residents, unlike many Latin American neighbors.

## IT / Digital Nomad
VITEM XIV is one of the most affordable DN visas in the G20 at $1,500/mo. Key hubs: São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Florianópolis, Salvador, Belo Horizonte. Internet infrastructure solid in major cities. Cost of living is moderate to low relative to dollar income.

## Yacht / Maritime
Extensive Atlantic coastline (7,491 km). Major ports: Rio de Janeiro, Santos, Salvador, Recife. Foreign-flagged yachts may enter under temporary admission. Brazilian yacht registration available but complex. Emerging superyacht infrastructure in Angra dos Reis and Ilhabela.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Russian passport holders need a visa for Brazil (e-visa available since 2025). Digital nomad VITEM XIV is accessible. Consulates in Moscow, Istanbul, Dubai. Brazil maintains neutral stance; no special restrictions for Russian applicants. Russian-speaking community in São Paulo and Rio.

## Community Pulse
VIPER is one of the most competitive investment residencies in Latin America with a clear path to permanent status and citizenship. VITEM XIV is cheap and increasingly popular. Bureaucracy remains the main friction: apostilles, certified translations, and processing times. Banking system accessible to residents.

## Status
- **Last monitored**: 2026-09-13
- **Recent changes**: MigranteWeb digital platform rollout (2026). VITEM XIV in-country conversion option. E-visa requirement for US/Canada/Australia travelers.

## Country Card

### General description
Brazil is the largest country in South America and the fifth-largest in the world by area, with over 211 million people. It has the largest economy in Latin America and is a member of BRICS and MERCOSUR. Brazil offers a well-structured immigration system with the VIPER investor visa (from BRL 600K), the VITEM XIV digital nomad visa ($1,500/mo — one of the cheapest in the G20), and a relatively fast 4-year path to citizenship. The Brazilian passport provides 170 visa-free destinations and MERCOSUR mobility rights.

### Well-known strong points
- One of the cheapest digital nomad visas in the G20 ($1,500/mo threshold)
- Competitive investor residency (VIPER from ~$115,000 USD business investment)
- Fast citizenship path: 4 years (1 year for spouses)
- MERCOSUR residency rights for citizens and permanent residents
- Large, diverse economy with opportunities beyond tourism/real estate
- Low cost of living relative to North America/Europe (Numbeo index 28.0)

### Well-known problems
- Very high violent crime rates — homicide rate ~20–25 per 100,000 in major cities
- Bureaucratic complexity (apostilles, certified translations, multiple agencies)
- Worldwide taxation for residents — no territorial system unlike many Latin American peers
- Portuguese language barrier — Spanish is not sufficient for naturalization
- Infrastructure quality varies dramatically between regions (Southeast vs. North/Northeast)
- Economic volatility and currency depreciation risk for foreign-income earners
