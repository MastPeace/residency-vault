## Overview
Argentina's immigration is managed by the Dirección Nacional de Migraciones (DNM). Argentina offers one of the fastest citizenship paths in the Americas — 2 continuous years of legal residence. The main residency routes are the Rentista (passive income), Pensionado (retiree), Investor, and the Digital Nomad Visa (180 + 180 days). A Citizenship by Investment program (USD 500,000) is expected to launch in late 2026 under Decree 524/2025. Decree 366/2025 (May 2025) tightened residency requirements significantly. ^[https://www.argentina.gob.ar/migraciones]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporaria — Inversionista (Art. 23, Ley 25.871)
- **Conditions**: Statutory minimum ARS 1,500,000 (~$2,000 USD). In practice, productive investment via SUACE from ~$70,000 USD with business plan and local employment. Real estate investment may also qualify. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/argentina-residency]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1-year temporary, renewable. Permanent residency after 3 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 2 continuous years (without exits) from establishment of any legal residency; permanent residency not required before citizenship application.
- **Official source**: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/migraciones
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (the statutory threshold is absurdly low but practical enforcement varies; SUACE track is more reliable).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Rentista (Art. 23(d), Ley 25.871)
- **Conditions**: Passive income ≥ 5× Argentine minimum wage (SMVM). At May 2026 SMVM ARS 363,000: ~$1,500/mo USD. Income from rentals, dividends, royalties, annuities from abroad. Must be transferred through authorized banking channels. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/argentina-residency]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable up to 3 years. Permanent residency after 3 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 2 continuous years of residence (any legal category).
- **Official source**: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/migraciones
- **Community verdict**: Works well (low threshold; the 2-year citizenship clock is the main attraction).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Residente Transitorio — Nómada Digital (Disposición 758/2022, Art. 24(h) Ley 25.871)
- **Conditions**: Only for nationals of visa-waiver countries (US, Canada, UK, EU, Australia, etc.). No published minimum income — typical requirement $2,000–$2,500/mo from foreign sources. Work must be remote for foreign employer/clients. Cannot work for Argentine entities. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/argentina-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 180 days, renewable once for another 180 days (360 days max). This is a transitory residence — does NOT count toward permanent residency or citizenship clock.
- **Path to citizenship**: Not direct; must switch to Rentista or other temporary category to start the 2-year clock.
- **Official source**: https://ctoro.cancilleria.gob.ar/en/visa-digital-nomads
- **Community verdict**: Works well for short stays (cheap, fast); dead-end for long-term residency goals.

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporaria — Trabajo
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with Argentine employer. No separate "Blue Card."
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 2 continuous years.
- **Official source**: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/migraciones
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (Argentine employer registration required; AFIP (tax authority) compliance is complex).

### startup_entrepreneur
Subsumed under the Inversionista category. Business plan + qualifying investment required.

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporaria — Trabajo (Art. 23(a), Ley 25.871)
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with Argentine entity; employer registered with AFIP.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 2 continuous years.
- **Official source**: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/migraciones
- **Community verdict**: Mixed.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporaria — Estudiante
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in recognized institution; proof of financial means.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student years count toward 2-year clock.
- **Official source**: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/migraciones
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Reunificación Familiar (Art. 22(a), Ley 25.871)
- **Conditions**: Sponsor with valid Argentine residence; spouse, minor children, parents.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Spouse: exemption from 2-year residency requirement (no minimum).
- **Official source**: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/migraciones
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Pensionado
- **Conditions**: Guaranteed pension ≥ ~$1,700–$2,000/mo USD from government or private source.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable up to 3 years. Permanent residency after 3 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 2 continuous years of residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/migraciones
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### citizenship_by_investment
- **Official name**: CBI — Citizenship by Investment (Decree 524/2025, July 2025)
- **Status**: Legal framework published; NOT YET OPERATIONAL pending secondary regulations from Ministry of Economy. Expected launch late 2026.
- **Expected conditions**: USD 500,000 in productive business investment, OR USD 1,000,000 in government bonds. Direct citizenship without 2-year residency requirement. Processing: 30 business days expected.
- **Official source**: https://www.argentina.gob.ar
- **Community verdict**: Pending launch; do not commit funds until regulations are published.

## Citizenship
- **Standard route**: 2 continuous years of legal residence in Argentina. Under Decree 366/2025, ANY exit during the 2-year period can reset the clock at judicial discretion. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/argentina-citizenship]
- **Marriage/child**: Spouses and parents of Argentine citizens are exempt from minimum residency.
- **Language**: Basic Spanish proficiency (assessed in interview; no formal exam).
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted without restriction.
- **Processing**: Citizenship applications transferred from federal courts to DNM as of October 2025 (Decree 366/2025). Timeline: 2–3 years from filing.
- **Passport**: Henley rank 15th, ~170 visa-free destinations; Mercosur mobility rights.

## Tax
- **Residency trigger**: 183+ days in any 12-month period, or center of vital interests, or Argentine residency card.
- **Tax residents**: Worldwide income taxed at progressive rates 5%–35%.
- **No territorial system**: Argentina taxes worldwide income.
- **Milei administration**: Tax simplification and potential flat-tax reforms under discussion; not enacted as of mid-2026.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Argentina's DN visa is cheap and fast but short (360 days max) and does not lead to residency. Buenos Aires is the primary hub with a large expat/nomad community and solid coworking infrastructure. Internet is good in BA, variable elsewhere. Inflation is a major concern and distorts costs; the USD blue rate provides significant arbitrage for foreign income earners.

## Yacht / Maritime
Atlantic coastline from Buenos Aires to Patagonia. Major ports: Buenos Aires, Mar del Plata, Ushuaia. Yacht registration possible but limited superyacht infrastructure. Ushuaia is a gateway to Antarctica.

## Notes for RU Citizens
Russian passport holders are visa-free for tourist entry (90 days). Rentista and Pensionado routes accessible. Consulates in Moscow (limited operations), Istanbul, Dubai. No special restrictions beyond standard requirements. Buenos Aires has a Russian-speaking community. Argentine banks may require DNI (residency card) for account opening.

## Community Pulse
Argentina's 2-year citizenship timeline is among the fastest globally and is the primary draw. Decree 366/2025's "no exits" rule is the biggest recent tightening — applicants must plan for 2 continuous years in-country. The DN visa is a useful short-term bridge but not a path to citizenship. Inflation and economic instability remain the main practical concerns. The pending CBI program, if launched at $500K, would be a game-changer.

## Status

- **Recent changes**: Decree 366/2025 (May 2025) — tightened residency continuity, citizenship processing moved to DNM. Decree 524/2025 (July 2025) — CBI framework. SMVM minimum wage updates alter Rentista thresholds periodically.

## Country Card

### General description
Argentina is the second-largest country in South America, stretching from the subtropics to Patagonia. It has a highly educated population, a European-influenced culture, and one of the fastest citizenship paths in the world — 2 continuous years of legal residence. The economy has been plagued by chronic inflation and currency crises, but the parallel (blue) dollar exchange rate provides significant purchasing power for foreign-income residents. Buenos Aires is a major cultural capital with strong infrastructure for expats and digital nomads.

### Well-known strong points
- Fastest citizenship path in the Americas: 2 continuous years (no minimum for spouses of Argentines)
- MERCOSUR mobility — Argentine citizens enjoy residency rights across South America
- Strong passport (170 visa-free destinations, Henley rank 16)
- Low effective cost of living for USD/EUR earners at the parallel (blue) exchange rate
- Buenos Aires is a world-class city with European architecture, culture, and healthcare
- Rentista income threshold among the lowest in the region (~$1,500/mo)

### Well-known problems
- Chronic inflation (100%+ annually) — prices, contracts, and salaries in constant flux
- Decree 366/2025: ANY exit during 2-year citizenship qualifying period can reset the clock
- Digital nomad visa is a dead-end: 360 days max, does NOT count toward citizenship
- Worldwide taxation with no territorial option — foreign income fully taxable for residents
- Currency controls and parallel exchange rate create practical FX friction
- CBI program (Decree 524/2025) promised but not yet operational as of mid-2026
