## Overview
Uruguay's immigration is managed by the Dirección Nacional de Migración (DNM) under the Ministry of Interior. The system is straightforward: temporary residency (2 years), permanent residency (indefinite), then legal citizenship by naturalization. Uruguay offers one of the simplest digital nomad permits — 180 days, renewable once, no minimum income requirement, ~USD 30 fee. Citizenship requires 3 years of continuous residence (married/family) or 5 years (single). The Uruguayan passport provides ~150 visa-free destinations including Schengen. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/uruguay-digital-nomad-visa]

## Per-Program Detail

### investment_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia Permanente (radicación)
- **Conditions**: No fixed investment minimum published. Economic solvency assessed case by case by DNM. Real estate ownership, business activity, bank deposits all serve as evidence. Unlike Paraguay/Panama, Uruguay does not have a codified "investor visa" with published thresholds. ^[https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/corporate-immigration-2026/uruguay]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent residency (indefinite). No temporary stage.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years continuous residence (family ties) or 5 years (single). Clock starts from residency filing date, not card issuance date.
- **Official source**: https://www.gub.uy/direccion-nacional-migracion
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (discretionary; no published threshold means uncertainty; well-prepared files with lawyer succeed).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia — Rentista
- **Conditions**: Proof of sufficient regular income from abroad (rentals, dividends, pension). No published minimum — DNM assesses case by case. ~USD 1,500/mo is the practical benchmark. ^[https://www.uruguayxxi.gub.uy/en/invest/investors-guide/entry-requirements/download]
- **Duration / renewal**: Temporary 2 years → permanent residency.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3–5 years depending on family ties.
- **Official source**: https://www.gub.uy/direccion-nacional-migracion
- **Community verdict**: Works well (flexible; discretionary but generally reasonable for applicants with demonstrable means).

### digital_nomad_visa
- **Official name**: Hoja de Identidad Provisoria para Nómadas Digitales (Decree 238/022)
- **Conditions**: Remote work for foreign employer/clients/own company. **No minimum income** — sworn statement (affidavit) of self-sufficiency. No employer letter required. Open to ALL nationalities. Individual application only (no dependents). ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/uruguay-digital-nomad-visa]
- **Duration / renewal**: 180 days, renewable once (12 months max). Fee: ~USD 30, paid inside Uruguay.
- **Path to citizenship**: DN time does NOT count toward residency/citizenship clock. BUT: can convert to traditional temporary residency without leaving Uruguay.
- **Official source**: https://www.gub.uy/direccion-nacional-migracion
- **Community verdict**: Works well (cheapest and simplest nomad permit in Latin America; no income minimum; but 1-year max and no dependent inclusion).

### highly_skilled_employment
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporal — Trabajo
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with Uruguayan company. HIP (Hoja de Identidad Provisoria) for contracts under 6 months.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years temporary, renewable. Convertible to permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3–5 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.gub.uy/direccion-nacional-migracion
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### startup_entrepreneur
- **Official name**: Residencia Permanente (business/entrepreneur route)
- **Conditions**: Active business in Uruguay + evidence of economic solvency. No published capital minimum; discretionary.
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent residency.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3–5 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.gub.uy/direccion-nacional-migracion
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (open-ended; best navigated with local legal counsel).

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporal — Trabajo
- **Conditions**: Employment contract with Uruguayan employer. Alternative: HIP for contracts <6 months. ^[https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/corporate-immigration-2026/uruguay]
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years (temp) or 6 months (HIP).
- **Path to citizenship**: 3–5 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.gub.uy/direccion-nacional-migracion
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### student_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia Temporal — Estudiante
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in Uruguayan institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Course duration.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student time may count; case-by-case.
- **Official source**: https://www.gub.uy/direccion-nacional-migracion
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Reunificación Familiar (via Residencia)
- **Conditions**: Family tie to Uruguayan citizen or legal resident. MERCOSUR nationals have simplified route.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years temp → permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years for married applicants or those with Uruguayan children.
- **Official source**: https://www.gub.uy/direccion-nacional-migracion
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Residencia — Rentista / Pensionado
- **Conditions**: Pension or passive income; assessed case by case. No published minimum but ~USD 1,500/mo practical benchmark.
- **Duration / renewal**: 2 years temp → permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3–5 years.
- **Official source**: https://www.gub.uy/direccion-nacional-migracion
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### citizenship_by_investment
Not available. The "Tax Holiday 2.0" (Law 20.446) offers an 11-year tax exemption for foreigners investing ~USD 2,000,000+ in real estate, but this is a tax benefit, not a citizenship program.

## Citizenship
- **Naturalization (legal citizenship)**: 3 years of continuous legal residence for applicants with family in Uruguay (married, civil union, common-law partnership, dependent children in Uruguay). 5 years for single applicants. ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/uruguay-citizenship]
- **Clock starts** from date of arrival and residency filing — NOT from permanent residency card issuance. This is uniquely generous.
- **Physical presence**: At least 183 days/year during qualifying period.
- **Economic integration**: Must show genuine ties — property, business, employment, or profession.
- **Language**: Conversational Spanish required; no formal exam but assessed during judicial interview.
- **Processing time**: 12–24 months after filing.
- **Dual citizenship**: Permitted. No renunciation required.
- **Maintenance**: Naturalized citizens who live abroad for 3+ consecutive years may lose voting/holding-office privileges (not the legal status itself). Returning restores them.

## Tax
- **Residency trigger**: 183 days in Uruguay in a calendar year, or center of vital interests.
- **Tax system**: Worldwide income. Progressive rates 0%–36% (IRPF). ^[https://goldenharbors.com/articles/uruguay-citizenship]
- **Tax Holiday 2.0** (Law 20.446): 11-year exemption from Uruguayan income tax for qualifying foreign investors (~USD 2,000,000 in real estate). Foreign-source income not taxed during holiday.
- **Digital nomads**: Foreign-source income NOT taxed while on the digital nomad permit (non-resident status). Crossing 183 days triggers tax residency.
- **Wealth tax**: Yes (Impuesto al Patrimonio), progressive.

## IT / Digital Nomad
- DN permit is the cheapest and simplest in Latin America: ~USD 30, no income threshold, open to all nationalities. But: only 6+6 months, no dependents, doesn't count toward citizenship clock.
- Montevideo has good internet infrastructure and a growing tech scene (Uruguay is a major software exporter per capita).
- Long-term nomads should convert to temporary residency (Rentista) after the DN year.

## Yacht / Maritime
Not a major yachting hub. Montevideo and Punta del Este have marinas. No special yacht/captain residency program comparable to Panama or Malta. Uruguay is a prominent flag state for commercial vessels.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian citizens: visa-free for tourism up to 90 days. DN permit and residency applications are nationality-blind.
- Residency applications filed with DNM; documents must be apostilled and translated into Spanish.
- Banking: straightforward with cédula de identidad (Uruguayan ID).
- Uruguay has no specific restrictions targeting Russian applicants. It maintains a non-aligned, neutral foreign policy.
- Montevideo has a small Russian-speaking community.

## Community pulse
- **Overall**: Uruguay is a high-trust, stable democracy with strong institutions. Residency is accessible, citizenship is earned through genuine presence, not shortcuts.
- **Processing**: Residency card issuance 4–8 months; citizenship 12–24 months after qualifying period.
- **Rejection risk**: Low for well-prepared applications. DNM is discretionary but reasonable.
- **Cost**: Government fees are low; legal fees moderate (lawyer recommended for residency and citizenship).

## Country Card

### General description
Uruguay is South America's smallest Spanish-speaking country, wedged between Brazil and Argentina on the Atlantic coast. It is the region's most stable democracy, with the highest rule-of-law and freedom scores in Latin America (WJP 0.72, FH 96/100). Uruguay offers the cheapest and simplest digital nomad permit in the region (~$30, no minimum income, 6+6 months), a discretionary but generally reasonable residency system, and a citizenship path of 3–5 years. The cost of living is the highest in South America (Numbeo index 47.0), comparable to southern Europe. The passport provides ~157 visa-free destinations including Schengen.

### Well-known strong points
- Highest rule of law (WJP 0.72) and freedom (FH 96/100) scores in Latin America
- Cheapest digital nomad permit in the region: ~$30, no income minimum, open to all nationalities
- Fast citizenship: 3 years (family) / 5 years (single) — clock starts from filing date, not card issuance
- Strong institutions, reliable judiciary, low corruption — a genuine liberal democracy
- Tax Holiday 2.0: 11-year income tax exemption for ~$2,000,000+ real estate investors
- MERCOSUR mobility for citizens and permanent residents

### Well-known problems
- Highest cost of living in South America (Numbeo 47.0) — prices comparable to Portugal or Spain
- Digital nomad permit is short (12 months max) and does NOT count toward citizenship clock
- No published investment/residency thresholds — DNM uses case-by-case economic solvency assessment (uncertainty)
- Worldwide taxation (0%–36% progressive) — no territorial option
- Small, insular economy — limited job market, few expat services outside Montevideo
- Naturalized citizens who live abroad 3+ consecutive years may lose voting/holding-office privileges
