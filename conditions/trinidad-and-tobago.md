## Overview
Trinidad and Tobago (T&T), the wealthiest economy in the Anglophone Caribbean (oil and gas), offers **no citizenship or residence by investment**. The pathway is conventional: work permit → five years' continuous lawful residence → permanent residence → naturalisation. Citizenship decisions are discretionary (Minister of National Security), not a right. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidadian_and_Tobagonian_nationality_law]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Work Permit / Resident Status (Immigration Act, Ch. 18:01)
- **Conditions**: Valid job offer; permits renewed annually. After five continuous years of lawful residence, a person may apply for permanent residence (Section 5(3)/50(1) of the Immigration Act). ^[https://nationalsecurity.gov.tt/appforms/residence]
- **Duration / renewal**: Work permit 1 year renewable; permanent residence indefinite.
- **Path to citizenship**: PR for 5 years → naturalisation (discretionary).
- **Official source**: https://nationalsecurity.gov.tt/appforms/residence
- **Community verdict**: Works well (clear legal route but slow and discretionary; no online portal).

### family_reunification
- **Official name**: Sponsorship of spouse / parent / grandparent by a citizen or resident
- **Conditions**: Sponsor is a T&T citizen/resident; proof of relationship and support.
- **Duration / renewal**: Aligned with sponsor's status.
- **Path to citizenship**: Marriage to a citizen shortens the naturalisation clock.
- **Official source**: https://nationalsecurity.gov.tt/appforms/residence
- **Community verdict**: Works well for spouses of citizens.

## Citizenship
Naturalisation requires good character, adequate English, intent to reside, 12 months' continuous residence immediately before application, plus 5 years' aggregate residence (or government service) within the preceding 7 years. Dual citizenship recognised since 1988. Grant is at the Minister's discretion. ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidadian_and_Tobagonian_nationality_law]

## Tax
Personal income tax is progressive up to 25% (30% for very high earners); corporate tax ~30%. No capital gains tax. Territorial for foreign income in many cases. No special expat regime.

## IT / Digital Nomad
No digital-nomad or remote-work visa. CARICOM/CSME skilled nationals can move freely, but there is no general remote-worker route.

## Yacht / Maritime
Oil-and-gas-driven maritime economy (Point Lisas, Port of Spain). Chaguaramas is a yachting/boat-repair hub. Yacht registration available under the T&T flag.

## Notes for RU Citizens
No fast track; standard work-permit route only. Crime in Port of Spain is a frequent expat concern (FH notes organised-crime violence). Naturalisation is discretionary, so outcomes are unpredictable even after qualifying periods.

## Community Pulse
Expat forums describe T&T as economically comfortable (oil wealth) but procedurally slow: residence and naturalisation are discretionary, in-person (no online portal as of 2026), and can take years. The 5-year-residence → PR → 5-year-PR → citizenship ladder means a realistic ~10-year horizon.

## Country Card

### General description
Trinidad and Tobago is the industrial and financial hub of the Anglophone Caribbean, with a diversified oil-and-gas economy and one of the region's highest incomes per capita. It is a parliamentary democracy (republic since 1976) with a multi-ethnic population of African and Indian descent.

### Well-known strong points
- Highest GDP per capita in the Anglophone Caribbean (~$32,000 PPP)
- Strong regional passport (Henley 144, Schengen/UK access)
- Democratic (FH 82/100); vibrant media and civil society
- English-speaking; no capital gains tax

### Well-known problems
- High violent crime driven by organised gangs
- No residence/citizenship-by-investment; slow, discretionary naturalisation
- Corruption concerns among officials
- No digital-nomad or passive-income visa
