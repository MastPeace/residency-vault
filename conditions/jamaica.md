## Overview
Jamaica has no citizenship-by-investment or digital nomad programme. Residency is obtained through work permits, permanent residence applications, or family ties. English is the official language, simplifying integration. Naturalization requires a minimum of 5 years of residency (with the last 12 months continuous). Permanent residence processing takes 6–8 months. Dual citizenship is recognized. ^[https://congenjamaica-ny.org/citizenship-by-naturalization-marriage]

## Per-Program Detail

### employment_residency
- **Official name**: Jamaica Work Permit
- **Conditions**: Binding job offer from Jamaican employer; application via Ministry of Labour and Social Security. Employer must demonstrate no suitable local candidate. Work permit or Exemption Certificate required. All aliens 16+ intending to stay over 6 months must register as aliens (J$1,000 fee). ^[https://www.jamaicacgmiami.org/citizenship.html]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable. Fee varies by category and nationality.
- **Path to citizenship**: Work permit years count toward the 5-year naturalization requirement. Must have permanent residence.
- **Official source**: https://www.pica.gov.jm
- **Community verdict**: Works well (English-speaking; established process; employer-driven; some sectors face local-labour pressure).

### passive_income_residency
- **Official name**: Permanent Residence (independent means)
- **Conditions**: Prove financial status and means of support; valid passport; two passport photos; reasons for seeking permanent residence; two reputable references. Applications referred to Jamaica; processing 6–8 months. ^[https://www.jamaicacgmiami.org/citizenship.html]
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent Resident status; may be withdrawn at Minister's discretion.
- **Path to citizenship**: After 5 years residency (last 12 months continuous), apply for naturalization.
- **Official source**: https://www.pica.gov.jm
- **Community verdict**: Mixed (discretionary; 6–8 month processing; no published minimum income figure).

### retired_residency
- **Official name**: Permanent Residence (retiree/pensioner route)
- **Conditions**: Proof of pension or other independent means of support; same permanent residence application requirements as above. Many retirees combine with property purchase. No fixed statutory income threshold published.
- **Duration / renewal**: Permanent.
- **Path to citizenship**: 5 years residency → naturalization.
- **Official source**: https://www.pica.gov.jm
- **Community verdict**: Works well (popular with returning diaspora and retirees; English-speaking; strong RU/UK/Canadian expat presence in Montego Bay, Ocho Rios).

## Citizenship
- **By naturalization**: Minimum 5 years residency; last 12 months immediately preceding application must be continuous. Also: aggregate 4 years in the 5 years before application. Good character, permanent residence and livelihood, involvement in Jamaican society, intent to reside. ^[https://congenjamaica-ny.org/citizenship-by-naturalization-marriage]
- **By marriage**: Spouse of Jamaican citizen; application via R1 Form; US$260–300 fee; processing ~24 months.
- **By descent**: Children born abroad to Jamaican parents.
- **Dual citizenship**: Fully recognized (since 1999 constitutional amendment allowing dual citizenship).
- **Passport**: Visa-free/visa-on-arrival to ~90+ countries (CARICOM, UK, EU Schengen, China).

## Tax
- Territorial: foreign-source income not taxed for non-domiciled residents.
- Personal income tax: 25% up to JMD 6,000,000; 30% above.
- No capital gains tax. No inheritance tax. No wealth tax.
- No digital nomad tax regime.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Not available. No dedicated digital nomad visa. Remote workers typically use tourist entry (visa-free for many nationalities; up to 90 days, extendable). For longer stays, work-permit or permanent residence routes apply. No formal legal framework for nomads.

## Yacht / Maritime
Jamaica has an international ship registry (Maritime Authority of Jamaica). Yacht registration possible. Major marinas in Montego Bay, Port Antonio, Kingston. No yacht-residence programme.

## Notes for RU citizens
- Russian nationals require a visa to enter Jamaica (no visa-free). 
- Work permit and permanent residence routes available but no dedicated program for RU expats.
- English-speaking environment; large diaspora return migration.
- Warmer reception than some Caribbean jurisdictions; no blanket restrictions.

## Community pulse
- Jamaica is more lifestyle-migration than passport-acquisition destination. English-speaking, culturally vibrant, large diaspora.
- Immigration bureaucracy is document-heavy and slow (6–8 months for permanent residence).
- Work permits employer-driven; 80/20 local-foreign labour ratio considerations.
- Popular with returning Jamaicans and long-stay retirees.

## Country Card

### General description
Jamaica is the third-largest Caribbean island, known globally for its cultural influence (reggae, cuisine, athletics) and tourism brand. Its economy is service-driven with tourism as the dominant sector, supplemented by mining (bauxite) and agriculture. Jamaica offers permanent residence routes for retirees and independent-means applicants, with a moderate 5-year path to naturalization. The country operates a territorial tax system, has no capital gains or inheritance tax, and English is the official language. Dual citizenship is fully recognized since 1999.

### Well-known strong points
- English-speaking with no language barrier for most expats
- Territorial tax system: foreign-source income not taxed for non-domiciled residents
- Moderate 5-year path to citizenship with dual citizenship fully recognized
- Visa-free access to ~90 destinations including UK, EU Schengen, China
- Rich cultural heritage and lifestyle; large diaspora communities
- No capital gains tax, inheritance tax, or wealth tax

### Well-known problems
- High crime rate; violent crime concentrated in certain urban areas
- Bureaucratic immigration processing: 6–8 months for permanent residence
- No dedicated digital nomad visa; remote-work framework absent
- Limited economic opportunities; high youth unemployment
- Healthcare infrastructure limited outside Kingston and tourist zones
- Permanent residence is discretionary with no published minimum income
