## Overview

Serbia is an EU candidate country (not yet a member). Passport ranks ~38th globally with visa-free access to ~135 destinations. Russian citizens enjoy visa-free entry (30 days). Serbia permits dual citizenship. The most common pathway for independent relocants is business registration (LLC) → temporary residence → permanent residence (3 years) → citizenship application (3 more years). ^[https://legalclarity.org/serbia-citizenship-requirements-descent-and-naturalization]

## Per-Program Detail

### Business Registration Residence (startup_entrepreneur)

- **Official name**: Privremeni boravak po osnovu osnivanja privrednog društva (Temporary residence based on company registration)
- **Conditions**: Register a Serbian LLC (DOO); minimum share capital ~RSD 100 (~€1), though practical minimum is higher for bank account opening. No minimum turnover or employee requirement. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/serbia-residence-permit]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable annually while company exists and taxes are filed.
- **Path to citizenship**: Temporary residence (3 years) → permanent residence (3 years) → citizenship application. Total: ~6 years. ^[https://legalclarity.org/serbia-citizenship-requirements-descent-and-naturalization]
- **Official source**: [Welcome to Serbia — Foreign Nationals' Portal](https://welcometoserbia.gov.rs/home)
- **Community verdict**: Works well — most popular route for independent relocants. Business registration is quick (1–3 days). Main friction: bank account opening for non-residents. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/serbia-residence-permit]

### Property Ownership Residence (passive_income_residency / retired_residency)

- **Official name**: Privremeni boravak po osnovu vlasništva nad nepokretnostima (Temporary residence based on real estate ownership)
- **Conditions**: Own residential property in Serbia. No minimum property value specified. Must demonstrate sufficient means of support. ^[https://www.mup.gov.rs/wps/portal/en/information/Temporary+residence]
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable annually.
- **Path to citizenship**: Same 3+3 year path.
- **Official source**: [MUP — Temporary residence](https://www.mup.gov.rs/wps/portal/en/information/Temporary+residence)
- **Community verdict**: Works well — straightforward if you own property. No minimum value makes it accessible.

### Highly Skilled Employment / Employment Residency

- **Official name**: Jedinstvena dozvola za boravak i rad (Single Permit for residence and work)
- **Conditions**: Valid employment contract with a Serbian employer. Employer must justify hiring a foreign national. Combined residence + work authorization. ^[https://welcometoserbia.gov.rs/residence-and-work-permit]
- **Duration / renewal**: Up to 1 year, renewable with continued employment.
- **Path to citizenship**: Same 3+3 year path.
- **Official source**: [Welcome to Serbia — Residence and Work Permit](https://welcometoserbia.gov.rs/residence-and-work-permit)
- **Community verdict**: Works well — Single Permit system simplified the process (previously separate residence + work permits).

### Student Residency

- **Official name**: Privremeni boravak po osnovu studiranja
- **Conditions**: Enrollment in an accredited Serbian educational institution.
- **Duration / renewal**: Duration of studies, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: Student years count toward temporary residence (3 years → PR).
- **Official source**: [MUP — Temporary residence](https://www.mup.gov.rs/wps/portal/en/information/Temporary+residence)
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

### Family Reunification

- **Official name**: Spajanje porodice
- **Conditions**: Spouse or minor child of a Serbian resident or citizen. Marriage to a Serbian citizen grants residence.
- **Duration / renewal**: 1 year, renewable.
- **Path to citizenship**: 3 years of marriage + permanent residence for citizenship by marriage.
- **Official source**: [MUP — Temporary residence](https://www.mup.gov.rs/wps/portal/en/information/Temporary+residence)
- **Community verdict**: Works well.

## Citizenship

- **By naturalization**: 3 years of permanent residence (after 3 years temporary = ~6 years total uninterrupted legal residence). ^[https://legalclarity.org/serbia-citizenship-requirements-descent-and-naturalization]
- **By marriage**: 3 years of marriage to a Serbian citizen + permanent residence.
- **By descent**: Available if at least one parent is a Serbian citizen.
- **Release from previous citizenship**: Generally required by law, but dual citizenship is permitted in practice for many cases (Serbia does not aggressively enforce renunciation). ^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_nationality_law]
- **Language**: Serbian language proficiency not formally tested for naturalization (unusual for Europe).
- **Timeline**: ~6 years total. Processing time: 6–12 months after application.

## Tax

- **Personal income tax**: Flat 10% (capped for high earners). ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/serbia-residence-permit]
- **Corporate tax**: 15% (one of the lowest in Europe).
- **Social security**: ~14% (employee + employer contributions).
- **No wealth tax, no inheritance tax**.
- **Double taxation treaties**: ~60 countries including Russia.
- **Tax residency**: 183 days physical presence or center of vital interests in Serbia.

## IT / Digital Nomad

Serbia does not have a formal [[digital-nomad-visa|digital nomad visa]] visa. However, the business registration (DOO) route is the de-facto pathway for freelancers and remote workers: incorporate a Serbian LLC, obtain residence as director/owner, pay 10% flat income tax. Belgrade and Novi Sad are vibrant tech hubs. The 10% flat tax is attractive for IT professionals. No minimum stay requirement to maintain the residence permit (though absence >10 months in 3 years breaks continuity for PR). ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/serbia-residence-permit]

## Yacht / Maritime

Not applicable. Serbia is landlocked. Limited river boating on the Danube and Sava.

## Notes for RU citizens

Serbia is one of the most accessible destinations for Russian citizens: visa-free entry (30 days), no sanctions, direct flights from Russia, widely spoken Russian/Serbian mutual intelligibility, accessible banking (some banks serve RU clients). Business registration is straightforward (1–3 days). The 6-year path to citizenship is among the fastest in Europe. Serbia has been a primary relocation destination for Russian IT professionals and entrepreneurs since 2022.

## Community Pulse

Expats report generally smooth processes. Main complaints: bureaucratic delays, bank account opening difficulties for non-residents. The Single Permit system and online portal (welcometoserbia.gov.rs) have improved efficiency. Backlog: moderate. Rejections: rare for properly documented applications.

## Status

- **Last monitored**: 2026-10-12
- **Recent changes**: Single Permit system operational. Online application portal (Foreign Nationals' Portal) functioning.
- **Watch items**: EU accession progress. Potential changes to 3-year temporary residence requirement.

## Country Card

Serbia is a Balkan country at the crossroads of Central and Southeast Europe, landlocked but positioned on the Danube River corridor. An EU candidate country (not yet a member), it operates a flat 10% personal income tax and a straightforward business-registration pathway to residence (~6 years to citizenship). Serbian and Russian are mutually intelligible, making Serbia the #1 relocation hub for Russian-speaking IT professionals and entrepreneurs since 2022.

### Well-known strong points
- Fastest citizenship path in the Balkans (~6 years) with no formal language test
- Flat 10% income tax + 15% corporate tax — among the lowest in Europe
- Business registration (DOO) takes 1–3 days; de-facto digital nomad pathway
- Russian visa-free (30 days), widely spoken Russian, no sanctions
- Vibrant tech hubs in Belgrade and Novi Sad; growing startup/VC ecosystem
- Dual citizenship permitted in practice

### Well-known problems
- Bureaucratic delays in residence permit processing (MUP)
- Bank account opening for non-residents can be difficult and slow
- No formal digital nomad visa — business registration is the only independent pathway
- Belgrade cost of living has risen sharply post-2022 (rents up 30–50%)
- Landlocked — no coastline access
- EU accession stalled; political erosion of media and civil liberties (Freedom House: Partly Free, 56/100)
