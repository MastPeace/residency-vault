## Overview
Serbia is an EU candidate country (not yet a member). It offers accessible residence permits and a relatively short path to citizenship: 3 years of temporary residence + 3 years of permanent residence = ~6 years total before citizenship application. Russian citizens enjoy visa-free entry (30 days). Serbia permits dual citizenship. ^[https://mycitizensagency.com/serbian-citizenship]

## Residence
**Temporary residence** (boravak): issued on grounds of employment, business, property ownership, education, family reunification, marriage to a Serbian citizen. ^[https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/serbia-residence-permit]
- Valid: up to 1 year, renewable
- Single Permit (Jedinstvena dozvola): combined residence + work authorization for employed workers
- Property ownership: no minimum value specified for residence by property
- Business registration: a popular route — incorporate a Serbian LLC, deposit capital (~€1)
- Applications submitted online through the Foreign Nationals' Portal

**Permanent residence**: available after 3 years of continuous temporary residence (with permissible absences: total ≤ 10 months or one absence ≤ 6 months). ^[https://mycitizensagency.com/serbian-citizenship]

**Fees (2026)**:
- Submission: ~430 RSD (~€4)
- Temporary residence approval: ~23,450 RSD (~€200)
- Single Permit: ~22,770 RSD (~€194)

## Citizenship
- 3 years of permanent residence (after 3 years temporary = ~6 years total)
- Release from previous citizenship generally required (exceptions exist)
- Dual citizenship permitted in many cases
- Citizenship by marriage: 3 years of marriage + permanent residence
- **Note**: Serbia does not have citizenship-by-investment

## Tax
Flat income tax: 10% (capped for high earners). Corporate tax: 15% (one of the lowest in Europe). Social security: ~14%. No wealth tax. Serbia has double taxation treaties with ~60 countries including Russia.

## IT / Digital Nomad
Serbia does not have a formal [[digital-nomad]] visa, but the business registration route serves as a practical substitute for freelancers and remote workers, similar to [[georgia]], but the business registration route serves as a practical substitute for freelancers and remote workers. Belgrade and Novi Sad are vibrant tech hubs with strong startup communities. Serbia's 10% flat income tax is attractive for IT professionals.

## Yacht / Maritime
Not applicable. Serbia is landlocked (limited river boating on the Danube).

## Notes for RU citizens
Serbia is one of the most accessible destinations for Russian citizens: visa-free entry (30 days), no sanctions, direct flights from Russia, widely spoken Russian/Serbian mutual intelligibility, and accessible banking. The business registration route is straightforward. Serbia has been a primary relocation destination for Russian IT professionals and entrepreneurs since 2022. The 6-year path to citizenship is among the fastest in Europe.
